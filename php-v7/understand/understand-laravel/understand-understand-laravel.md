# understand/understand-laravel

> 标签: Understand.io, laravel, logs, understand

## 简介

Laravel 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12 service provider for Understand.io

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
