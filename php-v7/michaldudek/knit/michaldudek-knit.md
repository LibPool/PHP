# michaldudek/knit

> 标签: database, datamapper, mongo, mysql, odm, orm, pgsql, popo, postgresql

## 简介

Knit ties your PHP objects with your database of choice in a simple way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
