# dynoser/base85

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

base85 encoder and decoder. ASCII85, vwx85, and vc85 (visually distinctive characters cyrillic)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
