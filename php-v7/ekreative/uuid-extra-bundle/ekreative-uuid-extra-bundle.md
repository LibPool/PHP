# ekreative/uuid-extra-bundle

> 标签: bundle, converter, denormalizer, form, guid, identifier, normalizer, param, paramconverter, ramsey, symfony, uuid

## 简介

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
