> 标签: L8, elocrypt, eloquent, eloquent aes, eloquent encryption, encrypt, encryption, l5, l9, laravel, laravel5, laravel5 aes, laravel5 encryption

## 简介

Automatically encrypt and decrypt Eloquent attributes with ease.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
