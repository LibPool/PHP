# aminembarki/nl2li

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, nl2li

## 简介

Simple Twig filter to turn a multiline text field into list items.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
