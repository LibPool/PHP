# aminembarki/hijridates

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, hijri-dates

## 简介

Craft plugin convert Gregorian date to Hijri date and vice versa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
