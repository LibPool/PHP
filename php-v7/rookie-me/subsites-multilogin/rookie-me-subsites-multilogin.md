# rookie-me/subsites-multilogin

> 标签: cms, multilogin, silverstripe, subsites

## 简介

Allow a single login process to propogate across multiple Subsite Domains.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
