# 10up/10up-experience

> 标签: 10up, wordpress

## 简介

The 10up Experience plugin configures WordPress to better protect and inform clients, aligned to 10up's best practices

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
