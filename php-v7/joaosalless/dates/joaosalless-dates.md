# joaosalless/dates

> 标签: business-days, datas comemorativas, dates, dias úteis, feriados, holidays, horário de expediente, joaosalless, office-hours, work-days

## 简介

Date library for identification of holidays or commemorative dates (national, state, municipal), automatic calculation of working days and office hours.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
