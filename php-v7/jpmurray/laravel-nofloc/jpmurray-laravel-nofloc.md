# jpmurray/laravel-nofloc

> 标签: FLoC, laravel, privacy

## 简介

Adds a header response that should be opting out your site from being used for Google's FloC tracking.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
