# fruitware/yandex-geocoder-bundle

> 标签: api, bundle, geo, geocode, geocoder, maps, symfony, yandex

## 简介

Symfony2 bundle for interactions with geo-coding Yandex.Maps

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
