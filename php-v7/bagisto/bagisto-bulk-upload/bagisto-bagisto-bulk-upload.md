# bagisto/bagisto-bulk-upload

> 标签: bagisto, bulk upload, product upload

## 简介

Add products using CSV or XLS in bagisto.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
