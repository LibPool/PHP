# digitaldiff/craft-content-subscriptions

> 标签: Craft, content, mail, subscription

## 简介

Send automatic email notifications whenever you publish new content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
