# isotopsweden/wp-syncs

> 标签: multisite, plugin, post, syncs, term, wordpress

## 简介

Syncs synchronizes posts and terms between multisites

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
