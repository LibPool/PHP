# tarioch/pheal-bundle

> 标签: EVE API, EVE Online, Pheal, PhealNG, symfony

## 简介

Adds Pheal support to your Symfony2 project.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
