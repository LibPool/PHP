# stinger/ckeditor-image2-bundle

> 标签: CKEditor, image2, symfony

## 简介

Symfony2 Bundle to integrate the CKEditor plugin Image2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：http://ckeditor.com/addon/image2
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/Stinger-Soft/CKEditorImage2Bundle.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/stinger%2Fckeditor-image2-bundle
