## 简介

Read-only runtime, SAPI, stale-worker, configuration, and IaC auditor for PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) and Node.js October 2026 advisories.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
