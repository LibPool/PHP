# nineinchnick/yii2-audit

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Extensions to the Yii 2 PHP framework allowing tracking and viewing change history of a model.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
