# aligent/orocommerce-abn-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

OroCommerce Bundle to add an ABN to the customer registration form and Customer Entity

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
