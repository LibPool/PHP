# campaignchain/channel-citrix

> 标签: GoToWebinar, campaignchain

## 简介

Connects to Citrix services such as GoToMeeting or GoToWebinar.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
