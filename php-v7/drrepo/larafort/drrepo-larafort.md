# drrepo/larafort

> 标签: gateway, laravel, payfort, payment

## 简介

Larafort is a simple package to process payments throught PAYFORT Online Payment Gateway. (Using wshurafa/laravel-payfort)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
