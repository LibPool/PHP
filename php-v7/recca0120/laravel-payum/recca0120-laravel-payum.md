## 简介

Rich payment solutions for Laravel framework. Paypal, payex, authorize.net, be2bill, omnipay, recurring paymens, instant notifications and many more

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
