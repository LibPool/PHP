# alkoumi/carbon-date-translator

> 标签: arabic, arabic-date-translator, carbon, carbon-date, carbon-date-translator, date, date-translator, translator

## 简介

Laravel package to translate carbon date to our Amazing 💝 Arabic language even the future dates. to look like [منذ ٥ دقائق] , [بعد سبع ساعات]

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
