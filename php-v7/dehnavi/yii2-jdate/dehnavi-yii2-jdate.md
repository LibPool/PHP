# dehnavi/yii2-jdate

> 标签: Jalali, date-range, datepicker, persian-calendar, yii2, yii2-extension

## 简介

Jalali date and time utilities and a range-capable date picker for Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
