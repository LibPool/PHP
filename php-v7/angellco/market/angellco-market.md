# angellco/market

> 标签: Craft, cms, commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, market, marketplace, multi-vendor

## 简介

A fully-stocked multi-vendor solution for Craft Commerce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
