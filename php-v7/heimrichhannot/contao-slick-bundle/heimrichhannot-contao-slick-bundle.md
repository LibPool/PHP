# heimrichhannot/contao-slick-bundle

> 标签: carousel, contao, slick, slider

## 简介

A content slider/carousel for contao based on kenwheelers slick carousel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
