# tibemolde/embedder

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, embedder

## 简介

Embed videos (YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook) easily!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
