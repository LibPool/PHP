# qy-upup/sm-ai-pose-generator

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides a streamlined and robust integration for leveraging the sm-ai-pose-generator API, simplifying the process of generating AI-powered pose estimations from images.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
