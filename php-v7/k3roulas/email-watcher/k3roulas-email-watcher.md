# k3roulas/email-watcher

> 标签: email, imap, pop3, trigger, watcher

## 简介

Email watcher : trig an action on a new email (protocol pop3, imap)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
