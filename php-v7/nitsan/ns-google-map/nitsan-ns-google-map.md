# nitsan/ns-google-map

> 标签: T3Planet, TYPO3 CMS, direction, google, map, search

## 简介

TYPO3 Google Map Extension - Introducing the TYPO3 Google Map Extension! This TYPO3 extension enables you to seamlessly add and customize Google Maps to your website with ease. Designed with responsive layouts and SEO-friendly features, it ensures a smooth and optimized experience for your visitors.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
