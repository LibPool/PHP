# nitsan/ns-news-slick

> 标签: News Slider, Slick Slider, TYPO3 CMS, nitsan

## 简介

News Slick Slider - This extension allow you to create beautiful slider for your news images on your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
