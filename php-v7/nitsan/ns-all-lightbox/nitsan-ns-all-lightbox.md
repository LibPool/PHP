# nitsan/ns-all-lightbox

> 标签: Darkbox, T3Planet, TYPO3 CMS, colorbox, fancybox, lightbox, magnific popup, prettyPhoto

## 简介

TYPO3 All-in-One Lightbox & Sliders - Introducing the TYPO3 All-in-One Extension, a plugin that effortlessly integrates popular jQuery slider plugins into your website with just a few clicks! Enjoy a range of slider options including Nivo Slider, Royal Slider, Owlcarousel, Sliderjs Slider, and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
