# nitsan/ns-cookies

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, cookie bar, gdpr, nitsan, privacy

## 简介

Easily install and configure simple cookie-bar to make GDPR compliant your TYPO3 site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
