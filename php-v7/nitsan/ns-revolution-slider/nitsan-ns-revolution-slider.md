# nitsan/ns-revolution-slider

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, revolution, slider

## 简介

TYPO3 Revolution Slider - Create powerful and modern sliders for your TYPO3 website using the official Revolution Slider integration. Easily build sliders with drag-and-drop, 250+ templates, and 2000+ pre-built elements with advanced transitions and effects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
