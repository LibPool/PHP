# zgldh/discount-and-coupon

> 标签: coupon, discount

## 简介

The core logic about discount and coupon. Useful to manage discount campaign, defining coupon and calculate the final price.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
