# onmotion/yii2-gallery

> 标签: blueimp, gallery, module, photo, yii2

## 简介

blueimp gallery with fileupload in your Yii2 application

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
