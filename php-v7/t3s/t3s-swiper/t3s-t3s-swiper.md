# t3s/t3s-swiper

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, content_blocks

## 简介

Swiper is the most modern free mobile touch slider with hardware accelerated transitions and amazing native behavior.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
