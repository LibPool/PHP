> 标签: Agenda, ERP, crm, invoice, order, proposal, sme, stock

## 简介

Dolibarr ERP & CRM is a modern and easy to use web software to manage your business

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
