# dnadesign/silverstripe-googlesitesearch

> 标签: googlesitesearch, search, seo, silverstripe

## 简介

Add Google Site Search to your SilverStripe Site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
