# venustheme/magento2-product-list

> 标签: landofcoder, landofcoder product list, magento 2 product list, product list for magento 2, product list magento 2

## 简介

Venustheme Magento 2 Product List supports you to create a wanted list of products based on attribute, types, conditions of items.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
