# m2e/fulfillment-by-amazon-fba-multi-channel-mcf-magento-adobe-commerce

> 标签: Cross-Channel Order Fulfillment, E-commerce Fulfillment Solutions, Fulfillment by Amazon, Multi-Channel Fulfillment, amazon fba, amazon mcf, amazon mcf magento 2, ecommerce, m2e, magento

## 简介

M2E Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF) is a Magento trusted extension that integrates your Adobe Commerce system with MCF, allowing sellers to use Amazon's Multi-Channel Fulfillment service to ship orders across various sales channels, not just Amazon

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
