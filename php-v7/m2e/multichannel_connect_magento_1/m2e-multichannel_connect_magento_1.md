# m2e/multichannel_connect_magento_1

> 标签: Magento 1, TikTok , extension for magento 1, kaufland, kaufland global marketplace, m2e, magento multichannel, manage orders, onbuy, otto, otto market, sell on marketplace, shein, temu, tiktok shop

## 简介

M2E Multichannel Connect is a Magento 1 extension that lets you add products to Shein, Temu, TikTok Shop, OnBuy, Kaufland, and Otto while syncing orders and inventory. List your products and start selling with M2E

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
