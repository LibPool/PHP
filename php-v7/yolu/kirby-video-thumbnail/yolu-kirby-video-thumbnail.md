# yolu/kirby-video-thumbnail

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Kirby Panel plugin that auto-generates a thumbnail from a video frame on upload, with a scrubber to pick the exact frame.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
