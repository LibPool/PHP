> 标签: ababil, cli, cli-app, console, developer, developer-tools, portfolio, terminal

## 简介

Beautiful CLI portfolio with gradient colors and emoji support - showcase your skills, projects, and contact info in the terminal

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
