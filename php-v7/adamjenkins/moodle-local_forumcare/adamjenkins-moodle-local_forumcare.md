# adamjenkins/moodle-local_forumcare

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Lets students report problematic posts in mod_forum forums, with configurable auto-moderation (hide posts, suspend users) and a teacher review queue.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
