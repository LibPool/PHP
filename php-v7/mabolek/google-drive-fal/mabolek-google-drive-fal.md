# mabolek/google-drive-fal

> 标签: extension, google drive, typo3

## 简介

Google Drive™ for FAL: Use Google Drive™ as File Storage in your TYPO3 installation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
