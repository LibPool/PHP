# dd/evolutioncms-snippets-ddgetdocumentfield

> 标签: MODX Evo, dd group, dd studio, ddgetdocumentfield, divandesign, evo, evo cms, evolution cms, evolutioncms, get document field, modx, modx evolution

## 简介

Snippet gets the necessary document fields (and TVs) by its id.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
