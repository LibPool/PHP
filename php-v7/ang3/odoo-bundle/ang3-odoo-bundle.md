# ang3/odoo-bundle

> 标签: api, bundle, client, database, dbal, object, odoo, orm, persistence

## 简介

Symfony bundle to manage your Odoo databases.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
