# robinsaillard/google-place-autocomplete-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides a Google Place Autocomplete Type, the most minimalist, unobtrusive way possible.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
