# drupal/rightup

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A media, news and magazine site template recipe for Varbase, providing a modern recipe-first approach to initializing editorial and newsroom sites.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
