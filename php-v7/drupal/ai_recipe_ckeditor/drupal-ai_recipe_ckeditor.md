# drupal/ai_recipe_ckeditor

> 标签: CKEditor, ai, drupal, recipe

## 简介

Adds AI integration to the CKEditor.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
