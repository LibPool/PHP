# drupal/agora_transparency

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A transparency and open government site template for Drupal CMS, for councils, public bodies and organisations that publish accountability information.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
