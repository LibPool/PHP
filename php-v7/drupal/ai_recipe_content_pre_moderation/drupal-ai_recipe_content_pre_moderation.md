# drupal/ai_recipe_content_pre_moderation

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds an AI-assisted pre-moderation workflow for Article content before publication.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
