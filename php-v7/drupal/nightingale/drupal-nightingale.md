# drupal/nightingale

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

An accessible, component-driven Drupal CMS site template implementing the GOV.UK Design System and NHS standards for public sector agencies, campaigns, and research portals.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
