# drupal/varbase_privacy

> 标签: Consent management, Varbase recipes, consent, cookie, drupal recipe, drupal recipes, recipe, varbase recipe

## 简介

A recipe that provides default cookie consent options and privacy settings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
