# insolita/fakerprovider

> 标签: faker

## 简介

Faker Provider for Russian Shop - product titles, models, materials,units,sizes, categories, attributes, trademarks

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
