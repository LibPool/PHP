# payone-gmbh/magento-2

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

PAYONE payment gateway for all German online and offline payment methods including PayPal, all major Credit Cards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
