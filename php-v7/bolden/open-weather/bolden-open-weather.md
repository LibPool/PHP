# bolden/open-weather

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, weather

## 简介

Integrate weather information from any location right into your Craft CMS 3 website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
