# dlnsk/h-rbac

> 标签: Policy, ability, gate, hierarchical, laravel, permission, rbac, role

## 简介

Based on native Laravel's gates and policies. Hierarchical RBAC with callbacks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
