# xety/cake3-sluggable

> 标签: cake3, cakephp, fields, orm, plugin, records, slug, sluggable

## 简介

A simple Cake3 plugin to slug fields and find records by slug.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
