# gsylvestre/symfony-faker-fixtures

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Fast generation of realistic fake datas for your database. Symfony/Doctrine/Faker/Make super combo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
