# typisttech/remove-medium-cross-links

> 标签: medium, plugin, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Remove Medium cross links footer from WordPress posts

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
