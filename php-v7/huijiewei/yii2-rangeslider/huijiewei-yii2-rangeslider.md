# huijiewei/yii2-rangeslider

> 标签: assets, extension, rangeslider, yii2

## 简介

Ion.RangeSlider. Is an easy, flexible and responsive range slider with tons of options.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
