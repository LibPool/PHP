# fortrabbit/yii2-artisan-bridge

> 标签: artisan, console, craftcms, laravel, symfony, yii2

## 简介

Forked from ostark/yii2-artisan-bridge. Brings the ease of Artisan commands and the power of Symfony console to Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
