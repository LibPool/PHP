# cleentfaar/conan-console-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The CLConanConsoleBundle adds interaction with the Symfony console to your Conan CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
