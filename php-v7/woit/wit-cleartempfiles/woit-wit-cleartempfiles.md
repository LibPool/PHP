# woit/wit-cleartempfiles

> 标签: cleartempfiles, extension, processedfiles, typo3, wit, woit

## 简介

Simplify file maintenance in TYPO3 with our extension, designed to quickly and effectively clear processed files from your fileadmin directory.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
