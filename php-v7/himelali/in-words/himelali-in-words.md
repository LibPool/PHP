# himelali/in-words

> 标签: Bangla, Chinese, amount in words, arabic, bengali, french, hindi, lakh crore, laravel, million billion trillion, number to words, numbers in words, php, spanish, spell out numbers, urdu

## 简介

Convert numbers to words in 14 languages (English, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Chinese, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, German, Japanese) with native (lakh/crore, 万/亿) or international (million/billion/trillion) numbering.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
