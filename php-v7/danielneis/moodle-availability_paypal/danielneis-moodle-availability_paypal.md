# danielneis/moodle-availability_paypal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

An Availability Condition for Moodle to require a PayPal payment to access activities and resources.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
