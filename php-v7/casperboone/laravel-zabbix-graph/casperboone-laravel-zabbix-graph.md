# casperboone/laravel-zabbix-graph

> 标签: laravel-zabbix-graph

## 简介

Get nice looking graphs of your Zabbix devices in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
