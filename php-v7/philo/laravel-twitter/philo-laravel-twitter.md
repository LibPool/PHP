# philo/laravel-twitter

> 标签: laravel, laravel-4, twitter

## 简介

A simple Laravel 4 service provider for including the TwitterOAuth library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
