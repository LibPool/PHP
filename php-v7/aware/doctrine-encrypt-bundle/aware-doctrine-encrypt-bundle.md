# aware/doctrine-encrypt-bundle

> 标签: Defuse, decrypt, doctrine, encrypt, halite, symfony

## 简介

Encrypted symfony entity's by verified and standardized libraries

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
