# clavicula-nox/pendingactions-bundle

> 标签: bundle, delay, symfony

## 简介

Symfony PendingActionsBundle. A simple way to delay and schedule the execution of heavy tasks !

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
