# league/climate

> 标签: cli, colors, command, php, terminal

## 简介

PHP's best friend for the terminal. CLImate allows you to easily output colored text, special formats, and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
