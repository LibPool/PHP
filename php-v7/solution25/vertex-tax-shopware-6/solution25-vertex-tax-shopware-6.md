# solution25/vertex-tax-shopware-6

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Vertex O Series REST API v2 integration for Shopware 6.7 that enables real-time US sales tax calculation, shipping tax handling, and address validation in checkout and order flows.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
