# ameos/ameos_sharebyemail

> 标签: ameos, cms, email, sharebyemail, sharing, social, tipafriend, typo3

## 简介

Share a link by email (tipafriend replacement) Provides a sharing link and a form to send the link by email

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
