# pug/laravel

> 标签: dialect, html, jade, laravel, lumen, php, phtml, phug, presentation, pug, render, template, views

## 简介

Pug (ex-Jade) and Pug-Blade templates support for Laravel and Lumen, see: https://github.com/BKWLD/laravel-pug

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
