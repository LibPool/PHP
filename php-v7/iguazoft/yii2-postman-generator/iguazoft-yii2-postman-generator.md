# iguazoft/yii2-postman-generator

> 标签: Postman, api, documentation, phpdoc, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 extension to generate Postman collections from PHPDoc annotations

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
