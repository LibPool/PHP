# jbaron/sitemapcrawler

> 标签: Sitemap, TYPO3 CMS, cache warmup

## 简介

TYPO3 extension for parsing a sitemap and fetching its entries to warm up caches.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
