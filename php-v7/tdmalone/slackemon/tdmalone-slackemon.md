# tdmalone/slackemon

> 标签: php, pokemon, slack

## 简介

Inspired by Pokémon Go, now you can collect Pokémon and battle your teammates on Slack!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
