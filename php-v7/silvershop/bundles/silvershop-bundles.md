# silvershop/bundles

> 标签: bundle, ecommerce, shop, silvershop, silverstripe

## 简介

Bundle products for SilverShop: sell a product that groups other products and adjusts component stock on purchase.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
