# systemcode/customer-identity-brazil-enhanced

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Separate CPF, CNPJ, Social Name and Trade Name fields for Brazil Customer Identity.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
