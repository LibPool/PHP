# systemcode/customer-address-autocomplete-brazil

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Brazilian postcode mask and address autocomplete for Magento customer address forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
