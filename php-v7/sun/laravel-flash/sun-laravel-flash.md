# sun/laravel-flash

> 标签: bootstrap, flash, flash message, laravel, message, php

## 简介

Sun Flash helps you to add flash messages to your Laravel application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
