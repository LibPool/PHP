# goalgorilla/open_social_upgrade_patches

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A package to help with patch selection during the Drupal 9 to Drupal 10 upgrade of Open Social

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
