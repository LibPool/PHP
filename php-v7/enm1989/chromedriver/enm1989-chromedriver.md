# enm1989/chromedriver

> 标签: Chromedriver, chrome, selenium, webdriver

## 简介

Composer distribution of Chromedriver. Adds a executable to your composer bin directory.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
