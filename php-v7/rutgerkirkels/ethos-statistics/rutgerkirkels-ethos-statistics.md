# rutgerkirkels/ethos-statistics

> 标签: ethos, plugin, statistics, wordpress

## 简介

Display ethOS mining statistics on your Wordpress site

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
