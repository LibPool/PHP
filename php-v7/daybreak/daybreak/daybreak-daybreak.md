# daybreak/daybreak

> 标签: absence, holidays, laravel, livewire, punch clock, time, time tracker, timesheet, vacation, working time

## 简介

Simple timesheets and vacation management for small businesses.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
