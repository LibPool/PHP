# nordcomputer/showoutofstockprice

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds price to out-of-stock products and shows Swatches for configurable out-of-stock products

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
