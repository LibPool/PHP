# paygate-to/paygate-php

> 标签: USDC, USDT, bitcoin, credit card, crypto, no kyc, paygate, paygate.to, payment gateway, payments, white label

## 简介

PHP SDK for PayGate.to: card and crypto payments without KYC, instant payouts to your own wallet, and signed callback verification.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
