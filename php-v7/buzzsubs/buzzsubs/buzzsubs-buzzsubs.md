# buzzsubs/buzzsubs

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Buzzsub’s cutting edge gamified email subscriber app gives you the ability to make your pop-ups truly interactive.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
