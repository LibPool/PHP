# meema/laravel-cloudfront

> 标签: Meema, aws, cdn, cloudfront, laravel

## 简介

Easily & quickly integrate your application with AWS CloudFront.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
