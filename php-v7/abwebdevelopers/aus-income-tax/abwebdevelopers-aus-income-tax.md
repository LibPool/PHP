# abwebdevelopers/aus-income-tax

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Calculates withheld amounts from gross income as per the Australian Tax Office PAYG (pay-as-you-go) tax tables

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
