# derhansen/plain-faq

> 标签: FAQ, TYPO3 CMS, extbase, fluid

## 简介

Plain FAQ - FAQ extension with list and detail view.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
