# gfaugere/monolog-colored-line

> 标签: color, colour, log, monolog

## 简介

A simple Formatter that adds level-dependant colors to your logs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
