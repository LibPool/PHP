# raphael-gmbh/ra-watermark

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS

## 简介

Stamps a file-metadata hint text (e.g. an AI-generation disclosure) directly into the pixels of processed images.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
