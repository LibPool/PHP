# chrmue/cm_membermaps

> 标签: OpenStreetMap, contao, googlemaps, leafletjs, map, member, nominatim

## 简介

Contao Memberlist with Google Map or OpenStreetMap

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
