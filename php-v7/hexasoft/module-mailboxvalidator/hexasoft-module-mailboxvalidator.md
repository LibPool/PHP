# hexasoft/module-mailboxvalidator

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

MailboxValidator Magento 2 extension enables you to block invalid, disposable, free or role-based email from using your service. This extension perform real time validation to verify email address.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
