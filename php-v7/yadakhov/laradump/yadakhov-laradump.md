# yadakhov/laradump

> 标签: laravel, migration, mysql, mysqldump

## 简介

A mysqldump wrapper for laravel.  Dump your table schemas and data into files.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
