# kaapiii/concrete5-laravel-mix

> 标签: concrete5, laravel-mix

## 简介

Add versioned Laravel Mix files (based on your mix-manifest.js) to concrete5 themes and packages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
