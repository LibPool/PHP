# alexgiuvara/imgproxy

> 标签: image, imgproxy, laravel, micro-service, proxy, resize

## 简介

Laravel Service Provider for imgproxy: Resize your images instantly and securely

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
