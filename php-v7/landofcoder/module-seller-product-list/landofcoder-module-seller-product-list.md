# landofcoder/module-seller-product-list

> 标签: landofcoder, landofcoder seller product list, magento 2 seller product list, marketplace, multi-vendor magento 2, product list magento 2, seller product list for magento 2, sellerproduct list magento 2

## 简介

Landofcoder Magento 2 Seller Product List supports you to create a wanted list of products based on attribute, types, conditions of items.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
