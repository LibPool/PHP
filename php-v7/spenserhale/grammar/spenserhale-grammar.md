# spenserhale/grammar

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Grammatical utility class for pluralizing, singularizing, and finding the appropriate indefinite article to a word (a/an)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
