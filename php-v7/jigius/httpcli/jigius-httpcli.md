# jigius/httpcli

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

An yet another trivial HTTP client. The goal is its realization with of Elegant Objects conceptions by Egor Bugaenko.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
