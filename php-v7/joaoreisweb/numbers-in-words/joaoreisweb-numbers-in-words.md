# joaoreisweb/numbers-in-words

> 标签: converter, currency, currency-transformer, in, money, number, number-transformer, numbers, numbers-to-text, short-scale, string, to, words

## 简介

This library convert numbers into words in Portuguese and English. Short and long scale.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
