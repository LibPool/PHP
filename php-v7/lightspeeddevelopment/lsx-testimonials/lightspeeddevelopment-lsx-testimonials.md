# lightspeeddevelopment/lsx-testimonials

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The LSX Testimonials extension adds the “Testimonials” post type, which you can display front-and-centre on your site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
