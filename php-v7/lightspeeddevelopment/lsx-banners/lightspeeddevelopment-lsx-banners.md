# lightspeeddevelopment/lsx-banners

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The LSX Banners extension adds advanced banner configuration options to your WordPress site running LSX theme.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
