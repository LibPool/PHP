# adactive-sas/saml2-bridge-bundle

> 标签: Bridge, SAML2, adactiveSas, bundle, identity-provider, idp, saml, saml-idp, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Symfony bundle that provide a SAML Identity Provider (idp).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
