# katalyst/mdn-esn

> 标签: esn, katalyst, mdn, meid

## 简介

Classes for formatting and converting Mobile Device Numbers (MDN) and Electronic Serial Numbers (ESN)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
