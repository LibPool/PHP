# brokerexchange/elasticscout

> 标签: elasticsearch, laravel, scout, search

## 简介

Laravel Scout Driver for Elasticsearch 6 and 7

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
