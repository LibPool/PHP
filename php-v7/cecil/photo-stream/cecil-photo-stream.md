# cecil/photo-stream

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Photo Stream is a self hosted static website for your photos, with really goods loading performances, powered by Cecil.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
