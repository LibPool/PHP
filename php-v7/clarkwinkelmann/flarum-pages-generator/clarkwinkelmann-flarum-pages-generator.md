# clarkwinkelmann/flarum-pages-generator

> 标签: flarum, pages

## 简介

Generate pseudo-static pages from markdown and HTML files for Flarum

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
