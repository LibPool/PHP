# clarkwinkelmann/flarum-ext-comicsans

> 标签: comicsans, extension, flarum

## 简介

Turn text into Comic Sans for no reason

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
