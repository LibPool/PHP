# clarkwinkelmann/flarum-ext-popular-discussion-badge

> 标签: flarum, popular

## 简介

Adds a popular badge to discussion based on replies or number of views

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
