# delatbabel/fluents

> 标签: eloquent, fluent, l5, laravel, laravel5

## 简介

Convert Eloquent Model objects to and from Fluent objects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
