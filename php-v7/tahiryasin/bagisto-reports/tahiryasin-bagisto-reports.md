# tahiryasin/bagisto-reports

> 标签: bagisto, bagisto-reports

## 简介

Sales, Inventory, Shipping, Refunds and Inventory reports for Bagisto opensource.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
