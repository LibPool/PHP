# decisionrules/decisionrules-php

> 标签: DecisionRules, engine, rule, rule engine

## 简介

Simple library for connecting your app to your DecisionRules.io

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
