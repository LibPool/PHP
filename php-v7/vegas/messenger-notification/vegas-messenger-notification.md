# vegas/messenger-notification

> 标签: Messenger, laravel, max, notifications, telegram

## 简介

Unified Telegram and MAX notification channels for Laravel with direct and relay transports

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
