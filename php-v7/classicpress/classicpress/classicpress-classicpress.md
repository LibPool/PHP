# classicpress/classicpress

> 标签: classicpress

## 简介

The CMS for Creators. Stable. Lightweight. Instantly Familiar. Also known as the WordPress fork.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
