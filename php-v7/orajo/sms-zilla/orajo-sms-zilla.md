> 标签: HostedSms, Infobip, SzybkiSMS, cisco, clickatell, library, orange, serwersms, sms, smsapi, smscenter, smsplanet

## 简介

PHP library for sending SMS through various gateways

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
