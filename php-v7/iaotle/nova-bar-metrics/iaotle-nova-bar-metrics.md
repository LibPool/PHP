# iaotle/nova-bar-metrics

> 标签: bar chart, frequency distributions, laravel, nova

## 简介

A Laravel Nova tool for bar chart metrics and frequency distributions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
