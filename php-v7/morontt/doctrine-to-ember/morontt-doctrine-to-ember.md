# morontt/doctrine-to-ember

> 标签: doctrine2, ember.js, emberjs, orm

## 简介

Conversion models Doctrine2 to the models Ember-Data

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
