# savvywombat/wiki-lite

> 标签: laravel, savvy wombat, savvywombat, wiki, wiki lite, wiki-lite, wikilite

## 简介

Wiki-Lite: light weight wiki for Laravel 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
