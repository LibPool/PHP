# miniframe/social-login

> 标签: atlassian, bitbucket, facebook, github, gitlab, google, instagram, linkedin, login, microsoft, oauth, reddit, twitter

## 简介

Addon to enable social login providers for the Miniframe PHP Framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
