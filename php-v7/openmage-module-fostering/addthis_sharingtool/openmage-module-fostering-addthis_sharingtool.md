# openmage-module-fostering/addthis_sharingtool

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

AddThis makes it easy for any visitor to share your content to over 350 services. Developed by AddThis.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
