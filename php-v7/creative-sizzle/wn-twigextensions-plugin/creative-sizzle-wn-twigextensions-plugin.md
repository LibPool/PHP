> 标签: extensions, leftpad, lowercase, strftime, time_diff, truncate, twig, uppercase, wordwrap

## 简介

Register more Twig filters for your OctoberCMS templates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
