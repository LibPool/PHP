# simplethings/form-serializer-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

(De-)Serialization of Object-Graphs into XML/JSON based on Form Types

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
