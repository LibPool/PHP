# concrete5-community/quick_tabs

> 标签: addon, cms, concrete, concrete5, concreteCMS, content, package, tab, tabs

## 简介

A ConcreteCMS/concrete5 package to easily add tabs to your pages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
