# masmaleki/zoho

> 标签: Zoho, crm, laravel

## 简介

Masmaleki Zoho provide an elegant and easy way to communicate with Zoho CRM.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
