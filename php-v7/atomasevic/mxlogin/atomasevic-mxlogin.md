# atomasevic/mxlogin

> 标签: email, gmail, hotmail, registration

## 简介

Improve your registration/signup pages with links to user's email provider inbox.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
