# hubipe/huqrpayment

> 标签: hungary, payment, qr

## 简介

QR payment library for Hungary (according to the Magyar Nemzeti Bank standard version 001)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
