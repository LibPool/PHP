# raimx/stream

> 标签: event-driven, io, non-blocking, pipe, reactphp, readable, stream, writable

## 简介

Event-driven readable and writable streams for non-blocking I/O in ReactPHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
