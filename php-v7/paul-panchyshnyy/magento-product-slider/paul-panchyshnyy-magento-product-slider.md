# paul-panchyshnyy/magento-product-slider

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Helps admin to easily add different fully responsive products sliders to the any section of the page

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
