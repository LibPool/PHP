# prolix/knp-snappy-bundle

> 标签: bundle, knp, knplabs, pdf, snappy

## 简介

Easily create PDF and images in Symfony2 by converting Twig/HTML templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
