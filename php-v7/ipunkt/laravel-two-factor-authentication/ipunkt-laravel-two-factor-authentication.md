# ipunkt/laravel-two-factor-authentication

> 标签: 2fa, Authentication, laravel, rfc-6238, two-factor

## 简介

Two factor authentication (2FA) with TOTP tokens. (RFC 6238)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
