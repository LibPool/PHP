# yieldstudio/symfony-fake-sms-notifier

> 标签: dev-tool, email, notifier, sms, symfony

## 简介

Fake SMS (as email during development) integration for Symfony Notifier.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
