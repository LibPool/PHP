# au/au_group_notices

> 标签: AU, elgg, groups, notices, plugin

## 简介

Allows group owners to add context-sensitive text notices to sidebar, base or top of group pages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
