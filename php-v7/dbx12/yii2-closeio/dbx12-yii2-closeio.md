# dbx12/yii2-closeio

> 标签: close-crm, closeio, crm, extension, yii2

## 简介

An extension to connect your closeio / close.com leads to your application

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
