# luitd/lu-study-catalogue

> 标签: extension, typo3

## 简介

Study catalogues (Course catalogue, Program catalogue) TYPO3 extension for University of Latvia

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
