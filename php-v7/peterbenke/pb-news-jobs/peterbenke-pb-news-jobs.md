# peterbenke/pb-news-jobs

> 标签: extension, frontend, jobs, news, typo3

## 简介

Extends the extension news with additional fields for jobs offers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
