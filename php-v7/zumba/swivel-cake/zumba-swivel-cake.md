# zumba/swivel-cake

> 标签: Behavior, cakephp, component, helper, plugin, swivel

## 简介

A CakePHP plugin to provide easy access to zumba/swivel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
