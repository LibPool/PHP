# omise/omise-magento

> 标签: creditcard, debitcard, internetbanking, magento, omise, payment

## 简介

Accept payments on your Magento 2 website with Omise

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
