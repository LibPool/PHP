# outhebox/blade-flags

> 标签: blade, country flags, flags, language flags, laravel

## 简介

A package to easily make use of country & language flags in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
