# pluritech-total-voice/total-voice-php

> 标签: Total Voice, php, pluritech, sms

## 简介

This package has the aim of send SMS with Total Voice Api.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
