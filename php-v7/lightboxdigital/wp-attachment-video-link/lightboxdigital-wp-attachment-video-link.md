# lightboxdigital/wp-attachment-video-link

> 标签: videos, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Simple script to attach video URLs to attachments - great for manual video thumbnails.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
