# svobik7/yii2-video-embed

> 标签: embed, extension, video, widget, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 Extension for Generating Video Embed Codes from Video URLs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
