# rickcy/yii2-rabbitmq

> 标签: MQ, extension, php-rabbit, rabbit, rabbitmq, yii2

## 简介

Wrapper based on php-amqplib to incorporate messaging in your Yii2 application via RabbitMQ.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
