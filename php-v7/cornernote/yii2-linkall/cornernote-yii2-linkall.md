# cornernote/yii2-linkall

> 标签: link, linkall, many to many, yii2

## 简介

Behavior to handle saving multiple many to many related records in Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
