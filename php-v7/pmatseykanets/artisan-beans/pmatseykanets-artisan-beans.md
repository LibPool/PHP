# pmatseykanets/artisan-beans

> 标签: artisan, beanstalkd, job, laravel, queue

## 简介

Easily manage your Beanstalkd job queues right from the Laravel artisan command

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
