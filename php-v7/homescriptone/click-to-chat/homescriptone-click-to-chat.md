# homescriptone/click-to-chat

> 标签: Customer support, add-on, chat, statamic, whatsapp

## 简介

Add a Click to Chat button on your Statamic website to enable easy communication with customers on WhatsApp.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
