# christiaan/emailfeed

> 标签: email, email to rss, rss

## 简介

Simple tool that reads a mailbox and writes the recent mails into a rss feed

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
