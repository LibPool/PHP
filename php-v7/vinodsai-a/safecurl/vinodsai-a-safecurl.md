# vinodsai-a/safecurl

> 标签: curl, safe, safecurl, ssrf, websec

## 简介

A drop-in replacement for 'curl_exec', designed to prevent SSRF attacks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
