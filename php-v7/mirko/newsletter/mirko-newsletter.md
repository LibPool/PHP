# mirko/newsletter

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, email, newsletter, statistics, typo3

## 简介

TYPO3 extension to send any pages as Newsletter and provide statistics on opened emails and clicked links.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
