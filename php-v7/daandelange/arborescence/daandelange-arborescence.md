# daandelange/arborescence

> 标签: kirby-cms, kirby-panel-plugin, kirby-plugin, kirby-section-plugin, kirby5

## 简介

A tree menu for your kirby (v5) panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
