# initphp/cookies

> 标签: cookie, cookies, hmac, initphp, security, signed-cookies

## 简介

Signed, tamper-evident cookie manager for PHP with per-key TTL support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
