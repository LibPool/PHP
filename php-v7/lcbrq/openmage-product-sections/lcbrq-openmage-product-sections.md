# lcbrq/openmage-product-sections

> 标签: magento, mahocommerce, openmage

## 简介

Simple addon that allows products sections management in your Magento 1 / OpenMage store

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
