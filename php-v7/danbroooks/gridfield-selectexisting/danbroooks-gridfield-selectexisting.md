# danbroooks/gridfield-selectexisting

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Manage relationships in Gridfield by ticking/unticking from a list of existing records

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
