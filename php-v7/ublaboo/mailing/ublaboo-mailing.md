# ublaboo/mailing

> 标签: extension, log, mail, mailing, nette

## 简介

Extension for Nette Framework: Easy & object-oriented way of sending & logging mails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
