# augustash/commerce_stripe_enhanced

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Closes the gaps between commerce_stripe and a real storefront: Affirm alongside saved cards, express wallets at the head of checkout, and a removable saved card.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
