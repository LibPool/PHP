# glaivepro/hidevara

> 标签: laravel, security, whoops

## 简介

Laravel millipackage that hides variables from getting dumped in the Whoops page when your app crashes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
