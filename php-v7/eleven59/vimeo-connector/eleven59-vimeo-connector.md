# eleven59/vimeo-connector

> 标签: Backpack Addon, VimeoConnector

## 简介

Helper class to connect to Vimeo and download video information based on video ID

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
