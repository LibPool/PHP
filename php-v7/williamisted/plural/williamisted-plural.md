# williamisted/plural

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, plural

## 简介

Simple plural plugin for Craft - Usage: {{ entries | length | plural('result') }}

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
