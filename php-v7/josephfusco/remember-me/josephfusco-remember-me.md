# josephfusco/remember-me

> 标签: wordpress

## 简介

Plays the chorus of Remember Me from Disney's Coco everytime you check Remember Me on the WordPress login page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
