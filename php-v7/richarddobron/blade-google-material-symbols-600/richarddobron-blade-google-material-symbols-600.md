# richarddobron/blade-google-material-symbols-600

> 标签: blade, google, laravel, material, symbols

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Googles Material Design Symbols (600) in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
