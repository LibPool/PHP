# b13/ai-label

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, ai, ai act, compliance, disclosure, labelling, transparency

## 简介

AI Label - Disclose AI-generated and AI-modified content as required by Article 50 of the EU AI Act: flag records in the backend, track editorial review, and mark flagged content in the frontend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
