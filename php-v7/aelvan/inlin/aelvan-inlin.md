# aelvan/inlin

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, criticalcss, inline, inlining, svg

## 简介

A simple plugin for inlining stuff in your templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
