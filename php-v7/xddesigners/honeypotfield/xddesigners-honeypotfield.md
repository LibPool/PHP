# xddesigners/honeypotfield

> 标签: Honeypot, protection, silverstripe, spam

## 简介

Adds a honeypot in addition to the nocapcha field

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
