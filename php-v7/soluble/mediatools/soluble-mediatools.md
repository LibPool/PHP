> 标签: audio, audio extraction, conversion, extraction, ffmpeg, ffprobe, multimedia, subtitle, thumbnail, thumbnailing, transcoding, video, video processing, video transcoding, webvtt

## 简介

FFMpeg video/audio/subs conversions, thumbnails, audio extraction, query...

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
