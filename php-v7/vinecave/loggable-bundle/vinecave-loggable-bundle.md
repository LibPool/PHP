# vinecave/loggable-bundle

> 标签: Autowiring, bundle, injection, log, monolog, symfony

## 简介

Bundle used for fast and convenient way to log any service in Symfony.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
