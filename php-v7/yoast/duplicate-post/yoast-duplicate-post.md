# yoast/duplicate-post

> 标签: clone, copy, post, wordpress

## 简介

The go-to tool for cloning posts and pages, including the powerful Rewrite & Republish feature.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
