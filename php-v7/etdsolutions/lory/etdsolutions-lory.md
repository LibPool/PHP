# etdsolutions/lory

> 标签: carousel, infinite, javascript, lean, slider, swipe, touch, vanilla

## 简介

Touch enabled minimalistic slider written in vanilla JavaScript.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
