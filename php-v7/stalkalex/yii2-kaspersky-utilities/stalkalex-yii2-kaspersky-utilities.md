# stalkalex/yii2-kaspersky-utilities

> 标签: antivirus, components, extension, yii2

## 简介

Just wrapper for Kaspersky antivirus console commands

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
