# magepow/pdfinvoicefrontend

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Pdf invoice frontend for magento 2 help customers can download detailed pdf invoice just like when shop owner in admin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
