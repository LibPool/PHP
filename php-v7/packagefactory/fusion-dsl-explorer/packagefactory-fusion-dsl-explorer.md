# packagefactory/fusion-dsl-explorer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

cli commands to simulate dsl-transpilation or to remove a fusion dsl from your codebase

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
