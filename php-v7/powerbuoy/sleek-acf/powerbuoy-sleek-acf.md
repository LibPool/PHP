# powerbuoy/sleek-acf

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Improves ACF in a number of ways like nicer flexible content titles, collapsed flexible content layouts, better looking relationship field and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
