# th3mouk/cms-siteplan-bundle

> 标签: cms, plan, site, siteplan, sonata

## 简介

Symfony CMSSiteplanBundle to generate site plan of SonataPageBundle and personals routes

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
