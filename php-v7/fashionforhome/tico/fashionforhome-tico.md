# fashionforhome/tico

> 标签: Scrum, board, framework, jira, kanban, laravel, printer, ticket

## 简介

This Tool is for printing JIRA tickets by ticket numbers on DIN A6 paper to manage your projects on a physical Kanban/Scrum board.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
