# belvg/sso-fpbundle

> 标签: SSO, oauth, oauth2

## 简介

Integration with knpuniversity/oauth2-client-bundle to provide services

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
