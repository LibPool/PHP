# itx/keep-scroll-position

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Restores the scroll position of the TYPO3 backend module body after a full page reload (e.g. after hide/delete/move actions), instead of jumping back to the top.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
