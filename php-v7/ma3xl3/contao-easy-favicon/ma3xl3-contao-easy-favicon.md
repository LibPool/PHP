# ma3xl3/contao-easy-favicon

> 标签: apple touch icon, contao, favicon, ico

## 简介

This extension allows you easily to add a favicon and apple touch icon to your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
