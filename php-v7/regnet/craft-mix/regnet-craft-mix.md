# regnet/craft-mix

> 标签: Craft, alpine, cms, craftcms, mix, project, sprig, tailwind

## 简介

Craft CMS boilerplate with TailwindCSS, AlpineJS with Laravel Mix

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
