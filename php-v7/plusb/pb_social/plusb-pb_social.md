# plusb/pb_social

> 标签: Social Media Streams, TYPO3 CMS, facebook, imgur, instagram, linkedin, pb_social, pinterest, plus B, plusB, tumblr, twitter, tx_news, vimeo, youtube

## 简介

Pb_social enables you to get posts from social media networks into your TYPO3 system. Currently supported social media networks are: Facebook, Imgur, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Tumblr, Twitter, YouTube, Vimeo and TYPO3 Extension tx_news

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
