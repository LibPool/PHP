# lcherone/encryption

> 标签: CBC, Rijndael, aes, encryption, hmac

## 简介

A sha256 HMAC key, RIJNDAEL_256 AES CBC @ 1024 cost encryption class.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
