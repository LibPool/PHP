# tianfuunion/mark-wechat

> 标签: mark-wechat, markengine, markwechat, tianfuunion, wechapay, wechat-pay

## 简介

The Tianfu Union Mark Engine Payment for wechat.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
