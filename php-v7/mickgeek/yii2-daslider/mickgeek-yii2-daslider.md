# mickgeek/yii2-daslider

> 标签: extension, parallax, slider, widget, yii2

## 简介

Content slider with delayed animations and background parallax effect.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
