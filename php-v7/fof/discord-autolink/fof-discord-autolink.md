# fof/discord-autolink

> 标签: Invite, autolink, discord, embed, flarum

## 简介

Turn Discord invite, event, channel and message links in posts into rich cards

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
