# i-lateral/silverstripe-fancy-form-scaffolder

> 标签: Forms, admin, scaffold, silverstripe

## 简介

Allows you to defined CMS form structure via config.yml

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
