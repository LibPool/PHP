# plato-creative/plato-silverstripe-installer

> 标签: installer, plato creative, silverstripe

## 简介

Custom built SilverStripe installer with Foundation

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
