# corviz/br-gpdpl

> 标签: anonymization, lgpd

## 简介

A lib that provides a series of data anonymizers, compliant with brazilian General Personal Data Protection Law (aka LGPD, in pt-br)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
