# insenseanalytics/laravel-telescope-pruning

> 标签: laravel, laravel telescope, pruning, telescope

## 简介

Intelligently trim your Laravel Telescope entries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
