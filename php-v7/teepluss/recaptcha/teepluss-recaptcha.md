# teepluss/recaptcha

> 标签: captcha, laravel, nocaptcha, recaptcha

## 简介

Google No CAPTCHA reCAPTCHA for Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
