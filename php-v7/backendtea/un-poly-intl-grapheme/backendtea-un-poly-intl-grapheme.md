# backendtea/un-poly-intl-grapheme

> 标签: intl-grapheme, polyfill, removal

## 简介

Removes the symfony/polyfill-intl-grapheme polyfill from your project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
