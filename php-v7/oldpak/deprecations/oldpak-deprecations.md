# oldpak/deprecations

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A small layer on top of trigger_error(E_USER_DEPRECATED) or PSR-3 logging with options to disable all deprecations or selectively for packages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
