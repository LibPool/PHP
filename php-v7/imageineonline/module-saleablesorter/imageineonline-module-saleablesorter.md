# imageineonline/module-saleablesorter

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Saleable Sorter | Sort product listing based on In Stock status allowing you to push out of stock products to the end of the page

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
