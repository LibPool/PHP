# waaseyaa/ai-development

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Waaseyaa local AI-development plane — a require-dev metapackage. Never a production dependency.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
