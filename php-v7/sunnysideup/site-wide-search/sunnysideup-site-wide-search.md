# sunnysideup/site-wide-search

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, dataobjects, find, search, silverstripe, site wide

## 简介

Search your entire silverstripe site!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
