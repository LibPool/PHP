# sunnysideup/avoid-child-deletion

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, archive, careful, cascade deletes, cms, delete, home page, homepage, link, parents and children, silverstripe

## 简介

Avoid deleting pages that have children or are the home page of your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
