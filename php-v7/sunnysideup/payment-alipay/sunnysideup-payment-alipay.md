# sunnysideup/payment-alipay

> 标签: alipay, ecommerce, payment, silverstripe, wechat

## 简介

Allows e-commerce payments to be made with wechat / Alipay

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
