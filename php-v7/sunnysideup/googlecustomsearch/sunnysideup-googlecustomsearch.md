# sunnysideup/googlecustomsearch

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, custom, full text search, gcse, google custom search, search, services, silverstripe

## 简介

Add google custom search to your silverstripe website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
