# restruct/silverstripe-signed-asset-urls

> 标签: X-Accel-Redirect, assets, protected, signed-urls, silverstripe, x-sendfile

## 简介

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
