# flowcms/flowcms

> 标签: flowcms

## 简介

FlowCMS is a free, open source, simple content management powered by Laravel, AlpineJS and TailwindCSS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
