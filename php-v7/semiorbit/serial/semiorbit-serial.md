# semiorbit/serial

> 标签: 16 chars alphanumeric, base36, serial, serial number

## 简介

Generate a Serial Number of alphanumeric 16 random chars in PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
