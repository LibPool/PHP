# sanyisasha/yii2-google-analytics

> 标签: analytics, extension, ga, google, gtag, yii2

## 简介

A package to easily handle Google Analytics in Yii2 projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
