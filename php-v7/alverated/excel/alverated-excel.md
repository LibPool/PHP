# alverated/excel

> 标签: PHPExcel, batch, csv, excel, export, import, laravel

## 简介

An eloquent way of importing and exporting Excel and CSV in Laravel 4 with the power of PHPExcel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
