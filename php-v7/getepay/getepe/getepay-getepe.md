# getepay/getepe

> 标签: extension, getepay, india, magento, magento 2, netbanking, payment gateway, payments, plugin, wallets

## 简介

GetePay Magento 2 plugin for accepting payments.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
