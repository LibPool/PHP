# slm/mail

> 标签: elastic email, elasticemail, email, laminas, mailgun, mandrill, mezzio, postage, postmark, send grid, sendgrid, transactional email

## 简介

Integration of various email service providers in the Laminas\Mail

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
