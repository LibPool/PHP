# coroowicaksono/nova-carousel

> 标签: card, carousel, laravel, nova, slider

## 简介

A Simple Slider Card in Laravel Nova's Dashboard using Vue-Caraosel. Starting create your own dashboard with Nova Carousel can save your time and help you maintain consistency across cool elements of your cards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
