# chrisdenend/flarum-ext-generate-usernames

> 标签: flarum

## 简介

A Flarum extension to generate usernames and present them in a drop down at registration

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
