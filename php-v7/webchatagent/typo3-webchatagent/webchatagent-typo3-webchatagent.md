# webchatagent/typo3-webchatagent

> 标签: Customer support, Live Chat, ai chatbot, chatbot, extension, typo3

## 简介

AI chatbot and live chat for TYPO3: answers visitors from your website content, collects contact requests and hands chats to your team. Servers in Germany, data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
