# forci/epay-bundle

> 标签: epay, epay bundle, epay.bg, epay.bg bundle, forci, wucdbm

## 简介

A Symfony2 bundle for working with the epay.bg payment gateway.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
