# matatirosoln/pantone-converter

> 标签: cmyk, colors, hex, pantone, rgb

## 简介

Convert from a named Pantone coated colour to Hex, RGB or CMYK variants

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
