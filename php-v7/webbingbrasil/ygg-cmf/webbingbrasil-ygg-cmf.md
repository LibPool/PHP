# webbingbrasil/ygg-cmf

> 标签: admin, cmf, cms, framework, laravel, rad

## 简介

CMF to support back-office projects with RAD approach.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
