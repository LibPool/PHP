# xmugenx/flarum-ext-username-chinese-support

> 标签: Chinese, flarum, username

## 简介

Support Chinese username and lower min characters to 2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
