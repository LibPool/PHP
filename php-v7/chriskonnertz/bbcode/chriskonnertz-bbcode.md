# chriskonnertz/bbcode

> 标签: bbcode

## 简介

A naive attempt at a BBCode 'parser' written in PHP. It uses regex and thus fails at complex, nested tags.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
