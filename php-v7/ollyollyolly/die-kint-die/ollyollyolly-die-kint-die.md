# ollyollyolly/die-kint-die

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

That's German, for "The Kint, the". Adds sd() and ddd() (debug then die) functions back into Kint-PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
