# zephis/tiny-png-bundle

> 标签: pimcore, tinypng

## 简介

Pimcore Bundle - Minifies your PNG and JPEG Assets on uploading to Pimcore

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
