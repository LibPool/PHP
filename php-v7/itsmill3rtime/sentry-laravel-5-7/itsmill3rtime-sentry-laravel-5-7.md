# itsmill3rtime/sentry-laravel-5-7

> 标签: errors, laravel, logging, sentry

## 简介

Laravel integration for Sentry (https://sentry.io)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
