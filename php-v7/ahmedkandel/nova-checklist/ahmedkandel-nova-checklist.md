# ahmedkandel/nova-checklist

> 标签: checklist, checklists, laravel, nova

## 简介

A Laravel Nova field to display a list of items to complete.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
