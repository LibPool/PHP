# crisp-chat/chatbox-and-helpdesk

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A module to add a chat widget on your website and show your customers carts and orders in your Crisp inbox.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
