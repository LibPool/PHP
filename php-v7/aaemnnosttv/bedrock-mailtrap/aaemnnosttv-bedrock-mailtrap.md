# aaemnnosttv/bedrock-mailtrap

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Capture outgoing mail in non-production environments with Mailtrap for Roots Bedrock WordPress installs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
