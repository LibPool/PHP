# checkmycomposer/commands

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Commands to check your Composer dependencies and synchronize your project with http://checkmycomposer.com.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
