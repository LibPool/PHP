# yaroslavmolchan/rbac

> 标签: acl, laravel, rbac

## 简介

Simple RBAC/ACL for Laravel 5.5 and more with caching and permission groups.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
