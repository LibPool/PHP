# symbiote/silverstripe-content-services

> 标签: file, filesystem, silverstripe

## 简介

An abstraction from reading and writing content directly to the filesystem.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
