> 标签: atom feeds, feed reader, feed writer, php, pop, pop php, rss feeds

## 简介

Pop Feed Component for Pop PHP Framework

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
