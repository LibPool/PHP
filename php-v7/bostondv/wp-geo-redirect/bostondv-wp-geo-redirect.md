# bostondv/wp-geo-redirect

> 标签: geo-redirect, geographical, geolocation, localization, multidomain, multilanguage, polylang, redirect, redirection

## 简介

Allows you to redirect visitors or switch language according to their country.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
