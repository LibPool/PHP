# mitloshuk/moneycare

> 标签: credit, moneycare

## 简介

PHP library for MoneyCare API. MoneyCare is the leading credit platform for PIC lending in stores. More than 15 solutions from banks and MFIs in 2 minutes

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
