# omegacode/t3-debugger-utility-standalone

> 标签: DebuggerUtility, debugger, typo3, var_dump

## 简介

A standalone port of the DebuggerUtility of the well known CMS TYPO3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
