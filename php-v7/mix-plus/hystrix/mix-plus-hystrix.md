# mix-plus/hystrix

> 标签: Microservice, circuitbreaker, hystrix, mix, swoole

## 简介

Microservice circuit breaker, reference spring hystrix, developed based on Swoole

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
