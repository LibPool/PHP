# fuko-php/open

> 标签: editor, fuko, fuko-php, link

## 简介

Generates links to open referenced files directly in your IDE or editor, or have it linked to an online repository

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
