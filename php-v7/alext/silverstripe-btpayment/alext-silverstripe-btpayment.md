# alext/silverstripe-btpayment

> 标签: braintree, card, credit, pay, payment, paypal, silverstripe

## 简介

Integrate Braintree payment form in Dropin UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
