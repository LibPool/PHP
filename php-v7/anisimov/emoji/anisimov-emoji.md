> 标签: decode, decode emoji, decoding, decoding emoji, detect emoji, detecting emoji, emoji, encode, encode emoji, encoding, encoding emoji, remove emoji, removing emoji

## 简介

Allows to detect emoji, remove emoji, encode emoji and decode emoji in string.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
