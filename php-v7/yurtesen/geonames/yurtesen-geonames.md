# yurtesen/geonames

> 标签: city, country, eloquent, geonames, laravel, location, timezone

## 简介

Geonames importer and models for Eloquent ORM

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
