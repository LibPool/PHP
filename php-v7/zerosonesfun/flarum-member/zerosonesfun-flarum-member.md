# zerosonesfun/flarum-member

> 标签: Users, members

## 简介

Give members more class. Adds a logged-in class to the forum's body tag. Also, includes two BBCodes. [members] & [guests]

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
