# igniterlabs/ti-ext-smsnotify

> 标签: notification, notify, tastyigniter

## 简介

Send SMS notifications to both restaurant and customer about an order or reservation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
