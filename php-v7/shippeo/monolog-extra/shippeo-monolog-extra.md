# shippeo/monolog-extra

> 标签: log, logging, psr-3

## 简介

An extension to Monolog that adds handlers, formatters and processors. Includes integrations for Symfony.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
