# noi/parsedown-newline

> 标签: extension, japanese, library, line break, markdown, newline, parsedown, parser, white space

## 简介

An extension of Parsedown that prevents unwanted white spaces in Japanese.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
