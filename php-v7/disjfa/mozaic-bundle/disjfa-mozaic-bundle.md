# disjfa/mozaic-bundle

> 标签: puzzle, symfony-ux

## 简介

Mozaic puzzle for symfony, can also be use in glynn-admin-symfony

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
