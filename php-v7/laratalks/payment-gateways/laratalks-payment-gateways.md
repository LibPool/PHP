# laratalks/payment-gateways

> 标签: Payline, payment, payment gateway, shaparak, zarinpal

## 简介

An abstract payment processor for Iranian payment providers, like Zarinpal, Payling and Saman bank.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
