# cornell-business/manage-multisite-plugins

> 标签: multisite, plugins, wordpress

## 简介

Provides more thorough plugin usage data for your entire WordPress multisite network.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
