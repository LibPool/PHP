# fynn-digital/sdk-php

> 标签: api, billing, fynn, payment, recurring billing, subscription

## 简介

Integrate the fynn subscription billing platform into your application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
