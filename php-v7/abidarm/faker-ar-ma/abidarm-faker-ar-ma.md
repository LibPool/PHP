# abidarm/faker-ar-ma

> 标签: ar_MA, arabic, faker, fakerphp, fixtures, locale, morocco

## 简介

Moroccan Arabic (ar_MA) locale providers for FakerPHP, using Traditional Arabic.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
