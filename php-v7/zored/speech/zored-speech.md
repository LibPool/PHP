# zored/speech

> 标签: json, json-rpc, jsonrpc, rpc, symfony2bundle, symfony3bundle

## 简介

JSON-RPC 2.0 for PHP. With Symfony 2.0 to 3.0 support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
