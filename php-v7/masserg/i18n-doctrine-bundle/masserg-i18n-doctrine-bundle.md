# masserg/i18n-doctrine-bundle

> 标签: doctrine2, i18n, internationalization, symfony4, translatable, translation

## 简介

Manage i18n for your doctrine objects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
