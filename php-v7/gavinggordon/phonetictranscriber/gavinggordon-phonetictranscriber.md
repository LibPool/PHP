# gavinggordon/phonetictranscriber

> 标签: International Phonetic Alphabet, Transcription, british, communication, educational, english, ipa, language, linguistics, php, pronounciation, transcribe, translation, v1.0.0

## 简介

A PHP class to transcribe English words into their IPA equivalent.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
