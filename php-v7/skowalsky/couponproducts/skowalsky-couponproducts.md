# skowalsky/couponproducts

> 标签: coupon, limit, limit to product, list, merconis, product, skowalsky

## 简介

Extends MERCONIS coupon functionality. The validity of a coupon can now be reduced to specific products.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
