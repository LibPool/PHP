# ssch/ssch-html5videoplayer

> 标签: mediaelementjs, videoplayer

## 简介

HTML5 audio and video player extension, based on extbase & fluid for TYPO3 CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
