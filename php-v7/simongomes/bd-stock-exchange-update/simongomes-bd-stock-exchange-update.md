# simongomes/bd-stock-exchange-update

> 标签: bangladesh, bangladesh share market, bangladesh stock exchange, bd share market, bd stock exchange, chittagong stock exchange, cse, dhaka stock exchange, dse, library

## 简介

PHP library to fetch bangladesh share market updates, including Dhaka and Chittagong Stock Exchange (DSE & CSE).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
