# wp-kit/magic-meta

> 标签: OOP, assley, eloquent, magic meta, postmeta, themosis, wordpress, wp-kit

## 简介

A wp-kit component that handles Eloquent appending and querying of PostMeta

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
