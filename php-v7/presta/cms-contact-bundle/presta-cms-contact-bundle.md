# presta/cms-contact-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, bundle, cms, prestaconcept, symfony-cmf

## 简介

PrestaCMS Contact bundle : Add contact block to PrestaCMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
