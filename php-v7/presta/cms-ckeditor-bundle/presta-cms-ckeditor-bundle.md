# presta/cms-ckeditor-bundle

> 标签: CKEditor, Symfony2, bundle, cms, prestaconcept, symfony-cmf, wysiwyg

## 简介

PrestaCMS CKEditor bundle : Integrates CKEditor in PrestaCMS with SonataMedia and page links

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
