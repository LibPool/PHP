# elzekool/koolrouter

> 标签: library, rest, router, routing

## 简介

Fast and flexible PHP5.3+ router with RESTfull support and reverse routing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
