# nyu8/flarum-email-filter

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Filter emails registering your Flarum site with whitelist, blacklist and even regular expressions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
