# fbf/laravel-pages

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Laravel 4 package for adding simple content managed pages to a website with banner image, heading, main image or YouTube video and body content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
