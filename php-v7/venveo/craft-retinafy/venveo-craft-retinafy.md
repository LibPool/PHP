# venveo/craft-retinafy

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, retinafy

## 简介

A direct remake of the Craft Retinafy plugin for Craft 2.0 by markgoodyear.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
