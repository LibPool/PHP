# wapmorgan/morphos-blade

> 标签: blade, morphology

## 简介

Adds @plural, @name, @numeral, @ordinal and @money tags to Blade for Russian pluralization and declenation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
