# bartlett/php-reflect

> 标签: reflection, reverse-engineer

## 简介

Adds the ability to reverse-engineer classes, interfaces, functions, constants, namespaces, traits and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
