# mariomka/disposable-email-detector

> 标签: disposable, email, emails, fake

## 简介

A PHP package to detect disposable emails based on ivolo/disposable-email-domains list.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
