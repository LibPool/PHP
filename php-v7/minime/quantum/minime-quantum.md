# minime/quantum

> 标签: collection, container, data type, parallel, php, quantum

## 简介

Quantum is unique kind of container made to manage parallel states of data structures.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
