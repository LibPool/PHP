# ehough/pagodabox-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Symfony2 bundle that makes it easy to configure and deploy your app on Pagoda Box.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
