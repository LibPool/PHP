# ice-cream/di

> 标签: dependency injection, dependency management, di, library

## 简介

ice-cream di (Dependency Injection) is my own take on DI, to keep it simple and easy to use.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
