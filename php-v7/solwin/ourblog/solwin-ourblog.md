# solwin/ourblog

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The Advanced Blog Magento 2 extension gives you a opportunity to communicate with your regular customers and casual visitors.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
