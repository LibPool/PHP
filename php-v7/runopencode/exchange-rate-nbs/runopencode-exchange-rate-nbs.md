# runopencode/exchange-rate-nbs

> 标签: exchange rate, national bank of serbia, nbs

## 简介

Plugin for 'runopencode/exchange-rate' library for fetching exchange rates from National Bank of Serbia.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
