# sdekkers/googlmapper

> 标签: google, laravel, map, mapping, maps, streetview

## 简介

An easy way to integrate Google Maps with Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
