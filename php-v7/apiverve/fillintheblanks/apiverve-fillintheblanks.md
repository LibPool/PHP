# apiverve/fillintheblanks

> 标签: api, apiverve, cloze test, completion puzzle, fill in the blank, fillintheblanks, idioms, vocabulary quiz

## 简介

Fill in the Blanks Generator creates completion puzzles from idioms, proverbs, quotes, science facts, and geography trivia.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
