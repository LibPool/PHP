# apiverve/spellingbee

> 标签: api, apiverve, spelling bee api, spelling bee generator, spelling bee json, spelling bee puzzle generator, spellingbee, word puzzle

## 简介

Spelling Bee Generator creates word puzzles with 7 letters where players form words using a required center letter.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
