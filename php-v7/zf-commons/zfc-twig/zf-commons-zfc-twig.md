# zf-commons/zfc-twig

> 标签: module, twig, zf2

## 简介

Zend Framework 2 Module that provides a Twig rendering strategy and extensions to render actions or trigger events from your templates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
