# wikp/payment-mtgox-bundle

> 标签: bitcoin, payment

## 简介

Bundle for creating payment system based on MtGox.com (Bitcoin cryptocurrency

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
