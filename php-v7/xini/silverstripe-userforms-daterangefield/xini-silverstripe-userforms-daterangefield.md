# xini/silverstripe-userforms-daterangefield

> 标签: date, range, silverstripe, userforms

## 简介

Adds a date range field consisting of two date fields with validation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
