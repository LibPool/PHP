# maatify/google-recaptcha

> 标签: handler, php, reCaptchaV2, recaptcha, recaptchav3

## 简介

Official PHP library for maatify.dev GoogleRecaptcha handler, known by our team

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
