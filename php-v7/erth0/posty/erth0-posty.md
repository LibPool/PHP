# erth0/posty

> 标签: articles, command, console, laravel-posty-cli, laravel-zero, posty, posty-cli, posty-console, posty-laravel-zero

## 简介

Posty is a simple way to manage your blog articles from the comfort of your computer with a simple to use CLI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
