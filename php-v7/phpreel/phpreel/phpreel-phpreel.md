# phpreel/phpreel

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

phpReel is a free, MIT open-source, subscription-based video streaming application that lets you create your platform for distributing content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
