# instituteweb/iw_master

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

iw_master is distribution for TYPO3 CMS for enterprise requirements. Easy deployable. Highly scalable.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
