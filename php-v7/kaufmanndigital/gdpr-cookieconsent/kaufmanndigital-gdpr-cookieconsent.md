# kaufmanndigital/gdpr-cookieconsent

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A ready-to-run package, that integrates an advanced cookie consent banner into your Neos CMS site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
