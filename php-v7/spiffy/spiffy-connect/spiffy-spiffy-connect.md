# spiffy/spiffy-connect

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

SpiffyConnect is a connector implementing OAuth1 and OAuth2 to make communicating with service providers as easy as possible.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
