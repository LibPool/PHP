# pantheon-systems/pantheon-hud

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provide situational awareness of the Pantheon platform from within your WordPress dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
