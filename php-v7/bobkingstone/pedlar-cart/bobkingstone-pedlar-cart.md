# bobkingstone/pedlar-cart

> 标签: ecommerce, laravel, shopping cart

## 简介

A simple drop in shopping cart for laravel ecommerce sites

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
