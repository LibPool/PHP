# tbritz/ffmphp

> 标签: converting, ffmpeg, preview, thumbnail, transcoding, video

## 简介

Convert media, save previews, and more. All with clean, elegant syntax that won't get in your way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
