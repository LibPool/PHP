# imanghafoori/laravel-responder

> 标签: laravel, laravel5, laravel5-package, php

## 简介

A minimal yet powerful package to give you opportunity to refactor your controllers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
