# comradeyanis/hyva-magento-ios-notifications

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Small module that replaced old boring Magento customer web notifications with new designed IOS 26 notification with effect of "Luquid Glass".

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
