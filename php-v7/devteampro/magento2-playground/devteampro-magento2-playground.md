> 标签: dev-tools, developer tool, devteampro, magento, magento extensions, magento playground, magento2, playground, quick test, sandbox, test environment, testing

## 简介

Playground for devs to quickly run & test services and methods.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
