# seasonal-holidays/seasonal-holidays

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Auto-creates seasonal promotions (Halloween, Black Friday, New Year) and shopping experince banner, topbar and hero

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
