# chllen/hyperf-service-micro-etcd

> 标签: etcd-adapter, hyperf, php, service, swoole

## 简介

A etcd adapter for service go-micro.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
