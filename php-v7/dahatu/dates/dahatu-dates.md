# dahatu/dates

> 标签: calendar, date converter, full dates

## 简介

A full dates that support all dates such gregorian, iranian, kurdish, hebrew, ... .

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
