# offerel/syncmarks

> 标签: bookmarks, firefox, plugin, roundcube

## 简介

Access your Firefox Bookmarks in Roundcube

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
