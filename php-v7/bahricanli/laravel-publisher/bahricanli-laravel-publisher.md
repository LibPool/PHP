# bahricanli/laravel-publisher

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Connects your Laravel app to content-manager.tr — publish, update and delete posts via a secure token-based API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
