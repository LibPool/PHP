# eclipxe/buzoncfdi-cfdireader

> 标签: cfdi, cfdi3.2, cfdi3.3, mexico, sat

## 简介

Library to read and validate a Mexican CFDI 3.2 and 3.3 (Comprobante Fiscal Digital por Internet)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
