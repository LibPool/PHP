# takaaki-mizuno/video-service-url-analyzer

> 标签: video, vimeo, youtube

## 简介

Video URL analyzer for famous video sharing services

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
