# hoa/kitab

> 标签: documentation, generate, library, search, test

## 简介

Kitab is the ideal companion for Documentation-Driven Quality: Render and Test your documentation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
