# auth0/auth0_drupal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Single Sign On for Enterprises + Social Login + User/Passwords. For all your Drupal instances. Powered by Auth0.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
