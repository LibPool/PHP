# opencartbot/nbu-qr-v3

> 标签: Banking, nbu, payment, qr, qr-code, ukraine

## 简介

PHP library for generating QR codes according to NBU (National Bank of Ukraine) standard version 003

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
