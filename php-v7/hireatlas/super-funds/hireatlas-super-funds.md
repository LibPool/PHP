# hireatlas/super-funds

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Pull the latest list of superannuation funds directly from the Australian Government, right into your Laravel application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
