# krautgortna/statamic-disqus

> 标签: comments, disqus, forum, statamic

## 简介

This Statamic Addon adds a Disqus comment section to your Statamic views

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
