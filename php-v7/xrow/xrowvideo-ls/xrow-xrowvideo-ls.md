# xrow/xrowvideo-ls

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A video datatype for eZ Publish with transcoding into multiple formats and bitrates and a HTML5 player with flash fallback.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
