# cognito/paymentexpress

> 标签: direct payment solutions, dps, gateway, merchant, omnipay, pay, payment, payment express, paymentexpress, pxaccess, pxpay, pxpost

## 简介

Payment Express (DPS) driver for the Omnipay payment processing library

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
