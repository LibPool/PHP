# flancer32/mage2_ext_csp

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Magento 2 module to collect CSP violations reports and to eliminate JS console warnings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
