# imonroe/bookscanner

> 标签: ISBN, barcode, bookscanner, imonroe

## 简介

Scan the barcode on a book, get back data on the book based on ISBN

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
