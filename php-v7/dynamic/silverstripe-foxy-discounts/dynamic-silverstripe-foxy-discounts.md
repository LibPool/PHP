# dynamic/silverstripe-foxy-discounts

> 标签: cms, silverstripe

## 简介

Offer discounts on purchase conditions for your Foxy products.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
