# ttek-plg/plg-skill

> 标签: EMS, GOALS, plugin, skills, tropotek

## 简介

EMS III Assessment plugin, Gives users the ability to fill in a questionnaire to assess students.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
