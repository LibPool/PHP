# ttek-plg/plg-ca

> 标签: Assessment, EMS, compentency, plugin, tropotek

## 简介

The University Of Melbourne - FVAS EMS Placement Compentency Assessment plugin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
