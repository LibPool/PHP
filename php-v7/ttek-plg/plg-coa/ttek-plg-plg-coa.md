# ttek-plg/plg-coa

> 标签: EMS, GOALS, plugin, skills, tropotek

## 简介

EMS COA plugin, Send Certificates Of Appreciation to companies/staff/students.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
