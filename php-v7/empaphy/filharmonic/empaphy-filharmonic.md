# empaphy/filharmonic

> 标签: Harmony, compatibility, portable, symfony

## 简介

A metapackage that ensures all your Symfony dependencies stay in sync.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
