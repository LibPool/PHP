# puz/multicolumnauth

> 标签: auth, authenticate, authorisation, column, columns, field, fields, laravel, multi, multiple

## 简介

Makes it possible to log in with both a username and / or email

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
