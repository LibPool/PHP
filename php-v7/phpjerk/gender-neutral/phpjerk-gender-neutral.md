# phpjerk/gender-neutral

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Make your text is gender neutral so people don't get their panties in a bunch. (and yes, men can wear panties too!)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
