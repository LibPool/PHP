# reliqarts/direct-translator

> 标签: creole, machine, statistical, translation, translator

## 简介

Direct Translator is a plug-and-play library that offers direct phrase and word replacement translation capabilities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
