# hansott/lastify

> 标签: hansott, lastfm, lastify, playlists, spotify, syncing

## 简介

Lastify syncs spotify playlists with your last.fm top tracks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
