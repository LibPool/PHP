# stephanwagner/laracms

> 标签: admin, cms, framework, laracms, laravel

## 简介

A highly customizable Content Management System based on the Laravel framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
