# udokmeci/yii2-beanstalk

> 标签: beanstalk client, beanstalkd, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 Beanstalk Client at the top of Paul Annesley's pheanstalk

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
