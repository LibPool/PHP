# luxifer/rabbitmq-management-bundle

> 标签: bundle, management, rabbitmq

## 简介

Symfony bundle to bridge richardfullmer/php-rabbitmq-management-api

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
