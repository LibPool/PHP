# iet-ou/open-media-player

> 标签: IET-OU, MediaPlayer, OpenUniversity, accessibility, audio, mediaelementjs, oembed, ouplayer, video

## 简介

A mainstream audio and video player service that puts accessibility front and centre. Built on MediaElement.js, CodeIgniter & oEmbed. From The Open University.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
