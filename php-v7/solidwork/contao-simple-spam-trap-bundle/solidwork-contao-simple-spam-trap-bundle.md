# solidwork/contao-simple-spam-trap-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Simple spam protection for Contao forms (honeypot + timestamp) for Contao 4.13

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
