# wolfgang-demeter/flarum-ext-bbcode-5star-rating

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Rate some things in Flarum with 5 stars - but in half-star increments!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
