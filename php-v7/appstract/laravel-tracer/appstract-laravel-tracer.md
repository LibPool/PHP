# appstract/laravel-tracer

> 标签: appstract, blade, hints, laravel, path, php

## 简介

Adds the paths of all loaded views/partials to your templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
