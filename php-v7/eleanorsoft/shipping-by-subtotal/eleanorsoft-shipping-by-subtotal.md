# eleanorsoft/shipping-by-subtotal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

EleanorSoft module with a new shipping method that calculates shipping cost as percent of subtotal

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
