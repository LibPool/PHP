# bigfootcms/auth

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Plug & Play Authentication Suite built around PHP-Auth specifically for BigfootCMS. Simple, lightweight and secure.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
