# betterbrief/silverstripe-pdf

> 标签: pdf, silverstripe

## 简介

Provide PDF renders of your webpages or objects using SSViewer, Snappy and wkhtmltopdf

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
