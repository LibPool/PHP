# vivutio/agency-pack

> 标签: agency, pack, tickets, tourism, vivutio

## 简介

What a travel agency of vivutio installs: the tickets it buys from carriers for travellers, and the rooms it requests from the camps it trades with.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
