# enggarasmoro/laravel-error-alert

> 标签: email, error-alert, laravel, monitoring, queue

## 简介

Low overhead error email alerts with queue or direct delivery for Laravel 6 through 13.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
