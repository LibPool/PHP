# tenth/my-total-comfort

> 标签: HVAC, Honeywell, IIoT, home automation, iot

## 简介

Interact with Honeywell Total Comfort Control thermostats and devices.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
