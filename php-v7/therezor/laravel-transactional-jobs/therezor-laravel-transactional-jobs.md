# therezor/laravel-transactional-jobs

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Submit laravel jobs inside transaction. Cancel job after rollback. Proceed after successful commit.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
