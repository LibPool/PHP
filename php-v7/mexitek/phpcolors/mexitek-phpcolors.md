# mexitek/phpcolors

> 标签: color, css, design, frontend, ui

## 简介

A series of methods that let you manipulate colors. Just incase you ever need different shades of one color on the fly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
