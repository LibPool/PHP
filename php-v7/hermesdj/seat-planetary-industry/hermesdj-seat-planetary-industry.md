# hermesdj/seat-planetary-industry

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provide additional tools to manage planetary industry for multiple accounts and work as a team in a corporation

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
