# serendipity_hq/bundle-commands-queues

> 标签: cronjob, cronjobs, queues, threading

## 简介

Manage queues of commands and processes them in your Symfony app.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
