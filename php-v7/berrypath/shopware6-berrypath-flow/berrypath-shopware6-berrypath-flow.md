# berrypath/shopware6-berrypath-flow

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Add guided product advice to Shopping Experiences and product pages with BerryPath Flow widgets, multilingual settings and assisted conversion measurement.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
