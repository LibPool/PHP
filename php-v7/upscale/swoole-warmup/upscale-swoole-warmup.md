# upscale/swoole-warmup

> 标签: cold, crawler, performance, pre-warm, prewarm, prime, swoole, url, warm, warm up, warm-up

## 简介

URL crawler to warm-up Swoole web-server

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
