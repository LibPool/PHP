# upscale/ext-swoole

> 标签: Open Swoole, ext-openswoole, ext-swoole, extension, openswoole, swoole

## 简介

Wrapper for ext-swoole interchangeable with ext-openswoole

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
