# crizzo/aboutus

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

About us is an extension which adds to phpBB 3.2/3.3 an about us or legal notice-page, where you can add your contact details and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
