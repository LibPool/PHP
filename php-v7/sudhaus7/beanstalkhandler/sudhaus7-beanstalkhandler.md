# sudhaus7/beanstalkhandler

> 标签: beanstalk, log, logging, monolog, psr-3

## 简介

Sends your logs to files, sockets, inboxes, databases and various web services

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
