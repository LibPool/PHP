# sudhaus7/mail-spool

> 标签: extension, mail, spool, swiftmailer, typo3

## 简介

Integrates swiftmailer spool transport for TYPO3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
