# yanzicms/yanzicms

> 标签: cms, yanzi, yanzicms

## 简介

Open Source Content Management. Yanzicms is the mobile-ready and user-friendly way to build your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
