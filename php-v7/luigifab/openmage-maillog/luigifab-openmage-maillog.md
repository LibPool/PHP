# luigifab/openmage-maillog

> 标签: openmage, openmage-module

## 简介

A module to show history of emails sent and to sync customers with Emarsys and Mautic for OpenMage.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
