# matthewbdaly/laravel-flatpages

> 标签: cms, flatpages, laravel, pages

## 简介

Laravel flat pages package. Inspired by Django's flatpages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
