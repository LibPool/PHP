# tearoom1/kirby-content-watch

> 标签: content, history, kirby, panel

## 简介

Kirby plugin that allows you to keep track of what has been changed and by whom.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
