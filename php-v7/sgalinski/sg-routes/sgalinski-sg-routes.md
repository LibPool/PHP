# sgalinski/sg-routes

> 标签: analytics, csv-import, htaccess, redirect, regex, rewrite, sys_redirect, typo3, typo3-extension, url-redirect

## 简介

Advanced Redirect Manager for TYPO3 - Advanced TYPO3 redirect management for sys_redirect with regex and host/language-aware matching, CSV/.htaccess import/export, categories and priority, automatic slug redirect workflows, plus hit/log analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
