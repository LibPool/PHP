# drbiko/slack

> 标签: laravel, slack, slim

## 简介

A simple PHP package (fork of php-slack/slack) for sending messages to Slack, with a focus on ease of use and elegant syntax.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
