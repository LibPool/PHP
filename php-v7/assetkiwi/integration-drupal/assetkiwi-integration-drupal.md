# assetkiwi/integration-drupal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Connects to asset.kiwi digital asset management platform with asset browsing, usage tracking, webhook sync, and image formatting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
