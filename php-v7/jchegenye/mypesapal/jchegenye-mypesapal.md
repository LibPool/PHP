# jchegenye/mypesapal

> 标签: getway, laravel, mypesapal, payment, pesapal

## 简介

A simplified PesaPal integration for your Laravel App.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
