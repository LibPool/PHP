# newball/spacetools

> 标签: Newball, Newlines, SpaceTools, line breaks, space, white space

## 简介

Generating white spaces and line breaks / newlines for repetitive and frequent use.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
