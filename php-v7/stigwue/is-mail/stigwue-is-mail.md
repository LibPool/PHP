# stigwue/is-mail

> 标签: email, validation

## 简介

A funny and hopefully non-annoying way to pick random names for unregistered users or anonymize identities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
