# hnto/bick

> 标签: batch, bick, rabbitmq, tracking

## 简介

A RabbitMQ wrapper with additional options (batch publish, tracking, message persistence)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
