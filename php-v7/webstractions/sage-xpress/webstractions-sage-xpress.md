# webstractions/sage-xpress

> 标签: roots, sage, webstractions

## 简介

Extends Sage ~9-beta with Blade directives, component providers, streamlined configurations, and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
