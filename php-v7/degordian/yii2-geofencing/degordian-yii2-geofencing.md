# degordian/yii2-geofencing

> 标签: degordian, extension, geofencing, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 ActionFilter for allowing or denying access based on the visitors geo location

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
