# exertis/yii2-save-with-audit-trail-behavior

> 标签: extension, yii2

## 简介

Adds a $model->saveWithAuditTrail() method to your Models allowing you to annotate changes to your data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
