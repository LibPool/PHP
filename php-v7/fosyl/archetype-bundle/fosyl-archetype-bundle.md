# fosyl/archetype-bundle

> 标签: archetypes, assortment, ecommerce, product, products, prototypes, shop

## 简介

Archetypes of models for your Symfony2 applications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
