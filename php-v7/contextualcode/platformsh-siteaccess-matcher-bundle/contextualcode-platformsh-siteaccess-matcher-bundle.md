# contextualcode/platformsh-siteaccess-matcher-bundle

> 标签: ezplatform, ezpublish, platformsh

## 简介

Platform.sh adopted eZ Platform Map\Host site access matcher.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
