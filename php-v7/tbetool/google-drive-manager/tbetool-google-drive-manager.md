# tbetool/google-drive-manager

> 标签: Anuj Sharma, Anuj TBE, TBE, drive-php-service, drive-sdk-php, google drive, google-php-sdk, the born engineer

## 简介

Based on Google PHP SDK. Enables using upload and download to Google Drive.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
