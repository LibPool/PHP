# pdir/business-reviews-bundle

> 标签: automobile, business, contao, franchise, hospitality, hotels, real estate, restaurant, retail store, reviews, website

## 简介

Easily display reviews from multiple platforms like Google My Business, Facebook and Trusted Shops on your Contao website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
