# thunderbug/tracker-quake-connection

> 标签: Call of Duty 1, Call of Duty 2, Call of Duty 4, call of duty, gameserver, master, modern warfare, php, quake, wolfenstein

## 简介

A library to retrieve information from quake master servers and game servers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
