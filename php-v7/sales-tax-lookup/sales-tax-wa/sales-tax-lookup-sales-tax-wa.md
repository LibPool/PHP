# sales-tax-lookup/sales-tax-wa

> 标签: WA tax lookup, sales tax, sales tax finder, washtington state

## 简介

A simple package to look up sales tax based on address in washington.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
