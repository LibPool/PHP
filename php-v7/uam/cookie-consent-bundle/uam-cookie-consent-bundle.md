# uam/cookie-consent-bundle

> 标签: EU cookie policy, bundle, symfony, twig

## 简介

Add a banner allowing the user to consent to your symfony app's cookie policy

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
