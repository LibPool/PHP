# sigbits/roomba-bundle

> 标签: roomba, roowifi, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle to communicate with your Roomba vacuum cleaner

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
