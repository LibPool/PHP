# spatie/checkout

> 标签: checkout, gateway, helper, laravel, payment, webshop

## 简介

A simple e-commerce checkout helper for Laravel 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
