# mandrew/silverstripe-quickfeedback

> 标签: feedback, member, silverstripe

## 简介

Creates a quick yes/no feedback form which can be added at the bottom of your site

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
