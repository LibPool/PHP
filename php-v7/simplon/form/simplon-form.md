# simplon/form

> 标签: calendar, fields, filters, form, phtml, rules, semantic-ui, templates, validation

## 简介

Data validation & flexible form rendering incl. semantic-ui structure.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
