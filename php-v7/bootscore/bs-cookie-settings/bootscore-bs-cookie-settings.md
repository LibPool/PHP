# bootscore/bs-cookie-settings

> 标签: bootscore, bootstrap, contact form 7, plugin, wordpress

## 简介

Adds a cookie consent banner to Bootscore

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
