# offworks/laraquent

> 标签: eloquent, exedra, laravel, mysql, orm, php-mysql

## 简介

An out of Laravel Eloquent 5.1 extended use, and as a provider to different microframeworks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
