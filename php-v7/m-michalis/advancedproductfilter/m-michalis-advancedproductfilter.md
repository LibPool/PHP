# m-michalis/advancedproductfilter

> 标签: magento, openmage, product filter

## 简介

Adminhtml Advanced Product Filter for OpenMage 20.x & Magento 1.9.x similar to promo rules.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
