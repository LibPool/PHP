# teltek/pumukit-moodle-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, bundle, moodle, pumukit, teltek, upload, uploader, videos

## 简介

Moodle bundle for Symfony2 to upload videos to Moodle and other features

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
