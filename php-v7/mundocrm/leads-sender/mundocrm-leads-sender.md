# mundocrm/leads-sender

> 标签: Forms, crm, leads, mundocrm, send, webhooks

## 简介

Vendor to send leads forms to mundocrm.ar.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
