# ucf/today-child-theme

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

WordPress theme for UCF Today - News and Stories from the University of Central Florida and Orlando.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
