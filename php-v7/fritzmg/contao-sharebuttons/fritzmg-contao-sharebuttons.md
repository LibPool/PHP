# fritzmg/contao-sharebuttons

> 标签: buttons, contao, facebook, share, social, twitter

## 简介

Simple Contao extension to provide share buttons as a module and content element

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
