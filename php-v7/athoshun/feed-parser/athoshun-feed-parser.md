# athoshun/feed-parser

> 标签: atom, feed, parse, parser, rss

## 简介

A simple library to parse the most important information from RSS and Atom feeds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
