# weblizards/tag-management-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Re-introduces the 'Tag and Snippet Management' feature which was deprecated in Pimcore 6.x and removed in Pimcore 10.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
