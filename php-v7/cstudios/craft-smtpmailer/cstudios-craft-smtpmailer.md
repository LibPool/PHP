# cstudios/craft-smtpmailer

> 标签: contact, email, form, mailer, smtp, templates

## 简介

A contact form plugin to send out emails in various ways

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
