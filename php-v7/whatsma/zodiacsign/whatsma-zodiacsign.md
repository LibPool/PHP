# whatsma/zodiacsign

> 标签: astrological sign, star sign, sun sign, zodiac sign

## 简介

Calculates the zodiac sign for a given day and month.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
