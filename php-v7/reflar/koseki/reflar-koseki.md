# reflar/koseki

> 标签: categories, category, classic hierarchy, extension, flagrow, flarum, reflar

## 简介

Tidy up your Flarum forum with a structured hierarchical layout.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
