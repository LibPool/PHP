# dfware/ar-linkmany

> 标签: active, link, many, many to many, many-to-many, record, relaton, yii2

## 简介

Provides support for many-to-many relations saving in Yii2 ActiveRecord

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
