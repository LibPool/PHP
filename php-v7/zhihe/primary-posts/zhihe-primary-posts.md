# zhihe/primary-posts

> 标签: extension, flarum, serialized, stories

## 简介

Mark posts as primary content for serialized stories and multi-chapter discussions

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
