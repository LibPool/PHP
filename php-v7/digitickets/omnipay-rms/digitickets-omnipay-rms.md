# digitickets/omnipay-rms

> 标签: gateway, merchant, omnipay, pay, payment, purchase, retail merchant services, rms

## 简介

Omnipay driver for Retail Merchant Services (RMS)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
