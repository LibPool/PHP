# nobrainerweb/accepted-payment-methods

> 标签: module, silverstripe

## 简介

Provides a simple module to show a user what payment methods are available on your shop

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
