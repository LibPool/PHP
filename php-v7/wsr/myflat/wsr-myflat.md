# wsr/myflat

> 标签: availability check, calendar, extension, typo3, vacation flat

## 简介

Flat manager, shows bookings of holiday flats in calendars, availability check included.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
