# alroniks/dtms

> 标签: date, datetime, fractions, interval, iso8601, microseconds, milliseconds, ms, time

## 简介

Classes for manipulate dates and intervals with microseconds precision

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
