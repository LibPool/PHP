# salvaracer/t3readspeaker

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Integrate ReadSpeaker webReader and docReader services to your website. Provides 30+ Languages, 50+ Voices, easy to use

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
