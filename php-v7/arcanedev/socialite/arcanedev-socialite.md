# arcanedev/socialite

> 标签: arcanedev, bitbucket, facebook, github, google, laravel, linked-in, oauth, twitter

## 简介

Laravel wrapper around OAuth 1 & OAuth 2 libraries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
