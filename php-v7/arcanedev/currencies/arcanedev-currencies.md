# arcanedev/currencies

> 标签: arcanedev, converter, currencies, currency, exchange, iso, laravel, money, rates

## 简介

A framework agnostic currency conversion and formatting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
