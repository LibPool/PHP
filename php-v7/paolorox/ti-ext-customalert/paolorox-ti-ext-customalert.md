# paolorox/ti-ext-customalert

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Full-screen customizable alert and announcement popup with blurred background, action buttons, and customer view logs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
