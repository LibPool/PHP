# commacodes/auto-convert-text-to-audio

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Automatically converts multilingual text to audio and attaches it to your Laravel models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
