# denissimon/formula-parser

> 标签: Evaluation, expression, formula, math, parser, parsing

## 简介

Parsing and evaluating mathematical formulas given as strings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
