# gongo/merciful-polluter

> 标签: magic_quotes_gpc, register_globals

## 简介

Emulate `register_globals` and `magic_quotes_gpc` in PHP 5.4 or higher

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
