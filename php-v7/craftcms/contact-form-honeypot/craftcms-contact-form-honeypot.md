# craftcms/contact-form-honeypot

> 标签: Honeypot, cms, contact, craftcms, form, yii2

## 简介

Add a honeypot captcha to your Craft CMS contact form

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
