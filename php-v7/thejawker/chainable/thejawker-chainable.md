# thejawker/chainable

> 标签: OOP, chaining, fluent, thejawker

## 简介

Exposes a Class that makes method chaining fluent and easy. Also exposes a Trait.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
