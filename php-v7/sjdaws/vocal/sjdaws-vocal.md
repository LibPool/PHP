# sjdaws/vocal

> 标签: eloquent, hydration, laravel, laravel4, laravel5, recursive, validation

## 简介

Recursive functionality for Eloquent in Laravel 4 and 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
