# devdesigns/genesis-customizer-support

> 标签: customizer, genesis, genesis-framework, wordpress, wordpress-plugin

## 简介

Adds customizer support to your Genesis child theme.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
