# andanteproject/recaptcha-bundle

> 标签: PHP7, google-recaptcha, google-recaptcha-v2, php, php74, symfony, symfony-bundle, symfony-form, symfony-formtype

## 简介

A Symfony Bundle to easily integrate Google reCAPTCHA into Symfony Form

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
