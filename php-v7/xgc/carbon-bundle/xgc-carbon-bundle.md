# xgc/carbon-bundle

> 标签: carbon, dbal, doctrine, symfony

## 简介

A doctrine adaptation of <a href='https://github.com/briannesbitt/Carbon' target='_blank'>nesbot/carbon</a>

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
