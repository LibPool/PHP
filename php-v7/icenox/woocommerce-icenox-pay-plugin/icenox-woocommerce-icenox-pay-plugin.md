# icenox/woocommerce-icenox-pay-plugin

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Connect your WooCommerce Store with IceNox Pay. The payment system for your online shop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
