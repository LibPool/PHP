# sakasoft/iyzipay-laravel

> 标签: actuallymab, iyzico, iyzipay, iyzipay-laravel

## 简介

Iyzipay (by Iyzico) integration for your Laravel projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
