# camilord/fam3x

> 标签: fam, famfamfam, free icon set

## 简介

FamFamFam Silk icons is a smooth, free icon set, containing over 700 16-by-16 pixel icons in strokably-soft PNG format. Containing a large variety of icons, you're sure to find something that tickles your fancy. And all for a low low price of $0.00. You can't say fairer than that.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
