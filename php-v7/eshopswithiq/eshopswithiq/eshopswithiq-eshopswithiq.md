# eshopswithiq/eshopswithiq

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This extension will integrate your eshop with EshopsWithIQ (https://eshopswithiq.com)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
