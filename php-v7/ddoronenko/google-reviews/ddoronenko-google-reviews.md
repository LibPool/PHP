# ddoronenko/google-reviews

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, business, google, review

## 简介

TYPO3 extension for simple Google reviews output according to the selected place.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
