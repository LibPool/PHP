# hypejunction/hypeshutdown

> 标签: elgg, plugin, shutdown

## 简介

Flushes the buffer after the response is sent allowing shutdown to continue in the background

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
