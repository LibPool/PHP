# imefisto/psr-swoole

> 标签: psr, psr-swoole, swoole

## 简介

Library to convert Swoole requests to PSR requests and PSR responses to Swoole responses

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
