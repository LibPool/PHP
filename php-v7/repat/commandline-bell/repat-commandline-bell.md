# repat/commandline-bell

> 标签: ascii, beep, bel, bell, flash, sound

## 简介

Makes the ASCII Bell sound or flashes terminal

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 4；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
