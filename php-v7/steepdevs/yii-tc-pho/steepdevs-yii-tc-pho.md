# steepdevs/yii-tc-pho

> 标签: pho, teamcity, yii, yii pho teamcity

## 简介

Pho integrated with Yii and TeamCity

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
