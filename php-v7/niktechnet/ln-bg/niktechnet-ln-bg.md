# niktechnet/ln-bg

> 标签: bulgarian, extension, flarum, language, locale, localization, niktechnet, translation

## 简介

Bulgarian language pack for Flarum

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
