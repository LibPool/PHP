# seanhayes-com/laravel-hcaptcha

> 标签: Abuse, captcha, hcaptcha, spam

## 简介

Client library for hCAPTCHA, a free service that protects websites from spam and abuse.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
