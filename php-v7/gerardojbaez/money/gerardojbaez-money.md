# gerardojbaez/money

> 标签: currency, formatter, money, money_format

## 简介

A simple and cross-platform alternative to PHP money_format(). 91 currencies supported, including INR.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
