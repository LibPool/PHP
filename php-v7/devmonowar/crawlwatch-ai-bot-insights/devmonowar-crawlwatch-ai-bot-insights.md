# devmonowar/crawlwatch-ai-bot-insights

> 标签: ai bots, ai crawler, ai-bot-traffic, ai-crawler-detector, block-gptbot, bot traffic, gptbot, llms txt, llms-txt-generator, seo, wordpress, wordpress-plugin

## 简介

Track AI bot traffic on WordPress – see GPTBot, ClaudeBot and 40+ bots, block unwanted crawlers, generate llms.txt.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
