# tinkers/yii2-upload-behavior

> 标签: extension, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 upload behavior which extends the functionality of mohorev/yii2-upload-behavior

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
