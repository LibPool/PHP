# percipiolondon/craft-stopwatch

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craft-stopwatch, craftcms, readtime

## 简介

This plugin calculates how much time it takes to read the page and watch the videos or listen to audio fragments

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
