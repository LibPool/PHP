# markustimtner/mt-grids

> 标签: Gridelements, TYPO3 CMS, typo3

## 简介

Predefined gridelements. Columns, accordions, tabs and slider

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
