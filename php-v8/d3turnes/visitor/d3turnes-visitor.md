# d3turnes/visitor

> 标签: clicks, counter, framework, hits, laravel, log, visitors

## 简介

log your visitors in db, page hits, and generate visit counter for Laravel 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
