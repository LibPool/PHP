# kadokweb/optimizer

> 标签: Facebook Tags, Kadok, KadokWeb, SMO, meta tags, open graph, seo, twitter card

## 简介

Optimizer makes it easy to bookmark your site tags, open graph and twitter card

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
