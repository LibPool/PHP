# cviebrock/eloquent-sluggable

> 标签: eloquent, eloquent-sluggable, laravel, slug, sluggable

## 简介

Easy creation of slugs for your Eloquent models in Laravel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
