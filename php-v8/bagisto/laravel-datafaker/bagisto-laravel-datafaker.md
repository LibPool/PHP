# bagisto/laravel-datafaker

> 标签: bagisto, fake categories, fake customers, fake products

## 简介

Create fake customers, categories and products in Bagisto.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
