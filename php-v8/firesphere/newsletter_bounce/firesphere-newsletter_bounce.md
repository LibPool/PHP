# firesphere/newsletter_bounce

> 标签: bounce, email, email bounce, newsletter, newsletter bounces, silverstripe

## 简介

Extra addon for the Silverstripe Newsletter module, to manage bounces and blacklisting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
