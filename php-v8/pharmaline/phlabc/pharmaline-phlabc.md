# pharmaline/phlabc

> 标签: TYPO3 Extension, TYPO3 Felogin, TYPO3 Frontend Login, security, typo3

## 简介

Pharmaline Extension to manage access to objects, content and actions with permissions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
