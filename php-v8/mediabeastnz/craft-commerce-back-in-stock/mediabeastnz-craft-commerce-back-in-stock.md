# mediabeastnz/craft-commerce-back-in-stock

> 标签: Craft, back-in-stock, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

A Craft CMS plugin for notifying Craft Commerce customers when products are back in stock.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
