# bmack/gen-z

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

No cap! Registers 'Gen Z Speak' as a backend language and translates all TYPO3 labels into American Gen-Z internet slang. It's giving productivity!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
