# raqmeyat/workspace

> 标签: laravel, multitenancy, raqmeyat, saas, spatie, tenancy, workspace

## 简介

Multi-tenant Workspace management for Laravel 12 and 13, built on spatie/laravel-multitenancy. Path, subdomain, and custom-domain tenancy, workspace settings, plans & subscriptions, and role-based staff management.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
