# raqmeyat/webrtc

> 标签: WebRTC, calls, coturn, laravel, raqmeyat, reverb, signaling, turn

## 简介

WebRTC signaling, call-session lifecycle and ICE/TURN credential service for Laravel 12 and 13.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
