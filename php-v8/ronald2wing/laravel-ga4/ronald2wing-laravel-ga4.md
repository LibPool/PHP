# ronald2wing/laravel-ga4

> 标签: analytics, blade, ga4, google-analytics, gtag, laravel, tracking

## 简介

Drop-in Google Analytics 4 (gtag) snippet for Laravel Blade.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
