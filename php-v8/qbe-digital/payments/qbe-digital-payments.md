# qbe-digital/payments

> 标签: bnpl, laravel, payment, payments, saudi, tabby, tamara

## 简介

A unified payments package for Laravel supporting Tabby, Tamara and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
