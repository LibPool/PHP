# plato-creative/silverstipe-platorecipe

> 标签: modules, plato, silverstripe

## 简介

Installs commonly used modules by PlatoCreative.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
