# skynettechnologies/typo3-skynetaccessibilityscanner

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

SkynetAccessibility Scanner Scan, monitor, and identify website accessibility issues across WCAG 2.0, 2.1, 2.2, ADA, and EAA.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
