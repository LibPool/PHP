# magmell-agentur/contao-boxes

> 标签: box, boxes, contao, content

## 简介

Provides flexible boxes for Contao CMS to manage content on a by-page basis.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
