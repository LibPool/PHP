# k2gl/sshsig

> 标签: ECDSA, Ed25519, allowed-signers, commit-signing, git, openssh, rsa, signature, ssh, ssh-keygen, sshsig, supply-chain, verify

## 简介

Pure-PHP SSHSIG signing and verification, compatible with ssh-keygen -Y.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
