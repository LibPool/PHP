# azzmin/cakephp-altcha

> 标签: ALTCHA, antispam, cakephp, cakephp5, captcha, spam

## 简介

Altcha proof-of-work spam protection for CakePHP 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
