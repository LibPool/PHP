# particle-academy/fancy-expr

> 标签: cross-language, evaluator, expression, interpreter, parser, particle-academy, sandbox, template, workflow

## 简介

A sandboxed expression evaluator: one grammar, three implementations, asserted against a shared conformance table. Paths, literals, logic, comparisons, ternaries and object/array literals -- with no eval, no host reach and no calls of any kind. A malformed expression throws; an absent path is null, and keeping those two apart is the point.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
