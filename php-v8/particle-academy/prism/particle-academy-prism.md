# particle-academy/prism

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A powerful Laravel package for integrating Large Language Models (LLMs) into your applications. Actively maintained fork of prism-php/prism.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
