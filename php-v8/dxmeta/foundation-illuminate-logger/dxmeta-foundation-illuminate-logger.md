# dxmeta/foundation-illuminate-logger

> 标签: laravel, logger

## 简介

Laravel LogWriter for 5.2 ~ 5.5, LogManager for 5.6 or newer.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
