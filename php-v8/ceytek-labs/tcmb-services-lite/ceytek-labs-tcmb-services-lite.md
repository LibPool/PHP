# ceytek-labs/tcmb-services-lite

> 标签: Central Bank of Turkey, banknote rates, currency, exchange rates, financial data, forex, lite library, php, tcmb, turkey

## 简介

A lightweight library for retrieving exchange rates and other data from TCMB's APIs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
