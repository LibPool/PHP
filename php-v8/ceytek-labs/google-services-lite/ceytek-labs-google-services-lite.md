# ceytek-labs/google-services-lite

> 标签: Google Services, automation, data updates, google api, google sheets, google-services-lite, lite library, php, sheets automation

## 简介

A lightweight library for seamless integration with various Google APIs in PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
