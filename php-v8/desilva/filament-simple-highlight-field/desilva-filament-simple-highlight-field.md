# desilva/filament-simple-highlight-field

> 标签: desilva, filament-simple-highlight-field, laravel

## 简介

Adds a simple read-only Highlight.js pseudo-field to FilamentPHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
