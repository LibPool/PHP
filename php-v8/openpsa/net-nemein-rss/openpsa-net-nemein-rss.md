# openpsa/net-nemein-rss

> 标签: atom, midcom, rss

## 简介

Aggregator that supports populating news items from remote RSS and Atom sources.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
