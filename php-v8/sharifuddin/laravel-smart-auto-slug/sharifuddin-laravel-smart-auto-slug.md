> 标签: auto-slug, customizable slug, eloquent, laravel, laravel-package, seo-friendly, seo-friendly-slug-generate, slug, slug-generator, unique auto slug, unique-slug

## 简介

Automatically generates clean, SEO-friendly, and unique slugs for Eloquent models with transaction-safe duplicate prevention.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
