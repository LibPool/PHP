# cloudwalk/infinitepay-woocommerce-plugin

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Offer to your clients the best interest in e-Commerce by using InfinitePay as your payment method.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
