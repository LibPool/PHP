# kamansoft/laravel-blame

> 标签: kamansoft, laravel, laravel-blame

## 简介

A tool to easily work with the updated_by and created_by fields on your laravel eloquent models

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
