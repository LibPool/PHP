# itxshakil/laravel-form-shield

> 标签: Forms, Honeypot, anti-spam, bot-protection, captcha, captcha-alternative, contact-form, inertia, laravel, laravel-package, livewire, no-captcha, rate-limiting, spam, spam-protection

## 简介

CAPTCHA-free spam scoring for Laravel forms: quarantine instead of reject, keep every verdict, and tune your rules against human-labelled data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
