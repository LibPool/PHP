# ilhanaydinli/cargo_tracking_for_woocommerce

> 标签: cargo, cargo tracking, kargo, kargo takip, shipping, shipping tracking, woocommerce cargo tracking, woocommerce kargo takip, woocommerce shipping tracking

## 简介

With the WooCommerce cargo tracking plugin, you can add as many cargo companies as you want, show cargo tracking links on the front and admin side, and send cargo tracking emails to users.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
