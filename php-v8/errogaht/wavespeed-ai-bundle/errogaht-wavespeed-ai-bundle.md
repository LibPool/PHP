# errogaht/wavespeed-ai-bundle

> 标签: ai, image-to-video, symfony, symfony-bundle, text-to-video, video-generation, wavespeed

## 简介

Symfony integration for WaveSpeedAI video generation, live model capabilities, pricing, uploads, predictions and verified webhooks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
