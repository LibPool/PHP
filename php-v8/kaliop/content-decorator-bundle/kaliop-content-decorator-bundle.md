# kaliop/content-decorator-bundle

> 标签: content, ibexa, repository-pattern, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Decorate your Ibexa content with custom model classes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
