# mulertech/scanner-ban-bundle

> 标签: ban, bundle, hardening, php, scanner, security, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle banning scanners on what they request, never on what they claim to be

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
