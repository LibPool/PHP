# mulertech/passkey-bundle

> 标签: Authentication, passkey, security, symfony, webauthn

## 简介

Symfony bundle for passkey (WebAuthn) sign-in: credential storage, key management page and the ceremony asset

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
