# gurzhii/laravel-sluggable-trait

> 标签: eloquent, laravel, sluggable

## 简介

A trait you can apply to Eloquent models to have slugs automatically generated on save.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
