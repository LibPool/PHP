# debugger84/zf2-graylog

> 标签: log, logging, zf2

## 简介

A Zend\Log writer and formatter helps you add Graylog logging to your ZF2 application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
