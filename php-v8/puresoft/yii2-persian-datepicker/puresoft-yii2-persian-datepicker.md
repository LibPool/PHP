# puresoft/yii2-persian-datepicker

> 标签: Jalali, calendar, date, datepicker, extension, persian, time, yii2

## 简介

This extension will display a Persian datepicker.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
