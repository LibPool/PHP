# plumtreesystems/sylius-article-plugin

> 标签: sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Plum Tree Systems article plugin is an extension for BitBagSyliusCmsPlugin. It adds your sections to the menu of your app

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
