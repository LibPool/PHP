# yii-dream-team/yii2-lockable-activerecord

> 标签: Behavior, activerecord, lock, yii2

## 简介

Pessimistic locking behavior for Yii2 ActiveRecord

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
