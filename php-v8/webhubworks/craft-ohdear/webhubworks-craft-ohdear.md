# webhubworks/craft-ohdear

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, oh dear, test

## 简介

Integrate the Oh Dear monitoring service into Craft CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
