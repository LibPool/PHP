# artisan-toolbox/qztray

> 标签: artisan-toolbox, laravel, qztray

## 简介

QZ Tray Connector bridges Laravel and your frontend through a unified abstraction for QZ Tray integration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
