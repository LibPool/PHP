> 标签: Craft, Forms, anti-bot, anti-bots, anti-spam, captcha, cms, contact, craft recaptcha 3, craft-plugin, craftcms, google, recaptcha, security, spam, validation

## 简介

Verifies via Google the site and secret codes required to verify humanity through reCAPTCHA v3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
