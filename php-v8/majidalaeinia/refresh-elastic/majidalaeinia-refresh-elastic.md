# majidalaeinia/refresh-elastic

> 标签: RefreshElastic, elasticsearch, laravel, test

## 简介

Refresh Elasticsearch indices(truncate and make them again based on their mappings) for your tests.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
