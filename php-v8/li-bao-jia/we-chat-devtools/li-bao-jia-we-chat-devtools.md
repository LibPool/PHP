# li-bao-jia/we-chat-devtools

> 标签: li-bao-jia, we-chat-devtools

## 简介

Instruct WeChat devtools to perform operations such as login, preview, and upload.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
