# swedbank-pay/magento2-checkout

> 标签: magento, payment, swedbank-pay

## 简介

Replaces checkout in Magento 2 with a onepage checkout with prepared Swedbank Pay elements

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
