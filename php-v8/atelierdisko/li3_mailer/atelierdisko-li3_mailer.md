# atelierdisko/li3_mailer

> 标签: li3, lithium, mail, mailgun, php, smtp, swiftmailer

## 简介

A plugin for sending email messages from your Lithium application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
