# evanrthompson/nova-ratings

> 标签: Rating, field, laravel, nova, stars

## 简介

A star rating field for Laravel Nova with half-star ratings, custom icons, and color customization.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
