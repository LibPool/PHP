# codewithdennis/filament-simple-alert

> 标签: CodeWithDennis, filament-simple-alert, laravel

## 简介

A plugin for adding straightforward alerts to your filament pages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
