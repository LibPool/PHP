# tylersatre/laravel-health-check

> 标签: laravel, laravel-health-check, lumen, lumen-health-check

## 简介

A package for checking the health of your Laravel/Lumen applications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
