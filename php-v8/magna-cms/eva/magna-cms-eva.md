# magna-cms/eva

> 标签: blog, dark-mode, editorial, laravel, magna, magna-blog, magna-cms, magna-pages, magna-theme, theme

## 简介

Eva — a warm, editorial journal theme for Magna CMS. Native to Magna Pages, styled for Magna Blog. Light & dark mode, Lighthouse SEO 100.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
