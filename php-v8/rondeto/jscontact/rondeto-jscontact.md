# rondeto/jscontact

> 标签: CardDAV, address book, contacts, converter, jmap, jscontact, rfc6350, rfc9553, rfc9554, rfc9555, rfc9982, vCard, vcf

## 简介

JSContact (RFC 9553) for PHP: a typed contact model with JSON reading, writing and validation, and vCard to JSContact conversion both ways (RFC 9555) that keeps what it does not understand.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
