# xylo/multiselect-two

> 标签: barcode, filament, laravel, multiselect, transfer-list, two-sides, xylo

## 简介

A Filament two-sided multiselect field with search, barcode scan, descriptions, and a natural accent theme. Compatible with Filament 4 and 5.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
