# edbizarro/cmcphp

> 标签: Coint Market Cap, bitcoin, coinmarketcap, ethereum, nano

## 简介

PHP wrapper for CoinMarketCap API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
