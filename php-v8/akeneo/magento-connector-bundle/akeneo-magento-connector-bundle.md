# akeneo/magento-connector-bundle

> 标签: PIM, akeneo, api, connector, e-commerce, ecommerce, magento, soap

## 简介

Akeneo PIM bundle to export to Magento platform

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
