# tackdigital/bedrock-understrap

> 标签: bedrock, composer, understrap, wordpress, wp, wp-config

## 简介

WordPress boilerplate with Composer and understrap integration. Based on roots/bedrock and understrap/understrap

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
