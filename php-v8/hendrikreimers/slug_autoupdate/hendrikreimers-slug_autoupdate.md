# hendrikreimers/slug_autoupdate

> 标签: pages, slug, typo3, uri, url

## 简介

Extends the TYPO3 Pages with an AutoUpdate of the Slug field, used for speaking URLs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
