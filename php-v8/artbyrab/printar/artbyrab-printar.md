# artbyrab/printar

> 标签: composer, debugging, package, php, pre, print_r, printar

## 简介

Print your objects and arrays with ease with Printar, a print_r output formatter

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
