# zhenjun/filament-audit-trail

> 标签: Audit, Auditable, activity, activity-log, admin-panel, audit-log, audit-trail, changes, compliance, diff, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, logging, zhenjun

## 简介

Zero-config audit trail for Filament panels: record who changed what, when, and see field-level diffs in a native UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
