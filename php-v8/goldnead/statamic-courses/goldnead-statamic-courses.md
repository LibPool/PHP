# goldnead/statamic-courses

> 标签: courses, drip, lms, progress, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Courses for Statamic: modules and lessons as entries, per-lesson state, sequencing and drip, and a progress rollup. Decides what a learner may open next; who may open the course at all is asked of statamic-entitlements.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
