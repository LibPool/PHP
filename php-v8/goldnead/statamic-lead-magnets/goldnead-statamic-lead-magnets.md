# goldnead/statamic-lead-magnets

> 标签: double-opt-in, download, gated-content, lead-magnet, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Confirm-first resource delivery: a visitor asks for a file, confirms the address, and the download link is signed, time-boxed and audited.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
