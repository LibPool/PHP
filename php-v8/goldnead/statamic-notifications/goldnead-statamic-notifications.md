# goldnead/statamic-notifications

> 标签: digest, notifications, realtime, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Persisted, brand-scoped notifications for Statamic: types, per-type preferences, in-app, immediate mail and deduplicated digests.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
