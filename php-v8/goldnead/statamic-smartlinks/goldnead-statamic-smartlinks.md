# goldnead/statamic-smartlinks

> 标签: music, smart-links, spotify, statamic, statamic-addon, streaming

## 简介

Smart links for music releases in Statamic: one page per song that sends listeners to their platform, platform detected from the URL, clicks counted, missing links filled from Spotify, Deezer and YouTube.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
