# goldnead/statamic-collapse-fieldtype

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Collapse-Fieldtype for Statamic to beautifully hide and show your fields.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
