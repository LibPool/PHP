# goldnead/statamic-community

> 标签: community, feed, rooms, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Community for Statamic: curated rooms, posts, comments and reactions, with a chronological feed and optional pre-moderation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
