# goldnead/statamic-payments

> 标签: checkout, ecommerce, mollie, payments, statamic, statamic-addon, stripe

## 简介

Take payments in Statamic with Mollie or Stripe: hosted checkout, a webhook that never trusts the caller, idempotent fulfilment.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
