# goldnead/statamic-affiliates

> 标签: affiliates, commission, joint-venture, partner-programme, referral, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

A partner programme for Statamic: partners, tracking links with a consent-aware cookie, commissions per offer (first payment, recurring, bumps, upsells), refunds that reverse them, a hold period, payout lists, joint-venture revenue splits and coupons that belong to a partner. Attribution comes from statamic-payments events, never from a request field.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
