# goldnead/statamic-marketing

> 标签: campaigns, email, mailcoach, marketing, newsletter, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Email marketing and newsletters directly inside your Statamic Control Panel — lists, double opt-in, campaigns, sending, and tracking built on LeadHub contacts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
