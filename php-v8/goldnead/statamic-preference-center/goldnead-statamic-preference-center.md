# goldnead/statamic-preference-center

> 标签: consent, magic-link, preferences, statamic, statamic-addon, unsubscribe

## 简介

One page for everything a person receives — mailing lists, product notifications, cadence and the blocks that override all three — assembled from whichever of the three sources is installed.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
