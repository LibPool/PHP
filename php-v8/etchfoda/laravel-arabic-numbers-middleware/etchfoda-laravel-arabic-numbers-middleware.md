# etchfoda/laravel-arabic-numbers-middleware

> 标签: Indian numbers, arabic, arabic numbers, eastern numbers, english numbers, laravel, laravel-arabic-numbers-middleware, middleware, numbers, yemenifree

## 简介

auto transforms arabic/eastern to eastern/arabic numbers for i.e ١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨ to 12345678

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
