# mapkyca/elgg-link-tracking

> 标签: analytics, elgg, plugin, tracking

## 简介

Provides mechanisms for clicks on urls placed on certain pages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
