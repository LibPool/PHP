# spicyapi/spicyapi

> 标签: API-Client, ai, image-generation, spicyapi, video-generation

## 简介

Official PHP client for SpicyAPI — image, video and text generation behind one API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://spicyapi.ai
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/Spicy-API/spicy-php.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/spicyapi%2Fspicyapi
