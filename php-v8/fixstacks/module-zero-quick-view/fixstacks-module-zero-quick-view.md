# fixstacks/module-zero-quick-view

> 标签: catalog, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, quick-view, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: "Quick view" on product cards: the product in a dialog with its photo, price and stock, and Add to Cart for a product with nothing to choose.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
