# fixstacks/module-zero-klarna

> 标签: buy-now-pay-later, klarna, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, payment, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: Klarna on its Hosted Payment Page for Zero Checkout: pay now, pay later or in instalments; authorised at checkout, captured on invoice.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
