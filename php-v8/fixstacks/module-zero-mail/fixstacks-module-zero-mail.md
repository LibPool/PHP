# fixstacks/module-zero-mail

> 标签: amazon-ses, email, email-log, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: email transport (Magento's own or Amazon SES), sent now or through a queue (RabbitMQ or the database), an email log with Resend, one sender, a test email.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
