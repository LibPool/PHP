# lucasbares/craft-emailobfuscator

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft emailobfuscator, craft-plugin, craftcms, email, mail, obfuscator, privacy, robots, rot13, spam, spam-protection

## 简介

A simple plugin that adds a twig tag to obfuscate email addresses (by rot13) in text fields.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
