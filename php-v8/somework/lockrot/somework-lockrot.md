# somework/lockrot

> 标签: Audit, abandoned, ci, composer, composer-plugin, composer.lock, dependencies, dependency-management, dependency-rot, maintenance, sarif, stale, supply-chain, unmaintained

## 简介

Finds abandoned, unmaintained and branch-pinned packages in composer.lock. Composer plugin and standalone PHAR with CI exit codes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
