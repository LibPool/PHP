# biztecheg/laravel-easy-pdf-word

> 标签: ZATCA, arabic, docx, invoice, laravel, mpdf, pdf, rtl, templates, word

## 简介

Generate PDF and Word documents in Laravel in any language, with first-class Arabic support (RTL, shaping, numerals, tafqeet, Hijri dates) and ready-made templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
