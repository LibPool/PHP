# cobbyio/cobby-connector-magento2

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This extension for Magento 2 provides a direct integration of the cobby management methods to your Magento 2 shop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
