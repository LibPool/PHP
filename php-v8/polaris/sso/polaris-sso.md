# polaris/sso

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

SSO for Polaris for PHP: per-organization SAML 2.0 and OIDC providers, verified domains, just-in-time provisioning, single logout, organization self-service and operator routes

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
