# bymayo/delete-account

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, delete account

## 简介

Allows users to delete their own account within Twig templates (Front end).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
