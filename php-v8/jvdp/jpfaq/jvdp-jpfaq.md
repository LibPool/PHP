# jvdp/jpfaq

> 标签: FAQ, asked, definitions, extension, frequently, glossary, help, questions, typo3

## 简介

jpFAQ - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) plugin. With categories, on-the-fly search, customer helpfulness tracking and comments.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
