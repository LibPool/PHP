# dr-benton/before-after-controllers-hooks-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides "@BeforeHook" and "@AfterHook" Annotations support for Symfony Controllers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
