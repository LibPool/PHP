# adachsoft/text-to-audio-contract

> 标签: audio, contract, dto, php, text-to-speech, tts

## 简介

Vendor-agnostic PHP contract and DTOs for text-to-speech (TTS) providers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
