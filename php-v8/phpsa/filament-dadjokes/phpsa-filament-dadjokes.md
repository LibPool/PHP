> 标签: cli, filament, fun, laravel, resource, ui, user, widget

## 简介

With DadJokes every time you load your control panel you'll be greeted by an epic dad joke on the dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
