# gupalo/geoip2

> 标签: city, client, country, geoip, geoip2, ip2location, maxmind, proxy

## 简介

Simple GeoIP Client for MaxMind and IP2Location Databases

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
