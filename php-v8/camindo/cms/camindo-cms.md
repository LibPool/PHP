# camindo/cms

> 标签: cms, content management, headless, multilingual, multisite

## 简介

camindo CMS - multi-site, multilingual content management in PHP. Installed into Core/ of a camindo/project by camindo/composer-plugin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
