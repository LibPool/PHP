# uam/postmark-swiftmailer-bundle

> 标签: bundle, postmarkapp, swiftmailer, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle to provide a Postmark SwiftMailer service

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
