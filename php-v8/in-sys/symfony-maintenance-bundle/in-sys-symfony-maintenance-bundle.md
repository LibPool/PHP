# in-sys/symfony-maintenance-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This bundle allows you to place your website in maintenance mode by calling two commands from your console. Symfony 6.4 / 7

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
