# opensolutions/oss-snmp

> 标签: opensolutions, oss, oss-snmp, oss_snmp, snmp

## 简介

A PHP SNMP library for people who hate SNMP, MIBs and OIDs!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
