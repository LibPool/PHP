# palcera/palcera_cms

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Palcera CMS - Drupal CMS extended with Schema.org, AI-ready content, and enhanced security.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
