# postare/filament-model-ai

> 标签: filamentphp, laravel, openai, plugin

## 简介

Integrate artificial intelligence into FilamentPHP, leveraging your Eloquent Model data as knowledge.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
