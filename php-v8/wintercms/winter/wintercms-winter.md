# wintercms/winter

> 标签: cmf, cms, laravel, winter, wintercms

## 简介

Free, open-source, self-hosted CMS platform based on the Laravel PHP Framework. Originally known as October CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
