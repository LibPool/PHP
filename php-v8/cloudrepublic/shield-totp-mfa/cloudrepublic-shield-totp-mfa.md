# cloudrepublic/shield-totp-mfa

> 标签: 2fa, MFA, authenticator, codeigniter, codeigniter4, google-authenticator, shield, totp

## 简介

Google/Microsoft Authenticator (TOTP) MFA action for CodeIgniter Shield, with remember-this-device support and a device management page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
