# mindpowered/colorconverter

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Converter to convert between various color systems such as RGB, CIELAB, CMYK, HEX, RGB, and XYZ

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
