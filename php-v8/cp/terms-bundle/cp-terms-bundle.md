# cp/terms-bundle

> 标签: bundle, symfony, terms, terms and conditions, terms of service, tos

## 简介

Symfony bundle to manage your app's terms of service

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
