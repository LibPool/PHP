# padosoft/askmydocs-connector-imap

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

IMAP email connector for AskMyDocs — ingest mailboxes (body, headers, attachments) as RAG knowledge.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
