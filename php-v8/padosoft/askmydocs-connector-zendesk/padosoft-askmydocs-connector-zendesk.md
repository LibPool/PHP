# padosoft/askmydocs-connector-zendesk

> 标签: Zendesk, askmydocs, connectors, customer-service, knowledge-base, laravel, rag

## 简介

Zendesk connector for AskMyDocs: resumable ingestion of Support tickets, comments, attachments, customers, organizations and Help Center articles, plus read-only live tools for the chat.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
