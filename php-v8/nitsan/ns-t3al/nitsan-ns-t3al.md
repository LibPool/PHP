# nitsan/ns-t3al

> 标签: AI Localization, crowdin, localization, typo3

## 简介

AI-Powered XLIFF Translation - Translate TYPO3 extension XLIFF files with AI and publish them to Crowdin from the TYPO3 backend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
