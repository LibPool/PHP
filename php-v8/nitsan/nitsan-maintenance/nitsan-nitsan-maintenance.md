# nitsan/nitsan-maintenance

> 标签: Maintanance, T3Planet, comingsoon, offline, template, typo3

## 简介

TYPO3 Maintenance Mode Extension - This TYPO3 Maintenance extension will help you with simple plug & play which have a special TYPO3 Page & TYPO3 Template. It would be useful on criteria like to set Site Coming to Soon Page, Maintenance of Site, Offline while Upgrading your site, etc.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
