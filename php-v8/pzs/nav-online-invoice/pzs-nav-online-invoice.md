# pzs/nav-online-invoice

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A PHP interface for Online Invoice Data Reporting of Hungarian Tax Office (NAV)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
