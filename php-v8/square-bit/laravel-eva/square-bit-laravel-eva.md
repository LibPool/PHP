# square-bit/laravel-eva

> 标签: api, email, laravel-eva, rule, squarebit, validation

## 简介

Keep fake or disposable emails out. A validation rule for your forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
