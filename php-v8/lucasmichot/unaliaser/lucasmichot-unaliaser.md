# lucasmichot/unaliaser

> 标签: Google Apps, account, auth, email, gmail, security

## 简介

Track and unalias Google Mail and Google Apps emails.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
