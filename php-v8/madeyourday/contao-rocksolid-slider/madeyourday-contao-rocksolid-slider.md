# madeyourday/contao-rocksolid-slider

> 标签: contao, jquery, slider

## 简介

Responsive Slider. Full Contao integration, latest touch technology and hardware acceleration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
