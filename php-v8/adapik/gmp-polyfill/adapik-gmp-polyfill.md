# adapik/gmp-polyfill

> 标签: Arbitrary-precision, bigint, bignum, gmp, math, polyfill

## 简介

Pure-PHP polyfill for the GMP extension, backed by brick/math

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
