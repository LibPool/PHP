# fojlerabbirabib/laravel-spa-analytics

> 标签: SPA, analytics, laravel, self-hosted, tracking

## 简介

Self-hosted, first-party analytics for Laravel — traffic, real-time visitors, funnels, goals and campaign tracking, with data that never leaves your own infrastructure.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
