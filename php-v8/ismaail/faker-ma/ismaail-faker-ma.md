# ismaail/faker-ma

> 标签: Fake data, ar_MA, arabic, faker, fakerphp, fr_MA, french, morocco

## 简介

Moroccan Faker providers for ar_MA and fr_MA locales — Person, Address, PhoneNumber, and bilingual PersonName.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
