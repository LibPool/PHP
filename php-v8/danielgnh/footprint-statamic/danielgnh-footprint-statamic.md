# danielgnh/footprint-statamic

> 标签: addon, analytics, heatmap, privacy, statamic, ux

## 简介

An Interactions tab on Statamic entries, from danielgnh/footprint: the sections visitors read, what they click, where they get stuck, drawn over the live page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
