# danielgnh/footprint

> 标签: analytics, heatmap, laravel, privacy, ux

## 简介

Anonymous UI interaction analytics for Laravel: the sections people read, what they click, where they get stuck, with nothing tied to a person.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
