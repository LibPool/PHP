# amsify42/paypal-masspayment

> 标签: amsify42, laravel, mass payment, payment, paypal

## 简介

This is a laravel 5 package for simply executing mass payment of paypal

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
