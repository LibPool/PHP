# titon/g11n

> 标签: g11n, i18n, intl, l10n, locale, message, titon, translate

## 简介

The Titon g11n package handles the localization and internationalization of your application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
