> 标签: Gemini, How-To, ai, anthropic, claude, documentation, help-center, knowledge-base, laravel, llm, ollama, onboarding, openai, playwright, product-demo, product-tour, screen-recording, screencast, tutorial, user-guide, video, walkthrough

## 简介

Automatic how-to and product demo videos for Laravel apps. Describe a flow; AI plans it from your code, a real browser records it, and it stays up to date when your UI changes. Help center, embeds, any screen size.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
