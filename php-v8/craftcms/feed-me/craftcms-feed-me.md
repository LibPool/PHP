# craftcms/feed-me

> 标签: Craft, cms, craftcms, feed me

## 简介

Import content from XML, RSS, CSV or JSON feeds into entries, categories, Craft Commerce products, and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
