# mattneal/gravityforms-fathom-tracking

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A WordPress mu-plugin that tracks your Gravityforms submissions in Fathom Analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
