# oni4i/qq-world

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Tool for working with https://h5.tu.qq.com/web/ai-2d/cartoon/index api. Convert your images to anime style

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
