# neodev3/yii2-iframe-viewer

> 标签: auto height, auto resize, extension, iframe auto height, iframe height, iframe-viewer, iframe-wuto-resize, yii2

## 简介

This will render website in iframe and auto resize according to the content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
