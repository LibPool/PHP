# rizqshadi/hijri-date

> 标签: arabic, calendar, date, hijri, islamic, ramadan, umm al-qura

## 简介

Hijri (Islamic) calendar for PHP: today's Hijri date by location, Gregorian <-> Hijri conversion (Umm al-Qura & tabular civil), and a Ramadan countdown.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
