# ernestdefoe/gauntlet

> 标签: Playoff, bracket, esports, flarum, flarum-extension, league, round robin, swiss, tournament

## 简介

Gauntlet — tournaments for Flarum. Single and double elimination, round robin and Swiss; solo or team entries; sign-ups with caps, check-in and a waitlist; seeding by rating; results reported by both sides, confirmed by staff, disputes handled; a live bracket that reads on a phone; and prediction brackets for real-world playoffs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
