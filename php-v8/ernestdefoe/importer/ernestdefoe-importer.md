# ernestdefoe/importer

> 标签: converter, discourse, flarum, flarum-extension, import, importer, migration, phpbb, vbulletin, webwiz, xenforo

## 简介

Importer / converter for Flarum 2 — migrate a forum into Flarum from phpBB, vBulletin (3/4/5), XenForo, MyBB, SMF, Vanilla, NodeBB, Invision, Discourse, Web Wiz Forums or Convoro. Web-based in the admin, runs in the background in batches with a live progress bar.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
