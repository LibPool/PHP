# ernestdefoe/cadence

> 标签: activity, flarum, flarum-extension, heatmap, profile, statistics

## 简介

Cadence — see a member's rhythm, not just their volume. An activity map on every profile, with the day you click actually opening that day.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
