# ernestdefoe/depot

> 标签: downloads, files, flarum, mods, resources

## 简介

Depot — a downloads section for Flarum 2: files with versions and changelogs, checksums, ratings and reviews, and category permissions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
