# ernestdefoe/herald

> 标签: announcements, bulk-mail, email, flarum, flarum-extension, newsletter

## 简介

Bulk email for Flarum, the way Invision Community does it: write once in your forum's own editor, personalise with {member_name}-style quick tags, filter who gets it, and send in the background.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
