# ernestdefoe/salvage

> 标签: archive.org, broken links, dead links, flarum, hotlink, images, link checker, link rot, wayback

## 简介

Fight link rot: copies hotlinked images in posts to your own storage before their source disappears, and finds dead links across the forum so staff can fix, replace, unlink or point them at an archive.org copy.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
