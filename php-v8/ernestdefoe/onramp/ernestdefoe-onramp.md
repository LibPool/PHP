# ernestdefoe/onramp

> 标签: Gamification, badges, engagement, flarum, flarum-extension, onboarding, quests

## 简介

Onramp — onboarding quests that turn new sign-ups into regulars. A short checklist that ticks itself as members do each step, rewards with FoF Badges and FoF Gamification points, and a funnel that shows where newcomers stop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
