# ernestdefoe/lobby

> 标签: channels, community, dark, flarum, flarum-extension, lobby, theme

## 简介

Lobby — a dark-first community theme for Flarum 2. Tags become #channels in a left sidebar with unread dots and mention badges, discussions read as chat-style rows with a "new since your last visit" divider, and an Online list sits on the right.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
