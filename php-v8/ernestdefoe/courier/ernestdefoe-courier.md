# ernestdefoe/courier

> 标签: email, flarum, notifications, reply-by-email

## 简介

Reply by email for Flarum — members answer a notification from their inbox and it becomes a post. No mail server to run, no DNS to set.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
