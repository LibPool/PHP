# ernestdefoe/chronicle

> 标签: activity, feed, flarum, flarum-extension, profile

## 简介

An Activity tab on every member's profile: the discussions they started, their replies, the posts they liked, their best answers and badges, newest first.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
