# ernestdefoe/ladder

> 标签: Gamification, flarum, flarum-extension, groups, post count, ranks

## 简介

Post-count ranks for Flarum. Members climb a ladder of groups, and hold exactly one rung at a time.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
