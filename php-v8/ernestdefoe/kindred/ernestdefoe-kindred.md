# ernestdefoe/kindred

> 标签: discussions, flarum, flarum-extension, related, similar

## 简介

Similar discussions at the foot of every discussion, the way traditional forums keep readers on the site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
