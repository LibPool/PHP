# ernestdefoe/masthead

> 标签: flarum, flarum-extension, magazine, masthead, portal, theme

## 简介

Masthead — a portal theme for Flarum 2 in the style of IPS 5 and XenForo UI.X. A navy masthead, a featured-story hero, a tag grid, latest discussions and sidebar widgets, with named presets and a dark mode.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
