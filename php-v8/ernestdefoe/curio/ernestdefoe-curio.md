# ernestdefoe/curio

> 标签: barcode, books, collections, collectors, flarum, games, vinyl, wishlist

## 简介

Members show off what they collect: games, vinyl, books, films, LEGO, cards, anything. Collections with photos, details, wishlists and trades, barcode look-up, and titles and descriptions in every language the forum speaks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
