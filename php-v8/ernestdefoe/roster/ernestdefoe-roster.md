# ernestdefoe/roster

> 标签: college football, extension, flarum, mlb, nba, nfl, nhl, roster, sports

## 简介

Every team, roster and player in one place. Browse by conference or division, open a club for its roster, then open a player for who he is. College football from CollegeFootballData; the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and league football from ESPN, which needs no key.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
