# ernestdefoe/greeter

> 标签: flarum, flarum-extension, onboarding, private message, welcome

## 简介

Welcome every new member with a private message, an email, or both — sent the moment they are actually in, written in your own words.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
