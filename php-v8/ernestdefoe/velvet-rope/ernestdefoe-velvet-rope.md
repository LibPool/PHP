# ernestdefoe/velvet-rope

> 标签: Blur, flarum, flarum-extension, guest, members only, metered, paywall, registration, sign up

## 简介

Turn readers into members on Flarum 2: give guests a set number of free discussions per day, week or month, then a lead-in and a sign-up prompt; hold back replies, images, attachments and embeds from guests or chosen groups, per tag; with the paywall structured data search engines ask for.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
