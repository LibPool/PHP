# ernestdefoe/cuecard

> 标签: canned responses, flarum, flarum-extension, macros, moderation, reply templates, saved replies

## 简介

Saved replies for Flarum. Shared macros for chosen groups and personal ones, written in the forum's own editor and inserted from the composer's Cue Cards button with search and placeholders; a reply can also lock, tag or mark itself the best answer.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
