# ernestdefoe/connect

> 标签: automation, extension, flarum, integration, rules, webhooks, zapier

## 简介

Automation for Flarum 2 — an embedded Zapier experience in your admin, an in-app if-this-then-that Rules engine, outgoing webhooks, and a scoped REST API. Works with Zapier, Make, IFTTT, n8n and anything that speaks webhooks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
