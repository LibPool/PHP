# ernestdefoe/club

> 标签: book club, flarum, marquee, reading, spoilers, watch club

## 简介

Book and watch clubs for Marquee titles: a monthly pick, a reading or watching schedule, spoiler-gated replies per section and a progress bar for every member.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
