# ernestdefoe/shutter

> 标签: competition, flarum, flarum-extension, gallery, photo contest, photography, voting

## 简介

Photo contests for Flarum 2: staff run themed contests on a schedule, members submit photos, the community votes or judges score, and winners are crowned on a podium with notifications and badges. A contest is a discussion.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
