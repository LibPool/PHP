# ernestdefoe/earshot

> 标签: audio, episodes, flarum, player, podcast, podcasts, rss

## 简介

Podcasts that live in your forum: every episode is a discussion, the player keeps going while members browse, and shows can be hosted on the forum or follow a feed hosted anywhere.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
