# ernestdefoe/reel

> 标签: composer, flarum, flarum-extension, gif, giphy, klipy

## 简介

GIF search in the composer for Flarum 2: trending and search results from GIPHY or KLIPY, one click to drop a GIF into a post. Works with Flarum's editor and Scribe.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
