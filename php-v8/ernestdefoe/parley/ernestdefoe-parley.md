# ernestdefoe/parley

> 标签: Messenger, chat, flarum, messages, online, presence

## 简介

Parley — chat for Flarum 2. See who is online and what they are reading, and message them in windows that stay open while you browse.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
