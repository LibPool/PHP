# ernestdefoe/since

> 标签: changes, edits, flarum, unread

## 简介

Since — what changed while you were away. Flarum shows a returning reader what is new; this shows what MOVED: the posts they had already read that were edited behind their back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
