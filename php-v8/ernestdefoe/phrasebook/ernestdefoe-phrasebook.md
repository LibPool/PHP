# ernestdefoe/phrasebook

> 标签: ai, claude, deepl, flarum, flarum-extension, i18n, language pack, linguist, localization, openai, translation

## 简介

Translate your forum's interface with AI, bring your own provider. Fills in the strings a language pack is missing for core and every installed extension, checks every ICU placeholder, lets you review each one, and saves them as Linguist overrides or exports a language pack. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, DeepL and Google Translate.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
