# ernestdefoe/header-nav

> 标签: flarum, header, links, menu, navigation

## 简介

Put your forum's navigation in the header and decide what goes where: reorder, rename, hide, change icons and add your own links.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
