# ernestdefoe/docket

> 标签: flags, flarum, flarum-extension, moderation, queue, report centre, reports

## 简介

A report centre for Flarum 2, built on Flags: report queues, auto-assignment rules, claim and assign, open / in progress / resolved / rejected, staff notes on every report, the outcome sent to the people who reported, and the report history of every post and member.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
