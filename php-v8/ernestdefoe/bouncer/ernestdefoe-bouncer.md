# ernestdefoe/bouncer

> 标签: ai crawler, bot, crawler, firewall, flarum, flarum-extension, gptbot, proof of work, rate limit, robots.txt, scraper

## 简介

Bot guard for Flarum 2, on your own server: turn away AI crawlers you don't want, verify real search engines by DNS, give suspicious clients a quick proof-of-work check, rate-limit bursts and keep a log for staff. No Cloudflare, no third-party captcha, nothing added for members.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
