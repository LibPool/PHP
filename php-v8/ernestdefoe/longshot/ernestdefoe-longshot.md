# ernestdefoe/longshot

> 标签: Gamification, betting, flarum, flarum-extension, leaderboard, sports, sportsbook, virtual currency

## 简介

Longshot — a virtual-points sportsbook for Flarum. Moneylines, spreads, totals and props on real games or your own events; singles and parlays; a member-vs-member exchange; settlement straight from the Picks data feed; weekly allowances, per-group limits and leaderboards. Points only: no real money, ever.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
