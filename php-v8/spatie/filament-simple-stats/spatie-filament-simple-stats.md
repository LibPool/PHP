# spatie/filament-simple-stats

> 标签: filament, filament-simple-stats, laravel, spatie

## 简介

Opinionated prebuilt stat widgets to quickly add to your Filament dashboards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
