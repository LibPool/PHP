# spatie/payment

> 标签: e-commerce, europabank, financial, gateway, laravel, payment, webshop

## 简介

A Laravel 5 package to verify and accept payments from payment gateways

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
