# vitisstudio/filament-choosable-chips

> 标签: Chips, Forms, badges, filament, filament-choosable-chips, filament-plugin, filamentphp, form-field, laravel, vitisstudio

## 简介

A FilamentPHP v5 form field that renders checkbox/radio options as dismissable, colorable, icon-bearing badge chips with a fluent options API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
