# copex/module-warranty-label

> 标签: compliance, consumer-protection, eu, garan, gewaehrleistung, guarantee, legal-guarantee, magento, magento-2-module, magento2, warranty

## 简介

Magento 2 EU harmonised notice on the legal guarantee of conformity and EU GARAN label for commercial guarantees of durability (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
