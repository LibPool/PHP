# j4s/validation

> 标签: arrays, cookies, filtration, get, post, session, superglobals, validation

## 简介

Classes for validation and filtration of superglobals, arrays and vars

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
