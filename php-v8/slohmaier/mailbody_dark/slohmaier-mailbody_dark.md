# slohmaier/mailbody_dark

> 标签: dark-mode, darkreader, elastic, plugin, roundcube, webmail

## 简介

Toggle DarkReader-based dark/light rendering of the HTML mail body in Roundcube (Elastic skin), without touching the rest of the UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
