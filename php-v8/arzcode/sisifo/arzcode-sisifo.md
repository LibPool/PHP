# arzcode/sisifo

> 标签: ai, automation, filament, laravel, mailbox

## 简介

Sisifo — autonomous email/inbox watcher and summarizer as a Filament v5 plugin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
