# arzcode/shared-secrets

> 标签: filament, filamentphp, laravel, livewire, one-time-link, passwords, secrets

## 简介

Share passwords and secrets from your Filament panel through self-destructing signed links.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
