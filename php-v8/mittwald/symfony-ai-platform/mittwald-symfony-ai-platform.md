# mittwald/symfony-ai-platform

> 标签: Bridge, ai, glm, mistral, openai, platform, qwen3, whisper

## 简介

Allows to use mittwald's AI Platform in your Symfony AI project.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
