# quittio/country-de

> 标签: E-Invoice, ZUGFeRD, din5008, germany, php, ustg, xrechnung

## 简介

Quittio country pack for Germany: UStG mandatory fields, tax presets, XRechnung profiles and BR-DE rules, DIN 5008 layouts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
