# quittio/country-be

> 标签: Belgium, E-Invoice, btw, e-facture, peppol, php

## 简介

Quittio country pack for Belgium: VAT presets and invoice mentions, Peppol BIS for domestic business invoices, enterprise numbers, default interest of the law of 2 August 2002.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
