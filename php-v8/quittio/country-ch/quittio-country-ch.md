# quittio/country-ch

> 标签: E-Invoice, MwSt, php, qr-bill, qr-rechnung, switzerland

## 简介

Quittio country pack for Switzerland: VAT presets and invoice requirements of the MWSTG, Swiss QR bill with payment part, 5 Rappen rounding.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
