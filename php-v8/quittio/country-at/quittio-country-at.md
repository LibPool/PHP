# quittio/country-at

> 标签: E-Invoice, austria, e-rechnung, ebinterface, php, ustg

## 简介

Quittio country pack for Austria: UStG mandatory fields, tax presets, CIUS-AT-NAT and CIUS-AT-GOV profiles, default interest under the UGB.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
