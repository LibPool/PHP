# deviscoding/propel1-bundle

> 标签: 1.x, 3.x, orm, persistence, propel, symfony

## 简介

Fork of PropelBundle with patches for PHP 7.x and Symfony 3.x compatibility.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
