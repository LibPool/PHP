# webdirect/ai-filters

> 标签: ai, ai-filters, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, laravel-ai, natural-language, table-filters

## 简介

AI-powered filters for Filament v4 & v5 panels. Filter any table with natural language through the official laravel/ai SDK.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
