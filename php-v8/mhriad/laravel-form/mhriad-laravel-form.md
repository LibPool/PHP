# mhriad/laravel-form

> 标签: form, form-builder, html, laravel

## 简介

A lightweight Laravel Form Builder for Laravel 10, 11 & 12 (replacement for LaravelCollective Form).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
