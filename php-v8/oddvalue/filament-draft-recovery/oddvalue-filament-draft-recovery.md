# oddvalue/filament-draft-recovery

> 标签: autosave, drafts, filament, filament-draft-recovery, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, oddvalue

## 简介

Auto-save draft & crash recovery for Filament create/edit pages, with swappable storage drivers (browser localStorage, database, or oddvalue/laravel-drafts)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
