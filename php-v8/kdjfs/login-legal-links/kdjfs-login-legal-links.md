# kdjfs/login-legal-links

> 标签: gdpr, imprint, kirby, kirby-cms, kirby-plugin, login, panel, privacy

## 简介

Adds links to the imprint, privacy policy and other legal pages to the Kirby Panel login

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
