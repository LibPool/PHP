# stinger/ckeditor-office2013-skin-bundle

> 标签: 2013, CKEditor, office, skin, symfony

## 简介

Symfony2 Bundle to integrate the CKEditor skin office 2013

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
