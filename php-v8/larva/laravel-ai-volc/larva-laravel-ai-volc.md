# larva/laravel-ai-volc

> 标签: ark, doubao, laravel, laravel-ai, seedance, seedream, tts, volc, volcengine

## 简介

Volcengine Ark (火山方舟) provider for Laravel AI: text, embeddings, TTS, Seedream images and Seedance video.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
