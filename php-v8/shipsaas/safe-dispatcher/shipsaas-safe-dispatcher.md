# shipsaas/safe-dispatcher

> 标签: laravel library, laravel safe dispatch ensure your Queue Job Msgs are totally tracked and resendable, laravel safe dispatcher

## 简介

Ensure your Queue msg is tracked and retryable when failed to dispatch.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
