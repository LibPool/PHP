# domprojects/codeigniter4-country

> 标签: codeigniter, codeigniter4, countries, country, helper, localization

## 简介

Translated country helper for CodeIgniter 4 projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
