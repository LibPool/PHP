# eadpucv/bo-skin

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Bo is a flavour of MediaWiki Chameleon skin, specially crafted for Amereida and later for Casiopea, which have separate layouts

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
