# ratespecial/credit-agency-rent

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Library to generate fixed-length credit agency rent reporting files (Equifax Insight, Experian Rental Exchange)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
