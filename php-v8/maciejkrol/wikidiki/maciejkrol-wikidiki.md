# maciejkrol/wikidiki

> 标签: translation, wikidiki, wikipedia

## 简介

WikiDiki lets you translate words (mostly nouns) across multiple languages based on wikipedia.org

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
