> 标签: analytics, autocomplete, bradsearch, graphql, magento2, search

## 简介

BradSearch Magento 2 Extension - Autocomplete, Search GraphQL, Product Features, and Analytics

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
