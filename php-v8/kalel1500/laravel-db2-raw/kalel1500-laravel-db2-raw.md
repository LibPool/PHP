# kalel1500/laravel-db2-raw

> 标签: db2, helper, laravel, package, query, raw

## 简介

laravel-db2-raw is a simple helper to execute raw DB2 queries in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
