# al-falaq/al-falaq

> 标签: astronomy, crescent, hijri, islam, prayer-times, qibla, umm-al-qura

## 简介

Offline, zero-dependency PHP library for Islamic astronomy: prayer times, Qibla, Sunnah markers, crescent visibility, and Hijri conversion.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
