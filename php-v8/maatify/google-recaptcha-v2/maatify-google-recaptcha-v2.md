# maatify/google-recaptcha-v2

> 标签: GoogleRecaptchaV2, google, handler, maatify, php, reCaptchaV2, recaptcha

## 简介

Unofficial Google Recaptcha V2 for PHP it's library for maatify.dev Google Recaptcha V2 handler, known by our team

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
