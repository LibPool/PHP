# maatify/hcaptcha

> 标签: handler, hcaptcha, maatify, php

## 简介

Unofficial HCaptcha for PHP it's library for maatify.dev HCaptcha handler, known by our team

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
