# maatify/google-auth

> 标签: 2fa, auth, google, handler, oauth2, php, totp

## 简介

Official PHP library for maatify.dev oauth2-client handler, known by our team

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
