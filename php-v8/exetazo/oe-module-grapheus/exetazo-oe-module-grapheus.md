# exetazo/oe-module-grapheus

> 标签: ai scribe, ambient documentation, clinical documentation, grapheus, medical coding, openemr, openemr-module, soap note

## 简介

Grapheus by Exetazo: AI scribe for OpenEMR 8.x. Records the visit, then drafts the SOAP note, problems, allergies, prescriptions (entered, never sent) and fee-sheet codes (unbilled) for the clinician to review. Requires a paid Grapheus subscription.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
