# consoletvs/invoices

> 标签: Erik Campobadal, create, customers, generate, invoices, laravel, open source, pdf

## 简介

Generate PDF invoices for your customers in laravel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
