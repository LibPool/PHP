# probeforge/seoforge

> 标签: Audit, blade, laravel, meta-tags, open-graph, optimization, schema-org, seo, structured-data, templates, twitter-cards

## 简介

Comprehensive SEO audit and automated fixing tool for Laravel 12 Blade templates. Analyze and optimize your application's SEO compliance with A/AA/AAA standards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
