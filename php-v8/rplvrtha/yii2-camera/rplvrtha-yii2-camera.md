# rplvrtha/yii2-camera

> 标签: camera, file, upload, widget, yii2

## 简介

A simple camera extension for Yii2 that uses the built-in camera on your phone.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
