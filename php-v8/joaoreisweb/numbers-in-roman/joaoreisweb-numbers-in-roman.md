# joaoreisweb/numbers-in-roman

> 标签: Numerals, converter, currency, in, number, numbers, numbers-to-text, roman, string, to

## 简介

This library convert numbers into roman numerals.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
