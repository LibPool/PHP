# will-wright1/magento2-product-feed

> 标签: adobe-commerce, google-merchant-center, google-shopping, magento, magento2, product-feed

## 简介

A production-ready Google Merchant Center product feed generator for Magento 2 and Adobe Commerce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
