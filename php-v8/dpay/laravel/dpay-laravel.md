# dpay/laravel

> 标签: checkout, dPay, edfali, laravel, libya, moamalat, payments, sadad, webhooks

## 简介

Official DPay Laravel package — hosted checkout in one fluent call, a signed-webhook route with typed events, dpay:doctor, and DPay::fake() for your tests. Laravel 10, 11, 12 and 13.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
