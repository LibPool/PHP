# shapecode/less-raven-bundle

> 标签: bundle, information, less, raven, sentry, shapecode, symfony

## 简介

Adds a new raven client service that sends less information.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
