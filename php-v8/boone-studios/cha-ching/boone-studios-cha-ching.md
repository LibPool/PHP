# boone-studios/cha-ching

> 标签: cashier, discord, laravel, notifications, purchases, revenue, slack, stripe, subscriptions, twilio

## 简介

Drop-in Laravel package that alerts you on Slack, Discord, or SMS when someone subscribes or purchases.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
