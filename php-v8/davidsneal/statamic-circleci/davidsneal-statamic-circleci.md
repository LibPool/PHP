# davidsneal/statamic-circleci

> 标签: CircleCI, addon, statamic

## 简介

A simple addon for Statamic v3, to trigger CircleCI workflows from the CP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
