# jalendport/craft-lockout

> 标签: Craft, Users, admin, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, lockout, maintenance

## 简介

Temporarily lock certain users out of the control panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
