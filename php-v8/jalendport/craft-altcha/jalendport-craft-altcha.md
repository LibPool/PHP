# jalendport/craft-altcha

> 标签: ALTCHA, Craft, anti-spam, captcha, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, spam

## 简介

Integrate Altcha's privacy-first spam protection into your Craft CMS forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
