# alpixel/mediabundle

> 标签: Symfony2, alpixel, bundle, media, symfony

## 简介

The AlpixelMediaBundle is a bundle managing media of all kinds for our projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
