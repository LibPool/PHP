# mattstein/craft-usesend

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, email, mail, transactional-email, transport, unsend, usesend

## 简介

Send Craft CMS email through the useSend API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
