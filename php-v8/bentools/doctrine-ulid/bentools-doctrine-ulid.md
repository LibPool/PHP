# bentools/doctrine-ulid

> 标签: doctrine, orm, ulid, uuid

## 简介

ULID (Universally Unique Lexicographically Sortable Identifier) support for Doctrine IDs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
