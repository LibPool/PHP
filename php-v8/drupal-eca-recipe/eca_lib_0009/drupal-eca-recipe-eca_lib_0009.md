# drupal-eca-recipe/eca_lib_0009

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Groups the title and body fields on the article form and blocks publishing while the body is still empty.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
