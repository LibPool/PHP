# alizharb/filament-activity-log

> 标签: activity-log, activity-tracking, audit-log, audit-trail, compliance, dashboard-widget, filament, filament-plugin, filament-v4, filament-v5, laravel, laravel-12, laravel-13, php, security, spatie, timeline

## 简介

A security-first audit control center for Filament v4 and v5 with tenant isolation, risk scoring, integrity checks, retention holds, timelines, and dashboards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
