# hda-ppl/hda-rights-management-check

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, backend, import schema, permissions, rights management

## 简介

TYPO3 14.3 backend module for checking imported HDA rights schemas against PPL Rights Management data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
