# cyllene-digital/sylius-ai-translation-plugin

> 标签: ai, anthropic, deepl, ecommerce, i18n, llm, localization, openai, sylius, sylius-plugin, symfony, translation

## 简介

AI-assisted translation management for the Sylius 2 admin: browse every translation catalogue, override any wording per channel without touching the files, and review AI suggestions (OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, DeepL) before they go live.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
