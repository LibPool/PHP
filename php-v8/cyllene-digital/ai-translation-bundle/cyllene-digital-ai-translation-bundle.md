# cyllene-digital/ai-translation-bundle

> 标签: ai, anthropic, deepl, i18n, openai, symfony, symfony-bundle, translation

## 简介

Symfony bundle to edit translations at runtime: database overrides applied over your translation files, AI suggestions (OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, DeepL) applied only after human review, coverage, and CSV/XLIFF import/export.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
