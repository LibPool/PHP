# volistx/lumen-rate-limiting

> 标签: Rate, laravel, limiter, lumen

## 简介

Lumen port of Laravel ThrottleRequests middleware

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
