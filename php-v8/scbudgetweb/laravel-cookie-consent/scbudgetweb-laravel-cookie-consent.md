# scbudgetweb/laravel-cookie-consent

> 标签: cookie consent, cookies, gdpr, laravel, pecr, uk gdpr

## 简介

A simple, compliant cookie consent banner for Laravel: Accept, Reject and Customise, with functional, analytics and marketing categories and automatic cookie discovery.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
