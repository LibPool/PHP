# betacalendars/calendar-presentation

> 标签: accessibility, calendar, date, localization, month-grid, php, presentation

## 简介

Framework-agnostic, localized and accessible calendar presentation models and renderers for PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
