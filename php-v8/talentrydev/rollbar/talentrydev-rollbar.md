# talentrydev/rollbar

> 标签: debugging, errors, exceptions, logging, monitoring

## 简介

Monitors errors and exceptions and reports them to Rollbar

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
