# leon0399/monolog-flysystem

> 标签: Flysystem, cloud logging, log, logging, monolog, monolog-handler, monolog-library

## 简介

Writes your Monolog records into Flysystem files.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
