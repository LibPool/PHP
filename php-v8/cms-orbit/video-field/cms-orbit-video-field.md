# cms-orbit/video-field

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A comprehensive video upload and multi-encoding system for CMS-Orbit with streaming support, transcoding, and thumbnails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
