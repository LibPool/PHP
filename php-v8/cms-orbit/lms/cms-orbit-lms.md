# cms-orbit/lms

> 标签: cms, course, elearning, laravel, lms, orbit

## 简介

CMS Orbit LMS: TutorLMS-style learning management (courses, sections, lessons, quizzes, enrollments) for the Orbit admin engine.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
