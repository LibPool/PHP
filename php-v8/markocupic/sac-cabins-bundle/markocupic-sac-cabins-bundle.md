# markocupic/sac-cabins-bundle

> 标签: Cabins, Contao CMS, SAC, bundle, extension

## 简介

Manage mountain club cabins in the Contao backend and display them in the frontend of your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
