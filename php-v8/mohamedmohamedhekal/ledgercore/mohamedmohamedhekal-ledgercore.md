> 标签: Accounting, ERP, accounting-engine, bookkeeping, double-entry, fintech, general-ledger, journal, laravel, laravel-package, ledger, php

## 简介

Double-entry ledger engine for Laravel ERP/fintech: balanced journals, immutable postings, balances, and period locks

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
