# lacus/cnpj-fmt

> 标签: Formatar, br, cnpj, format, formatting, mascara, mask, pt-BR

## 简介

Utility to format CNPJ (Brazilian Business Tax ID)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
