# lacus/cnpj-dv

> 标签: br, check, check-digit, check-digits, cnpj, pt-BR, verificador, verificar, verification, verify

## 简介

Utility to calculate check digits on CNPJ (Brazilian Business Tax ID)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
