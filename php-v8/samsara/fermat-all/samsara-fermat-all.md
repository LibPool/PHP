# samsara/fermat-all

> 标签: Gaussian, bigint, large numbers, math, numbers, poisson, statistics, stats

## 简介

A metapackage requiring Fermat and all of its modules.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
