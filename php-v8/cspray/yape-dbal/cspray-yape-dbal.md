# cspray/yape-dbal

> 标签: dbal, doctrine, enum

## 简介

An extension of cspray/yape that allows storing generated Enums in your Doctrine Entities

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
