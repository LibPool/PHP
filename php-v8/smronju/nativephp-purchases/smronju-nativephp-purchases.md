# smronju/nativephp-purchases

> 标签: billing, iap, in-app-purchase, laravel, mobile, nativephp, remove-ads, storekit

## 简介

One-time in-app purchases (non-consumables like "Remove Ads") with restore, via Google Play Billing and StoreKit 2 — no server, no receipt validation backend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
