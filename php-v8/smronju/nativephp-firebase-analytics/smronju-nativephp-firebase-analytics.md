# smronju/nativephp-firebase-analytics

> 标签: analytics, firebase, laravel, mobile, nativephp

## 简介

Google Analytics for Firebase on Android — installs the SDK and your google-services.json, logs custom events, and turns collection on or off for a user's opt-out.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
