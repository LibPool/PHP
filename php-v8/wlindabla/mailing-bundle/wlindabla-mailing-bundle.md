# wlindabla/mailing-bundle

> 标签: bundle, dkim, email, mailer, mailing, notifications, symfony, twig

## 简介

Symfony bundle for centralized management of transactional (system) and support/management emails, featuring Twig templates, priority queues, attachments, and DKIM signatures.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
