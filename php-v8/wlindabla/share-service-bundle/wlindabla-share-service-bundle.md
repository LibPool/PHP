# wlindabla/share-service-bundle

> 标签: Messenger, bundle, encryption, password, security, similarity, symfony, token

## 简介

Reusable cross-project Symfony services: ID encryption, token/password generation & hashing, browser/bot request validation, async method dispatch over Messenger, identity similarity detection, form violation extraction, and a thin Serializer facade.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
