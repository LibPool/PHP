# iantsch/wp-hbs-engine

> 标签: handlebars, lightncandy, plugins, wordpress

## 简介

Renders Handlebars templates within your WordPress Theme/Plugin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
