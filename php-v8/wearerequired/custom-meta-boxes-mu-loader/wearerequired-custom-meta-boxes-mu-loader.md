# wearerequired/custom-meta-boxes-mu-loader

> 标签: loader, meta-boxes, must-use, wordpress

## 简介

Loads Custom Meta Boxes as a must-use plugin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
