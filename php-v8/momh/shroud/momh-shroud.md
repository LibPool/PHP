# momh/shroud

> 标签: Anonymisation, database, gdpr, privacy, pseudonymisation

## 简介

Deterministic, CMS-agnostic pseudonymisation of a relational database: consistent fake identities, reversible ids, shape-preserving text replacement, verifiable coverage.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
