# yasseralsamman/saudi-national-address

> 标签: address, cities, districts, national-address, regions, saudi, saudi-arabia, spl

## 简介

Open dataset of Saudi Arabia regions, cities, and districts, with SPL-compatible 11-digit district IDs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
