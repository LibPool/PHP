# isgdev/isgdev-meta

> 标签: composer, gitlab, metadata, vendor

## 简介

Metadata and conventions for the isgdev GitLab group and Composer vendor namespace

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
