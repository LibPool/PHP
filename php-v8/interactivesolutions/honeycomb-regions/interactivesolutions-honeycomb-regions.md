# interactivesolutions/honeycomb-regions

> 标签: cities, city parts, continents, honeycomb, municipalities, package, regions, streets, world

## 简介

HoneyComb regions package. World continents, countries, cities, municipalities, city parts, streets. Availability to read, create and update.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
