# uptowork/opentbs

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

With OpenTBS you can merge OpenOffice - LibreOffice and Ms Office documents with PHP using the TinyButStrong template engine. Simple use OpenOffice - LibreOffice or Ms Office to edit your templates : DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, ODT, OSD, ODP and other formats.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
