# wp-pay-gateways/omnikassa-2

> 标签: gateway, omnikassa, pay, pronamic, smart, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Rabo Smart Pay driver for the WordPress payment processing library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
