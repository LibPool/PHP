# mohammadtrabelsi/laravel-translation-sync

> 标签: artisan, i18n, lang, laravel, laravel-modules, livewire, localization, translations

## 简介

Scan your Laravel app (views, Livewire components, services and laravel-modules) for translation keys and sync them across every locale with a single artisan command.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
