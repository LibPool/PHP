# khepin/drupal-transliterator-bundle

> 标签: slug transliteration

## 简介

Symfony bundle to use drupal transliteration (eg in doctrine sluggable extension).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
