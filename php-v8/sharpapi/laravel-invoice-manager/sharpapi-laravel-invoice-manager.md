# sharpapi/laravel-invoice-manager

> 标签: AI-powered, Accounting, Laravel-Invoice, SharpAPI, accounts-payable, ai-invoice-parser, artificial intelligence, finance, invoice-database, invoice-management, invoice-ocr, laravel, laravel-invoice-manager, laravel-nova, nova-resource, php

## 简介

AI-powered Invoice Manager for Laravel. Parse invoices with SharpAPI, store every extracted field in your database, and manage them with ready-made Laravel Nova resources.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
