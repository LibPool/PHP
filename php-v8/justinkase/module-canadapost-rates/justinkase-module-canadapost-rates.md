# justinkase/module-canadapost-rates

> 标签: canada post, canada shipping, magento 2, magento shipping module, magento2, shipping, shipping rates

## 简介

Magento 2 module to integrate Canada Post shipping rates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
