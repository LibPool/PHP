# se7enxweb/nl_cronjobs

> 标签: admin, cron, cronjob, exponential, exponential-legacy, extension, ezpublish-legacy, runcronjobs

## 简介

NL Cronjobs for Exponential: an administration tab that lists the cronjob parts of cronjob.ini, launches one in the background with a click, and shows and clears its output and error logs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
