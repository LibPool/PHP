# se7enxweb/nxc_saferpay

> 标签: e-commerce, exponential, exponential-legacy, legacy-extension, payment, payment gateway, saferpay, shop, six payment services

## 简介

Exponential extension that takes card payments through the Saferpay Hosting interface of SIX Payment Services: it creates the payment link, verifies and captures the payment when Saferpay notifies the site, approves the shop order's payment, and lists every transaction with its log in a SaferPay tab in the administration interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
