# se7enxweb/ezsurvey

> 标签: exponential, exponential-extension, ezpublish-legacy, form, poll, questionnaire, survey

## 简介

Survey datatype and admin module for Exponential: build questionnaires inside content objects, collect answers on the site, and review, edit and export the results in the administration interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
