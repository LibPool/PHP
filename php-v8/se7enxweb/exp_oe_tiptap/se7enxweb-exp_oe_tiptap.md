# se7enxweb/exp_oe_tiptap

> 标签: Exponential 6, exponential, ezoe, ezxmltext, legacy extension, online editor, tiptap, wysiwyg

## 简介

A Tiptap based online editor for ezxmltext fields in Exponential 6, switchable on the fly with the Online Editor (ezoe), with provider-agnostic AI hooks

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
