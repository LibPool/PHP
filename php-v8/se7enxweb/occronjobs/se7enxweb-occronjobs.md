# se7enxweb/occronjobs

> 标签: admin, cronjobs, crontab, exponential, extension, runcronjobs

## 简介

Exponential extension that shows the server crontab and the configured cronjob parts in the admin, starts runcronjobs.php parts on demand and shows their output.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
