# se7enxweb/novennotification

> 标签: Users, administration, digest, exponential, exponential-legacy, legacy-extension, notifications, subscriptions

## 简介

Exponential extension that lets administrators look up, change and delete the notification settings (item subscriptions and digest settings) of other users, from a Users notifications tab in the administration interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
