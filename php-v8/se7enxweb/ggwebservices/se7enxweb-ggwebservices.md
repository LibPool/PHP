# se7enxweb/ggwebservices

> 标签: debugger, exponential, exponential-extension, ezpublish-legacy, jsonrpc, rest, soap, webservices, wsdl, xmlrpc

## 简介

Webservices for Exponential: serve and call JSON-RPC, XML-RPC, REST and SOAP (with WSDL) from PHP and templates, a cross-domain proxy for Ajax calls, and a visual webservices debugger in the administration interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
