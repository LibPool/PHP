# se7enxweb/ownewsletter

> 标签: bounce, email, exponential, extension, mailing list, newsletter, subscription, tracking

## 简介

Exponential extension for newsletters: mailing lists, subscription with email confirmation, editions as content, skins, queued sending by cron, personalization, click and read tracking, bounce handling, blacklist and CSV import and export.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
