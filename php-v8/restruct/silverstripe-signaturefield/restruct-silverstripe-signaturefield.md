# restruct/silverstripe-signaturefield

> 标签: Forms, signature, silverstripe

## 简介

A signaturefield based on https://github.com/szimek/signature_pad

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
