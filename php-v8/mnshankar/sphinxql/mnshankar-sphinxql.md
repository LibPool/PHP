> 标签: Sphinx RT, laravel, laravel4, search, sphinxql

## 简介

Integrate SphinxQL with Laravel 4. Simplifies Laravel-Sphinx(RT) search

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
