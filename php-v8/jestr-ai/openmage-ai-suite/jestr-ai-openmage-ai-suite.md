# jestr-ai/openmage-ai-suite

> 标签: Gemini, ai, anthropic, chatbot, llm, magento1, mcp, openai, openmage

## 简介

AI-native suite for OpenMage / Magento 1: MCP server, admin copilot, storefront assistant, LLM discovery layer. Bring your own key (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
