# prestashopcorp/ps_facebook

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

PrestaShop Social with Facebook & Instagram allows you to easily feature your products on Facebook and Instagram so people can discover and buy them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
