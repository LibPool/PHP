# no404/laravel

> 标签: 301, 404, laravel, no404, redirect, seo

## 简介

no404 for Laravel: real server-side 301/302 redirects for your application's 404s, matched against your live catalogue.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
