# fintecture/payment-hyva

> 标签: Bank, payment

## 简介

Fintecture Payer par Banque (Pay by Bank) enables users to pay securely and directly from their bank account.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
