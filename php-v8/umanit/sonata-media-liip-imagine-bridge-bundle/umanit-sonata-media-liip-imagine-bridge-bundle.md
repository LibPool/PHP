# umanit/sonata-media-liip-imagine-bridge-bundle

> 标签: liip imagine, sonata media, symfony

## 简介

A bridge between LiipImagineBundle and SonataMediaBundle.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
