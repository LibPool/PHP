# gomdim-apps/slimmer

> 标签: archive, archiving, backup, bzip2, compression, compressor, file-compression, gd, ghostscript, gzip, image-compression, image-optimizer, pdf, pdf-compression, pdf-optimizer, retention, tar, zip, zstd

## 简介

PDF, image, and archive (Tar/Zip) compressor and extractor using Ghostscript, GD, tar and zip

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
