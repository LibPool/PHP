# omniconva/laravel

> 标签: ai, chat, ecommerce, laravel, omniconva, sales agent, webhooks

## 简介

Connect a Laravel store to OmniConva: product sync, AI chat orders and webhooks in a few lines.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
