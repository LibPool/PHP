# kryptoboersen/crypto-converter

> 标签: bitcoin, converter, cryptocurrency, currency, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Currency & Crypto Converter tag for Statamic - convert between EUR/USD/GBP/CHF and BTC/ETH/USDC with live rates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
