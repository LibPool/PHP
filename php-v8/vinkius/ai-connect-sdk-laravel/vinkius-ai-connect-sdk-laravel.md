> 标签: Gemini, agents, ai, anthropic, capabilities, connect, connectors, function-calling, laravel, openai, tools, vinkius

## 简介

Laravel integration for @vinkius/connect. Give every user of your AI app their own connectors and capabilities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
