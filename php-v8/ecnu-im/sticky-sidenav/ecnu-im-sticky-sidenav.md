# ecnu-im/sticky-sidenav

> 标签: flarum, sidenav, sticky

## 简介

Make the sidenav section sticky and scrollable. Also support hot-discussions widget.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
