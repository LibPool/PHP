# tig/persistent-shopping-cart-magento2

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Extends Magento 2's core Persistent Shopping Cart with sessions/cookies for Guests.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
