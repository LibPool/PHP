# flarum-lang/chinese-traditional

> 标签: flarum, locale, zh_Hant-locale

## 简介

Chinese (Traditional) language pack for Flarum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
