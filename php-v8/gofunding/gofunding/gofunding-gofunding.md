# gofunding/gofunding

> 标签: crowdfunding, framework, gofunding, yii2

## 简介

Gofunding is a crowdfunding platform

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://www.gofunding.ga/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/gofunding/gofunding.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/gofunding%2Fgofunding

## 获取地址

- Composer 安装：`composer require gofunding/gofunding`
- Packagist 仓库：https://repo.packagist.org/p2/gofunding/gofunding.json
- 许可证：MIT
