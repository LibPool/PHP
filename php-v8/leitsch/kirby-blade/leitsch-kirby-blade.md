# leitsch/kirby-blade

> 标签: blade, kirby, kirby-blade, kirby-plugin, laravel, template, template-engine, view

## 简介

Enable Laravel Blade Template Engine for Kirby 4 and Kirby 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
