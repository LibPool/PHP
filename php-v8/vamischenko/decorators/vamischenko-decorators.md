# vamischenko/decorators

> 标签: Streams, aes-256-cbc, encryption, hmac, media, psr-7, whatsapp

## 简介

PSR-7 stream decorators for WhatsApp media encryption and decryption

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
