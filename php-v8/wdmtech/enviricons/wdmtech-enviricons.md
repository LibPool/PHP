# wdmtech/enviricons

> 标签: environment, favicon, framework, laravel

## 简介

Environment-specific favicons for local, development and staging environments in Laravel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
