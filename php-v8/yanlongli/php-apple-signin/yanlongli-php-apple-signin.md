# yanlongli/php-apple-signin

> 标签: Authentication, JWK, JWKS, apple, apple login, apple-id, apple-sign-in, authorization, client-secret, es256, id-token, identity-token, ios, json-web-token, jwt, laravel, oauth2, oidc, php-apple-signin, rs256, sign in with apple, sign-in-with-apple, symfony, 苹果, 苹果登录, 苹果登陆, 苹果账号登陆

## 简介

Verify Sign In with Apple identity tokens (JWT) in PHP: Apple JWKS/RS256, audience/nonce validation and ES256 client_secret generation. 苹果登录/苹果登陆 PHP SDK。

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
