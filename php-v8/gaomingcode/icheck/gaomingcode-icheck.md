# gaomingcode/icheck

> 标签: accessibility, checkbox, checked, custom, disabled, field, form, iCheck, indeterminate, input, radio, replacement, skins, ui

## 简介

Highly customizable checkboxes and radio buttons (jQuery & Zepto).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
