# nutandc/nepali-date-converter

> 标签: BS, ad, bikram sambat, converter, date, nepal, nepali

## 简介

Nepali Date Converter (BS <-> AD) for Nepal-first date conversion.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
