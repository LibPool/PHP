# contaolabs/contao-email-download

> 标签: contao, download, e-mail, gated content, lead, lead magnet, pdf

## 简介

Download for e-mail address for Contao 4.13 and 5.x: visitors receive a protected, time-limited download link by e-mail. With request list and CSV export.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
