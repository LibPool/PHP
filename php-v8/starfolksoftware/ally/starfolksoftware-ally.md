# starfolksoftware/ally

> 标签: ally, laravel, starfolksoftware

## 简介

Manage contacts(e.g customers, suppliers, vendors etc) and addresses in your laravel applications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
