# atoms/symfony

> 标签: atoms, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle for Atoms: AtomsClient, the inbound callback stack, PSR-17/18 resolution, a Messenger queue bridge, and console wrappers around the atoms binary.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
