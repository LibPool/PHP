# pixelfed/pixelfed

> 标签: Federation, activitypub, framework, laravel, network, pixelfed, social

## 简介

Open and ethical photo sharing platform, powered by ActivityPub federation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
