# khudayberdiyev/uzbekistan-regions

> 标签: Uzbekistan, districts, laravel, mahalla, regions, soato, tuman, viloyat

## 简介

Uzbekistan regions, districts and quarters (SOATO) for Laravel: models, migrations, seeder and a ready-to-use multilingual REST API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
