# daacreators/creators-audio-rooms

> 标签: WebRTC, audio, laravel, live, rooms, voice

## 简介

Live audio rooms for Laravel: hosts, speakers and listeners with hand raising and moderation. Peer-to-peer WebRTC audio with signaling through your own app, so there is nothing extra to host.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
