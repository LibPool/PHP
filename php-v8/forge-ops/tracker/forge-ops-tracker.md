# forge-ops/tracker

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

ForgeOps error tracking client: captures unhandled exceptions (Laravel/Symfony integration, plus explicit capture anywhere else) and delivers them to ForgeOps over HTTP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
