# madebyclowd/laravel-auto-sequence

> 标签: autoincrement, concurrency, document-numbering, erp-numbering, gapless-sequence, invoice-number, laravel, order-number, pessimistic-locking, redis-lock, sequence, sequence-generator, serial-number, soft-deletes

## 简介

Automatically generate sequential invoice/order numbers (like INV-2026-0001) for Laravel Eloquent models. Safe from duplicates even under heavy concurrent traffic.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
