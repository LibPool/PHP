# kampute/civil-date

> 标签: civil-date, date-formatting, date-localization, date-parsing, gregorian-calendar, gregorian-date, hijri-calendar, hijri-date, islamic-calendar, islamic-date, jalali-calendar, jalali-date, persian-calendar, persian-date, persian-numerals

## 简介

Accurate Jalali, Gregorian, and Islamic date handling for PHP, with Persian calendar dates based on the official Nowruz rule.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
