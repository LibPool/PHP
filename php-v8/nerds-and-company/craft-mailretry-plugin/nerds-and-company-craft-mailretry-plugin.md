# nerds-and-company/craft-mailretry-plugin

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, mail retry

## 简介

Retry mails in background when they fail

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
