# fernandovaller/thumbnail

> 标签: thumbnail, video

## 简介

Get the thumbnail of youtube and vimeo videos from the url. The returned information is ID and URL of the thumbnail

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
