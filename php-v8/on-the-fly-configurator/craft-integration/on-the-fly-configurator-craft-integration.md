# on-the-fly-configurator/craft-integration

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, ontheflyintegration

## 简介

Integration of On The Fly to your eCommerce website. This allows users to navigate from OTF to the eCommerce cart with the OTF plugins added to their cart/basket so they can use the companies on payment gateways.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
