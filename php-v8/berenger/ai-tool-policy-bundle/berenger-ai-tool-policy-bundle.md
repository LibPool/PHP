# berenger/ai-tool-policy-bundle

> 标签: Policy, ai, approval, llm, symfony, symfony-ai, symfony-bundle, tools

## 简介

Symfony bundle providing a policy layer over the Symfony AI toolbox: tool filtering by tags, approval gating, and a debug command.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
