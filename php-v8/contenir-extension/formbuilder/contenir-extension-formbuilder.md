# contenir-extension/formbuilder

> 标签: Forms, contenir, contenir-extension, form-builder

## 简介

The form builder module for Contenir CMS: the Forms area, with forms, fields, notifications, webhooks and entries, over contenir/contenir-formbuilder.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
