# contenir-extension/tracking

> 标签: analytics, consent-mode, contenir, contenir-extension, tracking

## 简介

The Tracking add-on for Contenir CMS: the Google tag, Tag Manager, the Meta pixel and LinkedIn Insight, with Consent Mode v2 and a per-environment switch. The reference add-on.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
