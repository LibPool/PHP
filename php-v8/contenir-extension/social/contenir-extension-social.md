# contenir-extension/social

> 标签: contenir, contenir-extension, share, social, social-links

## 简介

The Social add-on for Contenir CMS: profile links for twelve networks, checked as they are saved, and share buttons chosen and ordered by editors. It replaces the CMS's Social Media Links plugin, with the same settings keys.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
