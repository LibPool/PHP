# mohsinqk/ai-filemetadata

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, ai, extension, fal

## 简介

Automatically generates FAL metadata for files by means of public LLMs (Fork with custom changes)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
