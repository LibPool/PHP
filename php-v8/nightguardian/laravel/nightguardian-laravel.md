# nightguardian/laravel

> 标签: error-tracking, exceptions, laravel, monitoring, nightguardian

## 简介

Laravel SDK for NightGuardian: self-hosted error and health monitoring that never slows down or breaks your app.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
