# gregsphotography/tinymce-nova

> 标签: editor, laravel, nova, tinymce, wysiwyg

## 简介

A Laravel Nova field that integrates TinyMCE editor with your local TinyMCE installation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
