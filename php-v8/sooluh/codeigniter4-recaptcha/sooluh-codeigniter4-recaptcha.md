# sooluh/codeigniter4-recaptcha

> 标签: google, recaptcha

## 简介

CodeIgniter 4 verifier for Google reCAPTCHA v2 and reCAPTCHA v3

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
