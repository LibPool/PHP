# sourcebroker/imageopt

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Optimize images created/resized by TYPO3 so they take less space. Safe as it does not optimize original images.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
