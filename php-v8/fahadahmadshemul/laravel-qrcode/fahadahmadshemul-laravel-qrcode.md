# fahadahmadshemul/laravel-qrcode

> 标签: generator, laravel, qr, qr-code, qrcode, reed-solomon

## 简介

Self-contained QR code generator for Laravel with the QR encoding engine implemented from scratch - no third-party QR libraries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
