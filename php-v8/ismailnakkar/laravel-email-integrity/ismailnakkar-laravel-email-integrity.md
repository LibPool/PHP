# ismailnakkar/laravel-email-integrity

> 标签: MX, bounce, disposable, email, fraud, laravel, suppression, throwaway, validation, webhook

## 简介

Refuse disposable and unroutable email domains at the point they are entered, and stop mailing addresses that bounced or complained, in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
