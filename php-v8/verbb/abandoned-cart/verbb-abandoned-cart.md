# verbb/abandoned-cart

> 标签: Craft, abandoned-cart, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Send multiple email reminders to customers that have abandoned their carts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
