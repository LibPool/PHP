# verbb/shield

> 标签: Akismet, Craft, anti-spam, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, shield, spam

## 简介

Harness the power of Akismet to shield your site from spam.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
