# verbb/metrix

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, metrix

## 简介

Display your analytics within Craft. Supports Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
