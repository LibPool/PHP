# verbb/stamped-io

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, stamped-io

## 简介

Send your order data from Craft Commerce to Stamped.io, for your customers to review.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
