# verbb/back-in-stock

> 标签: Craft, back-in-stock, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Subscribe to out of stock products to receive an email when back in stock.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
