# atriametal/sylius-cookie-consent-plugin

> 标签: consent, cookie, gdpr, rgpd, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Collects the cookie consent of the visitors of a Sylius shop, blocks scripts and embedded contents until they consent, and keeps a proof of each consent.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
