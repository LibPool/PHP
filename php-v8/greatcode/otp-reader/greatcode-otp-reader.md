# greatcode/otp-reader

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

OTP Reader - Thread-safe, atomic, file-based Google OAuth credential storage library in native PHP with Mail Reader & Gmail Driver

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
