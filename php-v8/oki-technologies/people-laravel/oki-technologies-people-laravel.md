# oki-technologies/people-laravel

> 标签: identity, jwt, laravel, oauth2, oidc, openid-connect

## 简介

Sign people in with People, verify their tokens and keep their identities current — in Laravel, with a guard, a sync command and a test fake.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
