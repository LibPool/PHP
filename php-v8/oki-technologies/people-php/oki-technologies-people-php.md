# oki-technologies/people-php

> 标签: JWKS, identity, jwt, oauth2, oidc, openid-connect, pkce

## 简介

Sign people in with People, verify their tokens and keep their identities current.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
