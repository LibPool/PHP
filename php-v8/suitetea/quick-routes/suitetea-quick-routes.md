# suitetea/quick-routes

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

QuickRoutes simplifies and shortens mundane and repeated declaration of routes and controller methods.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
