# clesson-de/silverstripe-graphing

> 标签: Chart.js, Forms, charts, graphing, silverstripe

## 简介

Chart form fields for Silverstripe 6 CMS, rendered with Chart.js. Display-only — no data storage.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
