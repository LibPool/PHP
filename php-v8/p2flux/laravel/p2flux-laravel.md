# p2flux/laravel

> 标签: USDC, base, checkout, erc20, laravel, payments, recurring, stablecoin, subscriptions

## 简介

Official Laravel integration for the P2Flux PHP SDK: USDC payments and subscriptions on Base, settled straight to your own wallet.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
