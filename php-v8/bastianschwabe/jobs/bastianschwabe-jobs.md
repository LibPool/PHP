# bastianschwabe/jobs

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, career, google for jobs, job posting, jobposting, jobs, schema.org, structured data, vacancy

## 简介

Job postings for TYPO3: filterable list, detail view and standards-compliant schema.org/JobPosting JSON-LD output for Google for Jobs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
