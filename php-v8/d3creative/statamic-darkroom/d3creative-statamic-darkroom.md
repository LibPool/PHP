# d3creative/statamic-darkroom

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Darkroom by D3 Creative - Generate images with Google's Nano Banana models from the Statamic Control Panel and save them straight into your asset library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
