# d3creative/statamic-tracer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Tracer by D3 Creative - A Statamic UTM Builder fieldtype that generates UTM-tagged URLs for entries: social, newsletter, and paid ads.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
