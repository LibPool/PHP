# sewlore/measurement-calculators

> 标签: calculator, drupal, fabric, measurement, sewing

## 简介

Local fabric stretch and recovery, button-centre spacing, and length conversion calculators for Drupal 11.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
