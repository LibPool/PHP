# brandonlinu/doctrine-utc-bundle

> 标签: datetime, doctrine, symfony, utc

## 简介

A Symfony Bundler to manage date and time in UTC with timezone support for Doctrine

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
