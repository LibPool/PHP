# ranierimachado/news-aggregator

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A multi-perspective news pipeline for Drupal 11. RSS ingestion, article-level AI bias and topic classification against a versioned rubric, story clustering into neutral events, consensus synthesis, semantic search and cost monitoring. Everything site-specific is config.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
