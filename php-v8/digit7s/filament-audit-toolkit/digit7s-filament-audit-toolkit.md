# digit7s/filament-audit-toolkit

> 标签: audit-log, audit-trail, filament, filament-plugin, laravel, livewire, record-history

## 简介

A read-only Filament 5 explorer and record history for Digit7s Laravel audit events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
