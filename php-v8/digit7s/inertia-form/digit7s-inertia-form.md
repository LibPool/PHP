# digit7s/inertia-form

> 标签: dynamic-forms, form-builder, inertiajs, laravel, vuejs

## 简介

A backend-driven form builder for Inertia.js and Vue 3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
