# systemin/sylius-google-merchant-center

> 标签: google merchant center, product feed, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

A Sylius plugin to integrate with Google Merchant Center.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
