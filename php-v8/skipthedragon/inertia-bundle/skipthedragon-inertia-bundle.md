# skipthedragon/inertia-bundle

> 标签: Bridge, bundle, inertia, monolith, php, symfony

## 简介

Inertia.js server-side adapter for Symfony 4, 5, 6 and 7.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
