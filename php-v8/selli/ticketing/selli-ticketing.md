# selli/ticketing

> 标签: Sellinnate, ai, chat-widget, customer-support, email-to-ticket, gdpr, headless, help-desk, helpdesk, knowledge-base, kpi, laravel, mcp, multi-tenant, rag, selli, sla, support, ticketing, tickets

## 简介

The headless, multi-tenant help-desk engine for Laravel: tickets on any model, workflow, SLA, routing, email & widget channels, AI with human hand-over, knowledge base, KPIs, MCP and RAG.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
