# bbwdadfg/digibouquetai-site-kit

> 标签: digibouquetai, digital-bouquet, flowers, gift-card

## 简介

Canonical URL helpers for DigiBouquet AI digital bouquets and AI gift cards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
