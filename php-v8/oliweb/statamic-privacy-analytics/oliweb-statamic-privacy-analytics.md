# oliweb/statamic-privacy-analytics

> 标签: Enhanced, alpinejs, analytics, cache, chart, chartjs, dashboard, performance, real-time, statamic, statamic-addon, statamic-analytics, tracking

## 简介

Self-hosted, privacy-first analytics for Statamic. No third-party data transfer by default — MaxMind geolocation, configurable retention, granular CP permissions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
