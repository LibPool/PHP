# forgemesh/pay-per-crawl-laravel

> 标签: 402, USDC, ai-crawlers, base, laravel, middleware, mpp, pay-per-crawl, x402

## 简介

Laravel middleware that charges AI crawlers per page with x402 / MPP USDC micropayments on Base, paid straight to your wallet. Hit analytics included.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
