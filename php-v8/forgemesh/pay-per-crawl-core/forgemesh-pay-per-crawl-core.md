# forgemesh/pay-per-crawl-core

> 标签: 402, USDC, ai-crawlers, base, mpp, pay-per-crawl, paywall, x402

## 简介

Framework-free core for ForgeMesh Pay-per-Crawl: charge AI crawlers per page with x402 / MPP USDC micropayments on Base, paid straight to your wallet.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
