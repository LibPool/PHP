# daa/addressable-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Symfony bundle to make entities addressable and geo spacial services for searching and comparing their latitude/longitude

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
