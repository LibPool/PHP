# amarenkov/laravel-mutable-content-shop

> 标签: cart, custom-fields, dynamic-fields, ecommerce, eloquent, laravel, money, orders, shop

## 简介

Shop domain for laravel-mutable-content: carts of users and guests, orders with item snapshots, price breaks and money totals, with services, without a UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
