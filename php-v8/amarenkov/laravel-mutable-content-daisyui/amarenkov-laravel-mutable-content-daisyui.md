# amarenkov/laravel-mutable-content-daisyui

> 标签: DaisyUI, blade, custom-fields, dynamic-fields, eloquent, jsonb, laravel, livewire, tailwindcss

## 简介

Server-rendered Blade, Livewire and daisyUI screens for laravel-mutable-content: forms, tables and management of fields and lists of values.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
