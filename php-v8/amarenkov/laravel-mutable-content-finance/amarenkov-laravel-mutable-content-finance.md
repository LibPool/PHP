# amarenkov/laravel-mutable-content-finance

> 标签: Accounting, custom-fields, dynamic-fields, eloquent, finance, laravel, money, personal-finance

## 简介

Personal finance domain for laravel-mutable-content: accounts, operations split by categories, transfers, debts and receipts, with services, without a UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
