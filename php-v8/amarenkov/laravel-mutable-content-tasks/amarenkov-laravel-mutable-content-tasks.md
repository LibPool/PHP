# amarenkov/laravel-mutable-content-tasks

> 标签: Tasks, custom-fields, dynamic-fields, eloquent, laravel, task-management, time-tracking

## 简介

Task domain for laravel-mutable-content: tasks, participants and time tracking with their services and events, without a UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
