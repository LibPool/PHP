# zf2timo/zf-mvc-expressive-bridge

> 标签: Bridge, PluginManager, expressive, mvc, zf2

## 简介

Builds a Bridge between Zend Expressive and Plugin Managers of Zend\MVC

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
