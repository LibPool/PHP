# denisgeomiq/eloquent-has-by-non-dependent-subquery

> 标签: builder, database, dependent, eloquent, has, illuminate, laravel, macro, query, whereHas

## 简介

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
