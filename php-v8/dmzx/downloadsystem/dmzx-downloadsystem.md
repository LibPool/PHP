# dmzx/downloadsystem

> 标签: downloadsystem, extension, phpbb

## 简介

This extension will add an easy download system to your forum, where you can offer your user to download files from your webspace.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
