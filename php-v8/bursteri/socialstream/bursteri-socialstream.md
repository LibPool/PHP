# bursteri/socialstream

> 标签: SocialStream, laravel, laravel socialite, oauth

## 简介

OAuth for Laravel, simplified. Fork of joelbutcher/socialstream with Laravel 13 support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
