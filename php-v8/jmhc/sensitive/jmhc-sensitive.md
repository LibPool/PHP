# jmhc/sensitive

> 标签: dfa, jmhc, sensitive

## 简介

Filter sensitive words based on determination of Finite Automata (DFA) algorithm.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
