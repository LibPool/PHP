# kg-bot/hookbox

> 标签: idempotency, inbox, laravel, replay, webhook

## 简介

Hookbox — the inbox for your Laravel webhooks. Receive, verify, dedupe, replay.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
