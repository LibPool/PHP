# litepie/teams

> 标签: Teams, collaboration, laravel, multi-tenant, permissions, roles, tenancy, workflow

## 简介

A modern, tenant-ready teams management package for Laravel 12+

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
