# keyvanarasteh/laravel-bones

> 标签: Devtools, Skeleton, debug, laravel, packages, routes, views, visualizer

## 简介

An X-ray for your Laravel application: explore its routes, view nodes and package skeleton in one clean dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
