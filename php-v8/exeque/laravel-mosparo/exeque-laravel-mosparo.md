# exeque/laravel-mosparo

> 标签: captcha, laravel, livewire, mosparo, spam

## 简介

mosparo spam protection for Laravel and Livewire: a configured client, a validation rule, a Blade box and a Livewire form that asks for fresh tokens after every check.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
