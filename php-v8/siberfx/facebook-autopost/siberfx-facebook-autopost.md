# siberfx/facebook-autopost

> 标签: autopost, facebook, graph-api, laravel, oauth, page, share, social

## 简介

Connect one Facebook Page to your Laravel app and share your models to it automatically when they go live, through the Graph API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
