# siberfx/social-autopost

> 标签: TikTok , autopost, facebook, instagram, laravel, linkedin, oauth, share, social, twitter, x

## 简介

Share your Laravel models to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, each switched on or off, and add more networks later.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
