# siberfx/mpesa-payment

> 标签: africa, daraja, kenya, laravel, lipa na mpesa, m-pesa, mpesa, payments, safaricom, stk push, tanzania, vodacom

## 简介

Modern M-Pesa payment gateway for PHP 8.4+ — Safaricom Daraja (Kenya) and Vodacom M-Pesa OpenAPI (Tanzania, DRC, Lesotho). Works standalone or with Laravel 12/13.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
