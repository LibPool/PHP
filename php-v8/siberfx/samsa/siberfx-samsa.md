# siberfx/samsa

> 标签: consumer-groups, event-log, event-sourcing, jetstream, kafka, laravel, nats, partitions, redis-streams, streaming

## 简介

Kafka's model of topics, partitions, offsets and consumer groups inside Laravel. Pure PHP, no broker required.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
