# siberfx/x-autopost

> 标签: autopost, laravel, oauth, posts-api, share, social, x

## 简介

Connect one X account to your Laravel app and share models to X automatically when they go live, through the Posts API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
