# siberfx/linkedin-autopost

> 标签: autopost, laravel, linkedin, oauth, posts-api, share, social

## 简介

Connect one LinkedIn account to your Laravel app and share models to LinkedIn automatically when they go live, through the Posts API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
