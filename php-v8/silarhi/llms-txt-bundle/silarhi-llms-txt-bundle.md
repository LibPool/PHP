# silarhi/llms-txt-bundle

> 标签: ai, geo, llm, llms, llms.txt, seo, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Build, dump and serve an llms.txt file from your Symfony application, the PrestaSitemapBundle way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
