# filisko/pdo-plus

> 标签: debug, log, pdo, php8, queries, sql, tracy

## 简介

PDO+ extends PDO in order to log all your queries. It also includes a Bar Panel for Tracy

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
