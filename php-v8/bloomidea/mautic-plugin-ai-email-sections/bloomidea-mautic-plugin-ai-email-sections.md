# bloomidea/mautic-plugin-ai-email-sections

> 标签: Mautic, ai, email, grapesjs, mjml, plugin

## 简介

Generates MJML sections in the GrapesJS email builder from a text description, with allowlist validation and preservation invariants.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
