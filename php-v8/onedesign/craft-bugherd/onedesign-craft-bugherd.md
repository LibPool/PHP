# onedesign/craft-bugherd

> 标签: Craft, bugherd, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Easily install Bugherd on your Craft CMS site

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
