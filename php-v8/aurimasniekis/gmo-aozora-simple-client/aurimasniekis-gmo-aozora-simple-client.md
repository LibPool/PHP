# aurimasniekis/gmo-aozora-simple-client

> 标签: aozora, client, gmo

## 简介

A simple client for accessing your bank information from https://gmo-aozora.com/

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
