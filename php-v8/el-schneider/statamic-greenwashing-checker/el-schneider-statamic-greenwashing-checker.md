# el-schneider/statamic-greenwashing-checker

> 标签: ai, greenwashing, statamic, statamic-addon, uwg

## 简介

Checks marketing copy for greenwashing risks under EU rules (EmpCo), with German UWG references.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
