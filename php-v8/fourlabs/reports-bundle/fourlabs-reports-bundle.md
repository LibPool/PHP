# fourlabs/reports-bundle

> 标签: QBJS, QBJSParser, QBJSParserBundle, Reports Bundle, ReportsBundle, doctrine, jQuery-QueryBuilder, jquery, orm, parser, php, querybuilder, querybuilder.js, querybuilder.js.org, reports, symfony

## 简介

This bundle helps you report on your Symfony entities, by using on fourlabs/qbjs-parser-bundle and querybuilder.js.org.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
