# logvice/phplogger

> 标签: logging, logvice, php logger, psr-3

## 简介

Sends your logs to LogVice.com web services or your personal LogVice installation or to a local file.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
