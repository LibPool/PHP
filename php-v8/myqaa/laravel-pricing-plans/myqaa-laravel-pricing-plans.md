# myqaa/laravel-pricing-plans

> 标签: laravel, plans, pricing, subscriptions

## 简介

A package provide pricing plans for Laravel. Forked from oanhnn/laravel-pricing-plans

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
