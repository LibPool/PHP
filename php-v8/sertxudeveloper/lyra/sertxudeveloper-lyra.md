# sertxudeveloper/lyra

> 标签: admin, laravel, panel

## 简介

Lyra is an administration panel for Laravel for bootstrap your app and turn ideas into reality.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
