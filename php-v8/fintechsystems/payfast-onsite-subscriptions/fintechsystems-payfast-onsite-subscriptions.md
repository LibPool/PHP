# fintechsystems/payfast-onsite-subscriptions

> 标签: laravel, payfast, subscription-billing

## 简介

A Laravel service provider and Livewire blades for Payfast subscription billing

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
