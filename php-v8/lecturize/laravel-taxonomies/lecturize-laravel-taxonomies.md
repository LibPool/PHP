# lecturize/laravel-taxonomies

> 标签: categories, eloquent, laravel, model, nestable, taxonomies, terms

## 简介

Simple, nestable Terms & Taxonomies (similar to WordPress) for Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
