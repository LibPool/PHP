# qubus/log

> 标签: Flysystem, json, logger, logging, psr-3, symfony-mailer

## 简介

PSR-3 logging with Flysystem, locked streams, PDO, and Symfony-backed Qubus Mail.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
