# bladewindui/calendar

> 标签: blade, bladewindui, calendar, content, events, laravel, tailwindcss

## 简介

BladewindUI accessible calendar — an inline month/week calendar for displaying and selecting dates or events, distinct from the input-focused Datepicker

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
