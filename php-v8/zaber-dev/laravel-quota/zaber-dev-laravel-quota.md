> 标签: budget, cache, calendar-periods, database, eloquent, laravel, middleware, quota, quotas, rate-limit, redis, usage-limits, usage-tracking

## 简介

Application-level quota management for Laravel with calendar periods, multiple storage backends, and expressive APIs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
