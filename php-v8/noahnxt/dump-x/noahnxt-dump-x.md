# noahnxt/dump-x

> 标签: NoahNxT, dump-x, laravel

## 简介

An extension on your daily dump commands. Forget loopig dumps in a loop to get a specific amount of dumps.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
