# webignition/simplytestable-pagecache-bundle

> 标签: cache, http, response, symfony, symfony4

## 简介

Symfony 4 bundle for creating/validating cacheable responses

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
