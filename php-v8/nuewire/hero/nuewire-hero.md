# nuewire/hero

> 标签: banner, cms, filesystem, hero, laravel, livewire, nuewire

## 简介

Hero content management with image/video storage, calls to action, ordering, and audit trails for Nuewire.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
