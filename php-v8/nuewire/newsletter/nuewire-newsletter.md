# nuewire/newsletter

> 标签: Broadcast, email, laravel, livewire, newsletter, nuewire

## 简介

Subscriber and queued newsletter broadcast management for the Nuewire Laravel platform.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
