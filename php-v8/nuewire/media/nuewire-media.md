# nuewire/media

> 标签: Album, filesystem, gallery, laravel, livewire, media, nuewire, revisionable

## 简介

Album and media-file management with revision history, filesystem storage, audit trails, and archive workflow for Nuewire.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
