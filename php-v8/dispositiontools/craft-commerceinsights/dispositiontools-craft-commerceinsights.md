# dispositiontools/craft-commerceinsights

> 标签: Craft, cms, commerceinsights, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Get the low down on how your Craft Commerce store is doing. Explore and export customer summaries, product purchases, and transactions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
