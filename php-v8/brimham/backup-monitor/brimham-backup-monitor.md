# brimham/backup-monitor

> 标签: backup, backup-monitor, laravel, monitoring, spatie

## 简介

Records and surfaces the health and history of your spatie/laravel-backup runs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
