# kalindussasinindu/laravel-dcb-lk

> 标签: carrier billing, dcb, hsenid, ideamart, laravel, mspace, otp, sri lanka, subscription

## 简介

Direct Carrier Billing for Sri Lanka (Ideamart & mSpace) - OTP subscription verification, status polling, and webhooks, behind one driver interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
