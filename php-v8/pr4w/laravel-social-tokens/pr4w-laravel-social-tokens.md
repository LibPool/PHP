# pr4w/laravel-social-tokens

> 标签: laravel, oauth, social media, socialite, tokens

## 简介

Persist, renew and manage OAuth social account tokens on top of Laravel Socialite.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
