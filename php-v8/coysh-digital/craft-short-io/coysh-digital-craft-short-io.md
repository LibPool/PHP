# coysh-digital/craft-short-io

> 标签: Craft, URL-Shortener, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, link-tracking, qr-codes, short-io, short-links

## 简介

Short links and QR codes for Craft CMS entries, powered by Short.io - created, renamed and retired automatically as you publish.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
