# fork/craft-akamai-invalidator

> 标签: Craft, akamai, cache, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Assign cache tags to pages and invalidate them on save.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
