# ianfoxdev/decline-codes

> 标签: decline-codes, dunning, fintech, iso8583, mastercard, payments, retry, stripe, visa

## 简介

Payment decline codes normalized across card networks and PSPs, and a retry policy that follows the networks' reattempt rules, with a source for every rule.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
