# darelabs/ipay-ghana-wordpress

> 标签: payment, wordpress-plugin

## 简介

Receive online payments on your WordPress site in Ghana.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
