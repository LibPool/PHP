# lightspeeddevelopment/lsx-mega-menus

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Create custom, full-width dropdown menus that contain images, widgets and more that seamlessly tie into your LSX Themed site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
