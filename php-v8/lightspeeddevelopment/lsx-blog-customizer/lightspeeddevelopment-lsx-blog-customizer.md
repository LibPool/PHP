# lightspeeddevelopment/lsx-blog-customizer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This extension gives you extra options to customize the blog of your LSX-powered WordPress site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
