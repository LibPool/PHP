# lightspeeddevelopment/lsx-team

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The LSX Team Extension provides a custom post type that allows you to easily show off the people that make up your business.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
