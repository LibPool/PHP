# bretterer/laravel-hashid

> 标签: bretterer, laravel, laravel-hashid

## 简介

Generate and use HashIds for your Laravel models, just like native ULIDs and UUIDs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
