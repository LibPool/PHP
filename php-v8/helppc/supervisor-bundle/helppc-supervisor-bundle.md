# helppc/supervisor-bundle

> 标签: api, rpc, supervisor, supervisorctl, supervisord, symfony

## 简介

Manage your Supervisor instances from a Symfony application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
