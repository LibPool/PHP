# ghanem/notify

> 标签: bootstrap, flash, laravel, notifications, notify

## 简介

Laravel 5 Flash Notifications with icons and animations and with a timeout

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
