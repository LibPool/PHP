# mikomagni/simple-likes

> 标签: Favorites, addon, bookmarks, cms, engagement, favourites, laravel, likes, reactions, simple likes, social, statamic, statistics

## 简介

Simple Likes is a lightweight addon for Statamic that lets visitors like, save, or favourite your content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
