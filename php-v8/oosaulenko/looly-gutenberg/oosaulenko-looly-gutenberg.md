# oosaulenko/looly-gutenberg

> 标签: easyadmin, editor, flexible-content, symfony

## 简介

A Symfony bundle designed for EasyAdmin that includes a versatile content editor.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
