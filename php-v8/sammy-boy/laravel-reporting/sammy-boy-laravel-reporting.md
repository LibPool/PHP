# sammy-boy/laravel-reporting

> 标签: event-driven, eventbus, laravel, microservices, reporting, snapshot

## 简介

Reporting publication layer for Laravel microservices. Owns HOW reporting messages are shaped and published; delegates transport to uzapoint/eventbus-core.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
