# concept7/laravel-forduty

> 标签: concept7, laravel, laravel-forduty

## 简介

Point browsers at your for.duty ingest endpoint with a Reporting-Endpoints header.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
