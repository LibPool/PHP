# hotte512/hug-eu-label

> 标签: eu-label, gpsr, shopware, shopware-plugin, warranty

## 简介

Displays the EU warranty label (Directive (EU) 2024/825, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960) and the GPSR manufacturer information (Regulation (EU) 2023/988, Art. 19) in the storefront and attaches the label to the order confirmation mail

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
