# cashier-pix/cashier-pix

> 标签: Asaas, billing, brasil, cashier, laravel, pix, pix-automatico

## 简介

Laravel Cashier-style recurring billing for PIX Automático (Asaas, Efí, Mercado Pago).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
