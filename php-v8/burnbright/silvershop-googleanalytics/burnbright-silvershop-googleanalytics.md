# burnbright/silvershop-googleanalytics

> 标签: analytics, google, shop, silverstripe

## 简介

Integrate SilverStripe shop sales with google analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
