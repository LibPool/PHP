# rhymedigital/isotope_quickproducts

> 标签: contao, isotope

## 简介

A Contao CMS extension that allows you to place a list of Isotope products as a Content Element.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
