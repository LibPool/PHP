# danibrutal/forker

> 标签: Tasks, fork, parallel, pcntl_fork, process

## 简介

A structured , safe, and easiest way to perform tasks parallely in PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
