# dvomedia/cruftflake

> 标签: cruftflake

## 简介

CruftFlake - a stab at a version of Twitter Snowflake but in PHP with a simple ZeroMQ interface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
