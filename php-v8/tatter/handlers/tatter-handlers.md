# tatter/handlers

> 标签: adapters, autoload, codeigniter, codeigniter4, drivers, handlers, modules

## 简介

Handler discovery and management, for CodeIgniter 4

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
