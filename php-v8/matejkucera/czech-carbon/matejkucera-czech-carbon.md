# matejkucera/czech-carbon

> 标签: carbon, czech, date, time

## 简介

Very small extension of Carbon, used for simple displaying of czech 'fine dates'.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
