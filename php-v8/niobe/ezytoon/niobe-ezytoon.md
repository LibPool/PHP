# niobe/ezytoon

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Ultra-efficient PHP encoder for TOON format. Drastically compress your data structures to minimize token counts and slash costs in AI prompts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
