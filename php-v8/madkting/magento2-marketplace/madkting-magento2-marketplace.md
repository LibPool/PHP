# madkting/magento2-marketplace

> 标签: madkting, magento 2, marketplace

## 简介

Turn your Magento Store in a Marketplace connecting it with Madkting

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
