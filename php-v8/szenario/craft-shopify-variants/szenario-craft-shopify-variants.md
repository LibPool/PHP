# szenario/craft-shopify-variants

> 标签: Craft, craft-plugin, craftcms, shopify, variants

## 简介

Pick individual Shopify product variants in Craft, as a field or a link type.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
