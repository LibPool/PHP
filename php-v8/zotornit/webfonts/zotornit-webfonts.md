# zotornit/webfonts

> 标签: fonts, google fonts, google webfonts, self-hosted, self-hosting, selfhosted, selfhosting, typo3, webfonts, zotorn, zotornit

## 简介

Easy way to use selfhosted webfonts on your website. Supported webfont providers: Google Webfont

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
