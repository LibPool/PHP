# cinghie/yii2-elasticemail

> 标签: elasticemail, yii2 elasticemail, yii2 newsletter

## 简介

Yii2 Elasticemail to manage Elasticemail Email Platform in a Yii2 site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
