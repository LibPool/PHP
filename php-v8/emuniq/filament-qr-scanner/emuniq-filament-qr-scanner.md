# emuniq/filament-qr-scanner

> 标签: barcode, camera, filament, filamentphp, html5-qrcode, laravel, livewire, photo-capture, qr, scanner, webcam

## 简介

Camera components for Filament: QR/barcode scanning and quick photo capture, drop-in anywhere Blade renders — pages, resources, relation managers and modals.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
