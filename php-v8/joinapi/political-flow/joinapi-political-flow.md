# joinapi/political-flow

> 标签: Authentication, ERP, business management, crm, filament, joinapi, laravel, multi-tenant, political management, political-flow, saas, socialite

## 简介

A comprehensive Laravel authentication and authorization system designed for Filament, focusing on multi-tenant political management.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
