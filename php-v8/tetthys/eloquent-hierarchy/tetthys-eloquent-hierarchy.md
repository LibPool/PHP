# tetthys/eloquent-hierarchy

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Lightweight Eloquent trait for self-referential (recursive) models with parent/children relations and O(1)/EXISTS() checks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
