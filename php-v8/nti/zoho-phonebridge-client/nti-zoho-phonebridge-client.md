# nti/zoho-phonebridge-client

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Zoho PhoneBridge Client to integrate your PBX or VoIP Service with Zoho CRM

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
