# zoxta/nova-cloudflare-card

> 标签: cache, cloudflate, laravel, nova

## 简介

Purge your CloudFlare's cache in two clicks from a Laravel Nova dashboard card.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
