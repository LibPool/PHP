# beebmx/kirby-sign

> 标签: kirby, kirby-plugin, kirby4, kirby5, page-signed, sign, signature, signed, url-signed

## 简介

Kirby Sign makes it easy to generate signed URLs for your Kirby site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
