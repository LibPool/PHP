# setono/sylius-video-plugin

> 标签: embed, product, sylius, sylius-plugin, video, vimeo, youtube

## 简介

Attach videos to Sylius products: uploaded files, external URLs and embed codes, with a Videos tab in the admin and rendering on the product page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
