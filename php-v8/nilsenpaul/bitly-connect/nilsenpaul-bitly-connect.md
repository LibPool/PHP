# nilsenpaul/bitly-connect

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This Craft CMS plugin makes it easy for you to create shortened URLs directly from your templates, through Bitly

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
