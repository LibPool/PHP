# uitrick/yii2-widget-upload-crop

> 标签: form, widget, yii2

## 简介

Form input widget that lets users to upload an image alongside its cropping dimensions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
