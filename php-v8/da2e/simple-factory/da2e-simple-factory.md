# da2e/simple-factory

> 标签: Simple, code, design, factory, pattern, php

## 简介

Mini-package implementing and providing a Simple Factory (design pattern) functionality.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
