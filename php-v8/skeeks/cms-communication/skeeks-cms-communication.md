# skeeks/cms-communication

> 标签: Messenger, cms, crm, email, imap, notifications, skeeks, yii

## 简介

Communications for SkeekS CMS CRM: mailboxes, messenger bots, conversations, client notifications and mailings linked to leads, companies and clients

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
