# thibedev/laravel-dropzone-chunk

> 标签: chunk, dropzone, laravel, upload

## 简介

Laravel 9 Service that make it easy to handle chunked upload (dropzone out of the box)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
