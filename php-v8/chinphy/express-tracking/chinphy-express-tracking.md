# chinphy/express-tracking

> 标签: EMS, courier, express, jd, logistics, sf-express, tracking, zto

## 简介

Unified express/courier tracking SDK for JD Logistics, SF Express, ZTO and China Post EMS with normalized output and optional raw trace retention.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
