# illuma-law/healthcheck-ai

> 标签: ai, health, illuma-law, laravel, llm

## 简介

Focused AI failover chain and agent registry health checks for Spatie's Laravel Health package.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
