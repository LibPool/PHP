# illuma-law/healthcheck-pgvector

> 标签: health, illuma-law, laravel, pgvector, postgres

## 简介

Focused pgvector extension health check for Spatie's Laravel Health package.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
