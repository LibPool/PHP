# tkisilverstripeteam/tkirequirements

> 标签: requirements, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds extra features to the standard SilverStripe requirements backend, such as requirement prioritisation, for easy ordering and grouping of requirements.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
