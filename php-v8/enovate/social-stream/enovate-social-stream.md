# enovate/social-stream

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, instagram, social-stream

## 简介

Pull posts from Instagram (and other social platforms) into Craft CMS templates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
