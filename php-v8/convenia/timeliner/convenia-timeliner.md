# convenia/timeliner

> 标签: ModelReflection, laravel, mirror, timeline

## 简介

Make a timeline in DynamoDB, mirror your models to timnelineformat

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
