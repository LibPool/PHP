# guyasyou/pure_cookies_notice

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Package for CMS concrete5 CMS that allows to notify users of anything, including the use of cookies (The Cookie Law Explained).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
