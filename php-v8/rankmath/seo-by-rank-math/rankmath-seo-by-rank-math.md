# rankmath/seo-by-rank-math

> 标签: plugin, wordpress

## 简介

Rank Math is the most powerful way to get BEST WordPress SEO tools added to your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
