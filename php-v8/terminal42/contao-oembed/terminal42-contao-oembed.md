# terminal42/contao-oembed

> 标签: embed, instagram post, timeline, tweet, twitter

## 简介

Embed a Tweet, a Twitter Timeline or an Instagram Post on your Contao website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
