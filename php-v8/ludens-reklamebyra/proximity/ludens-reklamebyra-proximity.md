# ludens-reklamebyra/proximity

> 标签: geo, longlat, proximity

## 简介

Find "things" nearby, or as far away as possible, based on longitude and latitude.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
