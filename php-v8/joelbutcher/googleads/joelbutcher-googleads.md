# joelbutcher/googleads

> 标签: Advertising, GoogleAds, google

## 简介

A lightweight and easy-to-configure extension for the Google Ads PHP Client, with OAuth2 support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
