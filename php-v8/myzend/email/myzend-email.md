# myzend/email

> 标签: Mailjet, gmail, html layout, html template, oop email, zf2

## 简介

MyZend Email is a ZF2 module to manage the process of sending emails with templates. You could also realy on MailJet/Gmail smtp.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
