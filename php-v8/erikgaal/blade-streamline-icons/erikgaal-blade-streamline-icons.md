# erikgaal/blade-streamline-icons

> 标签: blade-streamline-icons, erikgaal, laravel

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Streamline Icons in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
