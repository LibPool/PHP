# ethanjohnstone/silverstripe-shortcodes

> 标签: cms, shortcode, shortcodes, silverstripe, silverstripe-shortcodes, studiobonito, tinymce, video, vimeo, wysiwyg, youtube

## 简介

Adds shortcodes for inserting YouTube and Vimeo videos.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
