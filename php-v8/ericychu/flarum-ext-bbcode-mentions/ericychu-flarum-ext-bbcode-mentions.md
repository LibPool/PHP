# ericychu/flarum-ext-bbcode-mentions

## 简介

Mention and reply to specific posts and users. Only for BBCode WYSIWYG Editor extension.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
