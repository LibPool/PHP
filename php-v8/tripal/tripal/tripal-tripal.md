# tripal/tripal

> 标签: biological-data, drupal, model-organism, species, tripal

## 简介

Tripal is a toolkit to facilitate construction of online genomic, genetic (and other biological) websites.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://tripal.info
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/tripal/tripal.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/tripal%2Ftripal
