# gourmet/social-meta

> 标签: cakephp, facebook, gourmet, social, twitter

## 简介

Adds Facebook Open Graph and Twitter Cards support to CakePHP 3.x

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
