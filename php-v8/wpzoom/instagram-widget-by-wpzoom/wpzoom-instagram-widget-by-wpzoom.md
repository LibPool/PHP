# wpzoom/instagram-widget-by-wpzoom

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Simple and lightweight widget for WordPress to display your Instagram feed

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
