# kreativsoehne/magento-2-debit-payment

> 标签: composer-installer, debit, magento, magento 2, payment

## 简介

A simple Magento 2 extension for integrating debit payment

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
