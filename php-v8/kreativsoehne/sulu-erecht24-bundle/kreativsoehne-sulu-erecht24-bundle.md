# kreativsoehne/sulu-erecht24-bundle

> 标签: Datenschutz, Rechtstexte, e-Recht24, eRecht24, impressum, sulu, symfony

## 简介

Integrates legal texts (imprint, privacy policy) from the eRecht24 Rechtstexte API into Sulu 2.6 and Sulu 3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
