# protich/auto-join-eloquent

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Extend Eloquent to support auto-joining relationships and aliasing for SELECT, WHERE, ORDERBY, GROUPBY and HAVING clauses

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
