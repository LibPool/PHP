# hamedmax73/laravel-google-calendar

> 标签: calendar, event, google, laravel-google-calendar, schedule, spatie

## 简介

Manage events on a Google Calendar with google meet

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
