# iionly/hypewall_izap_videos_tidypics

> 标签: elgg, plugin

## 简介

Fixes quck link entry of hypeWall to select photo album to upload photos to (links for creating Tidypics albums and adding videos to iZAP Videos already built-in in hypeWall now).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
