# bherila/us-tax-advantaged-params

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Historical and current U.S. tax-advantaged account parameters. Retirement accounts are covered today: contribution limits, phase-outs, shared-limit allocation, and Roth-conversion tax effects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
