# andretx33/cryptid

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Simple and secure ID obfuscation for Laravel — ideal for encrypting UUIDs and internal IDs in URLs and forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
