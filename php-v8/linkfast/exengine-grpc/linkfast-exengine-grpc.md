# linkfast/exengine-grpc

> 标签: exengine, exengine-extension, gRPC, protobuf, roadrunner

## 简介

gRPC services for the ExEngine microframework on RoadRunner: contract-first from .proto files.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
