# innocenzi/deployer-recipe-forge

> 标签: deployer, deployment, laravel, laravel-force, zero-downtime

## 简介

Seamless zero-downtime deployment on Forge with Deployer

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
