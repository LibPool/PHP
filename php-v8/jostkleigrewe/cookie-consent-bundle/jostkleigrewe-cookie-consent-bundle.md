# jostkleigrewe/cookie-consent-bundle

> 标签: Datenschutz, asset-mapper, audit-log, bundle, cmp, consent, consent-management, consent-mode, cookie, cookie-banner, cookie-consent, cookie-modal, doctrine, dsgvo, e-privacy, ga4, gdpr, google-analytics, google-consent-mode, google-maps-embed, gtag, hotwire, privacy, stimulus, symfony, symfony-bundle, symfony-ux, symfony8, turbo, twig, twig-component, vimeo-embed, youtube-embed

## 简介

Symfony 8 bundle for GDPR/DSGVO cookie consent with Google Consent Mode v2, Twig components, Stimulus.js, and AssetMapper. Supports cookie, Doctrine, or combined storage. Includes YouTube, Vimeo, Google Maps embed components.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
