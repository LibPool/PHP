# normann/silverstripe-before-after

> 标签: before after, cms, elemental, image comparison, image slider, silverstripe

## 简介

A draggable Before/After image comparison slider for SilverStripe 6, available as a reusable DataObject widget and as an Elemental content block.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
