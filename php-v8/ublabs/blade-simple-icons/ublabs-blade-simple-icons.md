# ublabs/blade-simple-icons

> 标签: blade-simple-icons, laravel, ublabs

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Simple Icons in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
