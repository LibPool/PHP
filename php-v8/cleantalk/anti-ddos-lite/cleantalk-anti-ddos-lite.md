# cleantalk/anti-ddos-lite

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A small PHP app to protect your site against DDoS attack or crawling web site by bots.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
