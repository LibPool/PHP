# itf/vich-imagine-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Easy integration of VichUploaderBundle (upload) and LiipImagineBundle (processor)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
