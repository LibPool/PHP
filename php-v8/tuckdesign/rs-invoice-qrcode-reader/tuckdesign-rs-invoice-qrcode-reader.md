# tuckdesign/rs-invoice-qrcode-reader

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Reads QR codes from serbian fiscal invoice in pdf or image formats and gets json data

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
