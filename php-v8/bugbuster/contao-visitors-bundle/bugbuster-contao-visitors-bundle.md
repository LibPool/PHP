# bugbuster/contao-visitors-bundle

> 标签: bundle, contao, counter, hits, visitors, visits

## 简介

Contao 5 visitors bundle, allows you to count hits and visits of your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
