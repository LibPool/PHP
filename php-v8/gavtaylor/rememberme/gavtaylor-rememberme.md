# gavtaylor/rememberme

> 标签: keep me logged in, login, persistent login, plugin, remember me, roundcube, roundcubemail, session, webmail

## 简介

"Keep me logged in" for Roundcube: longer sessions only for browsers you choose, with encrypted tokens and a Remembered devices page

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
