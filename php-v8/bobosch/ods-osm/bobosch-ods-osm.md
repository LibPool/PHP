# bobosch/ods-osm

> 标签: OpenStreetMap, osm, typo3

## 简介

Add an interactive OpenStreetMap map to your website. Can also show other OpenLayers compatible maps.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
