# nexus-actors/serialization-msgpack

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Nexus msgpack serialization — dual-backend msgpack codec with native ext-msgpack and rybakit/msgpack fallback.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
