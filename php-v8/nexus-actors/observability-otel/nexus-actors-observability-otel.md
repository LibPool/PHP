# nexus-actors/observability-otel

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Nexus observability OpenTelemetry bridge — SDK-backed Tracer/Meter and an OTLP provider factory.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
