> 标签: activity-tracking, chronotrace, clean-code, laravel, laravel12, laravel13, pest, php8.3, php8.4, php8.5, productivity, testing, time-management, time-tracking

## 简介

Record and replay Laravel requests deterministically and generate tests from production traces.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
