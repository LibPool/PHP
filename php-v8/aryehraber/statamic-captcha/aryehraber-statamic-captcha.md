# aryehraber/statamic-captcha

> 标签: Forms, captcha, fcaptcha, hcaptcha, recaptcha, statamic

## 简介

Protect your Statamic forms using a Captcha service

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
