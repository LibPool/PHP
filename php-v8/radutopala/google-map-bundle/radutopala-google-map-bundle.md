# radutopala/google-map-bundle

> 标签: google map, infobox

## 简介

Provides a google map integration for your Symfony2 Project, together with InfoBox object.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
