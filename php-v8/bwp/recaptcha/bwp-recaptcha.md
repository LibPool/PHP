# bwp/recaptcha

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A WordPress plugin that utilizes the popular anti-spam library, reCAPTCHA, to help your blog stay clear of spams.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
