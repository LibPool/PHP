# rylxes/laravel-gdpr

> 标签: CCPA, compliance, consent, data-export, data-protection, gdpr, laravel, privacy, right-to-erasure, rylxes

## 简介

GDPR and CCPA compliance toolkit for Laravel with data export, right to erasure, consent management, and audit trails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
