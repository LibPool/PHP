> 标签: Content management system, Flatfile Laravel CMS, Laravel CMS, Open Source CMS, Open Source Laravel CMS, cms, flatfile cms

## 简介

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
