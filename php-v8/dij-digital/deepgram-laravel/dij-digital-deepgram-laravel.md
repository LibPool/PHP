# dij-digital/deepgram-laravel

> 标签: DIJ Digital, deepgram-laravel, laravel

## 简介

A Laravel package for integrating Deepgram’s AI voice services

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
