# justinholtweb/craft-carrier-gls

> 标签: MyGLS, carrier, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, gls, parcel-shop, shipping

## 简介

GLS through MyGLS (HR, HU, CZ, RO, SI, SK, RS) for Carrier — labels, COD, parcel shops and lockers, tracking.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
