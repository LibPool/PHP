> 标签: Craft, TikTok , cms, commerce, consent mode, conversion tracking, craft-plugin, craftcms, facebook pixel, ga4, google ads, google analytics, gtm, meta pixel, pinterest, server-side tracking, tape, tracking

## 简介

Conversion tracking for Craft CMS — Google Ads, GA4, Meta, TikTok and a dozen more pixels, wired to your content and your Commerce funnel, with Consent Mode v2 and server-side tracking.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
