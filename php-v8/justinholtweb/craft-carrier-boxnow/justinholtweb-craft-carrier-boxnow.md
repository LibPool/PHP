# justinholtweb/craft-carrier-boxnow

> 标签: boxnow, carrier, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, croatia, greece, lockers, shipping

## 简介

BOX NOW parcel lockers (Greece, Cyprus, Bulgaria, Croatia, Slovenia) for Carrier — locker picker, labels, COD and tracking.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
