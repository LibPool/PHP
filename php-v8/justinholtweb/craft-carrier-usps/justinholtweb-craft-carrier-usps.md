# justinholtweb/craft-carrier-usps

> 标签: carrier, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, shipping, usps

## 简介

USPS prices, labels, tracking, pickups and Post Office locations for Carrier, on the USPS APIs v3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
