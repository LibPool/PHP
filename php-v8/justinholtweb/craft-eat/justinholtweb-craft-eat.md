# justinholtweb/craft-eat

> 标签: XML feed, commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, csv feed, facebook catalog, google shopping, merchant center, product feed

## 简介

Product feeds for Craft Commerce — Google, Meta, Pinterest, Bing, TikTok and a dozen more, generated on a schedule and delivered wherever the channel wants them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
