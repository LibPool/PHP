# justinholtweb/craft-leads

> 标签: Craft, cms, conversion, craft-plugin, craftcms, lead-generation, modal, newsletter, optin, popup

## 简介

Popup and lead generation plugin for Craft CMS 5. Create modals, slide-ins, notification bars, and inline forms with targeting rules, email integrations, and analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
