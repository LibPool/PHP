# justinholtweb/craft-dispatch

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Lightweight email marketing and newsletter plugin for Craft CMS 5. Manage subscribers, send campaigns, and track delivery natively.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
