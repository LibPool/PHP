# justinholtweb/craft-telescope

> 标签: Craft, analytics, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, dashboard, ga4, google analytics, reporting, telescope

## 简介

Per-entry Google Analytics 4 reporting inside the Craft control panel — page views, visitors, sessions, timeline, geography, traffic sources and user journey for any entry with a public URL.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
