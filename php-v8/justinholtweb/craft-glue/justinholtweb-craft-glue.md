# justinholtweb/craft-glue

> 标签: content, craft-plugin, craftcms, deduplication, duplicates, editorial, entries, merge

## 简介

Merge two entries into one in Craft CMS — pick a winner field by field, combine what can be combined, and repoint everything that pointed at the entry you retired.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
