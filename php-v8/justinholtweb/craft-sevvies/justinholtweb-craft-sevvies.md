# justinholtweb/craft-sevvies

> 标签: Accounting, SEVDESK, buchhaltung, commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, datev, gobd, invoicing

## 简介

sevDesk invoicing for Craft Commerce — turn paid orders into correct German bookkeeping documents, with the right VAT rule, every time.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
