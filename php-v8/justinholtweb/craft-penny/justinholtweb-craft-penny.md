# justinholtweb/craft-penny

> 标签: Invite, client, content-collection, craft-plugin, craftcms, guest-editing, magic-link, one-time-login

## 简介

One-time content invites for Craft CMS — send a client a single-use link that lets them fill in exactly the entries, globals and fields you chose, and nothing else.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
