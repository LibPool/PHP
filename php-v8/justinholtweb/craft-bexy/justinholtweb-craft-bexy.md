# justinholtweb/craft-bexy

> 标签: Accounting, MwSt, bexio, commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, invoicing, switzerland, vat

## 简介

bexio integration for Craft Commerce — turn completed orders into bexio invoices or orders, push the payments that settle them, and pull bexio's verdict back into Commerce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
