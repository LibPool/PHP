# justinholtweb/craft-hitz

> 标签: craft-plugin, craftcms, events, exhibitors, floor-plan, hall-plan, hotspots, image-map, leaflet, trade-fair

## 简介

Interactive hall plans for Craft CMS. Draw halls, stands and facilities on a real-unit floor plan in the control panel, relate exhibitors to stands, and publish a zoomable, searchable plan on the front end — Donkeytail’s hit areas, grown into a trade-fair floor plan.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
