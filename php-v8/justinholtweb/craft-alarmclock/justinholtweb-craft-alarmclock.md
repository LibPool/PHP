# justinholtweb/craft-alarmclock

> 标签: cache, craft-plugin, craftcms, cron, notifications, post-date, publishing, queue, scheduled

## 简介

Scheduled publishing for Craft CMS that actually tells you it happened — cache clearing, notifications, retries, scheduled drafts and a diagnostic for “why isn’t my post live?”

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
