# justinholtweb/craft-redpen

> 标签: Craft, accessibility, cms, content quality, craft-plugin, craftcms, editorial, grammar, proofreading, readability, redpen, spelling, style guide

## 简介

Editorial QA for Craft — enforce your house style guide on every entry with banned terms, readability, accessibility and typography checks, plus optional LanguageTool and AI grammar review.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
