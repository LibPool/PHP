# justinholtweb/craft-bird

> 标签: Accounting, bookkeeping, btw, commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, eu, invoices, moneybird, vat

## 简介

Moneybird bookkeeping for Craft Commerce — turn paid orders into Moneybird invoices with EU-correct VAT, keep contacts in sync, register payments and credit refunds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
