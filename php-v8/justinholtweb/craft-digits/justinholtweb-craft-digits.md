# justinholtweb/craft-digits

> 标签: commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, digital-downloads, downloads, eu-vat, invoicing, license-keys, licensing

## 简介

Gated digital downloads and software licensing for Craft CMS — expiring links, download limits, versioned files, license keys with activation limits, a customer portal, refund revocation and EU VAT invoicing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
