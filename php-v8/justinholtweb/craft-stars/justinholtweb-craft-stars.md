# justinholtweb/craft-stars

> 标签: craft-plugin, craftcms, ratings, reviews, schema.org, testimonials

## 简介

Review & testimonial management for Craft CMS — star ratings, moderation, schema.org markup, and spam protection.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
