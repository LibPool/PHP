# justinholtweb/craft-yarn

> 标签: Audit, Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, debug, graph, orphans, related, relations, usage, yarn

## 简介

See every relation on a Craft site — what points at what, what nothing points at, and what breaks if you delete it.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
