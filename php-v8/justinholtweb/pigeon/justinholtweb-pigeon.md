# justinholtweb/pigeon

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, inbox, messaging, notifications, pigeon, support

## 简介

Two-way threaded messaging for Craft CMS 5 — customer support inboxes and user-to-user conversations with email, control panel, and on-site notifications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
