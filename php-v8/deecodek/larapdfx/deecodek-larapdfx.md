# deecodek/larapdfx

> 标签: CSS3, browsershot, chromium, flexbox, html-to-pdf, larapdfx, laravel, laravel-pdf, modern-pdf, pdf, pdf-generation, puppeteer

## 简介

Modern, production-ready PDF generation for Laravel with full CSS3 support, perfect rendering, and advanced features.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
