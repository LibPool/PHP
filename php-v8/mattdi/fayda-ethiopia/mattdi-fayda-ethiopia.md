# mattdi/fayda-ethiopia

> 标签: Ethiopia, OpenId, SSO, eID, esignet, fayda, fayda-id, laravel, mosip, oidc, verifayda

## 简介

Fayda (eSignet) OpenID Connect client for PHP with a Laravel bridge: PKCE, private_key_jwt, JWKS verification, and verified Fayda identity data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
