# lumaspdf/lumaspdf

> 标签: AcroForm, PDFA, Rendering, ZUGFeRD, barcode, facturx, ffi, pdf, pdf-editing, pdf-generation, pdfx, signature, xfa

## 简介

LumasPDF SDK for PHP - create, import, edit, render, sign and convert PDF. FFI binding over the native LumasPDF engine.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
