# gdpr_extensions_com/gdpr-extensions-com-grs

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Show your Google reviews in your website in a GDPR conform reviews slider content element. Powered by GDPR-extensions.com dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
