# fkrzski/robots-txt

> 标签: crawler, php8, robots, robots.txt, seo, web-crawler

## 简介

A modern, fluent PHP package for managing robots.txt rules with type safety and great developer experience

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
