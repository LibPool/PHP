# alfasix/alfakit-teams

> 标签: Teams, alfakit, invitations, laravel, multi-tenancy, roles, saas

## 简介

Jetstream-style teams for AlfaKit panels on top of multi-tenancy: team creation and settings, personal teams, members with configurable roles and permissions, email invitations, domain auto-join, seat limits and an optional billing seat hook.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
