# alfasix/alfakit-billing

> 标签: alfakit, billing, cashier, laravel, paddle, spark, stripe, subscriptions

## 简介

Subscription billing for AlfaKit panels: Stripe (Laravel Cashier), Paddle (Cashier Paddle) and Laravel Spark providers, a plans page and subscription middleware, for tenants or users.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
