# alfasix/alfakit-shield

> 标签: acl, alfakit, laravel, permissions, rbac, roles, spatie

## 简介

Roles and permissions for AlfaKit panels, built on spatie/laravel-permission: a role resource, permission and policy generation, and page and widget guards.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
