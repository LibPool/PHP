# alfasix/alfakit-forms

> 标签: Forms, alfakit, form-builder, laravel, submissions, surveys

## 简介

A Typeform-class form builder for AlfaKit: admins build multi-step forms with conditional logic in the panel, the public fills them on your own pages, and every answer is re-validated on the server.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
