# alfasix/alfakit-invoices

> 标签: alfakit, billing, credit-notes, invoices, laravel, pdf, quotes, receipts

## 简介

Invoices, quotes, credit notes and receipts for AlfaKit panels: exact integer money in any currency, inclusive, exclusive and compound taxes, atomic per-tenant numbering, live server-side totals, payments, overdue tracking, a branded print-ready document with a pluggable PDF renderer and a signed customer view link.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
