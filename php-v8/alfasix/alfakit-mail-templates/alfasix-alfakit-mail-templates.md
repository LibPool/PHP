# alfasix/alfakit-mail-templates

> 标签: alfakit, email, laravel, mail templates, merge tags, notifications

## 简介

Editable email templates for AlfaKit panels: admins edit the subject and body of AlfaKit's, its plugins' and your mails per locale with merge tags, preview them with sample data, send a test to themselves, reset to the default and browse the history.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
