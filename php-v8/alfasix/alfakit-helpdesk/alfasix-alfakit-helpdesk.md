# alfasix/alfakit-helpdesk

> 标签: alfakit, helpdesk, laravel, live-chat, sla, support, tickets

## 简介

A helpdesk inside AlfaKit panels: tickets with SLAs on business hours, a threaded conversation with internal notes, canned replies, an agent inbox with keyboard shortcuts, a customer portal, a contact form, an embeddable live chat widget and reports, real time when the panel is.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
