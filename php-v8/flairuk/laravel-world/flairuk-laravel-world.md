# flairuk/laravel-world

> 标签: aircraft, airlines, airports, cities, countries, iata, iso-3166, laravel, world

## 简介

Countries, cities, airports, airlines and aircraft for Laravel, joined up: one facade over the FLAIRUK data packages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
