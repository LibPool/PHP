# vittix/panchang

> 标签: astrology, calendar, hindu, horoscope, jyotish, kundli, laravel, panchang, php, vedic

## 简介

Vittix Vedic Panchang & Astrology Engine for PHP. Accurate Panchang, Kundli, and astrology calculations without native extensions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
