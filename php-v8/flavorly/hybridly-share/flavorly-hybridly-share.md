# flavorly/hybridly-share

> 标签: flavorly, hybridly, hybridly-flash, laravel

## 简介

A Quick way to flash & share Hybridly Variables from anywhere, persisting on session & processes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
