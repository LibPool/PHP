# flavorly/inertia-flash

> 标签: flavorly, inertia-flash, laravel

## 简介

A Quick way to flash & share InertiaJS Variables from anywhere, persisting on session.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
