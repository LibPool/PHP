# samhernandez/craft-shortcodes

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, shortcodes

## 简介

Use Wordpress-style shortcodes in your Twig templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
