# ruvelo/laravel-feedback

> 标签: canny, changelog, feature-requests, feedback, laravel, markdown, roadmap, voting, whats-new

## 简介

A feedback board, public roadmap and changelog for Laravel: people ask, vote and comment, you build, and they hear when it ships.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
