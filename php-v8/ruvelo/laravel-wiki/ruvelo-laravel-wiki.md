# ruvelo/laravel-wiki

> 标签: ai, documentation, knowledge-base, laravel, markdown, mcp, wiki

## 简介

A drop-in wiki for Laravel: Markdown pages, [[wiki links]], backlinks, full revision history with diffs, and search.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
