# campaignchain/operation-linkedin

> 标签: campaignchain, linkedin

## 简介

Collection of various LinkedIn operations (e.g. posting a news item).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
