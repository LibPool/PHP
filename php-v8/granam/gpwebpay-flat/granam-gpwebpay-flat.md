# granam/gpwebpay-flat

> 标签: flat, gp, gp webpay, parser, pay, report, webpay

## 简介

Parser of GP WepPay report in FLAT format

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
