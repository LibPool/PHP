# caiyun/micro

> 标签: Microservice, caiyun, consul, etcd, laravel, nacos, polaris, service-discovery, service-registry, zookeeper

## 简介

Laravel microservice toolkit with pluggable service discovery and registration (Nacos, Consul, Etcd, Zookeeper, Polaris, static, log).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
