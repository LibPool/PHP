# edgeinfinity/flarum-dice

> 标签: dice, flarum, random, roleplay

## 简介

Server-rolled inline dice for Flarum posts, with persistent results and configurable effects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
