# leeovery/laravel-idempotency

> 标签: idempotency, idempotent, laravel, laravel-idempotency

## 简介

Idempotency keys for Laravel side effects: an atomic once() and the claim, complete and completed primitives, backed by one unique index.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
