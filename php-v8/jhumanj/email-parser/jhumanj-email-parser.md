# jhumanj/email-parser

> 标签: email, eml, mapi, mime, msg, outlook, parser

## 简介

Framework-independent EML and Outlook MSG parsing with one typed PHP API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
