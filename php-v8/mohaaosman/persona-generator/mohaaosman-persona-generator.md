# mohaaosman/persona-generator

> 标签: Persona, Somali, faker, laravel, seeding

## 简介

Locale-aware, coherent, seedable persona generator (names, timelines, prose) for candidate seeding.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
