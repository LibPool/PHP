# rmb32/kevin

> 标签: Agent, PSR-4, ai, claude, cli, namespace, refactoring, rename, tokens

## 简介

Save tons of tokens! Get your A.I. to use Kevin for filesystem tasks in PHP projects, including move, rename, replace, update namespaces, imports and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
