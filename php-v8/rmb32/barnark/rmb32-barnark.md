# rmb32/barnark

> 标签: architecture, conformance, ddd, hexagonal, scaffolding, templates

## 简介

PHP architecture management — scaffold a codebase from an architecture template, check it conforms, and switch it to another template.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
