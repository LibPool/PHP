# semitexa/webhooks

> 标签: inbound, outbound, semitexa, signature-verification, webhooks

## 简介

Semitexa Webhooks - inbound verification/deduplication and outbound durable delivery with retries

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
