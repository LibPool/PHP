# minasm/carbon-uk

> 标签: Bank, british, business days, england, holiday, holidays, wales

## 简介

A basic wrapper for citco/carbon which returns business days between two given dates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
