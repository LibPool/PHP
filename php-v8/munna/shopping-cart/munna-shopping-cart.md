# munna/shopping-cart

> 标签: Ecommerce Shopping Cart, Laravel shopping cart, ecommerce, shopping cart

## 简介

Simple Laravel Shopping Cart Package. Store Your Cart Items On Local. No Need To Any Migration Or Database Query/Relation. Its More Flexible. Happy Laravel Coding...

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
