# creacoon/laravel-cloudfront-cookies

> 标签: creacoon, laravel, laravel-cloudfront-cookies

## 简介

A tiny utility to generate signed cookies to be used with AWS Cloudfront

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
