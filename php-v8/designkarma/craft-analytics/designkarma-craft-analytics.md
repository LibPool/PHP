# designkarma/craft-analytics

> 标签: Craft, analytics, craftcms, ga4, google analytics

## 简介

A simple Google Analytics 4 dashboard widget for Craft CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
