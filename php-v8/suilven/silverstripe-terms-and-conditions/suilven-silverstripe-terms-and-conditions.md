# suilven/silverstripe-terms-and-conditions

> 标签: and, conditions, legal, terms

## 简介

Add a terms and conditions field to a SilverStripe form.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
