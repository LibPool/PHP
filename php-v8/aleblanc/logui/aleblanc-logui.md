# aleblanc/logui

> 标签: debug, laravel, log, log-viewer, monolog, profiler, symfony

## 简介

Log viewer & lightweight profiler for Symfony & Laravel. File-based, zero-infra.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
