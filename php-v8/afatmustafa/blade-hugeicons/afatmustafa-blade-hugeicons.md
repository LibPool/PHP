# afatmustafa/blade-hugeicons

> 标签: Hugeicons, blade, laravel

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Hugeicons(Free version) in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
