# epmnzava/beempayments

> 标签: beempayments, epmnzava, mobilepayments

## 简介

A laravel lib that helps you to easily integrate with beem africa payments api.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
