# sjaakp/yii2-sortable-behavior

> 标签: Behavior, extension, order, sort, widget, yii, yii2

## 简介

Sort ActiveRecords and related records in Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
