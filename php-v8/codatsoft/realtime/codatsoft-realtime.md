# codatsoft/realtime

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Voice rooms with a LiveKit agent, user-to-user calls, and the realtime channel conventions shared by Codatsoft Laravel products.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
