# rquadling/phpunit-errorhandler

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A simple trait to add to your PHPUnit test allowing you to track and test PHP errors, warnings and notices

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
