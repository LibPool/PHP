# fabil/uberspacempc

> 标签: contao, email, mailbox, password, uberspace

## 简介

Easily change the passwords of the mailboxes from the hoster Uberspace.de in the frontend

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
