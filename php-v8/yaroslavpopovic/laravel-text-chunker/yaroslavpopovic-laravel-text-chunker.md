> 标签: ai, chunking-strategy, laravel, llm, markdown, openai, rag, text-chunker, text-processing, text-splitter, text-splitting, tokenizer

## 简介

Flexible, strategy-based text chunking for Laravel — fork of droath/laravel-text-chunker with Laravel 13 support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
