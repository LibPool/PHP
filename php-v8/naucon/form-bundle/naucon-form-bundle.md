# naucon/form-bundle

> 标签: Cross-Site-Request-Forgery, Forms, bundle, collection, csrf, form, formbundle, smarty, symfony, validation, xsrf

## 简介

Bundle to integrate naucon form package into the Symfony framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
