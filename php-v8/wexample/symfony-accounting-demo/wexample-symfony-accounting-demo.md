# wexample/symfony-accounting-demo

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A demo of symfony-accounting-ds: a French and a Belgian ledger, their documents, a bank statement and a fiscal year

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
