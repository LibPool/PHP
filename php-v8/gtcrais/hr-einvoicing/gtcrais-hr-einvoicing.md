# gtcrais/hr-einvoicing

> 标签: E-Invoice, EN 16931, HR CIUS, Moj-eRačun, e-racun, eracun, fiskalizacija, laravel, ubl

## 简介

Croatian e-invoices (Fiskalizacija 2.0) for Laravel: UBL 2.1 documents under the HR CIUS 2025, sent through Moj-eRačun

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
