# whilesmart/eloquent-feedback

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Customer feedback, reviews and testimonials for Laravel: a triage and sentiment layer over eloquent-forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
