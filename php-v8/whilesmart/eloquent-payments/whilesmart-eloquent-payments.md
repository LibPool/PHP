# whilesmart/eloquent-payments

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Polymorphic payment records for Laravel: gateway-agnostic audit trail attachable to invoices, expenses, subscriptions, or any payable.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
