# getodden/ses-tenants

> 标签: SNS, amazon-ses, dkim, email, laravel, multi-tenant, saas, ses

## 简介

Per-customer sending domains on Amazon SES for Laravel SaaS: DKIM and MAIL FROM verification, SES tenants, a guarded transport and verified SNS events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
