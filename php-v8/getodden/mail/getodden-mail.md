# getodden/mail

> 标签: amp-email, email, email-builder, filament, laravel, mjml, newsletter

## 简介

Responsive, slot-based email builder and compiler for Laravel: modular blocks, MJML/HTML import, merge tags, AMP, plain-text fallback, and pre-send audits.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
