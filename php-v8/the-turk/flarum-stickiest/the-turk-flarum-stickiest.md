# the-turk/flarum-stickiest

> 标签: discussion, flarum, pin, sticky, super-sticky

## 简介

Stick, super stick or tag stick discussions to the top of the list.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
