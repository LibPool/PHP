# leancaptain/lara-email

> 标签: MX, disposable, email, laravel, validation

## 简介

Minimal Laravel validation that rejects example and disposable email domains and requires usable MX records.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
