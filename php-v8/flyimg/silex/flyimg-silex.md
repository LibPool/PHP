# flyimg/silex

> 标签: microframework

## 简介

An adaptation of the Silex micro-framework for Flyimg needs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/flyimg/silex
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/flyimg/Silex.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/flyimg%2Fsilex
