# hellotext/magento

> 标签: adobe-commerce, ecommerce, hellotext, magento, magento2, marketing, sms, whatsapp

## 简介

Connect Magento Open Source stores to Hellotext: storefront activity, customer identity, orders and catalog/customer import.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
