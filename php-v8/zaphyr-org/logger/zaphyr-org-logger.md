# zaphyr-org/logger

> 标签: PSR3, logger, psr-3

## 简介

You want to know what's happening under the hood of your application? ZAPHYR provides a robust PSR-3 logging service.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
