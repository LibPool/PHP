# symkit/bundle-ai-kit

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

AI rules and optional skills for building Symfony bundles (best practices, SOLID, AbstractBundle, quality tooling)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
