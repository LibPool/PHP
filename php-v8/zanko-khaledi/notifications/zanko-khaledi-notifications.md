# zanko-khaledi/notifications

> 标签: async-notifications, laravel, laravel-notifications, laravel-package, laravel-queue, notification-driver, notification-pool, notification-service, notifications, php, queued-notifications

## 简介

A Laravel notification service for synchronous and asynchronous notifications with queued jobs and notification pooling.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
