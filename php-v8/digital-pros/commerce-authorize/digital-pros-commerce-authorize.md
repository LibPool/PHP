# digital-pros/commerce-authorize

> 标签: authorize, commerce, craftcms, omnipay, yii2

## 简介

Authorize.net payment gateway plugin for Craft Commerce 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
