# maer/google-auth

> 标签: google, laravel, oauth2

## 简介

Laravel 4 wrapper for the league/oauth2-client package. Simple white/blacklist specific e-mails or entire domains.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
