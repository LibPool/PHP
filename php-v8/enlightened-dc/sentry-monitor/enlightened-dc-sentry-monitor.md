# enlightened-dc/sentry-monitor

> 标签: exceptions, monitor, monitoring, sentry, time

## 简介

Sentry Monitoring Screen. Display the exceptions on a time line from your projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
