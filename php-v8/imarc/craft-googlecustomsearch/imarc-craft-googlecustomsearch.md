# imarc/craft-googlecustomsearch

> 标签: Craft, addsearch, craft-plugin, craft5, craftcms, google custom search, google site search, site search, vertex ai search

## 简介

A Craft plugin for site search via Google Custom Search, Google Vertex AI Search, or AddSearch.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
