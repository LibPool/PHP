# nails/driver-invoice-stripe

> 标签: Nails, Order Payments Module, Shed Collective, codeigniter, driver, stripe

## 简介

This is the "Stripe" driver for the Nails Orders & Payments module.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
