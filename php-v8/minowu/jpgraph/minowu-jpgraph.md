# minowu/jpgraph

> 标签: chart, data, graph, jpgraph, pie

## 简介

jpGraph, library to make graphs and charts, for chinese character

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

- 官网：http://jpgraph.net/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/minowu/JpGraph.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/minowu%2Fjpgraph

- Composer 安装：`composer require minowu/jpgraph`
- Packagist 仓库：https://repo.packagist.org/p2/minowu/jpgraph.json
- 许可证：QPL 1.0
