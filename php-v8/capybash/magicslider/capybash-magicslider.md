# capybash/magicslider

> 标签: banner, flarum, flarum-extension, hero, image, magic, slider

## 简介

Configurable image slider replacing the WelcomeHero on the forum home page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
