# yotech-ai/twill-cms-redirects

> 标签: cms, laravel, redirects, twill, twill-plugin

## 简介

Redirect management for Twill CMS, with a shared Plugins page for Yotech Twill packages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
