# shahrooz/payment

> 标签: gateway, idpay, laravel, laravel-toman, laraveltoman, payment, toman, zarinpal, آیدی پی, درگاه, زرین پال, پرداخت

## 简介

A simple stable Laravel package to handle popular payment gateways in Iran including ZarinPal and IDPay.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
