# jasbros2568/laravel-360-view

> 标签: 360, 360-view, blade-component, ecommerce, image-sequence, laravel, product-viewer, spin

## 简介

A dependency-free 360° product viewer (image-sequence spin) for Laravel: Blade component, drag / touch / keyboard rotation, autoplay, and helpers to upload and list frames.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
