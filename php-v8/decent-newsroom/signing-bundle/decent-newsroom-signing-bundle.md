# decent-newsroom/signing-bundle

> 标签: nip46, nostr, signing, symfony

## 简介

Server-side Nostr signing orchestration for extension, relay AUTH, and NIP-46 bunker flows.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
