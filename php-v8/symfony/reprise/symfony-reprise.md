# symfony/reprise

> 标签: assets, encore, rsbuild, symfony, vite

## 简介

Integrate Vite and Rsbuild with Symfony, a reprise of Webpack Encore Bundle.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
