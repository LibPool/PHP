> 标签: api, events, http, laravel, php-pusher-server, publish, pusher, realtime, rest, trigger

## 简介

A Pusher bridge for Laravel 5+

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
