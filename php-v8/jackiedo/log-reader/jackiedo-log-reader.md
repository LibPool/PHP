# jackiedo/log-reader

> 标签: Viewer, laravel, log, log-manager, log-reader, log-viewer, logs, reader

## 简介

An easy log reader and management tool for Laravel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
