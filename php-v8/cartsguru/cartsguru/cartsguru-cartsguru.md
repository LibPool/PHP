# cartsguru/cartsguru

> 标签: Marketing Automation, abandoned shopping cart, cart abandonment, conversion rate, ecommerce, email remarketing, retargeting

## 简介

Carts Guru - Multichannel solution to convert abandoned shopping carts into sales.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
