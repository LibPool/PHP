# tmonier/sylius-tier-pricing-plugin

> 标签: b2b, price-breaks, quantity-discount, sylius, sylius-plugin, tier-pricing, volume-pricing, wholesale

## 简介

Volume (tier) pricing for Sylius 2: quantity price breaks per channel, customer group, product or variant, applied in the cart, shown on product pages and in the cart as conditional prices, CSV import/export and API

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
