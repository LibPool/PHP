# tmonier/sylius-purchase-approval-plugin

> 标签: approval-workflow, b2b, company-accounts, order-approval, purchase-approval, spending-limit, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Purchase approval for Sylius 2 B2B company accounts: order and approval limits per member, pending orders held until a company approver approves them (no payment, shipment or invoice before), acknowledgement e-mail, expiry, shop, admin and API

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
