# tmonier/sylius-company-insights-plugin

> 标签: analytics, b2b, company-accounts, dashboard, purchasing, reporting, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Purchasing dashboard for Sylius 2 B2B company accounts: spend, orders, average basket and active buyers of the period with the variation against the previous period, reserved to the company administrators

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
