# tmonier/sylius-punchout-cxml-plugin

> 标签: ariba, b2b, cxml, oci, procurement, punchout, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

B2B PunchOut cXML/OCI connector for Sylius 2: lets your shop act as a PunchOut supplier for procurement systems (Ariba, SAP, Coupa…)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
