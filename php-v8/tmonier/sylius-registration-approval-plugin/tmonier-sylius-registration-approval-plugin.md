# tmonier/sylius-registration-approval-plugin

> 标签: access-control, approval, b2b, customer-account, registration, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Registration approval for Sylius 2: accounts registered on a channel stay pending until an administrator approves or rejects them; pending and rejected customers are treated as anonymous by the channel access plugin; approval and rejection e-mails; no account enumeration

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
