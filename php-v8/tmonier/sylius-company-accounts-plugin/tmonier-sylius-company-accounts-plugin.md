# tmonier/sylius-company-accounts-plugin

> 标签: b2b, company, company-accounts, customer-account, organization, roles, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

B2B company accounts for Sylius 2: companies with members and roles (admin, buyer, approver), company area in the customer account (members, company orders), admin CRUD, shop and admin API, voters, events, and an optional account owner bridge for the order-hold kernel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
