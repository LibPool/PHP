# tmonier/sylius-customer-asset-management-plugin

> 标签: b2b, customer-assets, equipment, spare-parts, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Customer asset management for Sylius 2: customers declare their equipment fleet and the catalogue can be filtered to the compatible spare parts

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
