# tmonier/sylius-agentic-feeds-plugin

> 标签: ChatGpt, JSON-LD, agentic-commerce, google-merchant-center, openai, product-feed, schema-org, stripe, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Product feeds for AI shopping assistants and comparison engines in Sylius 2: Google Merchant Center (XML/TSV), OpenAI product feed (jsonl.gz), Stripe Agentic Commerce catalog (CSV), per channel, locale and currency, with validation reports, stock/price deltas and Product JSON-LD

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
