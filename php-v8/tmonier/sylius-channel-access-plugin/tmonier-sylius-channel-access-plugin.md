# tmonier/sylius-channel-access-plugin

> 标签: access-control, b2b, channel, hidden-prices, login-wall, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Per-channel access mode for Sylius 2 (open, prices hidden, closed): anonymous visitors are redirected to the login on a closed channel, prices and purchase are hidden and refused server-side (storefront and shop API) when prices are hidden, with a configurable allow-list of exempt routes

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
