# tmonier/sylius-trade-credit-plugin

> 标签: b2b, credit-limit, net-30, payment-on-account, payment-terms, sylius, sylius-plugin, trade-credit

## 简介

B2B trade credit (payment on account) for Sylius 2: credit accounts with limits, net terms and end-of-month terms, atomic reservations, statements, admin open items and Factur-X payment terms

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
