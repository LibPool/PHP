# fluidstan/typo3

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

TYPO3 integration for FluidStan: boots TYPO3, registers its Fluid namespaces and resolves EXT: paths

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
