# troymccabe/bbyopen

> 标签: bby, bbyopen, best buy, geek squad, remix

## 简介

An adaptation of http://code.google.com/p/bestbuy-service-remix/ for making BBYOpen calls.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
