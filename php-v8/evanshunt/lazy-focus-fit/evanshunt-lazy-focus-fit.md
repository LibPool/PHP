# evanshunt/lazy-focus-fit

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Template methods to quickly make use of FocusPoint, LazySizes, and Object-fit

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
