# c10d/craft-recaptcha

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craft-recaptcha, craftcms, recaptcha

## 简介

Integrate reCAPTCHA v3 validation into your forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
