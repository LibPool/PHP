# codemonauts/craft-asset-autoversioning

> 标签: Craft, assets, cache, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

A Twig extension for Craft CMS that helps you cache-bust your assets.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
