# x-laravel/followable

> 标签: Follow, Followers, eloquent, followable, laravel, polymorphic

## 简介

Follows for Eloquent models with polymorphic followers, follow requests, mutual follows and cleanup of orphaned follows.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
