# nexus-cms/laravel

> 标签: api, cms, headless, laravel, nexus-cms

## 简介

Headless Laravel content store for Nexus CMS: REST API, edit-mode tokens, versioning, and force-update. No Filament dependency.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
