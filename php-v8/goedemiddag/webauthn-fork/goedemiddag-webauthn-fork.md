# goedemiddag/webauthn-fork

> 标签: darkghosthunter, laravel, webauthn

## 简介

Authenticate users with just their device, fingerprint or biometric data. Goodbye passwords!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
