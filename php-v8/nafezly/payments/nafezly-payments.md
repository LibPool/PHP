# nafezly/payments

> 标签: fawry, hyperpay, kashier, online payments, paymob, paypal

## 简介

Payment helper for Paypal, Paymob, Kashier, Hyperpay and Fawry

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
