# formatsoft/content-reminder

> 标签: Editorial Workflow, TYPO3 CMS, content maintenance, reminder

## 简介

Content Reminder - Reminders for TYPO3 pages: due dates, recurrence, assignment and archive to keep content up to date

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
