# azaharizaman/nexus-tax

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Framework-agnostic tax calculation engine with multi-jurisdiction support, temporal rate resolution, hierarchical tax structure, reverse charge mechanism, economic nexus determination, partial exemptions, and place-of-supply rules for cross-border services

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
