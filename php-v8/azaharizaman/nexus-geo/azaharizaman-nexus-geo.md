# azaharizaman/nexus-geo

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Framework-agnostic geographic and location services for the Nexus ERP system with cost-optimized geocoding and geospatial calculations

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
