# xmarcos/carbon-client-service-provider

> 标签: Metrics, carbon, graphite, log, provider, silex

## 简介

A Silex Service Provider for Carbon (Graphite's backend)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
