# bhuvidya/craft-btn-shortcodes

> 标签: Craft, button shortcodes, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Button shortcode support in Craft v3.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
