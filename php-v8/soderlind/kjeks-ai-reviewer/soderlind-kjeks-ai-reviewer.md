# soderlind/kjeks-ai-reviewer

> 标签: ai, consent-management, cookie-consent, gdpr, kjeks, privacy, tracker-classification, wordpress, wordpress-plugin, wp-ai

## 简介

AI-assisted category suggestions for unreviewed trackers in Kjeks: proposes a consent category for each newly discovered cookie or script.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
