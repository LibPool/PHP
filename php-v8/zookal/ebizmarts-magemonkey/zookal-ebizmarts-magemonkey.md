# zookal/ebizmarts-magemonkey

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

FULL, FREE and SUPPORTED integration with MailChimp Email Marketing and Mandrill Transactional Emails for Magento.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
