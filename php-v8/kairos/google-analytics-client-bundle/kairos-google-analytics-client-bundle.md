# kairos/google-analytics-client-bundle

> 标签: client, ga collect data, google-analytics, php-ga

## 简介

Symfony 2 bundle. Get data from google analytics

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
