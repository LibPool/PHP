# marcinwolnyeu/slopshape-php

> 标签: ai content detection, ai detection, ai detector, ai text detection, ai-generated text, discourse analysis, explainable ai, llm, nlp, slop, slopshape, text analysis

## 简介

Explainable AI text detector for PHP: scores how likely an English text is AI/LLM-generated from discourse structure, with per-feature evidence. No dependencies; offline (reduced accuracy) or with any LLM. Based on SlopShape (arXiv:2609.15369).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
