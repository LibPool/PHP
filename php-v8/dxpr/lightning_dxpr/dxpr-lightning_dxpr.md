# dxpr/lightning_dxpr

> 标签: DXPR, Lightning, acquia, drupal, marketing

## 简介

Drupal distribution with DXPR Marketing features, based on Acquia Lightning distribution

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
