> 标签: BI, BI-exchange, bank-indonesia, currency, exchange, laravel, nilai, nilai-tukar, rates, valas

## 简介

A package to fetch exchange rates from Bank Indonesia

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
