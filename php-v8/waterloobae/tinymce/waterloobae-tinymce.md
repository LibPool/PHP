# waterloobae/tinymce

> 标签: MathJax, filament, laravel, latex, livewire, rich-editor, tinymce

## 简介

TinyMCE and LaTeX support for Laravel, Livewire, and Filament

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
