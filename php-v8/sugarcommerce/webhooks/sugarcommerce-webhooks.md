# sugarcommerce/webhooks

> 标签: hmac, replay-protection, retry, signature, verification, webhooks

## 简介

Webhook signing, verification and retry scheduling. Stripe-style t=,v1= signatures with replay protection, constant-time comparison, and a retry policy that distinguishes the receiver's problems from your own. Sends through sugarcommerce/http-client.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
