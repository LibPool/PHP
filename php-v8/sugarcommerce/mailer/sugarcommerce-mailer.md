# sugarcommerce/mailer

> 标签: cloudflare, email, mailer, mime, postmark, resend, ses, smtp, transactional

## 简介

Transactional email for self-hosted PHP. One Message, many transports: Postmark, Resend, Amazon SES, Cloudflare Email, SMTP, plus log and in-memory for development. Full MIME serialisation with inline images, attachments and RFC 2047 headers. Header-injection safe. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
