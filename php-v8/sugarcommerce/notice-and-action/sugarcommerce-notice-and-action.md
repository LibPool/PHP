# sugarcommerce/notice-and-action

> 标签: dmca, dsa, moderation, notice-and-action, statement-of-reasons, takedown, trust-and-safety

## 简介

The structure a hosting service's notice-and-action process needs: the elements a report or a copyright notice must have before it can be acted on (EU Digital Services Act Article 16, US DMCA section 512(c)(3)), the statement of reasons a restriction must come with (DSA Article 17), and public references for reports. Returns codes, never wording, so every sentence stays yours to translate. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
