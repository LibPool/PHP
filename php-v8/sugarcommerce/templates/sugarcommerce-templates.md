# sugarcommerce/templates

> 标签: blade, escaping, php, templates, view

## 简介

A small Blade-like template engine for plain PHP: auto-escaping by default, layouts and sections, includes, and compiled output cached to disk. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
