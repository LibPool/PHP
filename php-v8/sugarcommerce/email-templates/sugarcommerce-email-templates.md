# sugarcommerce/email-templates

> 标签: components, email, html-email, layouts, templates, transactional

## 简介

Declarative transactional email rendering: layouts, operator-editable bodies, and typed components. Tags are escaped for the context they land in, and an unresolved tag never reaches a customer. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
