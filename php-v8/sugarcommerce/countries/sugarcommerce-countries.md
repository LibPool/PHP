# sugarcommerce/countries

> 标签: countries, country-codes, eu, flags, iso-3166, sanctions, vat

## 简介

ISO 3166-1 country data: codes, names, emoji flags, EU membership, sanctioned and elevated-risk territories. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
