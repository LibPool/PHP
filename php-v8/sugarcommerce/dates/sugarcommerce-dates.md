# sugarcommerce/dates

> 标签: clock, date, date-range, relative-time, reporting, time, timezone, utc

## 简介

UTC-first date handling: freezable clock, safe timestamp parsing, relative times, and reporting ranges with exclusive ends. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
