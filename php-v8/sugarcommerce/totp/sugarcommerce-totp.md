# sugarcommerce/totp

> 标签: 2fa, MFA, authenticator, base32, hotp, otp, rfc4226, rfc6238, totp, two-factor

## 简介

TOTP (RFC 6238) and HOTP (RFC 4226) two-factor authentication codes. Zero dependencies, verified against the official RFC test vectors. Works with Google Authenticator, 1Password, Bitwarden and Authy.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
