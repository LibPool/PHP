# rapidez/postcode

> 标签: address-lookup, postcode, rapidez

## 简介

Generic postcode/address lookup for Rapidez, with pluggable drivers for Postcode.eu, Pro6pp and Postcodeservice

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
