# pablorsk/laravel5-mail-assertions

> 标签: email, laravel, library, phpunit, spinen

## 简介

PHPUnit mail assertions for testing email in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
