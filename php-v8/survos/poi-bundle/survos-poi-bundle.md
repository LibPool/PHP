# survos/poi-bundle

> 标签: OpenStreetMap, geo, nominatim, poi, symfony

## 简介

Points of interest (churches, schools, shops, parks, ...) resolved from OpenStreetMap/Nominatim within a known geographic context.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
