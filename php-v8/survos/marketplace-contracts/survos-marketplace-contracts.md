# survos/marketplace-contracts

> 标签: MercadoLibre, commerce, contracts, ebay, listing, marketplace

## 简介

Provider-neutral contracts and models for listing items on online marketplaces (eBay, Mercado Libre, ...). Framework-agnostic: no Symfony kernel, no container, no HTTP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
