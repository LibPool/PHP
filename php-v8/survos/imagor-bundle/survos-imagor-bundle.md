# survos/imagor-bundle

> 标签: blurhash, image, imagor, resize, symfony, thumbhash, thumbnail

## 简介

Symfony bundle for signed imagor URLs (presets, crops, meta) against the Survos imagor build

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
