# survos/classifier-bundle

> 标签: classification, jev, tags, taxonomy, typesafe

## 简介

Assign controlled labels to text with interchangeable strategies (rules, Jev), and benchmark them against each other

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
