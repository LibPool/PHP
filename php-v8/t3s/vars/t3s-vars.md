# t3s/vars

> 标签: CKEditor, Google Business Profile, TYPO3 CMS, TYPO3 Extension, address, global variables, google maps, placeholder, rte, variables

## 简介

Global variables for TYPO3 v14: maintain company, address, phone, email etc. once as site settings and use them as {{placeholders}} in RTE, Fluid and TypoScript. CKEditor 5 dropdown, automatic tel/mailto/URL links and an "Address" content element with schema.org markup, opening hours, social media links, vCard download and optional Google Maps (route button, map with two-click privacy). Optional JSON-LD and per-language values.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
