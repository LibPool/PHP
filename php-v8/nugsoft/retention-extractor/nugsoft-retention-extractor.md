# nugsoft/retention-extractor

> 标签: churn, laravel, nugsoft, retention

## 简介

Pushes client activity and subscription data from a Laravel product into Retention Intel, and applies the licence decisions it sends back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
