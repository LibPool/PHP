# stephenlake/nova-snowball

> 标签: laravel, nova, theme

## 简介

A beautiful contrast of dark blue and white packaged for Laravel Nova as a theme.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
