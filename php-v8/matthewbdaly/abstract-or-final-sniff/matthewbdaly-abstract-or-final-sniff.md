# matthewbdaly/abstract-or-final-sniff

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A CodeSniffer sniff that requires that all classes be either abstract or final

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
