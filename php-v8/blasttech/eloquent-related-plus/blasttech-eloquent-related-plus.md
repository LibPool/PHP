# blasttech/eloquent-related-plus

> 标签: eloquent, join, laravel, model, orderby

## 简介

Adds search and order functionality to Laravel Eloquent related models

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
