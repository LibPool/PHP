# clapp/omnipay-otphu

> 标签: hungary, magyar, merchant, omnipay, otp, otpbank, pay, payment, purchase

## 简介

Experimental Omnipay v2.5 Payment Gateway for https://www.otpbank.hu

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
