# au/notification_subjects

> 标签: AU, elgg, notification subjects, notifications, plugin, subject

## 简介

Provides more descriptive email/message subjects for object notifications

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
