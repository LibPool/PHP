# au/notifications_email_disable

> 标签: AU, elgg, email, notifications, notifications email disable, plugin

## 简介

Adds an admin widget to disable email notifications to users with invalid addresses

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
