# gumphp/parse-video

> 标签: bilibili, chinese-social-media, douyin, kuaishou, parser, video, weibo

## 简介

A PHP library to parse video information from various Chinese social media platforms

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
