# backboneit/contao-multimedia-jwplayer

> 标签: contao, multimedia, player, video

## 简介

JW-Player 5 integration into backboneIT multimedia extension for Contao CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
