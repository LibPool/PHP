# mailosaur/mailosaur

> 标签: automation, email, mailosaur, sms, testing

## 简介

The Mailosaur PHP library lets you integrate email and SMS testing into your continuous integration process.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
