# teltek/pumukit-expired-video-bundle

> 标签: bundle, expiration, expired, pumukit, symfony, teltek, videos

## 简介

Bundle for PuMuKIT to configure the automatic expiration of a video

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
