# roundly-consulting/shops-for-laravel

> 标签: ecommerce, laravel, orders, products, roundly-consulting, shops

## 简介

Manage single or multiple shops with products, categories, and orders in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
