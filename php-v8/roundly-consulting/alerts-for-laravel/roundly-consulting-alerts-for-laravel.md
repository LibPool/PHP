# roundly-consulting/alerts-for-laravel

> 标签: alerts, health-checks, laravel, monitoring, notifications, roundly-consulting

## 简介

Schedule recurring health checks against any notifiable model, send throttled alert notifications when they fail and dispatch events when they fail, escalate or recover.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
