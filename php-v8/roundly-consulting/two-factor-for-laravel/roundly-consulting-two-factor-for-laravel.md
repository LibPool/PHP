# roundly-consulting/two-factor-for-laravel

> 标签: 2fa, Authentication, MFA, laravel, otp, roundly-consulting, totp, two-factor

## 简介

Native RFC 6238 TOTP two-factor authentication for Laravel — encrypted secrets, single-use recovery codes and replay protection with zero third-party crypto.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
