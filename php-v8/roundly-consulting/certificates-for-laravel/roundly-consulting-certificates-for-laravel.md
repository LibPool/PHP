# roundly-consulting/certificates-for-laravel

> 标签: cert-manager, certificates, kubernetes, laravel, roundly-consulting, tls

## 简介

Manage and provision TLS certificates for your domains from Laravel via pluggable providers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
