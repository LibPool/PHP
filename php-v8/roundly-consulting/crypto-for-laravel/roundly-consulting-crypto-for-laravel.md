# roundly-consulting/crypto-for-laravel

> 标签: COSE, JOSE, JWS, aead, aes-256-gcm, base64url, crypto, encryption, hmac, hotp, laravel, roundly-consulting, totp, webauthn

## 简介

Native, tested cryptographic and encoding primitives for Laravel: JWS/JOSE, TOTP/HOTP, WebAuthn signature verification, HMAC, authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM), CSPRNG tokens, and codecs — zero-config, à la carte.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
