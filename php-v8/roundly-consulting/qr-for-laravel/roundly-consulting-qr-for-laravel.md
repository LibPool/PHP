# roundly-consulting/qr-for-laravel

> 标签: 2fa, IBAN, bysquare, epc, girocode, laravel, otpauth, pay-by-square, qr, qr-code, roundly-consulting, sepa, svg

## 简介

Native QR codes for Laravel: an ISO/IEC 18004 encoder with optimal segmentation, one optimised SVG renderer, and typed payloads for otpauth 2FA, EPC SEPA transfers and PAY by square — zero third-party dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
