# roundly-consulting/tickets-for-laravel

> 标签: helpdesk, issue-tracker, laravel, roundly-consulting, service-desk, support-desk, ticketing, tickets

## 简介

Headless helpdesk ticketing core: categories, swappable statuses, replies and internal notes, assignment, priorities, due dates, custom fields, guest requesters and optional projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
