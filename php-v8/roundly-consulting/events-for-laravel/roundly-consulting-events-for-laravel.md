# roundly-consulting/events-for-laravel

> 标签: JSON-LD, check-in, event-management, events, geolocation, iCalendar, ics, laravel, roundly-consulting, rsvp, venues, waitlist

## 简介

Real-world events for any Eloquent app: reusable venues with near-me search, ticket tiers priced in Money, RSVPs with a race-free waitlist, QR check-in, co-hosts and invite-only events, iCalendar feeds and schema.org JSON-LD.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
