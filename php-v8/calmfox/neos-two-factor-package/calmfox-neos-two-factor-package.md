# calmfox/neos-two-factor-package

> 标签: 2fa, Neos, flow, passkey, security, totp, two-factor, webauthn

## 简介

Two-factor authentication for the Neos CMS backend: a passkey (fingerprint, face or device PIN) or an authenticator app (TOTP), with a policy and per-user management in the backend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
