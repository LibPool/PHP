# calmfox/inpost-sylius

> 标签: inpost, paczkomaty, parcel-locker, shipping, shipx, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

InPost (ShipX) for Sylius 2: parcel locker selection in checkout on the official map (Geowidget) or a list of nearest points, one-click dispatch from the order page, labels and tracking numbers. No changes to your shop entities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
