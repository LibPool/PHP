# fnn/fnn-powermail-altcha

> 标签: gdpr, typo3

## 简介

Altcha Captcha for Powermail - Accessible and GDPR compliant proof-of-work (pow) captcha for powermail, no external service required

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
