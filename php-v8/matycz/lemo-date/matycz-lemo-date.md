# matycz/lemo-date

> 标签: czech, date, diff, easter, holidays, public holidays, slovak

## 简介

Date utilities: difference between dates in days, months and years, and Czech and Slovak public holidays with their history

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
