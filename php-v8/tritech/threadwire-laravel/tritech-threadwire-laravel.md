# tritech/threadwire-laravel

> 标签: laravel, laravel-package, otp, standard-webhooks, threadwire, webhooks, whatsapp, whatsapp-api, whatsapp-otp

## 简介

Official Laravel package for Threadwire: send WhatsApp messages through your own linked numbers with built-in ban protection, verify phone numbers with WhatsApp, and receive signed webhooks as Laravel events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
