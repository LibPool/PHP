# bpmore/statamic-reel

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, captions, statamic, transcript, wcag, webvtt

## 简介

Caption and transcript governance for the video and audio on a Statamic site. Reel does not generate captions: it checks the ones you have, edits them, and refuses to publish media without them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
