# gp247/s-cart

> 标签: Free Shopping cart, Laravel marketplace, Laravel multiple store, Laravel multiple vendor, S-Cart, SCart ecommerce, SCart open source, gp247, laravel ecommerce

## 简介

Free Laravel e-commerce for business: shopping cart, cms content, and more...

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
