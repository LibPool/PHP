# kfirba/hebdate

> 标签: convert date, date, gregorian to hebrew, hebrew, hebrew to gregorian, jewish

## 简介

A library to convert jewish dates to gregorian dates and vice versa

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
