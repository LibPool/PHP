# laborb/statamic-bunny-stream

> 标签: Bunny.net, hosting, statamic-addon, streaming, video

## 简介

Manage and embed Bunny Stream videos in Statamic.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
