# bensomething/craft-placard

> 标签: Craft, OG Image, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, imagick, open graph, seo, share image, social card, social image, twitter card

## 简介

Open Graph images for every entry, made on your own server. No Chrome, no external service.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
