# serfix/content-ai-laravel

> 标签: blog, content, content-ai, laravel, seo, serfix

## 简介

Receive, store and publish Serfix Content AI articles in a Laravel app — SEO metas, BlogPosting schema, self-hosted images.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
