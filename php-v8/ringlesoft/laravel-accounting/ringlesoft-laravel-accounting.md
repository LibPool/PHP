# ringlesoft/laravel-accounting

> 标签: Accounting, charts of accounts, finance, ifrs, laravel, laravel-accounting, ledger, ringlesoft

## 简介

A double-entry accounting engine for Laravel - attach any model via a trait, post through templates or raw vouchers, and get balanced, immutable journal entries with multi-currency, multi-tenancy, fiscal periods, and financial reports built in.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
