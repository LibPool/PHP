# wismolabs/tracking

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

WISMOlabs Integration for Magento 2.4.7+, providing seamless order tracking, shipping notifications, and post-purchase marketing management.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
