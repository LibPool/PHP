# smensulaiman/japanese-year-converter

> 标签: Era, calendar, converter, date, gregorian, japan, php, seireki, wareki

## 简介

Convert Japanese era (Wareki) years to Gregorian (Seireki) years

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
