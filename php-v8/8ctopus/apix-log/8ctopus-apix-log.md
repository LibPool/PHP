# 8ctopus/apix-log

> 标签: apix, log, logger, logging, psr, psr-3, psr-log, tracker, tracking

## 简介

Minimalist, thin and fast PSR-3 compliant (multi-bucket) logger.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
