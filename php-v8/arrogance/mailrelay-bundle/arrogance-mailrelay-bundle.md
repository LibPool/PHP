# arrogance/mailrelay-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, bundle, mailrelay, smtp

## 简介

A bundle to send, track and manage emails sent with Mailrelay.com

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
