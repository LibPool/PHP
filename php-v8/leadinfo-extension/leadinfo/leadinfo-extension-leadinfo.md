# leadinfo-extension/leadinfo

> 标签: analytics, extension, lead, leadinfo, site-analytics, tracking-code, typo3

## 简介

Leadinfo enables you to see real-time which companies are visiting your website and what pages they’re viewing to increase your sales.3

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
