# pg-momik/no-nepali-profanity

> 标签: bad-words, devanagari, filter, moderation, nepali, profanity

## 简介

Profanity matcher for English, Romanized Nepali and Devanagari Nepali: leetspeak, stretched and spelled-out letters, Nepali postpositions, with care not to block real names.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
