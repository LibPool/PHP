# otatechie/filament-kinetics

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, filament-theme, filamentphp, laravel, theme

## 简介

A minimal theme for Filament 4 and 5.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
