# vulpo/seo-and-geo

> 标签: JSON-LD, Sitemap, llms-txt, redirects, seo, statamic, structured-data

## 简介

All-in-one SEO toolkit for Statamic: meta tags, sitemap, redirects, structured data, robots.txt and llms.txt.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
