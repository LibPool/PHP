# vulpo/cookies-and-consent

> 标签: consent-mode, cookie-consent, cookies, gdpr, privacy, statamic

## 简介

Cookie consent for Statamic: unlimited categories, individually toggleable services, Google Consent Mode v2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
