# thejoshsmith/commerce-xero

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, xero

## 简介

Send invoices to Xero including contacts, payments and even inventory updates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
