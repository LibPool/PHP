# mariodevv/nativephp-health-connect

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Android Health Connect for NativePHP Mobile: read steps, distance, calories, heart rate, weight, sleep and exercise, with deduplicated aggregates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
