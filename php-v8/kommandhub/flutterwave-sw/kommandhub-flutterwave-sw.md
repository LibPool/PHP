# kommandhub/flutterwave-sw

> 标签: Nigeria, africa, bank-transfer, checkout, ecommerce, flutterwave, mobile-money, payment, payment-gateway, refund, shopware, shopware-payment, shopware-plugin, shopware6, webhook

## 简介

Professional Shopware 6 integration for Flutterwave: accept secure payments via cards, bank transfers, and mobile money. Expand your reach across Africa with this reliable checkout solution.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
