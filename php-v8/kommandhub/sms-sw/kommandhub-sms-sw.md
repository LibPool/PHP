# kommandhub/sms-sw

> 标签: Africastalking, africa, sendexa, shopware, shopware6, sms, termii, twilio

## 简介

Transactional SMS for Shopware 6, sent from the Flow Builder alongside your emails, with multi-provider routing for Termii, Sendexa, Africa's Talking and Twilio.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
