# apps-inteligentes/email-tracking

> 标签: email-tracking, henryavila, laravel

## 简介

Track e-mail delivery with Mailgun Hooks. All data are stored in the database on Email model

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
