# onixsib/yii2-net-address-validators

> 标签: MAC, extension, ipv4, ipv6, validator, validators, yii2

## 简介

Validate IPv4, IPv6 and MAC for Yii 2 Framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
