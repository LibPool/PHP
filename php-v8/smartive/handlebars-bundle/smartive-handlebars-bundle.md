# smartive/handlebars-bundle

> 标签: handlebars, templating, twig

## 简介

This bundle integrates rendering of Handlebars templates into Symfony2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
