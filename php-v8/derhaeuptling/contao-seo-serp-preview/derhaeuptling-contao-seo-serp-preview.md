# derhaeuptling/contao-seo-serp-preview

> 标签: SERP, contao, preview, seo

## 简介

Contao SEO SERP Preview - shows how pages, news and events could look like in search engines.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
