# otim-otim/bookkeeper

> 标签: Accounting, bookkeeping, chart-of-accounts, double-entry, general-ledger, laravel, ledger, sacco

## 简介

A complete double-entry accounting engine for Laravel: chart of accounts, immutable journals, periods and closing, subledgers, invoices, bills, payments, tax, fixed assets, budgets, tills, bank reconciliation and financial statements.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
