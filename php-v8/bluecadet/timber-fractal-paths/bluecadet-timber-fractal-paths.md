# bluecadet/timber-fractal-paths

> 标签: fractal, plugin, timber, twig, wordpress

## 简介

A custom twig loader for Timber and Fractal

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
