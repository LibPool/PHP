# hamaka/silverstripe-matomo-ai-tracking

> 标签: ai, analytics, bot-tracking, chatbot, hamaka, matomo, middleware, silverstripe

## 简介

Track AI chatbot requests (ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User, ...) in Matomo AI Insights via server-side middleware.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
