# coreproc/nova-echo

> 标签: Broadcasting, WebSockets, echo, laravel, nova, real-time, socket-io

## 简介

Adds Laravel Echo with your broadcast configuration to your Laravel Nova app

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
