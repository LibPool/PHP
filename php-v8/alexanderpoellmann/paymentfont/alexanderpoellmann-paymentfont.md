# alexanderpoellmann/paymentfont

> 标签: font, icon, payment, webfont

## 简介

PaymentFont - A sleek webfont for your favourite payment operators and methods

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
