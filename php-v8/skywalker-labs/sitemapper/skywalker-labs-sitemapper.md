# skywalker-labs/sitemapper

> 标签: Sitemap, generator, google, google-news, html, indexing, laravel, php, search-engine-optimization, seo, sitemap-html, sitemap-index, sitemap-xml, sitemaps, symfony, webmaster-tools, xml

## 简介

A powerful, framework-agnostic Sitemap generator for PHP, Laravel, and Symfony. Create XML, HTML, and Google News sitemaps easily to boost your SEO.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
