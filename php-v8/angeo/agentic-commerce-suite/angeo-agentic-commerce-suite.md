# angeo/agentic-commerce-suite

> 标签: WebMCP, acp, adobe-commerce, aeo, agentic-commerce, ai-agents, answer-engine-optimization, b2b, brand-visibility, hyva, llms-txt, mage-os, magento2, mcp, product-descriptions, reorder, ucp

## 简介

One install for the whole Angeo suite for Magento 2: makes a store readable, discoverable and usable by AI agents — llms.txt, AI-bot robots.txt, rich JSON-LD, AEO audit, AI brand visibility, AI product descriptions, OpenAI product feed, UCP profile, catalog and checkout, MCP server, MCP checkout and reorder, WebMCP and AI agent activity.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
