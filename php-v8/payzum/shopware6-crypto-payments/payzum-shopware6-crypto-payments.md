# payzum/shopware6-crypto-payments

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Accept crypto and stablecoins (USDC/USDT, multi-chain) in Shopware 6 with Payzum. Non-custodial.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
