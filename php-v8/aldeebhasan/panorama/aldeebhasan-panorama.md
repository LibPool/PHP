# aldeebhasan/panorama

> 标签: Hasan Deeb, analytics, laravel, panorama, reporting, reports

## 简介

One wide view across every source: define filterable Laravel reports once, then aggregate, cache and generate them anywhere.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
