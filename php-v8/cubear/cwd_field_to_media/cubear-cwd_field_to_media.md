# cubear/cwd_field_to_media

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Drupal 8 or 9 custom module that creates a drush commands for migrating a field of a content type to a media field.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
