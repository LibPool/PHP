# faisal50x/blade-ionicons

> 标签: blade, blade-ionicons, faisal50x, ionicons, laravel

## 简介

A package to easily make use of ionicons in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
