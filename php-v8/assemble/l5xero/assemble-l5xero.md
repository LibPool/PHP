# assemble/l5xero

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Integrate into Xero financial system, to pull invoices and manage payments made. wrapper of calcinai/xero-php.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
