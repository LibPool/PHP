# iamtime/uzpay

> 标签: Humo, Uzbekistan, click, fiscal, ikpu, mastercard, mxik, octo, paycom, payme, payments, paynet, recurring, subscriptions, uzcard, uzum, visa

## 简介

Payme, Click, Uzum, Paynet and Octo (Visa, Mastercard) for Uzbekistan shops: merchant callbacks, Payme saved cards and recurring payments (Subscribe API), checkout links, fiscal receipt lines, a sandbox emulator.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
