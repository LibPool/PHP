# jambagecom/mbi-products-categories

> 标签: extension, shop, tt_products, typo3

## 简介

Hierarchical categories for tt_products. Works with tt_products and other tables.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
