# monaz/symfonyjdate

> 标签: Jalali, datetime, farsi, jalalidate, jdatetime, persian, shamsi, symfony, time

## 简介

Jalali (Shamsi) DateTime for Symfony2, Supports year higher than 2038

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
