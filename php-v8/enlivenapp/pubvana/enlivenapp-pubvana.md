# enlivenapp/pubvana

> 标签: blog, cms, codeigniter4, shield

## 简介

Pubvana CMS v2 — a modern blogging and content management system built on CodeIgniter 4 and Shield.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
