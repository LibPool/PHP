# grobolof/bot-payload

> 标签: bot, formatter, mapper, payload, telegram, vk, webhook

## 简介

Hydration of Telegram and VK bot webhooks and formatting of outgoing messages and keyboards

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
