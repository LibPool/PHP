# schnipseljagd/testdatabuilder

> 标签: mock, mocking, test, testing

## 简介

Helps you to build your mocks, stubs and test data in a nice oop way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
