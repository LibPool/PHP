# spyck/spreadsheet

> 标签: csv, excel, filter, parse, sdk, spreadsheet, tsv, txt

## 简介

Simple PHP tool for parsing CSV or Excel spreadsheets

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
