# wmd/craft-corvus-pay

> 标签: Craft, commerce, corvus, corvuspay, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, croatia, gateway, installments, payment

## 简介

CorvusPay payment gateway for Craft Commerce: hosted checkout, installments, refunds and partial refunds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
