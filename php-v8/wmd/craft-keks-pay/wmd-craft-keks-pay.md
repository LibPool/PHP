# wmd/craft-keks-pay

> 标签: Craft, commerce, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, croatia, gateway, keks, keks-pay, payment, qr-code, wallet

## 简介

KEKS Pay mobile wallet for Craft Commerce: app redirect or QR code at checkout, bank callback, refunds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
