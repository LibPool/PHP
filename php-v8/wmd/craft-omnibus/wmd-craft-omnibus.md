# wmd/craft-omnibus

> 标签: Craft, cjenik, cms, commerce, craftcms, omnibus, price history, sidrena cijena

## 简介

Omnibus and sidrena cijena for Craft Commerce: price history per variant, the lowest price in the 30 days before a reduction, the Croatian anchor price (NN 101/2026), and the daily machine-readable price list.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
