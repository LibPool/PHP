# ntavelis/auth-email

> 标签: AuthEmail, laravel

## 简介

Generates Email Authentication in Laravel >= 5.4 on top of artisan's make:auth command.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
