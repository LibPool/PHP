# hydrat/grogu-cms

> 标签: Hydrat, grogu-cms, laravel

## 简介

Quickly mount a Content Management System on top of Filament on Laravel. Chose your front stack, or go Headless.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
