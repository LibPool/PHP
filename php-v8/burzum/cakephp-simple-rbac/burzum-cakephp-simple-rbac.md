# burzum/cakephp-simple-rbac

> 标签: auth, authorization, rbac

## 简介

A simple to use and configure RBAC authorization adapter for CakePHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
