# vpsbg/laravel-pgp-mailer

> 标签: PGP, encryption, laravel, mail, mime, openpgp, vpsbg

## 简介

PGP encryption and signing of Laravel outbound mail.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
