# fuzzphony/fuzzphony

> 标签: doctrine, full-text, fuzzy, pg_trgm, postgresql, search, symfony

## 简介

Fuzzy search, in perfect harmony with your database. PostgreSQL-native full-text + trigram search for PHP and Symfony, without an extra service.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
