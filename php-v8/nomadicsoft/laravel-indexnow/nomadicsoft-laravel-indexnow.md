# nomadicsoft/laravel-indexnow

> 标签: indexnow, laravel, search-engine, seo, yandex

## 简介

Production-minded IndexNow notifications for Laravel, with queues, batching, deduplication, multi-host support, and diagnostics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
