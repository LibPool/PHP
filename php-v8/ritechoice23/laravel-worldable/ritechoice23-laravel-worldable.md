> 标签: Relationships, cities, continents, countries, currencies, eloquent, geo, geography, i18n, internationalization, l10n, languages, laravel, localization, location, model, polymorphic, ritechoice23, states, timezones, trait, world-data, worldable

## 简介

Make your Laravel application location-aware in seconds. Worldable is a package that provides a simple and efficient way to add Country, State, City, Currency, Language, and Timezone relationships to your laravel application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
