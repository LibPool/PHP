# trendyminds/craft-palette

> 标签: Craft, cmdk, craft cms, craft plugin, palette, spotlight

## 简介

A command palette to easily jump to specific areas within Craft

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
