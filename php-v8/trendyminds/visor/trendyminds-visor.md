# trendyminds/visor

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, dashboard, utility, visor

## 简介

A simple admin overlay to get to the relevant areas of the Craft CMS control panel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
