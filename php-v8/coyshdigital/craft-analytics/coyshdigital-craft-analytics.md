# coyshdigital/craft-analytics

> 标签: Craft, analytics, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, gdpr, privacy

## 简介

Privacy-first, consent-aware analytics for Craft CMS. First-party, cookieless by default, and it never sends your visitors' data anywhere.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
