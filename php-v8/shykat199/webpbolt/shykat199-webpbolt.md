# shykat199/webpbolt

> 标签: Webp, compression, converter, gd, image, image-conversion, jpg-to-webp, laravel, optimization, php, png-to-webp, watermark

## 简介

Convert JPG, PNG, GIF and BMP images to WebP fast. Lightweight, zero dependencies, powered by PHP GD.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
