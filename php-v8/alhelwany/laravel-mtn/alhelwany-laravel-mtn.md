# alhelwany/laravel-mtn

> 标签: Alhelwany, laravel, laravel-mtn

## 简介

A simple Laravel notification Channel that utilizes MTN Syria services to send SMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
