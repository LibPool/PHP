# vitordiniz22/craft-lens

> 标签: OCR, a11y, accessibility, ai, alt-text, alt-text-generator, asset-management, auto-tagging, craft-plugin, craftcms, duplicate-detection, face-detection, focal-point, image-analysis, image-quality, image-recognition, image-search, image-seo, image-tagging, logo-detection, nsfw-detection, watermark-detection

## 简介

Generates alt text, titles, and focal points for every image, flags quality issues, and makes your library searchable by what's in each photo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
