# d3yii2/yii2-datetime

> 标签: date, datetime, time, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 extension that adds helpers and behaviors for working with date and time

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
