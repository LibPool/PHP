# alpinedigital/craft-cookie-notice

> 标签: Craft, cms, cookies, craft-plugin, craftcms, notice

## 简介

A Craft CMS plugin that gives you the cookie notice functionality

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
