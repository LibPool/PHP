# omz13/kirby3-suncyclepages

> 标签: embargo, kirby3, plugin, sunrise, sunset

## 简介

kirby3 plugin to embargo pages until sunrise date and withdraw at sunset date

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
