# zerofront/module-zero-bought-together

> 标签: bought-together, cross-sell, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: "Often bought together" on the product page, from the orders: picks with checkboxes, a total and one Add to Cart.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
