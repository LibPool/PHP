# zerofront/module-zero-social-login

> 标签: magento, magento-open-source, magento2, oauth, openid-connect, social-login, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: sign in and sign up with Google, Apple or Facebook, without third-party libraries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
