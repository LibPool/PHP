# zerofront/module-zero-free-shipping-bar

> 标签: cart, free-shipping, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: "Add $12.50 more for free shipping" with a progress bar in the mini-cart, the cart and the checkout summary.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
