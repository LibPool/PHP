# zerofront/module-zero-unit-price

> 标签: base-price, eu, grundpreis, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, unit-price, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: the price per kilogram, litre, metre or piece under every price, as EU price indication rules ask (per 100 g / 100 ml for small packs).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
