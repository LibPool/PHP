# zerofront/module-zero-product-attachments

> 标签: attachments, downloads, magento, magento-open-source, magento2, product-files, zero

## 简介

Zero for Magento Open Source: files on product pages (manuals, data sheets, certificates): attached by SKU in the admin, a Downloads section with kind and size.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
