# pushery/billing-for-laravel

> 标签: billing, cashier, datev, dunning, e-invoicing, invoices, laravel, metered-billing, payments, saas, stripe, subscriptions, usage-based-billing, xrechnung

## 简介

Provider-neutral billing for Laravel: subscriptions, invoices, metered usage, dunning, tax and e-invoicing. Stripe-first, on provider-neutral contracts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
