# atldays/laravel-eloquent-join-relation

> 标签: atldays, eloquent, join, laravel, relation

## 简介

Join related Eloquent models and hydrate them as loaded relations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
