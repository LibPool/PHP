# scaleupstack/assert

> 标签: assert, assertion, check, validation

## 简介

A tiny wrapper around webmozart/assert that is easily extendable to throw project-specific assertions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
