# allgorithm/filament-actionguard

> 标签: action-guard, filament, filament-5, filament-plugin, laravel, preflight, validation

## 简介

Stop incomplete records and prevent data degradation across critical Filament actions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
