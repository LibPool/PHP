# reg2005/payassetslaravel

> 标签: Pay Assets, laravel, reg2005

## 简介

Laravel 5.2 pay assets for Bitcoin pay package (reg2005/BtcPay), Perfect money (reg2005/PmPay) and Qiwi (QiwiPay)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
