# yaleksandr89/archive-guard

> 标签: archive, extraction, gzip, security, tar, tgz, zip, zip-bomb, zip-slip

## 简介

Security-first inspection and controlled extraction of untrusted ZIP and TAR archives for PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
