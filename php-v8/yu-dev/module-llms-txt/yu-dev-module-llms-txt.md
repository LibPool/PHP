# yu-dev/module-llms-txt

> 标签: ai, ecommerce, geo, llm, llms-full.txt, llms.txt, magento, magento2, seo

## 简介

Magento 2 llms.txt generator: serves /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt per store view — a curated Markdown index of categories, CMS pages and featured products for AI assistants.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
