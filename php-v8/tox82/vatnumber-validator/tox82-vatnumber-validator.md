> 标签: TVA, eu vat, tva number, validate eu vat, validate vat number, validation, validator, vat, vat number, vat number validator, vat validator

## 简介

A simple VAT identification number validator, without any dependencies, NOT relying on the VIES service.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
