> 标签: analytics, bug-monitoring, bug-reporting, bug-tracker, bug-tracking, bugboard, crash-reporting, error-monitoring, error-reporting, error-tracking, exception-handling, exception-tracking, issue-tracking, laravel, logging, monitoring, observability, psr-18, symfony

## 简介

Official BugBoard SDK for PHP — report bugs as cards on your project board, with first-class Laravel and Symfony support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
