> 标签: eth-gas-api, eth-gas-station, ethereum, ethereum-gas-api, ethereum-gas-prices, ethereum-gas-tracker, ethereum-gasprice

## 简介

Powerful PHP library to retrieve live Ethereum gas prices.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
