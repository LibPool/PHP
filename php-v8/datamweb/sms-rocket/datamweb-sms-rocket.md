# datamweb/sms-rocket

> 标签: SMSRocket, codeigniter, codeigniter4, sms

## 简介

SMSRocket is a powerful package designed to enhance and simplify SMS communication in your CodeIgniter 4 project.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
