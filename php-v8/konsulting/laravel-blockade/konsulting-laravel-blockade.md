# konsulting/laravel-blockade

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A simple block for your Laravel app to prevent access without a known code, and to force to https if you wish.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
