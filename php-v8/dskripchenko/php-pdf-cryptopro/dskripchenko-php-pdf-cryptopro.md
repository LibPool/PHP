# dskripchenko/php-pdf-cryptopro

> 标签: PHP-PDF, cades, cryptopro, gost, pades, pdf, qes, signature

## 简介

CryptoPro CSP signer for dskripchenko/php-pdf: sign PDFs (PAdES, GOST R 34.10-2012) with a key held by CryptoPro CSP on the server, through the phpcades extension.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
