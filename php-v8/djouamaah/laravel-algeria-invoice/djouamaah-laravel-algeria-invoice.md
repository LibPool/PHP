# djouamaah/laravel-algeria-invoice

> 标签: Algeria, TVA, algerie, droit de timbre, dzd, facture, invoice, laravel, الجزائر, فاتورة

## 简介

Algerian invoices for Laravel: VAT, cash stamp duty (Finance Law 2025), mandatory mentions (decree 05-468), gap-free numbering, amounts in Arabic and French words, printable HTML and optional PDF.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
