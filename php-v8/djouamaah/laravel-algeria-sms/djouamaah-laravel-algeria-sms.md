# djouamaah/laravel-algeria-sms

> 标签: Algeria, Ooredoo, algerie, djezzy, laravel, mobilis, notification, otp, sms, الجزائر

## 简介

SMS for Laravel through Algerian providers: OTP codes, transactional and marketing messages, Algerian phone numbers (Mobilis, Djezzy, Ooredoo), notification channel, opt-outs and quiet hours.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
