# gulf/payments

> 标签: bnpl, brick-money, gcc, knet, laravel, mada, myfatoorah, orchestration, payment-gateway, payments, paymob, stripe, tabby, tamara, tap

## 简介

Configuration-driven, multi-gateway payment orchestration for Laravel. Drivers expose primitives; flows compose them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
