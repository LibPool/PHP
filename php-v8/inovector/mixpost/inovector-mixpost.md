# inovector/mixpost

> 标签: calendar-scheduling, content-scheduling, mixpost, open-sourced, self-hosted, social-media-management, social-platform-integration

## 简介

Self-hosted social media management software.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
