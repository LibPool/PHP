# calpo/flfl-plainize

> 标签: fluent, fluent-logger-php, logger

## 简介

convert log format. from fluent/fluent-logger-php::FileLogger to plain text.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
