# tastyigniter/ti-ext-broadcast

> 标签: Broadcast, notification, tastyigniter

## 简介

Push browser notifications in real-time from your TastyIgniter application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
