# ianreid/switcher

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, language, language switcher, locale, localization, site

## 简介

A flexible and easy-to-use sites switcher for Craft CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
