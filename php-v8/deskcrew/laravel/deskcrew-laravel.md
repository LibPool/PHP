# deskcrew/laravel

> 标签: Customer support, Knowledge Base, Live Chat, ai chatbot, ai support, blade, chat widget, contact form, crisp alternative, deskcrew, help center, help desk, helpdesk, inertia, intercom alternative, laravel, livewire, support tickets, tawk.to alternative, ticketing, zendesk alternative

## 简介

Live chat, AI support chatbot and helpdesk ticketing for Laravel: the DeskCrew widget as a Blade directive, plus a client that turns contact forms into tickets. Free plan.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
