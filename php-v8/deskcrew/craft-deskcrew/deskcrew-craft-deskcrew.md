# deskcrew/craft-deskcrew

> 标签: Craft, Live Chat, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, deskcrew, helpdesk, support, widget

## 简介

Add the DeskCrew AI support widget to your Craft CMS site: live chat, AI answers and a help center, no code.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
