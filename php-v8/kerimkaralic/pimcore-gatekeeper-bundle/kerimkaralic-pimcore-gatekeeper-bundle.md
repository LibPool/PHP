# kerimkaralic/pimcore-gatekeeper-bundle

> 标签: PIM, completeness, data-quality, pimcore, pimcore-bundle

## 简介

Completeness gate for Pimcore DataObjects: per-class required fields, profiles and languages, scored on save, with reports and a publish gate.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
