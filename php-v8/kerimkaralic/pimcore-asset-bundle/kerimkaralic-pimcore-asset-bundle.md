# kerimkaralic/pimcore-asset-bundle

> 标签: asset, pimcore, pimcore-bundle

## 简介

Automatically sorts Pimcore assets referenced by DataObjects and Documents into configurable folder structures.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
