# linkrobins/flarum-swoop

> 标签: email, flarum, mail, smtp

## 简介

Account emails for your Flarum forum, sent through Link Robins with one key. No SMTP, no DNS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
