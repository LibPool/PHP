# weboftalent/mappable-poi

> 标签: google maps, mappable, maps, points of interest, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds point of interest functionality to the mappable module.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
