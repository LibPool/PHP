# 1994/abandoned-cart-statamic

> 标签: abandoned cart, cargo, cart recovery, e-commerce, reminder, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Abandoned cart recovery for Cargo: reminder emails, with a link that brings the shopper's basket back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
