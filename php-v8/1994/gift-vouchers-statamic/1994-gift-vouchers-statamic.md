# 1994/gift-vouchers-statamic

> 标签: cargo, e-commerce, gift card, gift voucher, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Gift vouchers for Cargo: sell vouchers with fixed or chosen amounts, email them with a printable PDF, and let customers spend them at checkout with the balance carried over.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
