# 1994/laravel-mail-batch

> 标签: batch, laravel, mail, notifications, rate limit, resend

## 简介

Send mail and notifications in batches, paced to your provider's rate limits.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
