# 1994/reviews-statamic

> 标签: cargo, ratings, reviews, star rating, statamic, statamic-addon, testimonials

## 简介

Reviews and ratings for Statamic, collected on your own site: a review form for any entry, moderation in the Control Panel, star ratings, averages and review schema, with verified purchases from Cargo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
