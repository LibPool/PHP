# 1994/reviews-craft

> 标签: Craft, cms, commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, ratings, reviews, schema, stars, testimonials

## 简介

Reviews and star ratings for Craft CMS, collected on your own site: moderation, replies, photos, helpful votes, verified buyers with Craft Commerce, review request emails, review schema, and import or sync from other platforms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
