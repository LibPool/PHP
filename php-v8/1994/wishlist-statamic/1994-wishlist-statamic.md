# 1994/wishlist-statamic

> 标签: favourites, shortlist, statamic, statamic-addon, wishlist

## 简介

Wishlists for Statamic: let visitors save entries to named lists, kept in flat files, the database or the visitor's own browser, and share them with a link.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
