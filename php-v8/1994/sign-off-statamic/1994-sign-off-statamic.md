# 1994/sign-off-statamic

> 标签: approval, editorial, review, statamic, statamic-addon, workflow

## 简介

Multi-step editorial sign-off for Statamic Pro: editors submit changes for review, each step approves or rejects with comments, and nothing goes live until everyone has signed off.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
