# 1994/social-images-statamic

> 标签: OG Image, open graph, seo, share image, social image, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Social share images for Statamic: design Open Graph cards in the Control Panel, bind them to your entries' fields, and have them generated on save. Templates import and export as JSON.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
