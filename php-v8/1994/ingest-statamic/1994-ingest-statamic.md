# 1994/ingest-statamic

> 标签: cargo, csv, feed, google sheets, import, json, rss, statamic, statamic-addon, sync, xml

## 简介

Keep Statamic in sync with external feeds: JSON, XML, RSS, CSV, APIs and Google Sheets into entries, terms, globals, users and Cargo products, on a schedule, with dry runs and undo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
