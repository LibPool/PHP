# 1994/back-in-stock-statamic

> 标签: back in stock, cargo, e-commerce, restock, statamic, statamic-addon, waitlist

## 简介

Back in stock alerts for Cargo: shoppers leave their email on a sold-out product or variant, confirm it, and get one email when it's back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
