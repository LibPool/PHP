# 1994/ghostwriter-statamic

> 标签: Gemini, ai, claude, content, openai, statamic, statamic-addon, writing

## 简介

Learns a site's tone of voice and image style from what it has published, then drafts new entries in that voice from a short brief and a conversation, finds or makes images to match (or licenses stock photos from your own account), walks you through finishing each page, and plans what the site is missing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
