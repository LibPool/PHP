# 1994/cookie-connect-statamic

> 标签: consent mode, cookie consent, cookiebot, gdpr, onetrust, statamic, statamic-addon, usercentrics

## 简介

Connect your site to the cookie consent platform you already use: paste its ID, and get Google Consent Mode v2 in the right order.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
