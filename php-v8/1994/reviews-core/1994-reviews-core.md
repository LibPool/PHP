# 1994/reviews-core

> 标签: import, ratings, review schema, reviews, star rating

## 简介

The framework-free core shared by Reviews for Craft CMS and Statamic: averages and star breakdowns, spam checks, captchas, review schema, importing from other review platforms, syncing from them on a schedule, and the default styles.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
