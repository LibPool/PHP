# 1994/social-images-core

> 标签: OG Image, open graph, screenshot, social image

## 简介

The shared core of 1994's Social Images products: the template format, the page renderer, and the rendering contract with its drivers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
