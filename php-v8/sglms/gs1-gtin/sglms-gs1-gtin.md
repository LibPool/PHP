# sglms/gs1-gtin

> 标签: gs1, gtin, laravel, sglms

## 简介

Classes to create or validate GTIN (Global Trade Item Number) and GS1; includes barcode generation. Laravel 12 Service Provider.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
