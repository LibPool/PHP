# easyemailverification/php-sdk

> 标签: bounce, catch-all, disposable email, email checker, email list cleaning, email validation, email verification, verify email

## 简介

Official PHP client for the Easy Email Verification API: verify email addresses one by one, in batches of 50 or as bulk lists.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
