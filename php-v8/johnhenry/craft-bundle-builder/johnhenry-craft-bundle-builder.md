# johnhenry/craft-bundle-builder

> 标签: Bundle Products, bundle builder, bundle discounts, bundle inventory, bundle pricing, bundle vat, craft-commerce, gift boxes, hampers, kits, product bundles, variant selection

## 简介

Product bundles for Craft Commerce: one cart line at one price, with the customer choosing each part's variant and stock taken off each part.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
