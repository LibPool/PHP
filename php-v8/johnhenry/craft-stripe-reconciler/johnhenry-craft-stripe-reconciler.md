# johnhenry/craft-stripe-reconciler

> 标签: 3d secure, Stripe payments, abandoned carts, checkout recovery, craft-commerce, double charge, missing orders, payment reconciliation, stripe, stripe webhooks, unpaid orders

## 简介

Finds Stripe payments that went through but never finished in Craft Commerce, checks them with Stripe and completes the orders.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
