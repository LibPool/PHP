# johnhenry/craft-pricing-rules-relationship

> 标签: catalog pricing rules, craft-commerce, discounted products, landing pages, offer pages, pricing rules field, promotions, relations field, sale page, seasonal sale, special offers

## 简介

A field that ties Craft Commerce catalog pricing rules to any element, so offer pages list the products those rules cover.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
