# johnhenry/craft-accessibility-audit

> 标签: a11y, accessibility, accessibility checker, accessibility statement, alt text, axe-core, contrast, en 301 549, european accessibility act, vpat, wcag, wcag 2.2

## 简介

WCAG 2.2 accessibility audits for Craft CMS 5: scan every page, fix what fails, and keep the record, statement and VPAT to show for it.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
