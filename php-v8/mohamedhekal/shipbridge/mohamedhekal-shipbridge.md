# mohamedhekal/shipbridge

> 标签: carriers, courier, ecommerce, fulfillment, labels, laravel, laravel-package, logistics, php, returns, shipping, shipping-api, tracking

## 简介

Unified Laravel shipping abstraction for create, track, label, return, and exchange across carriers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
