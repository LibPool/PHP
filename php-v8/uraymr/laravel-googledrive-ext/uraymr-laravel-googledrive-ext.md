# uraymr/laravel-googledrive-ext

> 标签: Flysystem, GoogleDrive, adapter, filesystem, google-api-client, google-drive, google-drive-api, laravel, storage

## 简介

Seamlessly connect Laravel to Google Drive with a Flysystem v3 adapter and Gdrive helper — upload, list, and manage your Drive files effortlessly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
