# e9li/kirby-seo

> 标签: Sitemap, kirby, kirby-cms, kirby-plugin, open-graph, robots, seo

## 简介

Lightweight SEO tab, meta tags, Open Graph, robots.txt and sitemap.xml for Kirby

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
