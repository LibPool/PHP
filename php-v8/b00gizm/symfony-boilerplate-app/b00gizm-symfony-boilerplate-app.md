# b00gizm/symfony-boilerplate-app

> 标签: boilerplate, docker, starter-project

## 简介

Fork of symfony/symfony-standard to use with symfony-boilerplate.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
