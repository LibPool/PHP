> 标签: analytics, asn, bot-detection, crawler, dashboard, gdpr, ip-lookup, laravel, monitoring, online-users, privacy, real-time, security, self-hosted, traffic, visitor-tracking

## 简介

Traffic Sentinel for Laravel: tracks visitors + bots, online presence, and traffic stats.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
