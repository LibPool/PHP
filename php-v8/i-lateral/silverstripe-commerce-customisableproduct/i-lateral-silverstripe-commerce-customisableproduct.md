# i-lateral/silverstripe-commerce-customisableproduct

> 标签: commerce, customisable, product, silverstripe

## 简介

Silverstripe module that adds customisable products (adding dropdowns, radio buttons or textfields to a product)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
