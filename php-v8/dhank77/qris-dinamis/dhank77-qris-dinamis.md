# dhank77/qris-dinamis

> 标签: EMVCo, crc16, dynamic-qris, gpn, indonesia, payment, qr-code, qris, qris-dinamis

## 简介

Convert static QRIS to dynamic QRIS in PHP: parse, validate, inject amount & service fee, recalculate CRC16. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
