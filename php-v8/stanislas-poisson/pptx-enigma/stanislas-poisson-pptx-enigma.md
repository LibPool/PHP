# stanislas-poisson/pptx-enigma

> 标签: PowerPoint, extractor, office-open-xml, parser, php, pptx, speaker-notes, voice-over

## 简介

Extracts the voice-over texts written in the speaker notes of a PowerPoint file, grouped by speaker and reference.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
