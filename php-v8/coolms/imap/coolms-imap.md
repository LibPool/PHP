# coolms/imap

> 标签: condstore, email, idle, imap, mail, qresync, sasl, xoauth2

## 简介

An IMAP client for PHP that keeps mail in sync: CONDSTORE, QRESYNC, IDLE, SPECIAL-USE folders and MOVE, with TLS verified and nothing ever deleted. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
