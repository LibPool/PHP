# g4t/printly

> 标签: arabic, chrome, fonts, laravel, pdf, rtl

## 简介

Generate PDFs in Laravel with headless Chrome: first-class Arabic/RTL, custom fonts and colors.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
