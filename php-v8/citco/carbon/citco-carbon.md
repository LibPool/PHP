# citco/carbon

> 标签: Bank, british, england, holiday, holidays, wales

## 简介

This is a wrapper for nesbot/carbon which also calculates which days are British bank holidays (England & Wales only).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
