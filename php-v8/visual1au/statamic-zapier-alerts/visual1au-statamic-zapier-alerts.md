# visual1au/statamic-zapier-alerts

> 标签: Forms, alerts, statamic, webhooks, zapier

## 简介

Show alerts on Statamic form blueprint pages when Zapier webhooks are configured

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
