# boring-dragon/inertia-breadcrumbs

> 标签: breadcrumbs, inertia-breadcrumbs, inertiajs, laravel, robertboes

## 简介

Laravel package to automatically share breadcrumbs to Inertia

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
