# shopper/pricelist

> 标签: Shopper, b2b, e-commerce, laravel, price-list, pricing

## 简介

Price lists for Shopper: sale and override prices targeted by customer groups, time windows and quantity tiers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
