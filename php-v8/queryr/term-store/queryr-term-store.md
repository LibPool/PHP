# queryr/term-store

> 标签: DataModel, Fingerprint, lookup, terms, wikibase

## 简介

Persistence of Wikibase terms with lookups of those terms and by those terms

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
