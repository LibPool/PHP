# victorhugobatista/wp-eruptor

> 标签: fast route, pretty permalink, single page, wordpress

## 简介

Extend your single pages like lava in the water with less setup

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
