# charm/arraybuffer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Typed arrays for structs powered by FFI. Gives you the equivalent of UInt8Array, UInt16Array, UInt32Array, Float64Array and ArrayBuffer in JavaScript.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
