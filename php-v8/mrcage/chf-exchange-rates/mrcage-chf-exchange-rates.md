# mrcage/chf-exchange-rates

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Retrieves Swiss Franc exchange rates for foreign currencies based on data from the website of the Swiss Federal Customs Administration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
