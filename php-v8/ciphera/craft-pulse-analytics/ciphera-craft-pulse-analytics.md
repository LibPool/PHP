# ciphera/craft-pulse-analytics

> 标签: Craft, analytics, cms, cookieless, craft-plugin, craftcms, privacy

## 简介

Privacy-first web analytics for Craft CMS. No cookies, no personal data, and the script it adds to your pages is under 3 KB.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
