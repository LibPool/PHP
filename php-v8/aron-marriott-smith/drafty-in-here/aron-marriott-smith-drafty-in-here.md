# aron-marriott-smith/drafty-in-here

> 标签: drafts, wordpress

## 简介

Get email notifications of draft post sitting in your WordPress Blog waiting to be published.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
