# silvershop-suite/product-feed-meta

> 标签: commerce-manager, ecommerce, facebook, facebook-shops, instagram, meta, product-feed, rss, silvershop, silverstripe

## 简介

Meta (Facebook & Instagram) catalog channel connector for silvershop-suite/product-feed (CMS 6): an RSS 2.0 + g: namespace serializer and field map that turns the shared CatalogProductData into a Meta Commerce catalog feed for Facebook Shops, Instagram Shopping and Advantage+/dynamic ads. Reuses Google Merchant's g: field vocabulary with Meta-specific deltas (space-form availability, fb_product_category). Registers itself into the product-feed core's channel registry — no core change.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
