# silvershop-suite/withdrawal

> 标签: compliance, consumer-rights, ecommerce, herroepingsknop, herroepingsrecht, shop, silvershop, silverstripe, widerruf, withdrawal

## 简介

EU/NL right-of-withdrawal (herroepingsknop) for SilverShop: a persistent, guest-capable withdrawal button available for the whole 14-day cooling-off window, a minimal no-account withdrawal form, an immediate on-screen + emailed confirmation, and a per-line exemption contract. Defensible-design machinery — verify the statutory copy, deadlines and exemptions with legal/DPO before launch.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
