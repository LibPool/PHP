# 4myth/arabic-numbers

> 标签: 4myth, MAC, arabic, indian, macos, myth

## 简介

Laravel Middleware convert arabic numbers to english numbers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
