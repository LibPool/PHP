# dcodegroup/laravel-logged-inbound-email

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Multi-provider inbound email webhooks for Laravel (Mailgun, Postmark, SendGrid, SES, Mailpit, Resend) with logging and processing for every inbound email.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
