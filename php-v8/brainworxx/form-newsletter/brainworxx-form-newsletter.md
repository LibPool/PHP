# brainworxx/form-newsletter

> 标签: form, newsletter, newsletter2go

## 简介

form_newsletter combines the Form extension with the newsletter service Newsletter2go. It creates a finisher which can add new recipients to your Newsletter2go account.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
