# captchala/flarum

> 标签: captcha, captchala, flarum, security, spam

## 简介

Protect Flarum signup, login, password reset and posts with CaptchaLa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://captcha.la/integrations/flarum
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/Captcha-La/captchala-flarum.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/captchala%2Fflarum
