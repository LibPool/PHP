# boost/craft-multie

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, multie

## 简介

Effortless multi-site management. Streamline site configuration, bulk updates, and translations with ease..

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
