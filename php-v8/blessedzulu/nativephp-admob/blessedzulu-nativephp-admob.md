# blessedzulu/nativephp-admob

> 标签: admob, ads, google-admob, laravel, mobile-ads, nativephp, nativephp-plugin

## 简介

Google AdMob plugin for NativePHP Mobile - banner, interstitial, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, app-open ads, plus UMP consent and iOS App Tracking Transparency.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
