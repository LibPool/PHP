# nowo-tech/legal-kit-bundle

> 标签: bundle, cookies, frankenphp, gdpr, i18n, legal, legal-notice, lssi, nowo, privacy-policy, symfony, symfony-bundle, terms-of-use

## 简介

Operator-editable legal pages for Symfony: legal notice, privacy, terms and cookies per locale with a generic GDPR/LSSI seed in 7 languages, a placeholder gate for deploys, ops-seed portability, a DOM allowlist sanitiser and UiKit admin pages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
