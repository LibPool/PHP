# itinerisltd/gf-cookie-monster

> 标签: gravityforms, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Pre-populate Gravity Forms fields and hide query strings by cookies

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
