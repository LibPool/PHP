# florentingarnier/spam-protection

> 标签: Honeypot, captcha, proof-of-work, spam

## 简介

Invisible CAPTCHA alternative: honeypot, single-use timed tokens, proof of work, rate limiting, IP reputation and gibberish detection.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
