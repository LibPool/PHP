# florentingarnier/spam-protection-bundle

> 标签: captcha, form, spam, symfony

## 简介

Symfony integration of florentingarnier/spam-protection: a form type, its JavaScript solver and the IP list refresh command.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
