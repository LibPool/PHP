# rankbeam/laravel-seo-filament

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, laravel, meta-tags, seo

## 简介

Laravel SEO package for Filament 4 and 5 - a complete editor with Google and social previews, canonical controls, and fallback-source indicators.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
