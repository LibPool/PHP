# venveo/craft-boilerplate

> 标签: Craft, boilerplate, craft boilerplate, craftboilerplate, craftcms

## 简介

Craft CMS Boilerplate for spinning up new projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
