# cpsit/course-qcat-export

> 标签: course, events, export, extension, qcat, typo3

## 简介

Exports records from t3events_course to Open Qcat.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
