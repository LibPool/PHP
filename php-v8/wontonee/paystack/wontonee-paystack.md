# wontonee/paystack

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Paystack Payment Gateway for Bagisto - Accept payments across Africa with Cards, Bank Transfer, Mobile Money & USSD

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
