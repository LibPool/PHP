# anzenkodo/rss-atom-parser

> 标签: atom, feed, rss

## 简介

RSS & Atom feed parser for PHP. Very small and easy-to-use library for parsing your feeds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
