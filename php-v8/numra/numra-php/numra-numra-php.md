# numra/numra-php

> 标签: cod, fraud, morocco, numra, risk

## 简介

Numra PHP client — cash-on-delivery risk checks, outcome reporting and webhook verification for Morocco.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
