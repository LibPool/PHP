# monoverse/voicebot-laravel-sync

> 标签: ai, ai-sales-assistant, catalog, ecommerce, hmac, laravel, monoverse, sync

## 简介

Laravel package for Monoverse, the AI sales assistant: syncs your catalog and content to it with signed, incremental pushes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
