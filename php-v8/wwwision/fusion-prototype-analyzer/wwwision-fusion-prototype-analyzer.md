# wwwision/fusion-prototype-analyzer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Anaylze Neos Fusion prototypes and their usages by other prototypes

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
