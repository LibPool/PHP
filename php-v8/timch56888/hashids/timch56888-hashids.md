# timch56888/hashids

> 标签: bitly, decode, encode, hash, hashid, hashids, ids, obfuscate, youtube

## 简介

Generate short, unique, non-sequential ids (like YouTube and Bitly) from numbers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
