# jotham-lec/statamic-marketing-toolkit

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The marketing fundamentals for a Statamic website: SEO, tracking tags, Consent Mode, favicons and redirects, Search Console numbers, site reports with a score and conversion events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
