> 标签: API-Client, ai-content, bluesky, content-calendar, hashtags, instagram, linkedin, misar, misarpost, repurposing, scheduling, social-media, social-media-api, trends, twitter

## 简介

PHP client for MisarPost's social publishing API: schedule and publish posts across every connected channel, generate and repurpose content with AI, research trends, run PersonaForge and read analytics — retries and typed plan-limit errors with upgrade offers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
