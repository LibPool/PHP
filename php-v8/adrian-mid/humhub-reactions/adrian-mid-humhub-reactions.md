# adrian-mid/humhub-reactions

> 标签: emoji, humhub, humhub-module, like, reactions

## 简介

Add Facebook-style emoji reactions to HumHub posts and comments.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
