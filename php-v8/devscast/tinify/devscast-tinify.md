# devscast/tinify

> 标签: api, compress, images, tinify, tinyjpg, tinypng

## 简介

PHP client for the Tinify API. Tinify compresses your images intelligently. Read more at https://tinify.com.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
