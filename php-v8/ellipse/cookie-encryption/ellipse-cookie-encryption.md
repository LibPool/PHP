# ellipse/cookie-encryption

> 标签: cookie, encryption, middleware, psr-15, psr-7

## 简介

Psr-15 middleware allowing to encrypt cookies using defuse/php-encryption

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
