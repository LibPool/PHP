# mnurhad/spreedcore

> 标签: csv, excel, export, laravel, ods, phpspreadsheet, spreadsheet, xlsx

## 简介

Framework-agnostic fluent Excel/CSV/ODS export builder on top of PhpSpreadsheet, with first-class Laravel support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
