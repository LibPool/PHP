# simivar/riot-php

> 标签: LoL, Riot, httplug, league of legends, legends of runeterra, lor, psr-17, riot games, teamfight tactics, tft, valorant

## 简介

Dependency-injection based wrapper around Riot Games APIs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
