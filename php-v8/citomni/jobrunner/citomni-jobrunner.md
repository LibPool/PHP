# citomni/jobrunner

> 标签: PSR-4, background-jobs, citomni, citomni-framework, citomni-php, cli-worker, db-backed, deterministic, developer-tools, framework, green-by-design, high-performance, http-to-cli, job-runner, long-running-jobs, low-overhead, php, php8, status-tracking, workflow-automation

## 简介

Small DB-backed job runner for CitOmni: HTTP/UI-started long-running jobs executed by CLI workers with status, steps, logs, heartbeat, and results.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
