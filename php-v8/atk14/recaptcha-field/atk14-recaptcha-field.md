# atk14/recaptcha-field

> 标签: Forms, atk14, captcha, field, recaptcha

## 简介

A field for protecting forms against spam in ATK14 applications

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
