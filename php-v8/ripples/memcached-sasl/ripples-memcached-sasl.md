# ripples/memcached-sasl

> 标签: aliyun, laravel, memcached, ocs, sasl

## 简介

Custom cache extension of memcached sasl for laravel5, support aliyun ocs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
