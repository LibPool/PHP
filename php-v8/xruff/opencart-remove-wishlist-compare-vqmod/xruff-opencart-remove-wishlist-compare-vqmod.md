# xruff/opencart-remove-wishlist-compare-vqmod

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Vqmod modification removes wishlist and compare buttons on product, category, manufacturer, specials lists.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
