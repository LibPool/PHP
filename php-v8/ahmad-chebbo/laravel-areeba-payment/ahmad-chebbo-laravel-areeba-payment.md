> 标签: areeba, checkout, gateway, laravel, mastercard gateway, mpgs, payment, webhooks

## 简介

Laravel package for the Areeba (Mastercard Gateway) hosted checkout, refunds, card tokens and webhooks

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
