# ravage84/cakephp-fake-seeder

> 标签: cakephp, data, faker, fixtures, plugin, seeder

## 简介

A shell to seed your database with fake and/or fixed data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
