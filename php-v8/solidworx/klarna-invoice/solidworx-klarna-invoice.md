# solidworx/klarna-invoice

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The unofficial repo of Klarna Invoice service with Composer added. We use it with https://github.com/Payum/KlarnaInvoice

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://docs.klarna.com/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/SolidWorx/KlarnaInvoice.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/solidworx%2Fklarna-invoice
