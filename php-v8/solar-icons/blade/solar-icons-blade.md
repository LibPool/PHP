# solar-icons/blade

> 标签: Blade Icons, Solar Icons, blade, laravel, solar, svg

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Solar Icons in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
