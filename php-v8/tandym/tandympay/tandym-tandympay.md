# tandym/tandympay

> 标签: extension, gateway, magento, magento 2, payment, plugin, tandym

## 简介

Tandym Payments extension for Magento 2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
