# neckarpixel/contao-npnewsfilter-bundle

> 标签: archive, contao, date, filter, nachrichten, news, newslist, zeitfilter

## 简介

Time filter for the Contao news list module (Contao 4.9+ and 5.x): show only news younger or older than a given period, or from the current/previous month or year.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
