# curly_sanders/carbon-normalizer

> 标签: carbon, normalizer, serializer, symfony

## 简介

Normalizes to (and from) Carbon and native DateTime objects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
