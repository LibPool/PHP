# einvoicing/laravel

> 标签: EN16931, e-invoicing, laravel, peppol, ubl, vat

## 简介

Peppol e-invoicing for Laravel: validate, convert and look up documents with the einvoicing.dev API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
