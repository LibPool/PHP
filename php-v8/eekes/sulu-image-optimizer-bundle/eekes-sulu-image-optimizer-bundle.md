# eekes/sulu-image-optimizer-bundle

> 标签: Webp, avif, image, image-optimization, media, resize, sulu, sulu-bundle, svg-sanitizer, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Optimizes and resizes images before they are stored in the Sulu media library, with a log of what every upload saved

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
