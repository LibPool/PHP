# settleup/can-make-or-fake

> 标签: Settle Up, Try Settle Up, can-make-or-fake, laravel

## 简介

A simple helper for making classes easily instantiated and mocked.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
