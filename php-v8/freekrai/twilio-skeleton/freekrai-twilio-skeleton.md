# freekrai/twilio-skeleton

> 标签: laravel, rest, router, twilio

## 简介

A Laravel skeleton application for rapid development of Twilio apps

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
