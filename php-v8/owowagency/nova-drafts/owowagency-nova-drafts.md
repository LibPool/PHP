# owowagency/nova-drafts

> 标签: drafts, field, laravel, nova, optimistdigital

## 简介

A Laravel Nova field that allows you to make drafts of your current resources.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
