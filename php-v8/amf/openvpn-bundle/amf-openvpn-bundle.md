# amf/openvpn-bundle

> 标签: OpenVpnServer, VpnServer, openvpn, telnet, vpn

## 简介

A bundle for management of openvpn servers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
