# linuxstreet/wire-content-loader

> 标签: alpinejs, content, laravel, livewire, php

## 简介

Flexible way of loading Livewire component or Blade view anywhere on the page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
