# khalyomede/laravel-eloquent-uuid-slug

> 标签: binding, laravel, model, package, route, slug, uuid

## 简介

Use auto generated UUID slugs to identify and retrieve your Eloquent models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
