# yatmo/laravel

> 标签: blade, isochrone, laravel, listing, map, neighbourhood, poi, points-of-interest, property, proptech, real-estate, seo, yatmo

## 简介

Real estate maps, points of interest and neighbourhood data for Laravel: Blade components for the property map, the nearest schools, shops and public transport with travel times, and a cached, server-rendered neighbourhood text for SEO. Laravel 10 to 13. Powered by Yatmo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
