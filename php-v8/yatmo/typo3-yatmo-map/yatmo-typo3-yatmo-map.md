# yatmo/typo3-yatmo-map

> 标签: extension, fluid, map, neighbourhood, points-of-interest, real-estate, seo, typo3, viewhelper, yatmo

## 简介

Real estate map, points of interest with travel times and an indexable neighbourhood text on TYPO3 property pages, from Yatmo: three Fluid ViewHelpers (map, places, text), extension settings for the key and defaults, caching. TYPO3 12, 13 and 14.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
