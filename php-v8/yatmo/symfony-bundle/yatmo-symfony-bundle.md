# yatmo/symfony-bundle

> 标签: isochrone, listing, map, neighbourhood, poi, points-of-interest, property, proptech, real-estate, seo, symfony, symfony-bundle, twig, yatmo

## 简介

Real estate maps, points of interest and neighbourhood data for Symfony: the Yatmo bundle registers the typed client from .env, caches the neighbourhood text and the nearest places, and adds Twig functions yatmo_map, yatmo_text and yatmo_pois for property pages, indexable by search engines. Symfony 6.4, 7 and 8.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
