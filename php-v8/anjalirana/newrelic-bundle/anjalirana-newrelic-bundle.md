# anjalirana/newrelic-bundle

> 标签: metric, monitoring, performance

## 简介

Integrate New Relic into Symfony2 with ignore transactions update

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
