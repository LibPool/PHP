# gulaandrij/google-sheets-bundle

> 标签: google, google-sheets, sheets, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Symfony bundle exposing a higher-level Google Sheets service on top of revolution/laravel-google-sheets.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
