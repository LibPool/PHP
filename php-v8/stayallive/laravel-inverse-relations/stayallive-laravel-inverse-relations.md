# stayallive/laravel-inverse-relations

> 标签: eloquent, laravel, relations

## 简介

HasMany, HasOne and MorphMany Eloquent relations with inverse hydration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
