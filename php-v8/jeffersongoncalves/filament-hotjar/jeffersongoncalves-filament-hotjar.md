# jeffersongoncalves/filament-hotjar

> 标签: analytics, filament, filament-hotjar, filament-plugin, heatmaps, hotjar, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Filament plugin for Hotjar — tracking script injection and a Settings Page, powered by Spatie Laravel Settings, to manage your Hotjar site ID directly from your Filament panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
