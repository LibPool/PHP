# jeffersongoncalves/filament-zapsign

> 标签: assinatura-eletronica, e-signature, filament, filament-plugin, filament-zapsign, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, zapsign

## 简介

Filament plugin for ZapSign e-signatures: send documents to sign, track signers and status, cancel documents, download signed PDFs and inspect webhooks, on top of laravel-zapsign.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
