> 标签: cookieless, filament, filament-plugin, filament-simple-analytics, gdpr, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, privacy, simple-analytics, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Filament plugin for Simple Analytics: injects the tracking script into your panels and adds a settings page, powered by Spatie Laravel Settings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
