# jeffersongoncalves/laravel-open-hours

> 标签: business-hours, laravel, open-hours, opening-hours, schedule, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Business opening hours for Laravel, stored with spatie/laravel-settings: weekly schedule, holidays and special dates, timezone, isOpen/nextOpen helpers, a Blade directive and a status component.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
