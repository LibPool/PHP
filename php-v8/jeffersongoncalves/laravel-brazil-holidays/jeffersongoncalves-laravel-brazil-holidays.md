> 标签: brazil, business-days, dias-uteis, feriados, holidays, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, laravel-brazil-holidays

## 简介

Brazilian national holidays for Laravel: holiday list per year, holiday and business day checks, and business day arithmetic.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
