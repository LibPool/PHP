# jeffersongoncalves/filament-clarity

> 标签: analytics, clarity, filament, filament-clarity, filament-plugin, heatmaps, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, microsoft-clarity, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Filament plugin for Microsoft Clarity: injects the Clarity tracking tag into your panels and adds a settings page, powered by Spatie Laravel Settings, to manage the project ID.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
