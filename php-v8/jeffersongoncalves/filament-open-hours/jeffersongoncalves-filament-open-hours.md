> 标签: business-hours, filament, filament-open-hours, filament-plugin, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, open-hours, opening-hours, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Opening hours for Filament: manage the weekly schedule, holidays, special dates and timezone from a settings page and show an open/closed status widget, powered by laravel-open-hours.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
