> 标签: chat-widget, crisp, customer-support, filament, filament-crisp, filament-plugin, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, live-chat, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Filament plugin for Crisp: renders the live chat widget in your panels and adds a settings page, powered by Spatie Laravel Settings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
