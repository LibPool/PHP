> 标签: analytics, cookieless, gdpr, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, laravel-pirsch, pirsch, privacy, spatie-laravel-settings

## 简介

Pirsch for Laravel: inject the tracking script in your Blade layouts, with the settings stored in the database via spatie/laravel-settings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
