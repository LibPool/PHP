> 标签: 2fa, MFA, admin-panel, app-panel, edit-profile, filament, filament-starter-kit, filament-v5, laravel, laravel-starter-kit, livewire, multi-factor-authentication, multi-panel, profile-management, starter-kit, tailwindcss, whatsapp, whatsapp-connector

## 简介

Starter kit for Laravel 13 and Filament 5 with multi-panel structure, built-in Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), WhatsApp connector, and profile management.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
