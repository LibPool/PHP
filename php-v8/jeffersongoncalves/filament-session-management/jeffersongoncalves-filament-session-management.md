# jeffersongoncalves/filament-session-management

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, jeffersongoncalves, laravel, session, session-management, sessions

## 简介

A Filament plugin to read and manage Laravel's database sessions: see who is logged in, inspect decoded payloads, revoke and prune sessions from your panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
