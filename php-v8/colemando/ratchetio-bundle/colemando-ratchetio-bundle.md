# colemando/ratchetio-bundle

> 标签: Ratchet, debugging, errors, exceptions, monitoring

## 简介

Integrates Ratchet.io error reporting into your Symfony2 application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
