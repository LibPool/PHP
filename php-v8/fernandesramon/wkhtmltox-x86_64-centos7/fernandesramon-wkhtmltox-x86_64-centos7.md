# fernandesramon/wkhtmltox-x86_64-centos7

> 标签: centos7, wkhtmltoimage, wkhtmltopdf

## 简介

wkhtmltopdf and wkhtmltoimage binaries for CentOS 7 x86_64.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
