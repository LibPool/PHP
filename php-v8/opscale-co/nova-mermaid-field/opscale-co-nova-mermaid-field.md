# opscale-co/nova-mermaid-field

> 标签: diagram, field, laravel, mermaid, mermaid-js, nova, visualizer

## 简介

Read-only Mermaid diagram renderer for Laravel Nova, powered by mermaid.js.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
