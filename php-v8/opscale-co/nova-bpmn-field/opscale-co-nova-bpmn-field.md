# opscale-co/nova-bpmn-field

> 标签: bpmn, bpmn-js, field, laravel, nova, visualizer

## 简介

Interactive BPMN diagram viewer for Laravel Nova, powered by bpmn-js.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
