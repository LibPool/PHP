# rkcreation/socialstream

> 标签: Social Wall, api, php

## 简介

PHP helper to aggregate formatted social media posts from Facebook, Twitter, etc ...

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
