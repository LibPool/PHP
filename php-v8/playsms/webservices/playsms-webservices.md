# playsms/webservices

> 标签: bulk, playsms, sms, webservices

## 简介

playSMS Webservices Class. playSMS is a free and open source SMS portal software.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
