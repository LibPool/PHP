# yarri/email-parser

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Parses raw MIME email messages from strings or files into structured PHP objects, with automatic UTF-8 conversion, attachment detection, and built-in caching

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
