# ilovejackylee/name100-men

> 标签: cli, name-100, name-100-men, quiz, recall, trivia

## 简介

Terminal companion for the Name 100 Men recall challenge — practice offline, play online.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
