# jayeshmepani/lipimala

> 标签: devanagari, gujarati, iast, indic, round-trip, sanskrit, transliteration, vedic

## 简介

Exact round-trip IAST and extended Indic transliteration for Devanagari, Gujarati, plain English, canonical reverse conversion, and direct Gujarati/Devanagari conversion.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
