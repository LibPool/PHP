# luxsite/symfony-ecommerce

> 标签: e-commerce, easyadmin, ecommerce, mcp, multi-store, shop, symfony

## 简介

Symfony ecommerce LuxSite: open-source e-commerce engine on Symfony (Apache 2.0, platform developed by LuxSite)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
