# theline/typo3-lottie

> 标签: After Effects, bodymovin, extension, lottie, typo3

## 简介

TYPO3 CMS extension to enable rendering of Lottie/Bodymovin animations in frontend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
