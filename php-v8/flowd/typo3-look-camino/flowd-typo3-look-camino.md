# flowd/typo3-look-camino

> 标签: backend, camino, page module, preview, typo3

## 简介

Look previews for the TYPO3 Camino theme: Camino content elements in the page module, rendered with the real frontend templates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
