# balerka/yandex-direct-helper

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Yandex Direct Helper is a package for automatically updating advertising campaign strategies and expenses in Yandex.Direct.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
