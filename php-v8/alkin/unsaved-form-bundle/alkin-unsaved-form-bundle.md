# alkin/unsaved-form-bundle

> 标签: Forms, stimulus, symfony, symfony-ux, turbo, unsaved-changes

## 简介

A tiny Stimulus controller that warns about unsaved Symfony form changes before navigation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
