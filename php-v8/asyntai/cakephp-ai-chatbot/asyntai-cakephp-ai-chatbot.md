# asyntai/cakephp-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai-assistant, ai-chatbot, asyntai, cakephp, cakephp-plugin, chatbot, customer-support, live-chat

## 简介

Asyntai AI chatbot for CakePHP. Adds an AI chat assistant to your CakePHP app that answers visitor questions from your own content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
