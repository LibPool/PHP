# asyntai/mautic-ai-chatbot

> 标签: Live Chat, Mautic, ai, chatbot, integration, landing pages, lead capture, plugin

## 简介

Asyntai AI Chatbot for Mautic: an AI chat assistant on your landing pages that answers visitors around the clock and turns the email addresses and phone numbers they share into Mautic contacts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
