# asyntai/filament-ai-chatbot

> 标签: ai-assistant, ai-chatbot, asyntai, chatbot, customer-support, filament, filament-plugin, laravel, live-chat

## 简介

Asyntai AI chatbot for Filament. Adds an AI chat assistant to your Filament panels and Laravel pages that answers questions from your own content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
