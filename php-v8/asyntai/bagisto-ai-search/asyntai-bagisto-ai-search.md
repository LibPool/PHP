# asyntai/bagisto-ai-search

> 标签: ai, ai search, asyntai, bagisto, ecommerce, search, site search

## 简介

Asyntai AI Search for Bagisto: a search bar that understands what shoppers mean, with live prices and stock from your own catalogue.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
