# asyntai/neos-ai-chatbot

> 标签: Customer support, Live Chat, Neos, ai, ai chatbot, asyntai, chat widget, chatbot, flow, neos-plugin

## 简介

Asyntai AI Chatbot for Neos CMS: an AI chat assistant on every page that answers visitor questions from your own website content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
