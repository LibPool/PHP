# stenvdb/craft-fathom-analytics

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, fathom analytics

## 简介

Statistics and chart widgets for Fathom analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
