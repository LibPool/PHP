# puffinmail/module-lifecycle

> 标签: abandoned cart, email marketing, magento, magento2, newsletter, puffinmail

## 简介

PuffinMail for Magento 2: syncs customers, newsletter subscribers, orders, abandoned carts, products and birthdays to PuffinMail, email marketing for online stores.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
