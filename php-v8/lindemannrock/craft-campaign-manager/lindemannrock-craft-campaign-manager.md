# lindemannrock/craft-campaign-manager

> 标签: Craft, analytics, campaigns, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, email, invitations, sms, surveys

## 简介

Campaign management for Craft CMS surveys with SMS and email invitations, recipient tracking, and analytics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
