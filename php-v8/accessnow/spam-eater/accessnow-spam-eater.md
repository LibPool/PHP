# accessnow/spam-eater

> 标签: Honeypot, anti-spam, captcha-alternative, contact-form, form, gdpr, no-captcha, privacy, spam, spam-protection

## 简介

Stops form spam on any PHP site without CAPTCHAs: honeypot, JavaScript, timing and interaction checks, all on your own server.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
