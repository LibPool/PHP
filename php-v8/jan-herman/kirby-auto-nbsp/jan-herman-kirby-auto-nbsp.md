# jan-herman/kirby-auto-nbsp

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Replaces spaces with non-breaking spaces (&nbsp;) to prevent short words (like prepositions, conjunctions, and articles) from appearing at the end of a line.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
