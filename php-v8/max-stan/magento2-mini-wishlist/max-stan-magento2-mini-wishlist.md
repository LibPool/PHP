# max-stan/magento2-mini-wishlist

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Fully CSP-compatible slide-out wishlist drawer for Hyvä themes with inline product search, GraphQL-based add/remove

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
