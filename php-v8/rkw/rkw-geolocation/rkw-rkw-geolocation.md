# rkw/rkw-geolocation

> 标签: RKW, TYPO3 CMS, geolocation, google maps

## 简介

Locates addresses and zip codes with Google Geo-API and displays them in Google Maps

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
