# boxhead/soundcloud-sync

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, soundcloud sync

## 简介

Sync your Soundcloud track data into Craft Entries

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
