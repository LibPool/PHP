# digitalisweb/sassy

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Compiles SCSS on the server, keeps a model of every stylesheet WordPress enqueued and writes DevTools edits back into the partial they came from.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
