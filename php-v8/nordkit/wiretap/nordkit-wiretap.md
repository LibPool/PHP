# nordkit/wiretap

> 标签: Guzzle, http, laravel, monitoring, tracing, wiretap

## 简介

Tap into your app's HTTP traffic. Filter, redact, and store outbound requests with zero boilerplate.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
