# myth/postal

> 标签: codeigniter, codeigniter4, email, mailer, mailgun, postmark, resend, ses, smtp, transport

## 简介

A modern CodeIgniter 4 email replacement library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
