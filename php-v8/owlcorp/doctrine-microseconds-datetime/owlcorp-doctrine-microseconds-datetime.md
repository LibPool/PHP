# owlcorp/doctrine-microseconds-datetime

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds support of microseconds time formats to Doctrine ORM & Doctrine DBAL

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
