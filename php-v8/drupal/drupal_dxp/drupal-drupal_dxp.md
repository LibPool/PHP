# drupal/drupal_dxp

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Drupal DXP is an extension of Drupal CMS, built on Drupal core, aimed on being a quick start for a suite of CMS, MA and CDP capacities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
