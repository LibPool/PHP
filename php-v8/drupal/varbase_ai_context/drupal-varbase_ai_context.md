# drupal/varbase_ai_context

> 标签: AI Context, Context Control Center, Drupal AI, Varbase AI, Varbase recipes, ai, ccc, drupal recipe, drupal recipes, recipe, varbase recipe

## 简介

Pre-populates starter context items (Brand & Identity Guidelines, AI Editorial Rules, AI Safety Guardrails) for every Varbase site via the Context Control Center (CCC) module.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
