# oscar-team/laravel-truss

> 标签: DBML, data-dictionary, database, database-diagram, er-diagram, erd, introspection, laravel, laravel-10, mermaid, migrations, schema

## 简介

Oscar Mobility's Laravel 10 port of Alberto Arena's Laravel Truss: a live database structure viewer. Renders your schema (never data) as a scrollable, zoomable ER diagram.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
