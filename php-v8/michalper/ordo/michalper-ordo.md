# michalper/ordo

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

B2B + B2C marketing automation for Magento Open Source — reorder, offer expiry, credit limit, and order approval triggers alongside abandoned cart and lifecycle emails, no Adobe Commerce B2B or external MA subscription required.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
