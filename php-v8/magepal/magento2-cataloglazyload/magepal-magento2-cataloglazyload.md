# magepal/magento2-cataloglazyload

> 标签: catalog images lazyload, enhance website performance, lazy load, lazy load extension, lazy load image plugin, lazy-loading, lazyload images, lazyload images magento2, lazyloading, magento 2, magento faster page load, magento2

## 简介

Improve the load time of your Magento 2 categories pages by loading your images on demand with our Lazy Load Extension

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
