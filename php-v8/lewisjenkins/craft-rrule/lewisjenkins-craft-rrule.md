# lewisjenkins/craft-rrule

> 标签: Craft, calendar, craftcms, module, recurrence, rrule, scheduling, yii2

## 简介

RRULE helpers for Craft CMS (wraps rlanvin/php-rrule).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
