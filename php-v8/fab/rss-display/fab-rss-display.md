# fab/rss-display

> 标签: RSS feed, TYPO3 CMS, atom

## 简介

Fetch a RSS / Atom Feed and display its content on the Frontend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
