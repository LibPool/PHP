# fopost/laravel

> 标签: api, fopost, laravel, publishing, scheduling, sdk, social-media

## 简介

Official Laravel SDK for the FoPost API. Schedule and publish to +30 social platforms from your code.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
