# fopost/woocommerce

> 标签: auto-post, fopost, scheduling, social-media, woocommerce, wordpress

## 简介

WooCommerce add-on that posts your products to social media through FoPost when they are published, go on sale, or come back in stock.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
