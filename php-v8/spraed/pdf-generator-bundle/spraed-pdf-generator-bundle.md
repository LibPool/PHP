# spraed/pdf-generator-bundle

> 标签: bundle, generator, pdf, spraed

## 简介

This bundle creates (multiple) PDFs in Symfony from Twig/HTML templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
