# arraypress/wp-money

> 标签: currency, ecommerce, formatting, iso-4217, minor-units, money, wordpress

## 简介

Integer-minor-unit money formatting and parsing for WordPress. 136 currencies with the correct decimal exponent, so the twenty that are not two decimal places are right.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
