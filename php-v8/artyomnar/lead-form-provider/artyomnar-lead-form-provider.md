# artyomnar/lead-form-provider

> 标签: crm, lead-form, notifier, pipeline, telegram, wrapper

## 简介

Takes simple lead form from any web page and provide it into telegram

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
