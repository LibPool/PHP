# zenstruck/datagrid-bundle

> 标签: admin, administration, datagrid, grid

## 简介

Provides a sortable/filterable/paginated datagrid for your entities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
