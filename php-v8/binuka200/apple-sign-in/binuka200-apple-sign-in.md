# binuka200/apple-sign-in

> 标签: apple, jwt, oauth, oidc, sign-in

## 简介

Defensive, framework-neutral Sign in with Apple for PHP: OAuth flow, token verification, revocation, and notifications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
