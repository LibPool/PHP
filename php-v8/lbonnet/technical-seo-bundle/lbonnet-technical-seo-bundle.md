# lbonnet/technical-seo-bundle

> 标签: Audit, bundle, canonical, crawler, indexability, redirects, seo, symfony, technical-seo

## 简介

A Symfony bundle to crawl a site and audit technical SEO signals: canonical tags, indexing directives and redirects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
