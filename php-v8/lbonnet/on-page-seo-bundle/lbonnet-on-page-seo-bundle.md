# lbonnet/on-page-seo-bundle

> 标签: Audit, bundle, crawler, meta-tags, on-page-seo, seo, symfony

## 简介

A Symfony bundle to crawl a site and audit on-page SEO signals (titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt attributes).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
