# lbonnet/seo-bundle

> 标签: Audit, Sitemap, broken-links, bundle, canonical, crawler, hreflang, link-checker, on-page-seo, robots-txt, seo, symfony, technical-seo

## 简介

A Symfony bundle that crawls a site once to audit its links, on-page content and technical SEO signals: broken links, titles and descriptions, canonical tags, indexing directives, redirects, robots.txt, sitemaps and hreflang.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
