# anglemx/audit-bundle

> 标签: Audit, access review, angle, itgc

## 简介

Audit Bundle to produce automated reports for OS (Ubuntu) and DB (MySQL) privileged access

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
