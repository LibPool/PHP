# xi/fixtures

> 标签: Fixture, doctrine, factory, factorygirl, test

## 简介

Convenient creation of Doctrine entities in tests. Like Ruby's FactoryGirl.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
