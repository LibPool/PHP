# akrista/blade-islands

> 标签: blade, islands, laravel, react, svelte, vue

## 简介

Server-side Blade directives for React, Vue, and Svelte islands in Laravel. Maintained fork of eznix86/blade-islands.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
