## 简介

Show each user what is new right where it lives: pulsing beacons and contextual hints on the exact buttons, columns, fields and pages you just shipped in your Filament panel. Seen once per user, stored in the database, AI-agent ready.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
