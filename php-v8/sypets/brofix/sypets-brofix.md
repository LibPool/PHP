> 标签: TYPO3 Extension, broken link checking, broken link fixer, broken links, content quality, links, typo3

## 简介

Broken Link Fixer for TYPO3 (forked from TYPO3 linkvalidator)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
