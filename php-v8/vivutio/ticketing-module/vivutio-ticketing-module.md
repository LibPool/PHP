# vivutio/ticketing-module

> 标签: airline, symfony, ticketing, tourism, travel-agency, vivutio

## 简介

Ticketing for vivutio: tickets bought from airlines, ferries, coaches and railways for travellers, recorded by hand, issued, changed, refunded or voided, and billed to the traveller or a client.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
