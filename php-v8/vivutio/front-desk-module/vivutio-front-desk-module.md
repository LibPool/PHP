# vivutio/front-desk-module

> 标签: front-desk, hospitality, reception, symfony, tourism, vivutio

## 简介

The front desk for vivutio: a day's arrivals, guests in house and departures at a place, checking in and out, no-shows, and the units each stay is given.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
