# vivutio/payment-module

> 标签: payments, receivables, symfony, tourism, vivutio

## 简介

Payments for vivutio: what the organization is owed for its bookings and tickets, gathered from every module, and the payments received against it, recorded by hand.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
