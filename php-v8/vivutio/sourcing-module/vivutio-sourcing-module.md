# vivutio/sourcing-module

> 标签: accommodation, sourcing, symfony, tourism, vivutio

## 简介

Sourcing for vivutio: rooms requested from the accommodation partners an organization trades with, sent through the partner channel, and their replies recorded.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
