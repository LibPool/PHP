> 标签: ShouldBeUnique, artisan, diagnostics, laravel, lock, queue, redis, unique

## 简介

Find out why a ShouldBeUnique job won't dispatch: list and explain the unique-job locks held in your Redis cache store.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
