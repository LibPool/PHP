# fawno/agencias

> 标签: API-Client, agency, collection, efe, feed, media, news

## 简介

PHP library to easily integrate, map, and download news feeds from various news agencies (EFE, etc.).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
