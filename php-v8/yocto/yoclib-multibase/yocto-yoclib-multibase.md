# yocto/yoclib-multibase

> 标签: composer, multibase, multiformats, php, yoclib, yocto

## 简介

This yocLibrary enables your project to encode and decode Multibases in PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
