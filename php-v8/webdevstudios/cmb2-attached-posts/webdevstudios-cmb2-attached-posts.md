# webdevstudios/cmb2-attached-posts

> 标签: attached, metabox, plugin, post, relationship, wordpress

## 简介

Custom field for CMB2 for creating post relationships.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
