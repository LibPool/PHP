# nyumbani/essentials

> 标签: essentials, laravel, nyumbani

## 简介

Opinionated helpers, services, UIs, packages, and AI skills and rules that define how every Nyumbani internal app looks and is structured.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
