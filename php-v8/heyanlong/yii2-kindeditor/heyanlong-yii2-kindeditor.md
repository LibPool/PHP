# heyanlong/yii2-kindeditor

> 标签: extension, kindeditor, widget, yii2

## 简介

Yii 2 kindeditor is the WYSIWYG HTML editor of the Yii 2.0 widget.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
