# vipertecpro/consent

> 标签: ads, analytics, app-tracking-transparency, att, consent, gdpr, idfa, mobile, nativephp, privacy

## 简介

App Tracking Transparency prompt and a cross-platform consent store (analytics, ads, personalisation) with events — native, hand-written, for NativePHP Mobile.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
