# vipertecpro/photo-kit

> 标签: camera-roll, compress, exif, image, mobile, nativephp, photo-library, photos, resize, video

## 简介

Save images and videos to the photo library, compress, resize and fix EXIF orientation — native, hand-written, for NativePHP Mobile.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
