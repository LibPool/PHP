# ejosterberg/opensalestax-for-woocommerce

> 标签: opensalestax, sales-tax, tax, us-tax, woocommerce, wordpress

## 简介

WooCommerce plugin: replace TaxJar/Avalara with self-hosted OpenSalesTax — destination-based US sales tax at checkout.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
