# ejosterberg/opensalestax-opencart

> 标签: opencart, opencart4, opensalestax, sales-tax, tax, tax-calculation, us-tax

## 简介

OpenCart 4.x extension — destination-based US sales tax via the self-hosted OpenSalesTax engine

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
