# rcrowe/laravel-sentry

> 标签: error, laravel, reporting, sentry

## 简介

Tasty intergration of Laravel & Sentry for sweet reporting of your logs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
