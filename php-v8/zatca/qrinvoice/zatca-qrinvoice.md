# zatca/qrinvoice

> 标签: e-invocing, invoice, qr, qr-code

## 简介

A helper to generate the QR code and signed it for INVOICE e-invoicing

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
