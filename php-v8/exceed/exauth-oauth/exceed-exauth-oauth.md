# exceed/exauth-oauth

> 标签: auth, codeigniter, codeigniter4, exauth, google, oauth, social-login

## 简介

Social login (Google) for exAuth - CodeIgniter 4 authentication library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
