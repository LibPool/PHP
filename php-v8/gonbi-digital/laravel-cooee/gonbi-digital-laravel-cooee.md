# gonbi-digital/laravel-cooee

> 标签: cooee, errors, exceptions, health, heartbeat, laravel, monitoring, uptime

## 简介

Report your Laravel app's exceptions to Cooee, and answer its health checks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
