# m6web/store-receipt-validator

> 标签: apple, in app, itunes, purchase, receipt

## 简介

PHP receipt validator for Apple iTunes, Google Play and Amazon App Store Edit

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
