# hasinhayder/tyro-login

> 标签: Authentication, auth, blade, laravel, login, oauth, register, socialite, ui

## 简介

Complete authentication kit for Laravel 12 & 13 — 10 premium layouts, social OAuth, passkeys, TOTP 2FA, OTP, magic links, invitations, lockout, captcha and beautiful emails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
