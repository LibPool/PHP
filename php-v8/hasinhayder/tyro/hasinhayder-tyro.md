# hasinhayder/tyro

> 标签: Authentication, acl, api, authorization, laravel, permissions, privileges, rbac, roles, sanctum

## 简介

Tyro is a complete Authentication, Authorization, and Role & Privilege (RBAC/ACL) management solution for Laravel 12 & 13, built on Laravel Sanctum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
