> 标签: dev1191, districts, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, local-bodies, municipalities, nepal, nepali-address, provinces

## 简介

A comprehensive Nepali address plugin for Filament (v4 & v5) providing cascading selects, searchable columns, filters, infolists, and validation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
