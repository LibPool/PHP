# planafolio/finanzrechner

> 标签: calculator, etf, extension, finance, rechner, typo3, zinseszins

## 简介

Free German finance calculators (ETF savings plan, compound interest, capital gains tax, Vorabpauschale, pension gap and more) from Planafolio as a content element, with two-click privacy mode.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
