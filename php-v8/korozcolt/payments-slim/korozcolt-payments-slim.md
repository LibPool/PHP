# korozcolt/payments-slim

> 标签: Wompi, colombia, epayco, mercadopago, payments, psr-15, slim

## 简介

Slim 4 / PSR-15 adapter for korozcolt/payments-core: Wompi, MercadoPago and ePayco webhooks and checkout.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
