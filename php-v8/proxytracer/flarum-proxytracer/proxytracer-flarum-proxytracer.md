# proxytracer/flarum-proxytracer

> 标签: anti-spam, flarum, flarum-extension, proxy, security, tor, vpn-blocker

## 简介

Block VPNs, Proxies, and Tor nodes from signing up, logging in, or accessing your Flarum forum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
