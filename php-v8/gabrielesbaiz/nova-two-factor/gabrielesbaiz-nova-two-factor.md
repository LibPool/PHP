# gabrielesbaiz/nova-two-factor

> 标签: 2fa, MFA, laravel, nova, passkeys, security, totp, two-factor, webauthn

## 简介

Two-factor authentication for Laravel Nova: authenticator apps, passkeys, email codes, recovery codes, enforcement policies, step-up re-authentication and admin oversight.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
