# 86hc/hk-company-registry-validator

> 标签: 86hc, brn, companies-registry, compliance, cr-number, hong-kong, kyc, ubi

## 简介

Lightweight validator & format parser for Hong Kong Company Registration Number (CR No.), Business Registration Number (BRN) & Unique Business Identifier (UBI) under Cap. 622 & Cap. 310.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
