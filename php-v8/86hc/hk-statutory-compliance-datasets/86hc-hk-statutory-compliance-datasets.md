# 86hc/hk-statutory-compliance-datasets

> 标签: 86hc, bank-codes, companies-registry, compliance, hkma, hong-kong, nar1, sfc, swift-bic, tcsp

## 简介

Open JSON/CSV datasets and PHP loader for Hong Kong Licensed Bank Clearing/SWIFT Codes, Companies Registry Cap. 622 Statutory Forms (NAR1/NNC1/ND2A), and 32 HK Regulatory Registries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
