# astuteo/astuteo-pulse

> 标签: Craft, astuteo pulse, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Connecting Astuteo client sites to our monitor.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
