> 标签: crore, currency, desi-currency, desi-money-format, helpers, indian-currency, indian-currency-format, indian-paisa, lakh, laravel, money-format, paisa, rupee, tanka

## 简介

A little toolkit that teaches Laravel to count in rupees — because not every app runs on dollars.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
