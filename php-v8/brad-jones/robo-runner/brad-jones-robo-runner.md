# brad-jones/robo-runner

> 标签: custom, robo, runner, task

## 简介

A custom Robo Runner, that does not require an actual "RoboFile.php" just a class of your choosing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
