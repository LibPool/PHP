# kasera/kasera-pay

> 标签: indonesia, kasera, payment, payment-gateway, qris, virtual-account

## 简介

Official PHP SDK for Kasera Pay: accept QRIS and Virtual Account payments in Indonesia.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://pay.kasera.id
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/getkasera/kasera-pay-sdk.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/kasera%2Fkasera-pay

- Composer 安装：`composer require kasera/kasera-pay`
