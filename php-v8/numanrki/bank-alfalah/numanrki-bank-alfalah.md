## 简介

Bank Alfalah (Alfa) Payment Gateway SDK for PHP & Laravel. Supports Credit/Debit Card, Alfa Wallet, JazzCash, Raast QR, BNPL, Bank Account, and Card on Delivery.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
