# bohnmedia/contao-ideas-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Idea management for Contao 5: members submit ideas through a regular form, editors answer them in the back end, and a content element lists them in the front end.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
