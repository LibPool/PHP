# jibaymcs/filament-tour

> 标签: JibayMcs, driverjs, filament, filament-tour, filamentphp, laravel

## 简介

Bring the power of DriverJs to your Filament panels and start a tour !

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
