# innocode-digital/inncognito

> 标签: Cognito, aws, login, mu-plugin, oauth2, plugin, registration, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Login and Registration with user's AWS Cognito account.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
