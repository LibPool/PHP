# codein/recaptcha-enterprise-bundle

> 标签: bundle, captcha, form, recaptcha, recaptcha-enterprise, spam, symfony

## 简介

Symfony integration for Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (Assessments API). Fork of artack/recaptcha-enterprise-bundle.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
