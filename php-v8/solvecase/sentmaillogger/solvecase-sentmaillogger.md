# solvecase/sentmaillogger

> 标签: append, copy, email, event, imap, laravel, log, mail, message, pop3, sent

## 简介

Save a copy of laravel sent email in imap sent folder.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
