> 标签: block disposable, disposable, disposable check, email, email blacklist, email domain checker, email filtering, email validation rule, email validator, fake email, laravel, laravel custom rule, laravel email rule, temporary email, validation

## 简介

A Laravel package to detect and block disposable email addresses.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
