# jascha030/xerox

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Framework, heavily inspired by Symfony, Laravel and roots/bedrock, aiming to help relieve your "Word-Pression".

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
