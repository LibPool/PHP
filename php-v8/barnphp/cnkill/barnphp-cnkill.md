# barnphp/cnkill

> 标签: clean, cleaner, clear, cnkill, composer, delete, disk, free, node_modules, npkill, remove, space, storage, vendor, vendorkill

## 简介

Remove composer vendor and node_modules directories in your projects to save disk space.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
