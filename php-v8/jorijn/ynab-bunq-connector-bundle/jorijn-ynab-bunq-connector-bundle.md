# jorijn/ynab-bunq-connector-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Symfony 3.4 LTS compatible Bundle for uploading payments from the bunq Bundle to YNAB

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
