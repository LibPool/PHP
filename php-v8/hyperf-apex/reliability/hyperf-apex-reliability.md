# hyperf-apex/reliability

> 标签: apex, hyperf, idempotency, rate-limit, swoole

## 简介

Apex reliability suite (RateLimit, Idempotency, Outbox, Notify, Outbound) for Hyperf

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
