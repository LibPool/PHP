# aelvan/mailchimp-subscribe

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, mailchimp

## 简介

Simple Craft plugin for subscribing to a MailChimp audience.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
