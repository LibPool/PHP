# harmovich67/ai-translator

> 标签: Gemini, ai, inline-editor, laravel, localization, translation

## 简介

Framework-agnostic translations, inline live editing, full-page Gemini translation, and first-class Laravel integration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
