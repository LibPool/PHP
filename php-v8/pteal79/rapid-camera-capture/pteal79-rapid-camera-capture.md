# pteal79/rapid-camera-capture

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A NativePHP plugin for persistent camera capture — take multiple photos in one session, saved to mobile_public disk with UUID filenames.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
