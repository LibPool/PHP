# majistar/module-product-labels

> 标签: badges, hyva, mage-os, magento2, magento2-module, product labels

## 简介

Text and image product labels for Magento 2 and Mage-OS with a Hyvä storefront: product page, listings, cart, mini-cart and checkout.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
