# reactll/connect

> 标签: analytics, laravel, leads, orders, reactll

## 简介

Reactll Connect for Laravel: visits, clicks, form leads, sales and site health for your Reactll dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
