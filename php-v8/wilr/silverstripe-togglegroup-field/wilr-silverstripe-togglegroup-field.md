# wilr/silverstripe-togglegroup-field

> 标签: cms, field, form, silverstripe, toggle group

## 简介

Toggle group form fields for the Silverstripe CMS, styled after the shadcn/ui toggle group component. Provides drop-in replacements for OptionsetField and CheckboxSetField.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
