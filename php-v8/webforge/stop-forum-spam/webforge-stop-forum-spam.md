# webforge/stop-forum-spam

> 标签: anti-spam, spam

## 简介

a little tool to incorporate the great data from http://www.stopforumspam.com/

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
