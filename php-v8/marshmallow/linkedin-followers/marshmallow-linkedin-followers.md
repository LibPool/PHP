# marshmallow/linkedin-followers

> 标签: laravel, linkedin-followers, marshmallow-packages

## 简介

A simple Laravel package to get the amount of followers from a LinkedIn page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
