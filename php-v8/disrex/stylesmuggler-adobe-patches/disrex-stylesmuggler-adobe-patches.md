# disrex/stylesmuggler-adobe-patches

> 标签: apsb26-146, cve-2026-75650, mage-os, magento, patch, security, stylesmuggler

## 简介

Applies Adobe's official APSB26-146 (CVE-2026-75650) StyleSmuggler fix to Magento Open Source and Mage-OS automatically, detecting your version. No cweagans/composer-patches or enable-patching needed.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
