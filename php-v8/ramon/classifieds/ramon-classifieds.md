# ramon/classifieds

> 标签: ads, classifieds, discussion, flarum, marketplace

## 简介

Custom classified-ads composer, OLX-style listing pages and per-tag classifieds support for Flarum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
