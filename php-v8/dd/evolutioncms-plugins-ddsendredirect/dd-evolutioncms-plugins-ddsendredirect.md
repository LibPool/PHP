> 标签: MODX Evo, dd group, dd studio, ddsendredirect, divandesign, evo, evo cms, evolution cms, evolutioncms, modx, modx evolution, redirect url, send redirect

## 简介

Redirects from required non-existent URLs to required target URLs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
