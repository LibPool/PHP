# instride/opendxp-campaigns

> 标签: email-marketing, mailchimp, newsletter, opendxp, opendxp-bundle

## 简介

OpenDXP bundle for campaign management, including newsletter and email marketing features.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
