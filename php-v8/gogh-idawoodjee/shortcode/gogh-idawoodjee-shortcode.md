# gogh-idawoodjee/shortcode

> 标签: base62, encoder, short-url, shortcode, uuid

## 简介

Encode integers and UUIDs to short, URL-safe Base62 strings and back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
