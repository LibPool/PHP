# witify/notifications

> 标签: herald, laravel, notifications, witify

## 简介

Herald: the notifications of the Witify Laravel applications, with their previews, editable texts, per-user channel settings, inbox and schedule. PHP and Vue in one package.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
