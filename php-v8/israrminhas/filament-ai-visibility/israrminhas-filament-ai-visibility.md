# israrminhas/filament-ai-visibility

> 标签: ai-visibility, brand-monitoring, filament, filament-plugin, generative-engine-optimization, geo, laravel, llm-seo

## 简介

Track how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok answers, with competitor intelligence. A Filament v4 & v5 plugin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
