# anja/ghost-hunter-bundle

> 标签: assets, cleaner, symfony, twig, unused

## 简介

A quality assistant to hunt down unused files (templates & assets) in Symfony.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
