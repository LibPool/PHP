# venusian-surface/drawing

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Surface drawing: engine-free drawing contracts, the Painter and GPU regions over gpu.<engine>, the Rasterizer and five CPU engines over cpu.<engine>.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
