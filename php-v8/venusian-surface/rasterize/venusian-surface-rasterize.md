# venusian-surface/rasterize

> 标签: antialiasing, rasterize, scanline, surface, venusian

## 简介

Surface rasterize: shapes into coverage spans, hard-edged or anti-aliased, with the geometry in PHP or in C through ext-rasterize.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
