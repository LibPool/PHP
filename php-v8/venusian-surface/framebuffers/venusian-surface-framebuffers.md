# venusian-surface/framebuffers

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Surface framebuffers: the php storage driver (packings, PackedGrid, five buffer kinds), PixelMapper, and the framebuffer.<driver> manager.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
