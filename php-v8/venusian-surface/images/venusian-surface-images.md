# venusian-surface/images

> 标签: images, jpeg, png, surface, tiff, venusian

## 简介

Surface images: PNG, JPEG and TIFF bytes into RGBA8 framebuffers, through ext-gd and PHP or in C through ext-imgdec.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
