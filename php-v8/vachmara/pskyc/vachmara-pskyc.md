# vachmara/pskyc

> 标签: documents, gdpr, kyc, module, prestashop, security, verification

## 简介

Open source, GDPR-compliant KYC document verification for PrestaShop 8 and 9.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
