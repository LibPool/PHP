# hotash/octane-tenancy

> 标签: laravel, multi-database, multi-tenancy, tenancy

## 简介

Automatic multi-tenancy for your Laravel application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
