# pollora/metabox-bridge

> 标签: custom-tables, eloquent, meta-box, migrations, pollora, wordpress

## 简介

Connect Pollora to Meta Box data: migrations and Eloquent models for MB Custom Table.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
