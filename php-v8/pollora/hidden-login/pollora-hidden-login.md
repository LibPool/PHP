# pollora/hidden-login

> 标签: bedrock, brute-force, hardening, limit-login-attempts, login, security, wordpress

## 简介

Secures the WordPress login: serves it from a secret URL, answers wp-login.php and wp-admin with a 404, and locks out brute-force attempts

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
