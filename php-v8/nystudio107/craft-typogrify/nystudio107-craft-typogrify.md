# nystudio107/craft-typogrify

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, typogrify

## 简介

Typogrify prettifies your web typography by preventing ugly quotes and 'widows' and more

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
