# nystudio107/craft-icalendar

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, iCalendar

## 简介

Tools for parsing & formatting the RFC 2445 iCalendar (.ics) specification

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
