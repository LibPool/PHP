# nystudio107/disqus

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Integrates the Disqus commenting system into Craft CMS websites, including Single Sign On (SSO) and custom login/logout URLs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
