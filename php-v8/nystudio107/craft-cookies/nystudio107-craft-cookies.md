# nystudio107/craft-cookies

> 标签: Craft, cms, cookies, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

A simple plugin for setting and getting cookies from within Craft CMS templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
