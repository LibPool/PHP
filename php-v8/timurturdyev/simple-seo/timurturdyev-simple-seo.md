# timurturdyev/simple-seo

> 标签: JSON-LD, laravel, meta, open-graph, schema-org, seo, twitter-cards

## 简介

Framework-agnostic SEO head markup builder: meta, Open Graph, Twitter/X Cards and JSON-LD, with a lazy Laravel integration layer.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
