# cbcaio/image-attacher

> 标签: Flysystem, attacher, cbcaio, images, laravel, laravel5, upload

## 简介

Easy way to add images to your models trough polymorphic relationships

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
