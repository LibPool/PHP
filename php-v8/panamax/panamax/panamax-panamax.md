# panamax/panamax

> 标签: panamax, webtheory

## 简介

Collection of contracts and and implementations to facilitate design of psr-11 agnostic applications and frameworks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
