# harris21/laravel-fuse

> 标签: circuit-breaker, fuse, jobs, laravel, queue, resilience

## 简介

Circuit breaker for Laravel queue jobs. Protect your workers from cascading failures.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
