# sagoontech/magento2-price-decimal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Magento 2 Price Decimal, hide `.00` but show `.10`. So that $8.00 becomes $8 and $8.90 becomes $8.90

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
