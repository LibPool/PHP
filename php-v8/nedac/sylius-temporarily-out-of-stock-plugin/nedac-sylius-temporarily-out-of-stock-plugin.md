# nedac/sylius-temporarily-out-of-stock-plugin

> 标签: sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Sylius plugin that adds a ribbon to product images of products that are out of stock.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
