# micompress/micompress

> 标签: audio, compress, compression, image, jpeg, micompress, minify, optimize, pdf, png, video

## 简介

Compress images, PDFs, video, audio, documents & archives from PHP — powered by micompress.com

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
