# jack009/coming-soon-symfony-bundle

> 标签: bundle, coming-soon, maintenance, symfony

## 简介

A Symfony bundle to enable or disable a Coming Soon page for your application.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
