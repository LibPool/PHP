# ekumanov/flarum-ext-spam-alerts

> 标签: email, extension, flarum, moderation, notifications, spam

## 简介

Emails chosen moderators whenever FoF Anti-Spam automatically flags or holds a post or discussion.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
