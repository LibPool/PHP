# ekumanov/flarum-ext-auto-promote

> 标签: extension, flarum, groups, moderation, promotion, spam

## 简介

Promotes members to a trusted group once they have N approved posts, and lets staff hold suspected spam accounts back with a private watchlist.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
