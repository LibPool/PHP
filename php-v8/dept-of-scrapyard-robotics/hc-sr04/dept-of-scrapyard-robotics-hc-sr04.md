# dept-of-scrapyard-robotics/hc-sr04

> 标签: framework, hc-sr04, rangefinder, scrapyard-io, ultrasonic

## 简介

Drive HC-SR04 ultrasonic distance sensors over GPIO with PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
