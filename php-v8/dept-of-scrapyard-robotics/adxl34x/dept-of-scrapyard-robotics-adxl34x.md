# dept-of-scrapyard-robotics/adxl34x

> 标签: adxl343, adxl345, framework, scrapyard-io

## 简介

Drive ADXL343 & ADXL345 accelerometers over I2C/SPI with PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
