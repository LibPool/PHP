# dept-of-scrapyard-robotics/ssd1306

> 标签: framework, scrapyard-io, ssd1306

## 简介

Drive SSD1306 monochrome OLED displays over I2C/SPI with PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
