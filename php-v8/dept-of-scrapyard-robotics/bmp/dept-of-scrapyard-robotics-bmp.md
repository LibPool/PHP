# dept-of-scrapyard-robotics/bmp

> 标签: bmp280, framework, scrapyard-io

## 简介

Drive Barometric Pressure sensors from Bosch Sensortec over I2C/SPI with PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
