# deepayan/laraspeak

> 标签: chat, conversations, inbox, laravel, message, php, real-time, realtime

## 简介

laraspeak is a Laravel based realtime messaging, chatting and conversation system. It helps to develop users messaging, chatting and conversations in easy way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
