# yajima-tatsuro/jp-area-converter

> 标签: area, converter, japan, jo, php, square-meter, tsubo

## 简介

Convert between tsubo, jo, and square meters (Japanese area units).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
