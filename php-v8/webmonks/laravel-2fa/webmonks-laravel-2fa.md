# webmonks/laravel-2fa

> 标签: 2fa, Authentication, FIDO2, biometric, email, google-authenticator, hardware-keys, laravel, multi-auth, otp, plug-and-play, production-ready, recovery-codes, security, security-keys, sms, totp, twilio, two-factor, webauthn

## 简介

🔐 Plug & Play Laravel 2FA - Email OTP, SMS OTP, TOTP, WebAuthn/FIDO2, Recovery Codes with Multi-Auth Guard Support. Production-ready in 30 seconds!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
