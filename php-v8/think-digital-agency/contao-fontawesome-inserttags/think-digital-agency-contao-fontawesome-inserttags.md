# think-digital-agency/contao-fontawesome-inserttags

> 标签: FontAwesome, contao, contao-bundle, contao-extension, fa, font-awesome, fontawesome 6, icon, icons, insert-tag, inserttag, svg icons

## 简介

FontAwesome 6 insert tags for Contao 5 — use {{fas::rocket}}, {{far::circle-check}} or {{fab::github}} in any field, with optional modifier classes like spin or 2x.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
