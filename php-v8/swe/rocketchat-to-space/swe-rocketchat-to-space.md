# swe/rocketchat-to-space

> 标签: chat, jetbrains, migrate, php, rocket-chat, space

## 简介

Import your channels with its messages from Rocket.Chat to JetBrains Space.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
