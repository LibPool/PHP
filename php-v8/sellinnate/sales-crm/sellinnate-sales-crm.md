# sellinnate/sales-crm

> 标签: Sellinnate, crm, deals, kanban, laravel, livewire, pipeline, sales

## 简介

Sales pipeline CRM for Laravel: kanban board, deals, companies & people, activities, notes, email, documents, import, reports, saved views and MCP tools. Headless core with an optional Livewire UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
