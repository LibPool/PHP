# iranimij/openlabel

> 标签: badges, hyva, mage-os, magento2, openlabel, product-labels, stickers

## 简介

OpenLabel: product labels for Magento 2 and Hyvä. Rule-based labels with a dedicated indexer, zero layout shift, full page cache safe. Free and MIT.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
