# iranimij/openfilter

> 标签: ajax-filters, faceted-search, hyva, layered-navigation, mage-os, magento2, openfilter, opensearch

## 简介

OpenFilter: layered navigation for Magento 2 and Hyvä. Multi-select filters with correct counts, AJAX that is cache- and CSP-correct, price slider, SEO-safe URLs. Built on the OpenSearch Magento ships. Free and MIT.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
