# itul/quickbooks-desktop

> 标签: itul, laravel, quickbooks, quickbooks desktop

## 简介

The best way to connect to quickbooks desktop from laravel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
