# aqjw/shortcuts

> 标签: aqjw, filament, keyboard, laravel, shortcuts

## 简介

A Filament form field for recording and displaying custom keyboard shortcuts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
