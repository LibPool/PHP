# ernilambar/nepali-date

> 标签: BS, ad, bikram-sambat, calendar, conversion, converter, date, date-converter, gregorian, nepali, nepali-calendar

## 简介

Nepali (Bikram Sambat) calendar utilities and BS ↔ AD date conversion for PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
