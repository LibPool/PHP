# gravatarphp/gravatar-bundle

> 标签: bundle, gravatar-bundle, gravatarphp

## 简介

A bundle to integrate Gravatar into symfony

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
