# lenorix/datadis-client

> 标签: API-Client, CUPS, consumption, datadis, electricity, energy, psr-18, smart-meter, spain

## 简介

PHP client for Datadis, the platform where Spanish electricity distributors publish supply data: supplies, contracts, hourly and quarter-hourly consumption, maximum power, reactive energy and public open data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
