# mathis-vincent/maintenance-bundle

> 标签: bundle, maintenance, maintenance-mode, symfony

## 简介

A highly flexible and fully configurable Symfony bundle to handle multiple types of maintenance modes seamlessly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
