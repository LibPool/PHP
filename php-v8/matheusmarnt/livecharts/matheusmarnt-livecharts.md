# matheusmarnt/livecharts

> 标签: apexcharts, chartjs, laravel, livecharts, livewire, matheusmarnt

## 简介

Elevate your data visualization with reactive charts in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
