# karnoweb/livewire-datepicker

> 标签: Jalali, alpine, datepicker, laravel, livewire, persian

## 简介

Jalali/Gregorian datepicker for Livewire 3 & 4 with Alpine.js

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
