# tallyst/xf-redirect

> 标签: flarum, migration, redirect, seo, xenforo

## 简介

Permanent 301 redirects from old XenForo/vBulletin URLs to migrated Flarum content (SEO/link preservation).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
