# tempo/jsconfiguration-bundle

> 标签: configuration, javascript, symfony

## 简介

A pretty nice way to expose your configuration to your JavaScript.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
