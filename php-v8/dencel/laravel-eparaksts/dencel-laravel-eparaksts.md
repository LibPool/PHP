# dencel/laravel-eparaksts

> 标签: Authentication, SignAPI, eidas, eparaksts, laravel, signature

## 简介

Use Latvian eParaksts in your Laravel app.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
