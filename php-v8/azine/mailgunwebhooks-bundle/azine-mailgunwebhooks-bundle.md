# azine/mailgunwebhooks-bundle

> 标签: email, mailgun, symfony, webhooks

## 简介

Symfony bundle to capture Mailgun webhook events and send delivery-health notifications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
