> 标签: OpenRouter, ai, chatbot, groq, laravel, laravel-ai-chatbot, openai

## 简介

Laravel AI Chatbot — scoped AI chat for Laravel with Groq, OpenRouter, and OpenAI support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
