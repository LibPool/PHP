# dineshuprety/laravel-zip-bomb-guard

> 标签: laravel, security, validation, zip, zip-bomb

## 简介

Guard your Laravel app against zip bombs (decompression bombs) and zip-slip attacks: inspect any archive before you extract it.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
