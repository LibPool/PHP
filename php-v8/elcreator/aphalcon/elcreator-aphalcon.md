# elcreator/aphalcon

> 标签: evo, evocms, evolution, evolution-cms, latte, phalcon

## 简介

Phalcon inside Evolution CMS: one DI on the site's database, Phalcon services in Latte templates, and Phalcon routes that render CMS views

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
