# elcreator/amount

> 标签: Flysystem, backup, cloud, dropbox, evo, evocms, evolution, evolution-cms, google-drive, s3, storage

## 简介

aMount — mount cloud and remote storage (S3, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SFTP, WebDAV and more, via Flysystem) into the Evolution CMS manager: pick folders, and big files such as backups are uploaded in the background, per manager, within what that manager may see.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
