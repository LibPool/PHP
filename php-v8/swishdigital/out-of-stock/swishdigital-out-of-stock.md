# swishdigital/out-of-stock

> 标签: Craft, Out Of Stock, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Get notified when products are (almost) out of stock.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
