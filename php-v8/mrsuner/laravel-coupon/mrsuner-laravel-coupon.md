# mrsuner/laravel-coupon

> 标签: admin, boilerplate, coupon, discount, laravel, redemption

## 简介

Coupon lifecycle management (generation, validation, redemption) for Laravel, decoupled from billing via a single event. Works standalone and auto-wires into mrsuner/laravel-api-boilerplate.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
