# wieni/wmprotected_vocabulary

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds the possibility to protect taxonomy terms from being deleted when they are being referenced in certain fields.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
