# adrolli/filament-job-manager

> 标签: batches, failed-jobs, filament, jobs, laravel, monitor, queues, redis

## 简介

Manage Job Queues, Failed Jobs and Batches in Filament.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
