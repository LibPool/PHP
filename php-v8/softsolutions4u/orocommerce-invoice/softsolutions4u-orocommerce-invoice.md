# softsolutions4u/orocommerce-invoice

> 标签: ORO, OroCommerce, b2b, invoice, invoicing, payment, pdf, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Invoice management for OroCommerce Community Edition (CE) 7.0.3: create invoices from orders, PDF invoices, email notifications, bank-transfer details and storefront invoice payments.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
