# frozenplain/flarum-ext-connect

> 标签: flarum

## 简介

Optional sign-in to the FrozenPlain forum with a frozenplain.com account.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
