# justinbusschau/hmrc-gift-aid

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A library for charities and CASCs to claim Gift Aid (including Small Donations) from HMRC

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
