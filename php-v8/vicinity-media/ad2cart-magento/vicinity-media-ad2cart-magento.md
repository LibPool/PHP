# vicinity-media/ad2cart-magento

> 标签: magento2

## 简介

Enable customers to add items to their shopping cart on Magento, directly from a digital catalog.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
