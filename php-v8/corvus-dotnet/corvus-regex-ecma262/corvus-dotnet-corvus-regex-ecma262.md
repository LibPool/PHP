# corvus-dotnet/corvus-regex-ecma262

> 标签: ECMAScript, ecma262, extension, javascript, regex, regexp

## 简介

ECMA-262 (JavaScript) regular expressions as a PHP extension: the grammar and the answers of RegExp, every flag, UTF-8 and UTF-16, backed by Rust.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
