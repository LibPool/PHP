# capell-app/content-sections

> 标签: capell, cms, content-sections, filamentphp, laravel

## 简介

A free catalog of 17 reusable, blueprint-backed page sections (hero, FAQ, pricing, stats, team, comparison, timeline, and more) for Capell, rendered through layout-builder and block-library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
