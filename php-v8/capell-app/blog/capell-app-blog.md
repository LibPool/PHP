# capell-app/blog

> 标签: blog, capell, cms, filamentphp, laravel

## 简介

Editorial publishing for Capell: articles, archives, optional tag pages, related article widgets, multilingual output, and growth bridges.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
