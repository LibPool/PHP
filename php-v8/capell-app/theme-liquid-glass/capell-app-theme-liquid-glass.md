# capell-app/theme-liquid-glass

> 标签: capell, cms, laravel, theme

## 简介

A free Liquid Glass website theme for modern Capell sites with translucent surfaces, crisp content rhythm, and three polished presets.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
