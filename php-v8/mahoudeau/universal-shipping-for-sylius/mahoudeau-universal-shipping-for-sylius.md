# mahoudeau/universal-shipping-for-sylius

> 标签: address-autocomplete, geocoding, mondial-relay, pickup-point, sendcloud, shipping, sylius, sylius-plugin

## 简介

Shipping carriers for Sylius 2: pickup point picker at checkout, labels, tracking, and French address autocomplete. Mondial Relay through Sendcloud first.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
