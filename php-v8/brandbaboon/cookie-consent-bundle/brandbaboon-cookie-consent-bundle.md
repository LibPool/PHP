# brandbaboon/cookie-consent-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Symfony bundle to ask the visitors for their consent to use cookies to comply to AVG/GDPR.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
