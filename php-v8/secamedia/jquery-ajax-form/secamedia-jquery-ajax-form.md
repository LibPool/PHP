# secamedia/jquery-ajax-form

> 标签: ajax, form, jquery, wrapper

## 简介

wraps form submits with a callback and also submits clicked buttons

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
