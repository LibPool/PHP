# c975l/payment-bundle

> 标签: bundle, payment, stripe, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle providing the generic basket and checkout engine plus Stripe payments for the c975L ecosystem — any bundle plugs its own sellable items in through BasketItemProviderInterface.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
