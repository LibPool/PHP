# notedis/craft-notedis

> 标签: Craft, bug tracking, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, feedback, notedis, widget

## 简介

Embed a Notedis.com feedback widget on your Craft CMS site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
