# supermaker/ai-video-downloader

> 标签: UTM, ai-video, seo, supermaker, video-downloader

## 简介

Generate SuperMaker AI Video Downloader links (links + HTML anchors) pointing to https://supermaker.ai/video/.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
