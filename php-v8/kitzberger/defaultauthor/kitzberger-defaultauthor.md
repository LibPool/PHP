# kitzberger/defaultauthor

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Prefills the author/email fields in pages, sys_notes and news to the info of the currently logged in user.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
