# axeldotdev/laravel-airtable

> 标签: axeldotdev, laravel-airtable

## 简介

laravel-airtable allows you to import your Airtable data into your Laravel database.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
