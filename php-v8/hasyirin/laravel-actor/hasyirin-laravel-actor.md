# hasyirin/laravel-actor

> 标签: actions, activity-log, audit-log, laravel, laravel-actor, tracking

## 简介

A polymorphic action log for Eloquent models — track who did what, when.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
