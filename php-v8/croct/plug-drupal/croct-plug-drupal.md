# croct/plug-drupal

> 标签: croct, drupal, module, personalization, php

## 简介

Drupal module to plug your Drupal site into Croct.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/croct-tech/plug-drupal
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/croct-tech/plug-drupal.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/croct%2Fplug-drupal

- 0.1.0

## 获取地址

- Composer 安装：`composer require croct/plug-drupal`
- Packagist 仓库：https://repo.packagist.org/p2/croct/plug-drupal.json
