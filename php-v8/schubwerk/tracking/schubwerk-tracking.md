# schubwerk/tracking

> 标签: analytics, extension, schubwerk, tracking, typo3

## 简介

This plugin allows you to integrate Schubwerk server-side tracking into your website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
