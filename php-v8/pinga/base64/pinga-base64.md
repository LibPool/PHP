# pinga/base64

> 标签: URL-safe, base-64, base64, decode, decoding, encode, encoding, url

## 简介

Simple and convenient Base64 encoding and decoding for PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
