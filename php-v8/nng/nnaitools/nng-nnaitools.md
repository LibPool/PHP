# nng/nnaitools

> 标签: ChatGpt, accessibility, ai, alt text, typo3

## 简介

Automatically generate the alt- and title texts when saving images. Or batch-process all images in the fileadmin. Multilanguage support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
