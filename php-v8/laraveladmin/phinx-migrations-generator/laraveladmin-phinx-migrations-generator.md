# laraveladmin/phinx-migrations-generator

> 标签: dev-tool, migration, phinx, thinkphp

## 简介

Dev-only wrapper around odan/phinx-migrations-generator with an isolated runtime, avoiding think-migration's bundled Phinx fork.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
