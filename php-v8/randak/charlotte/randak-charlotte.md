# randak/charlotte

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Charlotte crawls through your website and captures data in a database, including links between pages, H1 elements, scripts, stylesheets, and keywords.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
