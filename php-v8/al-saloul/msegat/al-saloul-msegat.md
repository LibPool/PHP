# al-saloul/msegat

> 标签: laravel, msegat, notifications, otp, saudi-arabia, sms

## 简介

A Laravel package for the Msegat SMS gateway. Its goal is to remove the complexity.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
