# sgalinski/sg-ai

> 标签: accessibility, ai, alt-text, backend, content-generation, editorial-workflow, image-generation, openai, queue-processing, scheduler, seo, sgai, sgalinski, typo3, typo3-extension

## 简介

SGAI by sgalinski - AI editorial suite for TYPO3 with alt text generation, AI image and text creation, SEO title and description automation, technical SEO, content SEO and accessibility recommendations, queue-based background processing, backend dashboard and history tooling, and EU AI Act image transparency support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
