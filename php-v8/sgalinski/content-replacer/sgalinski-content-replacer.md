# sgalinski/content-replacer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Content Replacer & Term Linking - Replace defined terms in TYPO3 content with linked or formatted output, including TypoScript rules, categories, and RTE integration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
