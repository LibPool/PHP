# m2e/otto-adobe-commerce

> 标签: m2e, magento, otto, otto market

## 简介

M2E Otto Connect is an Adobe Commerce trusted (TM) extension that allows merchants of all sizes to integrate Magento-based system(s) into Otto Market fully

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
