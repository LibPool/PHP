# m2e/shein-magento-adobe-commerce

> 标签: m2e, magento, sell on shein, selling on shein, shein, shein connector, shein extension for magento 2, shein magento

## 简介

Start selling on Shein with M2E Shein Connect. This native Adobe Commerce extension lets you add and categorize Magento products on Shein while efficiently managing orders and inventory

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
