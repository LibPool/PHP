# m2e/temu-magento-adobe-commerce

> 标签: m2e, magento, sell on temu, selling on temu, temu, temu connector, temu extension for magento 2

## 简介

M2E Temu Connect is a trusted Adobe Commerce extension that lets you add and categorize Magento products on Temu while efficiently managing orders and inventory. List your products and start selling on Temu with M2E

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
