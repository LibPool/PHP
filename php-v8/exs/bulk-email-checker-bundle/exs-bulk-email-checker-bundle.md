# exs/bulk-email-checker-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, bundle, email, validation

## 简介

This bundle provides an email validation through 'Bulk Email Checker' api.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
