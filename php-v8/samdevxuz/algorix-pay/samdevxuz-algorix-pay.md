# samdevxuz/algorix-pay

> 标签: Uzbekistan, click, laravel, mtproto, p2p, payme, payment, userbot, uzum

## 简介

Userbot-based P2P payment receiver for Laravel — listen to bank notification bots (Click, Payme, Uzum) via MTProto and emit PaymentReceived events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
