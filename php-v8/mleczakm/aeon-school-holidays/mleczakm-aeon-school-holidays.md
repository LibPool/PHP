# mleczakm/aeon-school-holidays

> 标签: School, aeon, calendar, holidays, poland

## 简介

Polish school holiday calendars for Aeon PHP, including voivodeship-specific winter breaks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
