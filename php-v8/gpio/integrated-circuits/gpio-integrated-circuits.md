# gpio/integrated-circuits

> 标签: GPIO, integrated-circuits, scrapyard-io

## 简介

GeneralPurposeIO integrated-circuit bases: the bootable chip and the data-register breakout.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
