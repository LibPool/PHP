# gpio/digital

> 标签: GPIO, digital-io, scrapyard-io

## 简介

GeneralPurposeIO digital IO: the gpio.digital connection manager, driver, factory and pin transports.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
