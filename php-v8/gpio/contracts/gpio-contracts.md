# gpio/contracts

> 标签: GPIO, contracts, scrapyard-io

## 简介

GeneralPurposeIO contracts: transports, protocol enums and exceptions, the gpio dock resource, its sources and its mail.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
