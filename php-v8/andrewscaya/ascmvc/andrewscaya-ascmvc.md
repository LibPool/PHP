# andrewscaya/ascmvc

> 标签: ascmvc, framework, lightmvc, middleware, mvc, swoole

## 简介

A modular, event-driven and Swoole-enabled framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
