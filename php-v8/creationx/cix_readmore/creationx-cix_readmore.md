# creationx/cix_readmore

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Splits longer texts into preview and full content, with a reveal button. It offers customizable templates for easy styling.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
