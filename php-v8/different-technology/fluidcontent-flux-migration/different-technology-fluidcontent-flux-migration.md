# different-technology/fluidcontent-flux-migration

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Migrate your TYPO3 content from fluidcontent to flux - different.technology

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
