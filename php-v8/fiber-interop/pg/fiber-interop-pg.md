# fiber-interop/pg

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Cooperative pg_query() for fiber-interop/api: fiber\pg\query(), query_params(), start() and connect() on any adapter, waiting on the libpq socket.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
