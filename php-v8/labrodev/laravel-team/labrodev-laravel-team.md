# labrodev/laravel-team

> 标签: Teams, billing, labrodev, laravel, plans, saas

## 简介

Single-team accounts, member invitations, roles and plan/billing mechanics for Laravel SaaS applications — the Labrodev team core.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
