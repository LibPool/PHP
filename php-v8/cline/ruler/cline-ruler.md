# cline/ruler

> 标签: Context, DSL, Evaluation, business-rules, conditional-logic, fluent-interface, operators, php, proposition, rule-engine

## 简介

Fluent rule engine with proposition-based evaluation and 50+ operators for building conditional business logic

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
