# lyonstahl/fips

> 标签: census, county, federal information processing standard, fips, state, united states, usa

## 简介

Offline Census FIPS lookups for U.S. states, counties, and county equivalents.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
