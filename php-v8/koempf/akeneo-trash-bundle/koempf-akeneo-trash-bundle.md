# koempf/akeneo-trash-bundle

> 标签: PIM, akeneo, trash

## 简介

Put products, models and categories into own trash tables before deleting them in the original tables.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
