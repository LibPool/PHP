# wildbit/craft3-donottrack

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, do not track

## 简介

Craft 3 plugin for respecting Do Not Track headers sent by browsers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
