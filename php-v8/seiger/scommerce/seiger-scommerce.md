# seiger/scommerce

> 标签: catalog, e-commerce, ecommerce, ecommerce-tools, evocms, evolution-cms, online-store, shop

## 简介

A powerful, flexible, and extensible e-commerce solution for Evolution CMS, designed to build modern online stores with product catalogs, shopping carts, checkout, order management, payments, APIs, and integrations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
