# andronewille/sluis

> 标签: Anonymisation, Dutch, gdpr, offline, pii

## 简介

Masks personal data in Dutch text on its way into an AI system, and puts it back on the way out. Offline, no dependency.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
