# werkint/prerender-bundle

> 标签: MVC js, prerender

## 简介

Allows you to easily use prerender for your Symfony2 apps running Client-Side MVC

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
