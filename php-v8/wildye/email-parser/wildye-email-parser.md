# wildye/email-parser

> 标签: api, dkim, email, inbound, parser, transactional, webhook

## 简介

PHP client for the GhostParse transactional email API: send email and verify/parse inbound email webhooks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
