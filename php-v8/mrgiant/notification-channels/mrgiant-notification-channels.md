# mrgiant/notification-channels

> 标签: laravel, notification-channels, notifications

## 简介

A collection of custom notification providers, offering seamless integration with various channels to enhance your application's messaging capabilities.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
