# jarrett/rockpaperscissorsspocklizard

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Based on a game created by Sam Kass and Karen Bryla and made popular by "Big Bang Theory"

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
