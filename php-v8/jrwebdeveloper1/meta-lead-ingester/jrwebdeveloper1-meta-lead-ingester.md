# jrwebdeveloper1/meta-lead-ingester

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Standalone plug-and-play Composer package for capturing, verifying, and ingesting Meta Lead Ads in real-time.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
