# idci/extra-form-bundle

> 标签: Form Type, configuration, dynamic, form

## 简介

Symfony bundle that expand existing FormType feature to allow a dynamic form generation

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
