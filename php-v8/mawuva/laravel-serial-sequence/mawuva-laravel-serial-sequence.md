# mawuva/laravel-serial-sequence

> 标签: Ephraim Seddor, laravel, laravel-serial-sequence

## 简介

A flexible, transactional, and extensible serial number generator for Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
