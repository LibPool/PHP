# menuella/food-safety

> 标签: allergens, food-safety, i18n, lmiv, menu, restaurant

## 简介

Open allergen and additive vocabulary for restaurant menus: 28 allergen keys from EU Reg. 1169/2011 Annex II, 22 declarations, footnote codes, icons and 50 languages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
