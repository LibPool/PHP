# id-sign/business-registers

> 标签: DPH, ISIR, adis, ares, czech, dic, ico, insolvency, vat, vies

## 简介

Typed PHP client for Czech business registers: ARES, VAT register (ADIS), VIES and the insolvency register (ISIR).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
