# ojessecruz/simple-blog

> 标签: blog, jessecruz, laravel, livewire, markdown, simple-blog

## 简介

A Laravel lightweight blog to quickly publish and share ideas.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
