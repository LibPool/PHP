# ojessecruz/resend-inbox-bundle

> 标签: bundle, email, helpdesk, inbound, inbox, resend, symfony

## 简介

A shared email inbox for Symfony apps, powered by Resend inbound email

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
