# ojessecruz/laravel-resend-inbox

> 标签: email, inbound, inbox, jessecruz, laravel, livewire, resend

## 简介

A shared inbox for your Laravel admin on top of Resend inbound email: conversations, replies, delivery status and Livewire screens.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
