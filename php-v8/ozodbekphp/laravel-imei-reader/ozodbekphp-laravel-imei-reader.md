# ozodbekphp/laravel-imei-reader

> 标签: EAN13, barcode, base64, code128, code39, imei, imei-reader, laravel, luhn, php, qr-code

## 简介

High-performance PHP & Laravel package to decode barcodes and extract 15-digit IMEIs from Base64 images with Luhn validation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
