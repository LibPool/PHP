# onlinegame/panel

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Laravel Admin Package for The Control Group to make your life easier and steer your project in the right direction,Powered in Karo Studio

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
