# epiphany/service-cache-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, cache, container, service

## 简介

Cache or store responses from a Symfony2 service by annotating its methods

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
