# vintage/yii2-invisible-recaptcha

> 标签: captcha, google, invisible, re-captcha, widget, yii2

## 简介

Facade of Invisible reCAPTCHA by Google for Yii2 Framework

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
