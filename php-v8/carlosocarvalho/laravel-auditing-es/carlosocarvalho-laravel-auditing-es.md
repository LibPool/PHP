> 标签: Accountability, Audit, auditing, changes, elasticsearch, eloquent, es, history, laravel, log, logging, lumen, observer, record, revision, tracking

## 简介

A elasticsearch driver for the owen-it/laravel-auditing package. Allows storage of the audits in elasticsearch.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
