# mage-os/module-shopping-feed

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Product feed generation for Mage-OS and Magento Open Source, including Google Shopping, Local Inventory, and Promotions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
