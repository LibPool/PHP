# binarybuilds/filament-failed-jobs

> 标签: binarybuilds, failed jobs, filament, laravel, queued jobs

## 简介

A filament package to easily retry and prune failed jobs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
