# enso-san/blade-gms-s

> 标签: blade, google, laravel, material, symbols

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Googles Material Design Symbols (Sharp) in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
