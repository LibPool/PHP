# sohbatiha/nova-posts

> 标签: laravel, laravel-package, nova, nova-package, php, posts-crud

## 简介

A powerful package to add posts resource to nova panel .

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
