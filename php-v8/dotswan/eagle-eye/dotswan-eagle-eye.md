# dotswan/eagle-eye

> 标签: Audit, activity-log, audit-log, dotswan, laravel, loki, observability

## 简介

Driver-agnostic activity and audit logging for Laravel with automatic capture, privacy redaction, trace correlation, and database or Grafana Loki storage.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
