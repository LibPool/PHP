# enm1989/geckodriver

> 标签: firefox, geckodriver, selenium, webdriver

## 简介

Composer distribution of Geckodriver. Adds a executable to your composer bin directory.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
