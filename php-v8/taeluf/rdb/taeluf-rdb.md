# taeluf/rdb

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Custom property getters on beans, better error reporting, sql-verbs as an API. And some other quality of life stuff.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
