# mailtea/mailtea

> 标签: email, mailtea, newsletter, sdk, transactional-email, webhooks

## 简介

The official PHP SDK for Mailtea — send, schedule, and manage email from your app or AI agent.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
