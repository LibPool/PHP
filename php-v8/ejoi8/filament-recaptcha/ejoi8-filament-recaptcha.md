# ejoi8/filament-recaptcha

> 标签: captcha, filament, laravel, recaptcha, security, spam

## 简介

Configurable Google reCAPTCHA (v2 checkbox & v3 invisible) field for Filament forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
