# shazzoo/legal-pages-plugin

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Legal pages for Content Studio: terms and conditions, disclaimer, responsible disclosure and privacy policy.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
