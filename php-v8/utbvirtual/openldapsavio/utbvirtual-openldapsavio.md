# utbvirtual/openldapsavio

> 标签: auth, laravel, openldap, utbvirtual

## 简介

An openldap auth driver for Laravel 5 and UTB Autentication.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
