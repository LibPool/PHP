# geekcodev/filament-max-broadcasts

> 标签: Broadcast, filament, laravel, livewire, max, max messenger, newsletter

## 简介

Filament plugin: mass broadcasts to MAX messenger users, on top of geekcodev/laravel-max-client.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
