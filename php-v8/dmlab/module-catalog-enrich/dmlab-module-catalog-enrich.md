# dmlab/module-catalog-enrich

> 标签: PIM, ai, attribute enrichment, catalog enrichment, filters, jev, layered navigation, llm, magento 2, magento2, magento2-module, product attributes, product data quality, typesafe

## 简介

AI product attribute enrichment for Magento 2: fills empty select, multiselect and Yes/No attributes (layered navigation filters) from product descriptions, with review queue, auto-apply and revert.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
