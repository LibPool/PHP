# luisinder/social-metatags

> 标签: meta, open graph, opengraph, seo, social, tags, twitter, twitter cards

## 简介

Lightweight PHP library to generate common Twitter Card & Open Graph meta tags.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
