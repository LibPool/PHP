# anahkiasen/isaac-mod-manager

> 标签: isaac, mods

## 简介

IMM is a cross-plateform CLI-tool to manage your Workshop mods for Binding of Isaac

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
