# glueful/thallo-subscriptions

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Workspace SaaS billing for Thallo — platform plans and per-workspace subscriptions, as a removable capability pack.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
