# renderbit/faq-jsonld

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

WordPress plugin to manage FAQ items as CPT and inject FAQ JSON-LD with advanced caching and queueing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
