# nodonhq/filament-bodega-lite

> 标签: dark-mode, filament, filament-theme, laravel, small-business, theme

## 简介

Bodega Lite: a warm, highly legible Filament theme for shops and small businesses, with a soft dark mode.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
