# libra/libra-article-image-zooming

> 标签: ZendFramework, cms, libra, zf, zf2, zooming

## 简介

Add image zooming in articles of Libra CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
