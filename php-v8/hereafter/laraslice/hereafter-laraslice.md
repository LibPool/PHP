# hereafter/laraslice

> 标签: ai, blatui, clean-architecture, code-generator, flutter, laravel, laravel-13, llm, mcp, modular, odoo, vertical-slice

## 简介

Future-Ready Clean Architecture & Vertical Slice Ecosystem for Enterprise, Gov & SaaS (Laravel & Flutter). Complete End-to-End SDLC from Design to Deploy via Visual UI, CLI & AI Prompts (IDE & Web MCP). Auto-generates Web, Mobile APIs, RBAC, Seeding & Audit Trails. By Hereafter Solutions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
