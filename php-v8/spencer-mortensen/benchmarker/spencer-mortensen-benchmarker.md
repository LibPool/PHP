# spencer-mortensen/benchmarker

> 标签: benchmark, php

## 简介

Benchmark snippets of code, gathering the mean and standard deviation for each snippet

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
