# bitandblack/colors

> 标签: acb, adobe, adobe color book, cielab, cmy, cmyk, color, complementary, hex, hks, hsl, hsla, lab, pantone, php, ral, rgb, rgba, xyz

## 简介

Handle colors and their systems easily. Based on Adobe InDesigns color books.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
