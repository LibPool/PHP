# shipwake/laravel-ai-crawl

> 标签: ai, analytics, crawler, laravel, observability, shipwake

## 简介

Server-side AI crawler tracking middleware for Laravel applications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
