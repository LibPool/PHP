# sayamaapps/kirby-kehai

> 标签: csp, digistore24, gdpr, kirby, kirby-5, kirby-plugin, privacy, social-proof

## 简介

Honest social proof for Kirby CMS: real, recent purchases (Digistore24) as a small notice or a summary line, served from your own server. No third-party request when the page loads, no personal data, no cookies, no inline script.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
