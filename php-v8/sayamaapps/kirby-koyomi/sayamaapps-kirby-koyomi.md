# sayamaapps/kirby-koyomi

> 标签: JSON-LD, calendar, events, iCalendar, ics, kirby, kirby-5, kirby-plugin, privacy, schema-org

## 简介

Event calendar for Kirby CMS 5: events, reusable venues and organizers, status and online/hybrid attendance, ICS feeds, JSON-LD and RSS. No third-party requests; online meeting links stay private.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
