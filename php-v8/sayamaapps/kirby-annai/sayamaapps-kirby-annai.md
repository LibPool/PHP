# sayamaapps/kirby-annai

> 标签: JSON-LD, Sitemap, kirby, kirby-5, kirby-plugin, llms-txt, local-business, open-graph, robots-txt, schema-org, seo

## 简介

Everything search engines and AI agents read from a Kirby CMS 5 site: robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, JSON-LD for a local business, meta description, canonical, Open Graph, noindex and hreflang. Opening hours from Noren, events from Koyomi when installed. No styling.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
