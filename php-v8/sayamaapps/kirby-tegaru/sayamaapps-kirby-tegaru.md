# sayamaapps/kirby-tegaru

> 标签: exif, gps, images, kirby, kirby-5, kirby-plugin, mobile, panel, privacy, upload

## 简介

Lighter photo uploads from the phone into the Kirby Panel: JPEG, PNG and WebP photos are resized in the browser before they travel, and the re-encoded file carries no EXIF or GPS; the server keeps a byte and pixel cap as a safety net.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
