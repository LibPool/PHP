# cmsgears/widget-form-ajax

> 标签: ajax form, cmsgears, form, widget, yii, yii2

## 简介

The Ajax Form Widget for CMSGears. It provide options to generate ajax forms stored in db.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
