# nawasara/aspirations

> 标签: Nawasara, aspirations, citizen-report, complaints, govtech, laravel, sla

## 简介

Citizen complaint and aspiration channel for the Nawasara superapp framework — Lapor Bunda: binding SLA, automatic dispatch by category, in-department verification, and leadership monitoring.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
