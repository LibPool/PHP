# nawasara/pdam

> 标签: Nawasara, citizen-api, laravel, pdam, ponorogo, water-bill

## 简介

Water bill lookup for the Nawasara superapp framework: proxies the PUDAM Tirta Katong billing API, serves unpaid bills to logged-in residents, and keeps the connections they save.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
