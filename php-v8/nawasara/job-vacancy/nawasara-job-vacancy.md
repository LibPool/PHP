# nawasara/job-vacancy

> 标签: Nawasara, job, job-vacancy, laravel, recruitment, vacancy

## 简介

Job vacancy module for the Nawasara superapp framework — listing and detail, synced from an external service management platform. English port of nawasara/loker.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
