# siteation/magento2-hyva-icons-payment

> 标签: magento2, magento2-hyva, magento2-module, paymenticons

## 简介

Payment icons for Hyvä Themes — the Hyvä adapter over siteation/magento2-icons-payment (which owns the SVG library)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
