# in-square/seo-bundle

> 标签: seo, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Fork of leogout/seo-bundle with Symfony 7 support and extensible SEO tag attributes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
