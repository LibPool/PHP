# in-square/pimcore-breadcrumbs

> 标签: breadcrumbs, pimcore, pimcore-plugin

## 简介

Breadcrumbs builder for Pimcore documents with multisite and multilanguage support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
