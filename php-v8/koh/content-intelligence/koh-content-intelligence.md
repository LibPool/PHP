# koh/content-intelligence

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Content quality and governance analysis for TYPO3 — AI-Readiness scoring, Brand Voice consistency, freshness analysis, and audit trail for AI-generated content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
