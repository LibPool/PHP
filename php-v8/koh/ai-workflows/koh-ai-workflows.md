# koh/ai-workflows

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Agentic Workflow Engine for TYPO3 - orchestrates AI actions, content intelligence checks, semantic delivery and human reviews into pipelines

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
