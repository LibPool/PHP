# koh/structured-content

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Schema.org JSON-LD generation, AI-context annotations, and content-relationship management for TYPO3. Owns the full Schema.org generator stack (Article/WebPage/FAQ/Service/Product/Organization/...) plus audience/tone/channel inheritance and typed cross-content relationships.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
