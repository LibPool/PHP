# koh/sylius-robots-txt

> 标签: Agent, ai, content-signals, crawler, robots.txt, sylius

## 简介

Channel-aware robots.txt for Sylius with explicit AI crawler rules and Content Signals (RFC 9309).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
