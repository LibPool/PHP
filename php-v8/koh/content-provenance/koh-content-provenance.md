# koh/content-provenance

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Content provenance and signing for TYPO3 — Ed25519 digital signatures, audit trail, AI content tracking, and verification API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
