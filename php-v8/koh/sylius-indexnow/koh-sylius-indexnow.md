# koh/sylius-indexnow

> 标签: bing, ecommerce, indexnow, seo, sylius

## 简介

IndexNow for Sylius: announces changed, launched and removed products and taxons per channel host. Derived per-host keys, production only, a retrying queue.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
