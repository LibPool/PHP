# lukasbableck/contao-news-calendar-bundle

> 标签: backend, calendar, contao, kalender, nachrichten, news, plan

## 简介

This bundle provides a calendar view for news items in the Contao back end.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
