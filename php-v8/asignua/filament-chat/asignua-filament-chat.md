# asignua/filament-chat

> 标签: Messenger, chat, filament, filament-plugin, laravel, livewire, reverb, team-chat

## 简介

Internal team chat for Filament 5 panels: direct messages and groups, @mentions, replies, editing, reactions, read receipts, unread counters in navigation, tab title and favicon, a full chat page and a slide-over you can pin next to any page, and record references — drop a link to a panel record and it becomes a card. Real-time through Laravel Reverb, Pusher or Ably, or plain polling; ten languages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
