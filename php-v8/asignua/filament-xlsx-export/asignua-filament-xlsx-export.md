# asignua/filament-xlsx-export

> 标签: excel, export, filament, filament-plugin, laravel, openspout, xlsx

## 简介

"Download what is on the screen" as a real Excel file: the Filament 5 table's current filters, search and sort — or the selected rows — streamed straight to the browser, with numbers, dates, money and booleans as typed cells instead of text. A column picker with select-all, export options that change the query, frozen header and autofilter, no queue and no intermediate CSV; a trait also gives core's queued Exporter typed XLSX cells.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
