# asignua/filament-seo-files

> 标签: Sitemap, filament, filament-plugin, hreflang, laravel, llms-txt, robots-txt, seo

## 简介

The files crawlers and AI agents read — sitemap.xml, robots.txt, llms.txt and llms-full.txt — generated from your own models and managed from a Filament 5 panel. Pluggable page sources, reciprocal hreflang clusters with x-default, manual sitemap URLs that never shadow a real page, an automatic sitemap index past 50 000 URLs, per-language llms.txt, an editor page for robots.txt and llms.txt, and scheduled generation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
