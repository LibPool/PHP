# asignua/filament-rating

> 标签: Rating, filament, filament-plugin, filament-rating, laravel, review, stars

## 简介

Star rating for Filament 5: a form field, a table column and an infolist entry. Half stars, custom icons, tooltips, keyboard and screen-reader support. Successor of mokhosh/filament-rating.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
