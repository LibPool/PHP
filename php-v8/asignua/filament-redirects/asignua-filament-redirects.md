# asignua/filament-redirects

> 标签: 301, 404, filament, filament-plugin, laravel, not-found, redirects, seo

## 简介

A redirect manager and a 404 log for Filament 5 panels. The log aggregates the 404s your visitors and crawlers hit by path, with a referrer sample and client type, and drops scanner noise; one click turns a row into a redirect. Redirects are SEO-safe: they fire only on a real 404, are always one hop (chains are collapsed on save, loops refused), handle trailing slashes and HEAD, support multilingual paths, and are cached.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
