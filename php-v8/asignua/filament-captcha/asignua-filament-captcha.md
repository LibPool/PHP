# asignua/filament-captcha

> 标签: captcha, filament, filament-plugin, hcaptcha, laravel, livewire, recaptcha, turnstile

## 简介

Captcha field for Filament forms and Livewire components: reCAPTCHA v2/v3, Cloudflare Turnstile and hCaptcha with a server-side validation rule.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
