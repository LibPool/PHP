# asignua/filament-relation-manager-tabs

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, laravel, relation-manager, tabs

## 简介

Render Filament 5 relation managers as ordinary tabs of the record form: put RelationManagerTab::make(Manager::class) into the form's Tabs and the edit page has exactly one row of tabs — your form tabs and relation managers side by side — instead of a second row of tabs under the form. Lazy loading of the manager, collapsible sections, works on edit and view pages, no Filament view overridden.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
