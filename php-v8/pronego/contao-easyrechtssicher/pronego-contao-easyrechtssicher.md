# pronego/contao-easyrechtssicher

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

EasyRechtssicher integration for always up-to-date display of the legal notice (imprint), privacy policy, and cancellation policy.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
