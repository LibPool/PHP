# five-say/laravel-route-group

> 标签: group, laravel, object, route

## 简介

Object-oriented route group.use like this: $route->get(...)->as(...)->uses(...)->before(...);

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
