> 标签: 2fa, Authentication, auth, e-mail, laravel, laravel 7, laravel 8, laravel-module, laravel-package, laravel-plugin, twofactor

## 简介

Two-factor authentication for Laravel 7.x and 8.x via e-mail.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
