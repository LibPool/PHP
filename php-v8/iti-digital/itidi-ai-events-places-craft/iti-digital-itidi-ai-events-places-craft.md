# iti-digital/itidi-ai-events-places-craft

> 标签: Craft, Sitemap, craft-plugin, craftcms, events, itineraries, llms-txt, places, seo, tourism

## 简介

Server-side rendered, SEO-friendly Events, Places and Itineraries from the ITI Digital platform for Craft CMS, with clean URLs, structured data, sitemap, llms.txt and Trip Planner.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
