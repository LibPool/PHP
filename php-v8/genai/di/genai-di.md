# genai/di

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Popular DI attributes (#[Service], #[Repository], #[Configuration], #[Bean], #[Value]) and their processor, targeting GenAI\Container. Build-time (PHP 8).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
