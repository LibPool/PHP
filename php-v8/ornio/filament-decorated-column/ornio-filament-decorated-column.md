# ornio/filament-decorated-column

> 标签: badge, filament, filament-decorated-column, filament-infolists, filament-plugin, filament-tables, filamentphp, icon, laravel, ornio, table-column

## 简介

Badge and icon decorations before or after the text of Filament table columns and infolist entries. Needs no custom theme.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
