# axelferdinand/statamic-secretary

> 标签: ai, cms, content-assistant, editorial-workflow, email, openai, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Send an email to your site, and Secretary updates content or builds new pages with your own blueprints and modules — ready for review.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
