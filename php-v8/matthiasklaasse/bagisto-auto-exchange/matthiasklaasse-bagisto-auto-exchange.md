# matthiasklaasse/bagisto-auto-exchange

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Automatically add all currencies and exchange rates to your Bagisto website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
