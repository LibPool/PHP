# vtinnovations/migrator

> 标签: backup, clone, contao, migration, move-host, restore

## 简介

Contao Migrator — move a Contao 5 site to a new host. Manual (.tcmig) or direct server-to-server transfer with serialize-safe URL rewriting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
