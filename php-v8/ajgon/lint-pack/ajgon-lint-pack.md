# ajgon/lint-pack

> 标签: csslint, jshint, lint, phpcs, phpmd, twig

## 简介

Various linters for your Symfony2 project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
