# mageplaza/magento-2-social-login

> 标签: Amazon Login, Facebook login, FourSquare Login, Google login, LinkedIn Login, Microsoft Live Login, Social Login, Twitter login, Yahoo Login, facebook, google, login, magento 2, magento 2 social login, mageplaza, social proof, twitter

## 简介

Magento 2 Social Login extension is designed for quick login to your Magento 2 store without procesing complex register steps

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
