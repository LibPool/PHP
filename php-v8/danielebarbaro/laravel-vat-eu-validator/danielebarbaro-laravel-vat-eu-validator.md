> 标签: danielebarbaro, laravel, laravel-validator, laravel-vat-eu-validator, laravel-vat-validator, vat, vat-eu-validator, vat-validator

## 简介

A simple package that validates EU VAT numbers against the central ec.europa.eu database

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
