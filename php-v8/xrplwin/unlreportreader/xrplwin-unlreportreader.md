# xrplwin/unlreportreader

> 标签: composer, dunl, package, parser, php, report, transaction, unl, unlreport, xahau, xahaud, xrp, xrpl

## 简介

Fetches and parses UNLReports on Xahau network.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
