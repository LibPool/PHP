# stalirmc/mc-flarum-bridge

> 标签: Bridge, flarum, minecraft, paper

## 简介

Bidirectional bridge between Minecraft servers and a Flarum forum. Monorepo: the Flarum extension lives in flarum-extension/, the Paper plugin in mc-plugin/.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
