# codefareith/cf-google-authenticator

> 标签: 2fa, authenticator, extension, google, google-2fa, google-authenticator, google-tfa, php, security, tfa, timed-one-time-password, totp, totp-2fa, totp-tfa, two-factor-authentication, typo3, typo3-cms, typo3-cms-extension, typo3-extension

## 简介

Enable Google 2FA (two factor authentication) for both, frontend- and backend accounts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
