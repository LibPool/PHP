# displace/ai-toolkit

> 标签: ai, chunking, embeddings, matryoshka, rag, text-splitter, vectors

## 简介

Pure-PHP utilities for local-first AI pipelines: text chunkers, packed-vector math, MRL truncation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
