# mirays/marc

> 标签: bibliographic, iso2709, library, marc, marc21, marcxchange, marcxml, metadata, streaming, unimarc

## 简介

Streaming MARC 21 parser for PHP: ISO 2709, MARCXML and MARC-in-JSON, plus UNIMARC from MarcXchange

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
