# jovian/venusian-appkit

> 标签: Bridge, appkit, framework, macos, surface, venusian

## 简介

AppKit toolkit driver for the Venusian Surface bridge: connects PHP to NSApplication, opens its windows and builds their menu bars through ext-appkit.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
