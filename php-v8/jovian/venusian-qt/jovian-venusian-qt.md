# jovian/venusian-qt

> 标签: Bridge, framework, linux, qt, qt6, surface, venusian

## 简介

Qt 6 toolkit driver for the Venusian Surface bridge: connects PHP to QApplication, opens its windows and builds their menu bars through ext-qt.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
