# jovian/venusian-gtk

> 标签: Bridge, framework, gtk, gtk4, linux, surface, venusian

## 简介

GTK 4 toolkit driver for the Venusian Surface bridge: connects PHP to GtkApplication, opens its windows and builds their menu bars through ext-gtk.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
