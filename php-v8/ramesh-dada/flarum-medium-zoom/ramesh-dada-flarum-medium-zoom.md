# ramesh-dada/flarum-medium-zoom

> 标签: flarum

## 简介

Integrate medium-zoom into Flarum, which can provide the ability to zoom images.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
