# hans2103/magento2-optionfilter

> 标签: configurable, filter, inventory, layered-navigation, magento, magento2, stock

## 简介

Magento 2 module that filters configurable products from catalog views based on stock status of their variants

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
