# hisune/log2ck

> 标签: clickhouse, log, log2ck, monolog, realtime

## 简介

Real-time, high-performance reading of log files and writing to clickhouse.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
