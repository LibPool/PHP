# setechsolutions/eloquent-sales-force

> 标签: eloquent, forrest, laravel, model, orm, salesforce, sobjects, soql

## 简介

Work with Salesforce objects as Eloquent models: an Eloquent driver for SOQL and the Salesforce REST API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
