# npa/laravel-csv

> 标签: laravel, laravel-csv, livewire, npa

## 简介

A Laravel livewire CSV Importer, to handle importing millions of rows without a hustle.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
