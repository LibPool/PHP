# abdelrahmannourallah/yii2-sharerbox

> 标签: Facebook Share, sharerbox, whatsup, yii2

## 简介

The sharerbox extension for the Yii2 framework, help you to share post to facebook ,whatsup , twitter, and print

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
