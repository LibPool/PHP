# caprel/laravel-billing

> 标签: Dolibarr, billing, caprel, laravel, quota

## 简介

Quota, overrun and billing plumbing shared by the CAP-REL SaaS webservices: three allowances per metric, overrun billed per pack or unit at the monthly reading, acts pushed to smartmakersaasbilling

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
