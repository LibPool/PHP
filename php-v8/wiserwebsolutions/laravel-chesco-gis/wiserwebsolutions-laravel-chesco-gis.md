# wiserwebsolutions/laravel-chesco-gis

> 标签: ArcGIS, chester-county, featureserver, gis, laravel, parcels

## 简介

Fluent Laravel client for Chester County's ArcGIS REST FeatureServer datasets (starting with parcels/owners, extensible to zoning, floodplains, and municipal boundaries).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
