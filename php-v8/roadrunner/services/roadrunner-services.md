# roadrunner/services

> 标签: process, roadrunner, rpc, service-manager, services, supervisor

## 简介

Manage RoadRunner services from PHP: create, start, stop and inspect processes supervised by the RoadRunner service plugin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
