# hoceineel/filament-quick-action-dock

> 标签: actions, dock, filament, hoceineel, laravel, launcher, speed-dial

## 简介

A floating launcher for the Filament actions people reach for most, on every page of a panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
