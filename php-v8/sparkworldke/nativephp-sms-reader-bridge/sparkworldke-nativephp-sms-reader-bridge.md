# sparkworldke/nativephp-sms-reader-bridge

> 标签: android, laravel, mobile, nativephp, plugin, sms

## 简介

A NativePHP Mobile plugin that lets your Android app read SMS messages from the device inbox and listen for incoming SMS in real time.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
