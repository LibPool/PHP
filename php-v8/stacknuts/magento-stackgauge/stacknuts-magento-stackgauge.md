# stacknuts/magento-stackgauge

> 标签: fleet, magento2, monitoring, observability, patch-management, version-inventory

## 简介

Reports version, patch, and operational health data from a Magento store to a central StackGauge fleet dashboard, so an agency can see every client site's update/security posture at a glance.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
