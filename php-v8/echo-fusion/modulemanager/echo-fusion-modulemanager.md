# echo-fusion/modulemanager

> 标签: echoFusion, module, modules

## 简介

Module registry for the EchoFusion framework: modules declare their config, service providers, routes and templates as data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
