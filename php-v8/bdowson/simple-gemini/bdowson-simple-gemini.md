# bdowson/simple-gemini

> 标签: Gemini, ai, api, google-ai, llm, php

## 简介

A lightweight PHP package to send prompts to Google's Gemini AI and retrieve responses.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
