# makinuk/icalendar

> 标签: Invitation, alarm, calendar, event, google calendar, iCalendar, ical, ics, meeting, outlook, rfc5545, vevent, vtodo

## 简介

Generate RFC 5545 iCalendar (.ics) files in PHP: events, to-dos, alarms, recurring events and meeting invitations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
