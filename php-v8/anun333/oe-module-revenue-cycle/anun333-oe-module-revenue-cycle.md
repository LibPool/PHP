# anun333/oe-module-revenue-cycle

> 标签: billing, charge-review, openemr, openemr-module, revenue-cycle

## 简介

Revenue Cycle for OpenEMR: a charge review before claims go out, both directions (missed charges and charges the record doesn't support), as a Staff Worklist tab, with a look-back report.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
