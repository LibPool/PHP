## 简介

42 CFR Part 2 consents and disclosures for OpenEMR: each disclosure tied to its consent in OpenEMR's own Disclosures record, with the 2.32 notice and the accounting of disclosures.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
