# belsignum/powermail-voucher

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Add campaigns with voucher codes to request them with a powermail form.Imagine your client needs to provide voucher or ticket codes for fairs, now you can provide them with a Powermail form

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
