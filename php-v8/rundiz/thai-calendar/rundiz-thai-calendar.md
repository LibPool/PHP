> 标签: Thai calendar, appointment, calendar, day calendar, event, month calendar, php calendar, schedule, task, week calendar, year calendar

## 简介

The calendar in Thai language (also support multi languages).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
