# webwerkwien/contao-consho-licensecheck-bundle

> 标签: consho, contao, license-check, license-key, licensing

## 简介

Consho licence check - checks signed licence keys and daily confirmations in the installation, for several paid Contao extensions at once.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
