# webwerkwien/contao-ai-backend-bundle

> 标签: Agent, KI, OpenRouter, ai, anthropic, assistent, automation, automatisierung, backend, chat, claude, conpai, contao, contao-ai, ki-agent, ollama, openai, redakteur

## 简介

Contao 5 and 6 backend module with an in-browser AI agent for editors and admins, powered by symfony/ai and contao-ai-core-bundle

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
