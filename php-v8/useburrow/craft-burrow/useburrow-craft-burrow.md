# useburrow/craft-burrow

> 标签: Craft, agency, analytics, backfill, burrow, craft-commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, dashboard, ecommerce, event-tracking, formie, freeform, integration, monitoring, multi-tenant, observability, operations, orchestration, reporting, sync, visibility

## 简介

Connect your Craft site to Burrow — the orchestration layer for agency operations. Event tracking, system snapshots, and operational visibility for Freeform, Formie, and Commerce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
