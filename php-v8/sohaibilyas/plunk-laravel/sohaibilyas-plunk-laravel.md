# sohaibilyas/plunk-laravel

> 标签: email, laravel, mail, plunk, transactional, transport

## 简介

Laravel mail transport for Plunk with queue retry idempotency, attachments and self-hosted support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
