# manojkiran/filament-quick-view

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, laravel, quick-view, slide-over

## 简介

Quick view slide-overs for related records in Filament tables and infolists.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
