# yidas/csv

> 标签: Excel CSV, Quoting CSV, csv, csv reader, csv writer, php

## 简介

PHP CSV writer & reader with settings of quoting enclosure and encoding

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
