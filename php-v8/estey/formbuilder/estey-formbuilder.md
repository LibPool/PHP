# estey/formbuilder

> 标签: formbuilder, laravel, laravel4, prepend, selectWeekday

## 简介

Laravel 4 extended FormBuilder Class.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
