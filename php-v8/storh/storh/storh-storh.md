# storh/storh

> 标签: append-only-log, datastore, document-store, file-storage, jsonc, php, queue, storage, uuidv7, zero-dependency

## 简介

File-first records for PHP: JSONC document store, append-only segmented log, and log-backed queue. Zero dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
