# williamcho/flarum-rss

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Publishes an RSS 2.0 feed of publicly visible discussions at /rss, with admin-configurable item count and summary length.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
