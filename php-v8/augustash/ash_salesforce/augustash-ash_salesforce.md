# augustash/ash_salesforce

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Sends Drupal form submissions to Salesforce through the Web-to-Case or Web-to-Lead form the client's Salesforce admin generates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
