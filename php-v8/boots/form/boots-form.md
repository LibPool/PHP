# boots/form

> 标签: boots, form, utility, wordpress

## 简介

Use form elements with ease. Includes powerful utilities. Endless possibilities with built-in hooks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
