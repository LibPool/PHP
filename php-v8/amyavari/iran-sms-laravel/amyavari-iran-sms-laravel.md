# amyavari/iran-sms-laravel

> 标签: amyavari, iran, iran-sms-laravel, laravel, sms

## 简介

A simple and convenient way to send SMS through Iranian SMS providers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
