# automattic/jetpack-podcast

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Jetpack Podcast: the wp-admin podcasting experience, REST settings, and RSS feed customization.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
