# banimark/banimark

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Banimark - a self-hosted AI support desk for any PHP app (Laravel or standalone): bring your own AI key, your data never leaves your server. Answers from your own database, hands off to your team; support desk, tools and more as modules.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
