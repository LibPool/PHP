# contenir/contenir-seo

> 标签: JSON-LD, Sitemap, contenir, open-graph, robots, seo

## 简介

Framework-free SEO for Contenir: title templates, robots directives and robots.txt, site verification, JSON-LD Organization and WebSite, and an XML sitemap writer with images and an index past 50,000 URLs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
