# contenir/queue

> 标签: contenir, cron, jobs, queue, scheduler, worker

## 简介

Durable jobs, retries and schedules for Contenir CMS, with a cron-friendly worker. Framework free: PSR-11, PSR-3 and PSR-20, stored with php-db on MySQL or SQLite.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
