# contenir/contenir-mail

> 标签: contenir, imap, mail, mime, pop3, smtp

## 简介

Compose, parse, store and send text and MIME-compliant multipart e-mail messages. A maintained continuation of laminas/laminas-mail and laminas/laminas-mime.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
