# contenir/contenir-seo-laminas-mvc

> 标签: Sitemap, contenir, laminas, mvc, robots, seo

## 简介

Laminas MVC adapter for contenir/contenir-seo: /sitemap.xml and /robots.txt from the site's settings, an seo() view helper for titles, share defaults, verification and JSON-LD, and X-Robots-Tag on hidden copies. Replaces contenir/contenir-sitemap-laminas-mvc.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
