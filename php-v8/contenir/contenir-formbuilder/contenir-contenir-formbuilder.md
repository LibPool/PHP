# contenir/contenir-formbuilder

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Framework-agnostic form-builder engine for Contenir CMS — runtime-editable form definitions, builder-form construction, server-side submission validation, and their storage with php-db on MySQL or SQLite.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
