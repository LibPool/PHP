# contenir/contenir-consent

> 标签: consent, consent-mode, contenir, cookie-consent, csp, gdpr

## 简介

Framework-free cookie consent for Contenir: categories and their services, regional modes, Global Privacy Control, Google Consent Mode v2, script gating and the vanilla-cookieconsent banner's configuration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
