# contenir/contenir-social-laminas-mvc

> 标签: contenir, laminas, mvc, rel-me, share, social

## 简介

Laminas MVC view helpers for contenir/contenir-social: socialProfiles() writes a site's profile links and share() a page's share buttons, from the CMS's settings and the page's own metadata.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
