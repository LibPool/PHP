# mavenbird/magento2-reindex

> 标签: admin reindex, adobe commerce reindex, backend reindex, magento 2, magento2, reindex, reindexing magento2

## 简介

Reindex your Magento2 store quickly and easily from backend/admin, instead of command line.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
