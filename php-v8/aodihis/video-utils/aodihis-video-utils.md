# aodihis/video-utils

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, get video id, video utils, youtube id

## 简介

Twig filter to get the video id of youtube or Vimeo URL.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
