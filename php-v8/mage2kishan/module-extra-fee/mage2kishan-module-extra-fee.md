# mage2kishan/module-extra-fee

> 标签: checkout-fee, extra-fee, magento2, magento2-module, order-fee, panth, payment-fee, surcharge

## 简介

Panth Extra Fee — add configurable extra fees and surcharges to Magento 2 checkout based on payment method, customer group, country/region, products, categories, order amount, and more. Fixed, percentage, or combined fee calculation with full tax and refund support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
