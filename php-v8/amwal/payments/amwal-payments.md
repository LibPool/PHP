# amwal/payments

> 标签: Apple Pay, amwal, card, credit, debit, gateway, magento2, minicart quick checkout, module, payment, payments, pci, product widget, quick checkout, refund

## 简介

Amwal payment plugin for Magento 2 enables one click checkout for instantaneous purchases from product, mini-cart and cart pages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
