# bbcsky/yii2-payment

> 标签: alipay, app pay, payment, weixin, yii2

## 简介

The yii2 payment extension for alipay and weixin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
