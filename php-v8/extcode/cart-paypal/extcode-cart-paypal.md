# extcode/cart-paypal

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, cart, paypal, shopping cart

## 简介

Shopping Cart(s) for TYPO3 - PayPal Payment Provider

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
