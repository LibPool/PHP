# ex3mm/bank-payment-qr

> 标签: 56042, Bank, gost, payment, qr

## 简介

Generate bank payment QR codes (GOST R 56042-2014) for PHP 8.5+ and Laravel 12+

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
