# storetown/module-surcharge-max

> 标签: adobe-commerce, cutting-fee, extra-costs, fees, hyva, magento-2, magento-extension, magento2, payment-surcharge, shipping-surcharge, surcharge

## 简介

Commercial Magento 2 Surcharge extension — transparent cutting fees, oversized-shipping surcharges and category-based extra costs. Multi-rule engine, per-product override, bulk-assign, mixed-cart handling, country restrictions. Hyvä-compatible. Module source delivered after purchase.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
