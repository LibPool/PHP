> 标签: Europe, Mehrwertsteuer, MwSt, adobe-commerce, eu, hyva, magento 2, magento-extension, magento2, steuersaetze, tax, tax-rates, tax-rules, tax-sync, vat, vat-compliance, vat-rates

## 简介

Automatic EU VAT rate synchronization for Magento 2 — imports and updates all European tax rates including automatic Tax Rule creation. Free open-source under MIT.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
