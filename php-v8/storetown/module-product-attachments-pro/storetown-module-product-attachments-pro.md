# storetown/module-product-attachments-pro

> 标签: adobe-commerce, attachments, datasheet, downloads, hyva, magento-2, magento-extension, magento2, manuals, pdf, product-attachment, product-attachments

## 简介

Commercial Magento 2 Product Attachments extension — attach PDF manuals, datasheets, certificates, images and videos to products. Rule-based auto-assignment, inline preview, customer-group access control, CSV bulk import. Hyvä-native. Module source delivered after purchase.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
