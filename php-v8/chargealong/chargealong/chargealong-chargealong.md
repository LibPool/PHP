# chargealong/chargealong

> 标签: Electric Vehicle, australia, charging station locator, ev charger api, ev charging speed, ev charging stations, ev trip planner, open charge map, php

## 简介

Find EV chargers near a point, plan a road trip through charging stops, and look up how fast electric cars charge. Free and keyless.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
