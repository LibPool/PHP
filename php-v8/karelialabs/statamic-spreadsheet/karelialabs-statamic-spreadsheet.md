# karelialabs/statamic-spreadsheet

> 标签: bulk edit, csv, find and replace, inline edit, spreadsheet, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Edit Statamic entries in a spreadsheet: paste from Excel, fill down, find and replace, CSV in and out. Saves through your blueprints' own validation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
