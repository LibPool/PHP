# karelialabs/statamic-tickets

> 标签: check-in, event tickets, eventbrite alternative, qr code, rsvp, statamic, statamic-addon, stripe, stripe checkout, ticketing, tickets

## 简介

Sell event tickets from Statamic with Stripe Checkout: ticket types, capacity, QR tickets by email, check-in on a phone, refunds and attendee lists. No per-ticket fees.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
