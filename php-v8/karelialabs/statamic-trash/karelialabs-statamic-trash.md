# karelialabs/statamic-trash

> 标签: recycle bin, restore, soft delete, statamic, statamic-addon, trash, undelete

## 简介

A trash bin for Statamic. Deleted entries, terms, assets, globals, form submissions and users can be restored with one click.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
