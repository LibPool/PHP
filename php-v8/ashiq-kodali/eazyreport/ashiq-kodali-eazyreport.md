# ashiq-kodali/eazyreport

> 标签: barcode, chart, fastreport, handlebars, html, invoice, laravel, pdf, php, printing, qrcode, reporting, rtpl

## 简介

Modern banded document and invoice reporting engine for PHP and Laravel. Render .rtpl templates to printable HTML and vector PDF.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
