# helturkey/arabic-prosody

> 标签: al-khalil, arabic, arud, meter, poetry, prosody, scansion

## 简介

Dependency-free Arabic prosody (ʿarūḍ): vocalized text to binary scansion, rule-based meter detection for hemistichs, verses, whole poems and free verse, with foot-by-foot explanations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
