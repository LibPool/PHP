# eriksiemund/kirby-mux

> 标签: kirby, mux, stream, video

## 简介

Upload videos to MUX from the Kirby CMS panel. @forked from robinscholz/kirby-mux and dev-ofty/kirby-mux

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
