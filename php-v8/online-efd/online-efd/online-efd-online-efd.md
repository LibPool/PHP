# online-efd/online-efd

> 标签: efd, fiscal, laravel, tanzania, tra, vfd

## 简介

Reusable Laravel package for TRA (Tanzania) VFD/EFD integration: per-tenant certificates & credentials, registration, receipts and Z-reports. Plain PHP/JSON API, no UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
