# nedwors/hopper

> 标签: database, hopper, laravel, mysql, nedwors, sqlite

## 简介

Hopper lets you move between your local Laravel databases with ease, preserving data and easing development

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
