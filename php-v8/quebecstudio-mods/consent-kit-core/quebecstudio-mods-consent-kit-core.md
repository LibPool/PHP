# quebecstudio-mods/consent-kit-core

> 标签: consent, cookies, gdpr, loi25

## 简介

Framework-free core of Cookie Consent Kit: settings resolution, wording, templates and front-end assets, shared by the Craft CMS and Laravel integrations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
