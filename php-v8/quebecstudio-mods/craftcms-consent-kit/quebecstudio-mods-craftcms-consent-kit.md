# quebecstudio-mods/craftcms-consent-kit

> 标签: Craft, consent, cookies, craft-plugin, craftcms, loi25, rgpd

## 简介

Opt-in cookie consent for Craft CMS: no third-party cookie, script or embed loads before the visitor agrees. Built for Quebec's Law 25 and the GDPR.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
