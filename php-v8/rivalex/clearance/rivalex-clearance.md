# rivalex/clearance

> 标签: acl, admin-panel, authorization, flux, laravel, livewire, permissions, rbac, roles, spatie

## 简介

A complete admin panel (Livewire 4 + Flux UI) for managing permissions, roles, role ceilings and contextual authorization on top of spatie/laravel-permission - installable via a single artisan command, fully opt-in and configurable for exposure to admins or managers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
