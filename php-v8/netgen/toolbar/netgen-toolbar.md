# netgen/toolbar

> 标签: ibexa, netgen, toolbar

## 简介

Netgen Toolbar Bundle is an Ibexa bundle that implements a handy toolbar on your site with functions most used by the editors.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
