# khooz/oath

> 标签: Two Factor Authentication, authenticator, google authenticator, microsoft authenticator, oath, totp, two-step verification

## 简介

The TOTP based on [RFC6238](https://tools.ietf.org/html/rfc6238)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
