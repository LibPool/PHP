# commercekings/module-notification-infobip

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Infobip SMS, WhatsApp and Viber for CommerceKings Notification: three providers, authenticated reply and delivery report webhooks (STOP/START, undeliverable numbers), the opt-in confirmation, WhatsApp templates and quiet hours in the recipient's time zone.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
