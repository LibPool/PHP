# commercekings/module-app-health

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Magento health alerts for the shop team: cron, indexers, caches, maintenance mode, email settings, exception spikes, queue backlogs and failing emails, checked by cron every 5 minutes and sent through commercekings/module-notification (e.g. to Slack) on a change, with a daily summary and an optional heartbeat.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
