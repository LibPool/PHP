# pyrrah/openweathermap-bundle

> 标签: OpenWeatherMap, map, open, pyrrah, weather, weathermap, weathermap symfony5, weathermap symfony6

## 简介

The simple way to obtain currently weather from OpenWeatherMap API, for Symfony 5 to 6.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
