# acencyril/sentinelle-bundle

> 标签: fail2ban, intrusion-detection, ip-blocking, security, symfony, waf

## 简介

Blocks attackers, never your mail provider. Intrusion detection for Symfony.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
