# oyswonder/thinkcmf

> 标签: cmf, framework, orm, thinkcmf, thinkphp

## 简介

ThinkCMF based on ThinkPHP 5.1 , it is a free and open source Content Management Framework(CMF)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
