# con4gis/groups

> 标签: GroupsBundle, Invite, con4gis, email, frontend, groups, member, rights

## 简介

The groups brick of the Contao GIS-kit con4gis. A standalone frontend-group-administration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
