# vhar/laravel-videos

> 标签: dailymotion, eloquent, laravel, rutube, video, videos, vimeo, vk, youtube

## 简介

Attach videos from popular hosting platforms to Eloquent models

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
