# atannex/share

> 标签: bluesky, facebook, laravel, linkedin, mastodon, open-graph, share, share-buttons, social, social-share, twitter, web-share-api, whatsapp, x

## 简介

Share anything to any platform from Laravel: models, links, text or files to 35+ networks and apps, with correctly encoded intent links, per-platform text limits, signed and counted share redirects, copy/native/QR/embed actions, drop-in Blade buttons and share sheet, Open Graph tags and a link-preview inspector.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
