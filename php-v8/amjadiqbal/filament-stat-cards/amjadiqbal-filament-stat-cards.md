# amjadiqbal/filament-stat-cards

> 标签: dashboard, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, kpi, laravel, progress-bar, stats, stats-overview, widget

## 简介

Richer stat cards for the Filament 4 and 5 stats overview widget: progress bars, icon tiles, background, label and value colours, trends, badges and bar charts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
