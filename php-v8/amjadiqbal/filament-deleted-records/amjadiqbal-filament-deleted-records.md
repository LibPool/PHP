# amjadiqbal/filament-deleted-records

> 标签: deleted-models, eloquent, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, recycle-bin, restore, trash, undelete

## 简介

A recycle bin for Filament 4 and 5: browse, preview, restore or permanently delete records kept by spatie/laravel-deleted-models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
