# amjadiqbal/filament-rankforge-lite

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, laravel, meta-tags, open-graph, readability, seo, serp-preview, twitter-cards

## 简介

Free SEO fields for Filament 4 and 5: meta, Open Graph and Twitter tags, a live SEO and readability score, and a desktop/mobile SERP preview. Upgrades to RankForge without migrating content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
