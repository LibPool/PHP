# amjadiqbal/laravel-sweetalert2

> 标签: alert, alpinejs, blade, confirm-dialog, flash-message, laravel, laravel-package, livewire, modal, notification, popup, sweet-alert, sweetalert, sweetalert2, toast

## 简介

SweetAlert2 for Laravel: flash success, error and toast popups from controllers, fire them from Livewire or Alpine, and confirm deletes with a data attribute. SweetAlert2 11, no jQuery, CSP nonce aware.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
