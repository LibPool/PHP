# amjadiqbal/filament-billing-console

> 标签: billing, cashier, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, invoices, laravel, laravel-cashier, mrr, saas, subscriptions

## 简介

A free, read-only Filament 4 and 5 billing console for Laravel Cashier (Stripe): customers, subscriptions, invoices, payments and an MRR and churn summary in your panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
