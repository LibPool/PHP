# amjadiqbal/laravel-media-viewer

> 标签: audio-player, blade, hls, hls.js, laravel, laravel-package, livewire, pdf-viewer, pdf.js, pdfjs, private-files, range-requests, signed-urls, video-player, videojs

## 简介

Show PDFs, video and audio from any Laravel disk without leaking private files: Blade and Livewire components with a pdf.js PDF viewer (page navigation, zoom, search, thumbnails), a Video.js player with HLS (hls.js), signed expiring URLs and Range-aware streaming.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
