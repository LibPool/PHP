# amjadiqbal/filament-audience-sync

> 标签: Audience, brevo, email-marketing, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, mailchimp, mailing-list, newsletter, subscribers

## 简介

Email-marketing audience sync for Filament 4 and 5: subscribe, unsubscribe, tag and update your records in a Mailchimp audience or Brevo list, with queued bulk sync, a status column and list stats. Driver-based.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
