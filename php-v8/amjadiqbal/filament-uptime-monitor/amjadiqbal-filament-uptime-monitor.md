# amjadiqbal/filament-uptime-monitor

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, monitoring, ssl-certificate, status, uptime, uptime-monitor, website-monitoring

## 简介

A Filament 4 and 5 admin UI for spatie/laravel-uptime-monitor: add, edit, enable, disable and check uptime and SSL certificate monitors from your panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
