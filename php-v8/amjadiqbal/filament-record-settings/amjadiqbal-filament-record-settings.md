# amjadiqbal/filament-record-settings

> 标签: Settings, filament, filament-plugin, filamentphp, laravel, model-settings, preferences, tenant-settings, user-preferences, user-settings

## 简介

Edit per-record settings in Filament 4 and 5: per-user, per-team and per-tenant preferences from a settings page, a modal action or a resource form. Works with glorand/laravel-model-settings or a plain JSON column.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
