# enupal/yandex-translate

> 标签: enupal, yandex-translate

## 简介

Online service for machine translation for over 40 languages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/enupal/yandex-translate
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/enupal/yandex-translate.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/enupal%2Fyandex-translate

- Composer 安装：`composer require enupal/yandex-translate`
