# clickpress/contao-news-podcasts

> 标签: Podcast-Feed, contao, extension, itunes, podcast, rss

## 简介

Add audio files to your news and let Contao generate podcast compatible RSS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
