# spaanproductions/laravel-ai-usage

> 标签: ai, cost, laravel, livewire, tokens, usage

## 简介

Tracks the tokens and cost of every laravel/ai call, with a filterable Livewire dashboard, budgets and a synced AI model price list.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
