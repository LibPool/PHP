# webmenedzser/medium-com-meta-tag-parser-for-feed-me

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, feed me - medium.com meta tag parser

## 简介

Adds meta tag information to Medium.com feed - a Feed Me plugin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
