# stechstudio/laravel-helo-email

> 标签: HELO, email, helohq, laravel, mail, transport

## 简介

Send Laravel mail through Helo's API, with a client for the rest of the Helo API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
