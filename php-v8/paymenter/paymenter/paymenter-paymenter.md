# paymenter/paymenter

> 标签: billing, billing software, invoice, invoicing, laravel, open-source, payment, self-hosted, subscription

## 简介

Paymenter is a self-hosted, open-source, and free invoicing and billing software built with Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
