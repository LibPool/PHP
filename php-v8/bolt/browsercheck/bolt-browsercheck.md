# bolt/browsercheck

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

💻 This Bolt extension works with the User-Agent to detect devices (desktop, tablet, mobile, etc.), clients, operating systems, brands and models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
