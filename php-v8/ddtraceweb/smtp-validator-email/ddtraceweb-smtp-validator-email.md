# ddtraceweb/smtp-validator-email

> 标签: check email smtp, checkmx, smtp validator email, validate email, validator email

## 简介

Validate your email by smtp protocol

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
