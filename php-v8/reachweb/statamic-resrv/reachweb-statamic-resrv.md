# reachweb/statamic-resrv

> 标签: Resrv, Resrv App, availability, booking, reservations, statamic

## 简介

A booking engine for Statamic. Supports availability calendar and online reservations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
