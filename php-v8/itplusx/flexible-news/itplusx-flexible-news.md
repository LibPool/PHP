# itplusx/flexible-news

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, extension, news, pages as news, typo3

## 简介

Flexible news extension to use pages as news articles

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
