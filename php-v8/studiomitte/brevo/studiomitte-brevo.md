# studiomitte/brevo

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Integration of newsletter SaaS solution brevo.com (formally known as sendinblue) into TYPO3 CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
