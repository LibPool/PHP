# ultimate-multisite/the-perfect-wp-cron

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Event-loop job queue for WordPress. Executes WP Cron and Action Scheduler jobs at exact scheduled times with zero polling.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
