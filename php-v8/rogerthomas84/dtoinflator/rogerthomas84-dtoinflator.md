# rogerthomas84/dtoinflator

> 标签: dto, dtoinflator, helper

## 简介

DtoInflator is a library to inflate arrays or objects into their corresponding models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
