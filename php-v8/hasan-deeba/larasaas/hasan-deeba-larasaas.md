# hasan-deeba/larasaas

> 标签: Teams, announcements, billing, cashier, feature-flags, inertia, invitations, laravel, multi-tenant, pennant, react, saas, starter-kit, stripe, workspaces

## 简介

Drop-in SaaS engine for Laravel + React: Stripe billing (Cashier), multi-tenant workspaces & team roles, admin-managed plans and typed entitlements, scheduled announcements and Pennant feature flags — plugged into YOUR User model.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
