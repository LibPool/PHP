# hlavtox/qr-payment-string-generator

> 标签: epc, pay-by-square, payment, qr, qr-platba, sepa

## 简介

Generates Slovak PAY by square, Czech QR Platba and EPC payment strings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
