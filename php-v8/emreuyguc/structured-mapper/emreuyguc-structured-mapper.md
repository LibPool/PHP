> 标签: data-mapper, data-transformer, dto, dto-mapper, emreuyguc, entity-mapper, mapper, mapping, object-mapper, object-to-object, php, php-mapper, structured-mapper, transformation

## 简介

Structured Mapper is a PHP library that helps you to map your objects, such as entities to DTOs or models to entities, easily.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
