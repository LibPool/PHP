# anwar/soshare

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

soshare is a Social Share laravel package its support all over well known social network include fb,tw,lnkdinacebool,~twitter,linkdin , whatsapp, google plus.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
