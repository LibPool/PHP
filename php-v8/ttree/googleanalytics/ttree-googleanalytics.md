# ttree/googleanalytics

> 标签: Neos, analytics, tracking

## 简介

Simple TypoScript objects to integration Google Analytics tracking codes on your Neos CMS Project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
