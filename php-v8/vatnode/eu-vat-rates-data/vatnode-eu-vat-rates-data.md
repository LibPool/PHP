# vatnode/eu-vat-rates-data

> 标签: Europe, billing, dataset, digital-services-tax, e-commerce, eu, eu-vat, european-union, fintech, invoice, json, rates, sales-tax, tax, tax-data, vat, vat-moss, vat-number, vat-rates

## 简介

Official-source European VAT rates for 45 jurisdictions with daily-checked data, offline access, and VAT number format validation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
