# packstub/session-replay

> 标签: debugging, laravel, livewire, rrweb, self-hosted, session-recording, session-replay, support

## 简介

Session replay for Laravel: record what a person did in the browser with rrweb, keep the recording on your own disk and database, and watch it behind a gate you define — masked inputs by default, errors, failed Livewire requests and web vitals marked on the timeline, a link to the replay in every log line. packstub/filament-session-replay adds the sessions to a Filament panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
