# packstub/filament-form-builder

> 标签: Forms, contact-form, filament, filament-form-builder, filament-plugin, filament-v5, form-builder, laravel, no-code, packstub, submissions

## 简介

Form builder for Filament panels: build forms in the admin, render them with Blade, Livewire or JSON, collect and export submissions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
