# packstub/filament-rating

> 标签: Rating, filament, filament-plugin, filament-rating, filament-v4, filament-v5, laravel, livewire, packstub, star-rating, stars

## 简介

Star ratings for Filament v4 and v5: a form field, table column, infolist entry and average summarizer. Half stars, keyboard and screen-reader support, drop-in for mokhosh/filament-rating.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
