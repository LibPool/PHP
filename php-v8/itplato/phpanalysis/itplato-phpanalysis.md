# itplato/phpanalysis

> 标签: analysis, segmentation, word segmentation

## 简介

php chinese analysis, It's component-free word segmentation in language-php

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
