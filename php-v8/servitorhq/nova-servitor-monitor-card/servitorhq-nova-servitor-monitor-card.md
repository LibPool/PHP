# servitorhq/nova-servitor-monitor-card

> 标签: laravel, nova

## 简介

Simple card for Laravel Nova to show your website/monitor stats from Servitor.io

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
