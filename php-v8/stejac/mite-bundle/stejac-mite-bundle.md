# stejac/mite-bundle

> 标签: API-Client, mite, symfony, symfony-bundle, time-tracking

## 简介

Symfony bundle for the mite time tracking API: typed services and DTOs for every resource.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
