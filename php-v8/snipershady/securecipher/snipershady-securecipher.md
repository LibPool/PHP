# snipershady/securecipher

> 标签: IV, cipher, cryptography, decrypt, encrypt, securecipher

## 简介

A free, reliable and easy-to-use cipher to encrypt and decrypt strings

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
