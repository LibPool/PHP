# 3neti/x-commerce

> 标签: commerce, commercial-architecture, laravel, licensing, metering, pricing, x-change

## 简介

Commercial architecture and knowledge system for packaging, pricing, licensing, metering, and sustaining outcomes delivered through the x-change ecosystem.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
