# middlewares/encoder

> 标签: compression, deflate, encoding, gzip, http, middleware, psr-15, psr-7

## 简介

Middleware to encode the response body to gzip or deflate

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
