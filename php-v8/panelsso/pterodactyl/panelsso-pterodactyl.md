# panelsso/pterodactyl

> 标签: SSO, laravel, pterodactyl

## 简介

Single sign-on bridge that lets your clients log into their Pterodactyl panel account with one redirect.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
