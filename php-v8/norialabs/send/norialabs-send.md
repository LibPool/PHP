# norialabs/send

> 标签: email, laravel, mail, noria, notifications, onfon, ses, sms, transactional

## 简介

Laravel SDK, mail transport and SMS notification channel for Noria Send: send transactional email and SMS through one internal service instead of wiring a provider into every product.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
