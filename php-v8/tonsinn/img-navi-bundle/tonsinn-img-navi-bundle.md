# tonsinn/img-navi-bundle

> 标签: bildnavigation, contao, contao-bundle, contao5, contao6, content-element, image, navigation, twig

## 简介

Animated image navigation for Contao 5 and 6 - 2 to 6 image panels that expand on hover or tap to reveal a headline, text and a call-to-action button.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
