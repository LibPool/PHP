# koalamer/tideways-xhprof-free-scheduler

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A convenience class for integrating the free version of Tideways' Xhprof extension into your code.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
