# postiefs/laravel-sso

> 标签: SSO, entra-id, laravel, microsoft-365, postiefs

## 简介

Sign in to a Laravel app through the Postiefs SSO portal (Microsoft Entra ID), with portal-controlled sessions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
