# imanilchaudhari/yii2-social-share

> 标签:  extension,  social,  social share,  yii2 social, yii2

## 简介

This is a social share bar for yii2 projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
