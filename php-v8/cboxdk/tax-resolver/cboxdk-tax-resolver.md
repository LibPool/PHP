# cboxdk/tax-resolver

> 标签: address, boundary, cadastre, geocoding, jurisdiction, sales-tax, tax, vat

## 简介

Pure address→jurisdiction resolver over the Cbox Tax boundary dataset. No framework, no I/O, no geocoder — the shared brain the register verifies with and the tax engines resolve with.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
