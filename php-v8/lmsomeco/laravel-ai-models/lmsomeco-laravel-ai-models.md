# lmsomeco/laravel-ai-models

> 标签: ai, laravel, laravel-ai, llm, models

## 简介

List and cache the currently-available models for each provider configured in the Laravel AI SDK.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
