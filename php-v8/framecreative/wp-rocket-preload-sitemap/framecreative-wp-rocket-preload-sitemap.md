# framecreative/wp-rocket-preload-sitemap

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Limits preloading of WP Rocket cache pages to things in the sitemap - works off Yoast SEO sitemap by default.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
