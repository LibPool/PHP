# ar-sebastian/roundcube-skin-workbench

> 标签: dark-mode, elastic, roundcube, skin, workbench

## 简介

A modern, brand-neutral Roundcube skin based on Elastic, featuring light and dark modes, a compact navigation rail and responsive layouts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
