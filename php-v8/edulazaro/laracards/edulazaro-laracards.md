# edulazaro/laracards

> 标签: blog, images, laravel, og, open-graph, social-cards, svg

## 简介

Editorial social cards and blog covers for Laravel. SVG templates rendered with rsvg-convert or resvg, real font metrics for text fitting, and pluggable backgrounds (solid, Unsplash or AI-generated images).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
