# edulazaro/php-pop3

> 标签: client, email, mail, mailbox, pop, pop3

## 简介

A POP3 client in plain PHP: SSL or STARTTLS, certificate checks you control, password, APOP or OAuth sign-in, unique ids, headers or whole messages, and deletion only when asked. No extensions, no dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
