# delvesoft/psr15-symfony-bundle

> 标签: bundle, handler, middleware, psr-15, symfony

## 简介

Symfony bundle designed to bring PSR-15 fashioned middlewares to Symfony 5+ applications

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
