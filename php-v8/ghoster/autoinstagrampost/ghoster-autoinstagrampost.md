# ghoster/autoinstagrampost

> 标签: magento 2, magento 2 auto instagram post, magento 2 extension, magento auto instagram post

## 简介

Share your e-commerce products automatically through Instagram after you adding them to Magento

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
