# tempi-marathon/blade-meteocons

> 标签: Meteocons, blade, icons, laravel, weather

## 简介

A package to easily make use of Meteocons in your Laravel Blade views.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
