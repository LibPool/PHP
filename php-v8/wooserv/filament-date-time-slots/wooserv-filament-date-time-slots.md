# wooserv/filament-date-time-slots

> 标签: date-time-slots, filament-plugin, laravel, time-slots, wooServ

## 简介

A Filament date-time picker with configurable calendars, working hours, available slots, and booking constraints.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
