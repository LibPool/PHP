# oneup/contao-sentry-bundle

> 标签: contao, error, raven, sentry, tracking

## 简介

This bundle provides an easy integration of sentry.io for Contao 4.4.x and newer.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
