> 标签: email-validation, fraud, ipqs, ipqualityscore, laravel, phone-validation, proxy, vpn

## 简介

A simple and modern wrapper for the IPQualityScore (IPQS) API, tailored for Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
