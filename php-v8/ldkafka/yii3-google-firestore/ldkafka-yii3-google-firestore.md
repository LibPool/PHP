# ldkafka/yii3-google-firestore

> 标签: database, emulator, firestore, google-cloud, psr-18, rest, yii3

## 简介

Google Cloud Firestore for PHP 8.4 / Yii3 over the REST API, without gRPC or the Google SDK: documents, queries with cursors, batched writes, transactions, preconditions and field transforms through any PSR-18 client

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
