# webvpf/wn-share-plugin

> 标签: share, social, winter cms, wintercms, wintercms-plugin

## 简介

Share Buttons - plugin for Winter CMS. Displays share buttons.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
