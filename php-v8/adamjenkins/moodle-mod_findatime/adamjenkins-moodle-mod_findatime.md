# adamjenkins/moodle-mod_findatime

> 标签: availability, groups, meeting, moodle, scheduling

## 简介

A Moodle activity where the members of a course group mark their availability, see where it overlaps and confirm a meeting that goes into the group calendar

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
