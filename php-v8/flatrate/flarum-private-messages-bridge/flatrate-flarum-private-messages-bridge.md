> 标签: DM, direct messaging, direct messsage, flarum, friendsofflarum, pm, private message, private messaging

## 简介

Temporary FlatRate.wiki-maintained security-hardened Flarum 1.x private-message bridge.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
