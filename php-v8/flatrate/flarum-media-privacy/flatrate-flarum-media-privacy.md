# flatrate/flarum-media-privacy

> 标签: basename, flarum, fof-upload, privacy, upload

## 简介

FlatRate.wiki forum upload privacy guard for opaque FoF Upload basenames.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
