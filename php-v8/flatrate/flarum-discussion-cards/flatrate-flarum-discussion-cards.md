# flatrate/flarum-discussion-cards

> 标签: cards, discussion, flarum, flatrate

## 简介

High-context social-style discussion card presentation for FlatRate.wiki Flarum 1.8.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
