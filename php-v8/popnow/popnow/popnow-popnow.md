> 标签: API-Client, ai-image-generator, ai-music-generator, ai-video-generator, generative-ai, image-to-video, popnow, sdk, text-to-image, text-to-speech, text-to-video

## 简介

PHP SDK for the PopNow AI video, image and music generation API: quote, create and poll tasks, upload files, manage assets and verify webhooks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
