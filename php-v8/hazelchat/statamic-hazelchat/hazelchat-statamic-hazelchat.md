# hazelchat/statamic-hazelchat

> 标签: ai, chat, chat agent, chatbot, consent, lead capture, statamic, statamic-addon

## 简介

Adds the Hazelchat AI chat agent to Statamic sites from one pasted installation code. It answers visitors from the pages you approve and passes on their enquiries. Needs a Hazelchat account; the Free plan covers one website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
