# pikulsky/whatsapp-streams

> 标签: encryption, psr, psr-7, stream, whatsapp

## 简介

Encrypting and decrypting decorator streams for WhatsApp.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
