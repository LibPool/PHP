# klytron/laravel-google-drive-filesystem

> 标签: filesystem, google-drive, laravel, storage

## 简介

Google Drive filesystem adapter for Laravel (10, 11, 12, 13)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
