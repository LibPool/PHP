# alengo/sulu-redirect-bundle

> 标签: migration, redirect, seo, sulu, symfony

## 简介

CSV-driven permanent redirects for Symfony/Sulu sites: map legacy URLs to their new location at kernel.request.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
