# pagemachine/typo3-matomo-tracking

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Server-side Matomo Tracking in TYPO3 with emphasis on privacy: consentless tracking, GDPR/TDDDG-compliant, respects DNT/GPC

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
