> 标签: code-graph, code-intelligence, comment-policy, commented-out-code, copy-paste-detection, duplicate-code, fqcn, infocyph, php, references, syntax

## 简介

Standalone PHP quality gates and deterministic source-graph extraction.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
