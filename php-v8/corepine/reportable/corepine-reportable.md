# corepine/reportable

> 标签: blade, corepine, laravel, moderation, reportable, reports

## 简介

Reusable polymorphic reporting for Laravel models with a starter Blade UI.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
