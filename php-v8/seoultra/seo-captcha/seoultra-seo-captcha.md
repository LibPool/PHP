# seoultra/seo-captcha

> 标签: Honeypot, anti-spam, bot protection, captcha, form protection, gdpr, privacy, proof of work, recaptcha alternative

## 简介

Free, open-source, privacy-friendly captcha: invisible proof-of-work widget + PHP verification. No tracking, no cookies, no third-party requests — a self-hosted reCAPTCHA alternative.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
