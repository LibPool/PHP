# pressbooks/saxon-he

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides implementations of XSLT 2.0, XQuery 3.1, and XPath 3.1 at the basic level of conformance defined by W3C.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
