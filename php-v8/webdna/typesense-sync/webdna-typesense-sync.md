# webdna/typesense-sync

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, search, typesense

## 简介

Keep Craft content in a Typesense search index, and give search pages a safe, filtered search key, by declaring what to index.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
