# webdna/auditor

> 标签: Audit, Audit Log, Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, history, security

## 简介

An audit log for Craft CMS: who changed what, when, from where and what it said before, plus every sign-in and change of access, in one log.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
