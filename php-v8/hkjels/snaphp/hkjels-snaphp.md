# hkjels/snaphp

> 标签: SPDY, Simple, express, framework, snap, snaphp

## 简介

A lightweight, straightforward php-framework. Inspired by express & snap.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
