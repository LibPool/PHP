# armincms/annisa

> 标签: annisa, form, laravel

## 简介

A laravel form builder/manager based on laravelcollective package; inspired of joomla form builder.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
