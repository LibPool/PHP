# solophp/job-queue

> 标签: async, jobs, php, queue, scheduler

## 简介

A lightweight and reliable database-backed job queue for PHP with atomic claim, visibility timeout, and exponential backoff.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
