# dexterhd/yiidisquswidget

> 标签: disqus, widget, yii

## 简介

This is sample widget wich can help you to add DISQUS support in to your Yii site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
