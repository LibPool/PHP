# echodial/cleat

> 标签: authorize.net, billing, invoicing, keel, payments, subscriptions

## 简介

Self-hosted billing and invoicing for PHP: subscriptions, invoices, hosted pay pages and payment links over Authorize.net, with all billing state in your own MySQL/MariaDB.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
