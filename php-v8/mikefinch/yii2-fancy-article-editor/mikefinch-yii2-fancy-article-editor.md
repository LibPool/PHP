# mikefinch/yii2-fancy-article-editor

> 标签:  article,  editor, extension, yii2

## 简介

This extensions allows you create and edit articles on your yii2 website.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
