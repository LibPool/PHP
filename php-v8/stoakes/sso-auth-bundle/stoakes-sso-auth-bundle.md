# stoakes/sso-auth-bundle

> 标签: Authentication, SSO, Symfony2, cas, symfony3

## 简介

SSO authentication your Symfony2 and Symfony3 projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
