# xpiston/monetico

> 标签: 3dsecure, CIC, cmcic, credit-mutuel, laravel, monetico, monetico-paiement, payment, symfony

## 简介

Monetico Paiement (Crédit Mutuel / CIC) for PHP, Laravel and Symfony: sealed payment forms, verified notifications, capture and refund.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
