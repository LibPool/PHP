# vpndetection/symfony

> 标签: bundle, fraud-prevention, ip-intelligence, proxy-detection, symfony, tor, vpn, vpn-detection

## 简介

Official Symfony bundle for the VPNDetection API. Detect VPNs, proxies, Tor, hosting and CDN visitors on every request.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
