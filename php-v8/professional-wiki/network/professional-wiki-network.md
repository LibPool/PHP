# professional-wiki/network

> 标签: VISJS, chart, dynamic, force-directed graph, graph, incoming links, interactive, links, mediawiki, network, outgoing links, pages, visualization, what links here

## 简介

MediaWiki extension for adding interactive network visualizations to your wiki pages

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
