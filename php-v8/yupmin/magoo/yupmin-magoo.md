# yupmin/magoo

> 标签: Bleep out, Masking, credit card, email, mask, obfuscation, redact, security, sensitive

## 简介

PHP library to mask (redact) credit card numbers, emails and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
