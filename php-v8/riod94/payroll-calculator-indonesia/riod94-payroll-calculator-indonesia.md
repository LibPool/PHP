> 标签: TER, calculator, indonesia, pajak, payroll, pph, pph21, pph23, pph26, tarif efektif rata-rata, tax, withholding, withholding tax

## 简介

A comprehensive Indonesian payroll calculator library for PHP projects with tax calculations (PPh21, PPh23, PPh26).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
