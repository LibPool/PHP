# hydrakit/broadcast

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Tell other processes that something changed: a publisher over Redis pub/sub, listen tokens, and an SSE hub that fans broadcasts out to browsers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
