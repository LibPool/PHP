# tomac1/rockadmin

> 标签: admin, administration, backend, crud, datagrid, framework-agnostic, sdk

## 简介

A dependency-free PHP administration SDK. Mount it on a URL, describe your pages in configuration, and get a working admin.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
