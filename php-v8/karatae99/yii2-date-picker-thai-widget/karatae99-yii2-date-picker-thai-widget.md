> 标签: buddhist, calendar, datepicker, extension, thai, widget, yii 2, yii2

## 简介

Bootstrap DatePicker Thai Buddhist-Era (B.E.) widget for Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
