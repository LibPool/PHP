# purplespider/silverstripe-discourse-sso

> 标签: SSO, discourse, silverstripe

## 简介

Log in to Discourse with Silverstripe member accounts

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
