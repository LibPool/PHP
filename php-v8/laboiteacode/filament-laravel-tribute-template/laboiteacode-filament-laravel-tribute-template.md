# laboiteacode/filament-laravel-tribute-template

> 标签: filament, filament-plugin, filament-theme, laboiteacode, laravel, tribute-theme

## 简介

A free Filament v5 theme paying tribute to the laravel.com art direction. First of a series of homage themes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
