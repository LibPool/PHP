# laboiteacode/filament-activity-timeline

> 标签: Audit, activity, activity-log, activitylog, filament, history, laboiteacode, laravel, spatie, timeline

## 简介

The semantic activity timeline for Filament. Turn technical activity logs into clear, localized business events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
