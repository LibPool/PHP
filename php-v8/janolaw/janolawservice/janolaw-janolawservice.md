# janolaw/janolawservice

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, janolaw

## 简介

Janolaw Service module is used for individual generation and automatic update of legal documents for online presence, blogs and online shops / online sale in Germany.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
