# raffaelj/cockpit-videolinkfield

> 标签: cockpit, thumbnail download, vimeo, youtube

## 简介

Video thumbnail downloader from YouTube and Vimeo for Cockpit CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
