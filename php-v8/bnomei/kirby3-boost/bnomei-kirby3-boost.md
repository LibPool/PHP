# bnomei/kirby3-boost

> 标签: apcu, autoid, boost, cache, file, kirby, kirby-cms, kirby-plugin, lookup, memcache, memcached, memory, mysql, pages, performance, random, redis, sqlite, token, uuid

## 简介

Boost the speed of Kirby by having content files of files/pages/users cached, with fast lookup based on uuid.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
