# cable8mm/sentence-to-keywords

> 标签: keywords, searchengine, sentence

## 简介

Use a search engine(naver.com) to extract important keywords from sentences and correct typos.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
