# lapaygroup/doctrine-pdo-dblib

> 标签: dblib, doctrine, doctrine-dblib, mssql, pdo, pdo_dblib

## 简介

Doctrine driver for support of the pdo_dblib (MSSQL)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
