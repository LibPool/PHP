# tenbruggencate/stockalert-lite

> 标签: back-in-stock, gdpr, notify-me, shopware, shopware6, stock-alert, waitlist

## 简介

Shopware 6 back-in-stock alerts: "Notify me when it's back" on sold-out products, with double opt-in, Flow Builder events and per-sales-channel config.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
