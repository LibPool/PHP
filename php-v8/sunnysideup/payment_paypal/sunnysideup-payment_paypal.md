# sunnysideup/payment_paypal

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, e-commerce, ecommerce, pay now, pay pal, payment, paypal, shop, silverstripe

## 简介

add paypal to your payment options for the silverstripe payment and ecommerce modules

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
