# sunnysideup/widgetextensions

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, silverstripe, widgetextensions

## 简介

add widgets to your site - not just your silverstripe blog

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
