> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, colours, e-commerce, ecommerce, product colours, product variation colours, shop, silverstripe, variation colours

## 简介

add colours to your silverstripe e-commerce product variations for use in, for example, clothing stores.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
