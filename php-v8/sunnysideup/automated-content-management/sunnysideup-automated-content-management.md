# sunnysideup/automated-content-management

> 标签: ChatGpt, Open AI, ai, automated, automation, chat gpt, claude, cms, content editor, content management, lama, llama, llm, openai

## 简介

Use AI / LLM (like chatGPT) to manage your content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
