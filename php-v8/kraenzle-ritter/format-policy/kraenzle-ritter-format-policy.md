# kraenzle-ritter/format-policy

> 标签: archives, digital-preservation, format-policy, laravel, preingest, pronom, siegfried, tiff

## 简介

A format policy registry and preingest for Laravel: tools, commands and rules that normalise files before they are ingested, verified and recorded.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
