# kraenzle-ritter/clamav-scan

> 标签: antivirus, archives, clamav, clamd, digital-preservation, malware, virus-scan

## 简介

Scan files for malware with ClamAV: a small clamd client (INSTREAM over a Unix or TCP socket) and a fake scanner for tests.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
