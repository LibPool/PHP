# rawand201/kurdish-escpos

> 标签: ESC-POS, TSPL, arabic, badini, bidi, escpos, harfbuzz, kurdish, label-printer, laravel, pos, receipt, rtl, sorani, thermal-printer, zebra, zpl

## 简介

Print correctly shaped Kurdish (Sorani and Badini), Arabic and English receipts and labels on ESC/POS thermal printers and Zebra (ZPL) and TSC (TSPL) label printers. Pure PHP, no binaries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
