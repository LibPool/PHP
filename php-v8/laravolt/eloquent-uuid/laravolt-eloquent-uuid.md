# laravolt/eloquent-uuid

> 标签: eloquent, laravel, laravolt, uuid

## 简介

Universally Unique Identifier (UUID) for Laravel Eloquent

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
