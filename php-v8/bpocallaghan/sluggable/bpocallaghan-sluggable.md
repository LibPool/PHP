# bpocallaghan/sluggable

> 标签: eloquent, laravel, slug, sluggable, unique

## 简介

Provides a HasSlug trait that will generate a unique slug when saving your Laravel Eloquent model.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
