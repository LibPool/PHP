# mbagnall/nodeaccess

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides access control to Drupal nodes based on role. Access can also be controled by specific user. Drupal 9 ready.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
