# webimpian/laravel-log-central

> 标签: error-tracking, laravel, log-central, logging

## 简介

Ship logs and exceptions from a Laravel app to a Log Central server.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
