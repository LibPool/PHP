# jorisnoo/craft-cdn-asset-purge

> 标签: Craft, assets, cdn, cms, craftcms, purge

## 简介

Purge CDN URLs when Craft CMS assets are replaced, renamed, moved, or deleted.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
