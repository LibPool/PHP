# miolae/yii2-billing

> 标签: billing, finances, invoice, miolae, yii2, yii2-billing

## 简介

Flexible billing module to manage different accounts and pay systems and make transactions

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
