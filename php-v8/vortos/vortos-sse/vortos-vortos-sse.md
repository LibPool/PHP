# vortos/vortos-sse

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Vortos Server-Sent Events — realtime change nudges to browsers over a Mercure hub, with a Redis-backed signal and bounded in-PHP stream as the hub-less fallback

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
