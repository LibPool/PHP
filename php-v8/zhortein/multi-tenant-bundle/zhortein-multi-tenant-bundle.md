> 标签: Messenger, dns, doctrine, domain, hosting, mailer, multi-tenancy, multi-tenant, multitenant, orm, postgresql, saas, storage, subdomain, symfony, symfony-bundle, tenant

## 简介

A fail-closed Symfony 7.4 LTS and Symfony 8.x multi-tenant bundle with PostgreSQL >= 16 defense-in-depth support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
