# zhortein/auditable-bundle

> 标签: Audit, Auditable, Messenger, doctrine, history, log, symfony, symfony-bundle

## 简介

Attribute-based audit trail & history logging for Doctrine entities in Symfony, with optional Messenger async persistence.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
