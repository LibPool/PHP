# inboxili/inboxili-laravel

> 标签: email, inboxili, laravel, mail-transport, transactional-email

## 简介

Laravel integration for the Inboxili transactional email API: service provider, facade, queued job and mail transport

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
