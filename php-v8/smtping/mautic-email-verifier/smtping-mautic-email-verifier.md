# smtping/mautic-email-verifier

> 标签: Email Verifier, Mautic, disposable email, email validation, email verification, integration, plugin, smtping, spamtrap

## 简介

SMTPing Email Verifier for Mautic: verify contacts, block risky addresses on forms, route campaigns by verification result.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
