# blackaha/module-pricing-updater

> 标签: adobe commerce, blackaha, magento, magento2, price, price update, pricing, repricing, sku

## 简介

Keeps your Magento product prices in line with the prices you set in BlackAha: matched by SKU, applied automatically, every result reported back.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
