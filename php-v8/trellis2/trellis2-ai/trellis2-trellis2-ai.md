# trellis2/trellis2-ai

> 标签: ai-3d, image-to-3d, prompt-to-3d, trellis-2, trellis2

## 简介

Trellis 2 AI 3D generator metadata package for 2D to 3D, photo to 3D, and prompt to 3D workflows.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
