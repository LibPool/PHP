# pnscripts/pn-press

> 标签: blog, cms, filament, laravel, plugins, themes

## 简介

PN Scripts Press: a Laravel 13 + Filament 5 CMS with block pages, menus, media, SEO, revisions, and WordPress-style plugins and themes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
