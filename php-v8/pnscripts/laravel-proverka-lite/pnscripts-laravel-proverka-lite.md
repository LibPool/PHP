# pnscripts/laravel-proverka-lite

> 标签: Bulgaria, bulstat, egn, eik, laravel, lnch, validation, vat

## 简介

Bulgarian identifier validation for Laravel: EGN, LNCh, EIK/BULSTAT and VAT numbers with their real checksums, English and Bulgarian messages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
