# pnscripts/pn-invoice

> 标签: E-Invoice, EN16931, cii, einvoice, invoice, peppol, ubl, validation, xml

## 简介

PN Scripts Invoice: framework-agnostic PHP library to generate and validate EN 16931 e-invoices in UBL 2.1 and UN/CEFACT CII D16B syntax.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
