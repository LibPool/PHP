# dashzeveg/flarum-custom-head

> 标签: flarum

## 简介

Add custom HTML, scripts, and meta tags to the head section of your forum pages from the admin panel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
