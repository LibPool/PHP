# imagewize/elayne-legal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Child theme of Elayne for law firms: Legal Corporate and Legal Blue style variations, two-bar header, hero with enquiry form and filterable practice areas.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
