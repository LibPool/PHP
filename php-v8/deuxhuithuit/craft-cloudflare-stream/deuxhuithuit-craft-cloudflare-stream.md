# deuxhuithuit/craft-cloudflare-stream

> 标签: cloudflare, craft-cms, stream, video

## 简介

Upload your videos assets to Cloudflare Stream from Craft CMS assets interface

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
