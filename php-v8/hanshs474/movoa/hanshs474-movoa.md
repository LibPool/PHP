## 简介

Unlimited AI video generator with sound: on paid plans the tenth take of a shot costs what the first did, and your first clip is free to make right now. Official PHP SDK for Movoa (movoa.ai): text to video and photo to video with sound from PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
