# whitecube/laravel-cookie-consent

> 标签: compliant, cookies, eu, gdpr, laravel, whitecube

## 简介

Register, configure and ask for cookies consent in a EU-compliant way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
