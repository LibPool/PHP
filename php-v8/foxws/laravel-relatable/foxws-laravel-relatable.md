# foxws/laravel-relatable

> 标签: eloquent, foxws, laravel, laravel-relatable, related, relations

## 简介

Relate Eloquent models to other models, with a base score and boost to control their priority.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
