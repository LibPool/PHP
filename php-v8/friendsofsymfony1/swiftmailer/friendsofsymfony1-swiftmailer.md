# friendsofsymfony1/swiftmailer

> 标签: email, mail, mailer

## 简介

Fork of unmaintained Swiftmailer, free feature-rich PHP mailer

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 7；已收录于 php-v7, php-v8。
