# jweiland/sync-crop-areas

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, area, crop, jweiland.net, sync, synchronize, typo3, variants

## 简介

Sync first found crop area to all other CropVariants

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
