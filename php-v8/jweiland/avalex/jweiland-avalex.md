# jweiland/avalex

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, avalex, typo3

## 简介

avalex: Automated Legal Texts - Integrates automatically updated and legally compliant texts (Imprint, Privacy, Cancellation notice, and Terms and conditions) into your TYPO3 website. Protects against legal warnings through automatic updates via the avalex API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
