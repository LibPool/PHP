# jweiland/video-shariff

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, dsgvo, gdpr, shariff, typo3, video

## 简介

Video Shariff - This extension provides more privacy when embedding videos in frontend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
