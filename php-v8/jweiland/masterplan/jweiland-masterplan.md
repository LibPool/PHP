# jweiland/masterplan

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, masterplan, typo3

## 简介

Masterplan - Show projects with details, dates, images, and downloads.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
