# bjthecod3r/laravel-docket

> 标签: approvals, change-management, eloquent, issues, laravel, notifications, reports, state-machine, tickets, workflow

## 简介

Reporting and change management for Laravel: threaded reports with statuses, ownership, attachments, notifications and reviewable change proposals for any Eloquent model.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
