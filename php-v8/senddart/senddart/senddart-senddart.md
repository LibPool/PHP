# senddart/senddart

> 标签: api, email, marketing, sdk, senddart, transactional

## 简介

Official SendDart PHP SDK — send transactional and marketing email from your own verified domain.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
