# absolutoffice/xcurl

> 标签: curl, http, https, tls, transport

## 简介

HTTPS transport and policy client with cURL, PHP streams, and direct TLS socket capability fallback.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
