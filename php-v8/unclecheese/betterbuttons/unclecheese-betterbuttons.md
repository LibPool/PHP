# unclecheese/betterbuttons

> 标签: buttons, gridfield, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds new form actions and buttons to GridField detail form for usability enhancements.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
