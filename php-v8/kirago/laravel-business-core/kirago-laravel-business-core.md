> 标签: billing, business, business-core, core, invoice, jsonapi, kirago, laravel, laravel-package, multi-tenancy, multi-tenant, order, payment, product, tenancy

## 简介

A Laravel business core multi-tenancies management data structure: orders, products, invoices, payments and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 8；已收录于 php-v8。
