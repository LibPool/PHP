# cornford/packtpublr

> 标签: collect, ebook, free, learning, packtpub, redeem

## 简介

An easy way to collect free learning ebooks from Packtpub.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
