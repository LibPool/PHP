# verotel/flexpay-php-client

> 标签: billing.creditcard, cardbilling, credit card, flexpay, payment, verotel

## 简介

A client to Verotel FlexPay online payment gateway

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
