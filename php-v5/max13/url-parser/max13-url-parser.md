# max13/url-parser

> 标签: laravel, parser, url, url-parser

## 简介

URL parser for PHP, allow you to extract every piece of information of a URL/HOST, structured and clean.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
