# aklump/aspect-ratio

> 标签: aspect-ratio, calculator, cli, images, responsive, scaling

## 简介

Calculate absolute and nearby aspect ratios from a dimension set.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
