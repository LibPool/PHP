# vladkukushkin/yii2-articles

> 标签: articles, category, contents, module, seo, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 Articles to create, manage, and delete articles in a Yii2 site.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
