# rikusen0335/lang-japanese-extended

> 标签: extension, flarum, japanese, locale, translation

## 简介

Japanese language pack for Flarum's third-party extensions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
