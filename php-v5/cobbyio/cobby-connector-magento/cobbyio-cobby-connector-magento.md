# cobbyio/cobby-connector-magento

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This extension for Magento provides a direct integration of the cobby management methods to your Magento shop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
