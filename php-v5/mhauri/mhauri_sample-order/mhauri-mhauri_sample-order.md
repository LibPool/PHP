# mhauri/mhauri_sample-order

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Povides a solution to order a free sample of product through the normal checkout process, configured by a custom attribute.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
