# truongwp/sanitizer

> 标签: php, sanitization, sanitize, sanitizer

## 简介

Simple and stand-alone sanitizer library with no dependencies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
