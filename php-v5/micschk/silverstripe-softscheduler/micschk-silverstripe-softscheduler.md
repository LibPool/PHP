# micschk/silverstripe-softscheduler

> 标签: embargo, expiry, schedule, silverstripe, sitetree

## 简介

Adds a very simple way to schedule (Embargo/Expire) SiteTree items

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
