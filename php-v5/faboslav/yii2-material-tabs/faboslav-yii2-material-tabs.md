> 标签: click, design, extension, faboslav, material, md, mouse, ripple, tables, tabs, widget, yii2

## 简介

Animated Material Tabs widget for Yii2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
