# eloquent/depict

> 标签: dump, export, output, print, recursive

## 简介

A fast, recursion-safe, and succinct replacement for var_dump/var_export/print_r.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
