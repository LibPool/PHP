# bluecadet/bluecadet-wp-utils

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Bluecadet utilities for WordPress. Timber Helpers, CPT and CT registration, and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
