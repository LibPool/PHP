# farsh4d/laravel-bank

> 标签: Bank, gateway, internet payment gateway, ipg, laravel, payment, payment gateway

## 简介

A package to connect to any IPG to make a payment

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
