# fromholdio/silverstripe-embedfield

> 标签: cms, formfield, oembed, react, silverstripe, video

## 简介

A form field for adding oembed objects to pages or dataobjects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
