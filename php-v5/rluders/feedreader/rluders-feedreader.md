# rluders/feedreader

> 标签: cms, feed, october, octobercms, reader, widget, wordpress

## 简介

Feed Reader plugin for OctoberCMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
