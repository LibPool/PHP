# adiamond/cruddy

> 标签: admin, administrator, backend, cms, crud, dashboard, eloquent, laravel

## 简介

Backend interface for handling CRUD operations on your Laravel Eloquent models.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
