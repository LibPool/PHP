# dashifen/acf-sharing-settings

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

An ACF field group for a settings page relating to how a site appears on Twitter and Facebook.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
