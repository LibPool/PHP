# skimia/stump

> 标签: angular, cmf, cms, framework, laravel, skimia, stump

## 简介

Laravel 5 based One Page Content Management Framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
