# rasteiner/k3-awesome-picker

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Kirby 3 panel field that lets you choose a (free) icon from Font Awesome

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
