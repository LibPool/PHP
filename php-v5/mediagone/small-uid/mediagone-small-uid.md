# mediagone/small-uid

> 标签: UID, database, entity, guid, primary key, ulid, uuid

## 简介

Small Unique Identifier - Quite like an ULID, but half smaller (64 bits).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
