# scalecommerce/videooptimizer-shopware

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Deliver fast, adaptive, CDN-hosted videos in Shopware with VideoOptimizer - a privacy-friendly alternative to YouTube, Vimeo and self-hosting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
