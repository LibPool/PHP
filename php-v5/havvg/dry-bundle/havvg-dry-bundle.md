# havvg/dry-bundle

> 标签: dont-repeat-yourself, dry, helper

## 简介

A Symfony2 bundle providing helpers to ease usage of common patterns.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
