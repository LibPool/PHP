# anh/feed-builder

> 标签: atom, feed, generation, rss, rss2

## 简介

Library for building and validating RSS 2.0 and Atom 1.0 feeds

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
