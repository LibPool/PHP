# claudioldf/php-sass

> 标签: css, sass, scss

## 简介

Compiles your SASS .scss files to .css every time your run your app (in development) - forked from panique/php-sass

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
