# nimeso/dropzone

> 标签: Forms, cms, files, silverstripe, upload, uploader

## 简介

An HTML5 upload field for the CMS and frontend forms.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
