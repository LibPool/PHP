# sourcebroker/deployer-typo3-deploy-ci

> 标签: ci, deployer, gitlab, typo3

## 简介

Deploy for TYPO3 with Deployer at CI level

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
