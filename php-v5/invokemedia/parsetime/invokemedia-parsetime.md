# invokemedia/parsetime

> 标签: date, function, parse, time

## 简介

This function can parse times in sentences that are in a range like 11 AM to 5 PM or 5:30 to 1:21.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
