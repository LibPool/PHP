# yidas/phpexcel-helper

> 标签: PHPExcel, excel, php, phpexcel helper, sheet

## 简介

Creating Excel with easy and artistic way based on PHPExcel

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
