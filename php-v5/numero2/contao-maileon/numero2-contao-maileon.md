# numero2/contao-maileon

> 标签: api, automation, contacts, email marketing, form, maileon, newsletter

## 简介

Adds the possibility to send form data to Maileon.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
