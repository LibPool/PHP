# airani/yii2-telegram-log

> 标签: bot, log, telegram, yii2

## 简介

Yii 2.0 telegram log target, send logs to the telegram chats or channels

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
