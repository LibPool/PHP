# innomedio/sulu-form-landing-page-bundle

> 标签: form, innomedio, sulu, sulu-form, symfony

## 简介

Define redirects in Sulu forms after a successful form submit.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
