# bisonlab/sakonnin-bundle

> 标签: logging messages feedback symfony

## 简介

A message/log/feedback handling bundle with callback/replies capabilities

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
