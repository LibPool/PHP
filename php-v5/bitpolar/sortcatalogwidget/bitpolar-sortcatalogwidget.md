# bitpolar/sortcatalogwidget

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds the ability to sort a catalog widget by a product attribute in Magento 2.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
