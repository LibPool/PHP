# paymob/magento-payment

> 标签: paymob, paymob Payment

## 简介

Paymob payment for for Magento2 e-commerce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

- 源码仓库：https://gitlab.com/paymob-integrations/magento2.git
