# convergine/craft-brevo

> 标签: brevo, craftcms, mailer, mailer adapter, sendinblue

## 简介

Brevo (previously "SendInBlue") mailer adapter for Craft CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
