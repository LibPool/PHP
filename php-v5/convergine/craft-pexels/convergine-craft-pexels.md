# convergine/craft-pexels

> 标签: Craft, cms, convergine, craft-plugin, craftcms, pexels, royalty free photos, stock photos

## 简介

This plugin allows searching and downloading photos from "Pexels.com"

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
