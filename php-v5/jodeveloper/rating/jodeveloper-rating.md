# jodeveloper/rating

> 标签: Rate, Rating, eloquent, laravel, model, star, stars, system

## 简介

Laravel Eloquent Rating allows you to assign ratings to any model.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
