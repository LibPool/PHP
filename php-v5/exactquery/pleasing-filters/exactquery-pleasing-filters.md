# exactquery/pleasing-filters

> 标签: JS, assetic, css, minify, php, pleasing, prefix, sass, sassc, scss, vendor prefix

## 简介

Pleasing and Assetic  filters for prefixing CSS, compiling of SCSS, and minification of and JS assets.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
