# webbuilders-group/silverstripe-gridfielditemtype

> 标签: gridfield, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds type/class picking functionality to SilverStripe 4's GridField

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
