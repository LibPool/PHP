# ordermind/logical-authorization-bundle

> 标签: authorization

## 简介

Bundle for Symfony 4 supporting flexible and convenient model, field and route level permissions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
