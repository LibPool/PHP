# symbiote/silverstripe-datatransfer

> 标签: export, import, silverstripe, symbiote

## 简介

Assists with transferring data between Silverstripe installations

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
