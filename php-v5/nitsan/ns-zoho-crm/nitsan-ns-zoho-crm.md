# nitsan/ns-zoho-crm

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, Zoho, nitsan

## 简介

Easily install and configure your powermail form with zoho CRM, read more at documentation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
