# nitsan/ns-whatsapp

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, nitsan, whatsapp

## 简介

TYPO3 WhatsApp Chat Integration - Seamlessly integrate WhatsApp chat into your TYPO3 website with customizable Style 1 button. Live-Demo: https://demo.t3planet.com/t3-extensions/social-networking/whats-app

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
