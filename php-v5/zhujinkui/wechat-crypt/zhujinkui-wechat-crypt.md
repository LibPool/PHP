# zhujinkui/wechat-crypt

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This is the encryption and decryption of the complete user information including the sensitive data of Wechat.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
