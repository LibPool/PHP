# neilnz/nzbankaccountvalidation

> 标签: Bank account, new zealand, nz, validation

## 简介

A library to validate the checksums of New Zealand bank account numbers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
