# paragonie/easyrsa

> 标签: asymmetric, cryptography, public-key, rsa

## 简介

Simple and secure asymmetric encryption powered by PHPSecLib

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
