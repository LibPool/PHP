# asconsulting/isotope_product_attachment

> 标签: attachment, contao, file, isotope, product

## 简介

Product attribute that allows you to attach file(s) to a isotope product.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
