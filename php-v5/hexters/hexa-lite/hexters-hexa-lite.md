# hexters/hexa-lite

> 标签: Filament access control, Filament integration, Filament panel, Filament permission setup, Filament resource access, Filament widget permissions, Hexa Filament, Hexa installation, Hexa plugin, access permissions Filament, hexters ladmin, role and permission Filament, role management Filament, role management PHP

## 简介

Filament Hexa Lite is a effortless role and permission management plugin for Filament

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
