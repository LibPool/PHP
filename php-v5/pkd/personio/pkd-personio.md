# pkd/personio

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, extension, hr, personio

## 简介

Personio extension, based on extbase & fluid for TYPO3 CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
