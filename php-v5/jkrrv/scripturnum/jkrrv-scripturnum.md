# jkrrv/scripturnum

> 标签: Passage, bible, scripture

## 简介

Fit references of Scripture passages in just 32 bits (an unsigned integer)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
