# xr0m3oz/yii2-eauth

> 标签: Authentication, OpenId, eauth, extension, oauth, yii2

## 简介

Fork (Nodge/yii2-eauth) Yii2 EAuth Extension. EAuth allows to authenticate users with accounts on other websites (Google, Facebook, Twitter, etc).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
