# mediaweb/silverstripe-yearcalendar

> 标签: calendar, cms, events, module, schedule, silverstripe

## 简介

A calendar module to display events throughout the year

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
