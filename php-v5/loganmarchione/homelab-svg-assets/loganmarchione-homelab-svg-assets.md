# loganmarchione/homelab-svg-assets

> 标签: homelab, icons, svg

## 简介

Full-color SVG icons of homelab-related software, products, and brands

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
