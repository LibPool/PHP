# ameysam/payping

> 标签: PayPing, Payment-Verify, laravel, payment

## 简介

This is for simple (payment & verify payment) transaction by payping api.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
