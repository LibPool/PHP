# viewflex/forex

> 标签: conversion, currency, exchange rate, forex, money

## 简介

Providing live currency exchange rates in Laravel and Lumen, with configurable provider and caching.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
