# ramon/guest-cta

> 标签: CTA, extension, flarum, guest, login, signup

## 简介

Hide links from guests and show a Login/Sign Up CTA between posts in discussions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
