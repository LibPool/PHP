# matuck/kickasstorfeedhandler

> 标签: TorFeed, feed, kickasstorrents, rss, torrent

## 简介

Process torrent feeds from kickass torrents into usable items

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
