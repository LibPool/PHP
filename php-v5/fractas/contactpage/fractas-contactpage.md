# fractas/contactpage

> 标签: cms, contact, contact-page, module, silverstripe

## 简介

Simple & configurable SilverStripe module to notify admin on contact form submission

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
