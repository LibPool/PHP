# duzun/p2peg

> 标签: PRNG, RNG, cryptography, entropy, javascript, p2p, php, random, seed

## 简介

Peer to Peer Entropy Generator (or Random numbers generator with p2p seeding)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
