# arillo/logslackwriter

> 标签: error, log, logging, reporting, silverstripe, slack

## 简介

Publish SilverStripe errors and warnings into a Slack channel via webhook.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
