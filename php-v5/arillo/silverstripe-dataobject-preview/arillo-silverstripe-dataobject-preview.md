# arillo/silverstripe-dataobject-preview

> 标签: dataobject, preview, silverstripe

## 简介

Shows a preview of your dataobjects like the one you get for pages.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
