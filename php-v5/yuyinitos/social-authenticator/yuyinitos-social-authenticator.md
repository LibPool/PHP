# yuyinitos/social-authenticator

> 标签: Authentication, laravel, oauth, social, socialite

## 简介

Makes authenticating users with your DB or Social networks a Breeze!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
