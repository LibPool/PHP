# cleantalk/typo3-cleantalk

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

CleanTalk adds anti-spam protection that is invisible to users, without the use of captcha/reCaptcha.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
