# cleantalk/contacts-encoder

> 标签: anti-spam, bot-protection, contact-form, email-obfuscation, email-protection, gdpr, mailto, phone-number, privacy, spam-protection

## 简介

Email obfuscation for email addresses and phone numbers. Stops smart bots via the CleanTalk anti-spam cloud. Works with any framework or plain HTML.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
