# parfaitementweb/statamic-locale-suggester

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Statamic addon to suggest your visitors to read the page in their preferred language.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
