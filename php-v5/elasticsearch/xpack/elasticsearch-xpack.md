# elasticsearch/xpack

> 标签: client, elasticsearch, search, xpack

## 简介

XPack namespace for Elasticsearch-PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

- 官网：https://packagist.org/packages/elasticsearch%2Fxpack
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch-x-pack-php.git

## 获取地址

- Composer 安装：`composer require elasticsearch/xpack`
- Packagist 仓库：https://repo.packagist.org/p2/elasticsearch/xpack.json
- 许可证：Apache 2
- PHP 要求：>=5.4
