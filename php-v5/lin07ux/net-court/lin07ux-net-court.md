# lin07ux/net-court

> 标签: net court, sdk

## 简介

NetCourt is a SDK for HangZhou NetCourt notary: original confirmation and obtain evidence.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
