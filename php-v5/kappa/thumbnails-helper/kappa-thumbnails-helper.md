# kappa/thumbnails-helper

> 标签: Kappa, Thumbnails, framework, helpers, nette

## 简介

Simple and intelligent system for creating and work with thumbnails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
