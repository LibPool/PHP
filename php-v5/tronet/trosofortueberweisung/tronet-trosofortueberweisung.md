# tronet/trosofortueberweisung

> 标签: OXID, eshop, klarna, modules, online bank transfer, pay now, payment, sofort, sofortueberweisung

## 简介

This is the Sofort.-module for OXID eShop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
