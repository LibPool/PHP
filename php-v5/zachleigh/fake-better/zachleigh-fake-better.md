> 标签: copy, custom, faker, faker provider, laravel, real copy, testing

## 简介

Faker provider to use more realistic fake data, extensive Laravel support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
