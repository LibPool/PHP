# openstream/mage2_rappenrunden

> 标签: comsolit, currency, francs, rappenrunden, round, runden, swiss

## 简介

Rounds store prices to 1/20th or 1/10th - ie: to 0.05 or 0.1 of your currency

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
