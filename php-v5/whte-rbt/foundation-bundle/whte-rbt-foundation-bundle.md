# whte-rbt/foundation-bundle

> 标签: core, foundation, toolkit

## 简介

The WhteRbtFoundationBundle is just a small toolkit for Symfony projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
