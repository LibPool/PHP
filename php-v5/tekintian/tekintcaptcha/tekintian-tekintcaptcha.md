## 简介

腾讯验证码, 腾讯防水墙, 验证码, 滑动验证码, 智能滑动验证码PHP扩展, TekinTCaptcha - A Client library for Tencent Captcha, a service that protect websites from spam and abuse.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
