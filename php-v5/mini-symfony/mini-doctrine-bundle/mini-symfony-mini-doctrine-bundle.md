# mini-symfony/mini-doctrine-bundle

> 标签: doctrine, mini, symfony

## 简介

Doctrine bundle which does not depend on the symfony framework bundle

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
