# 10n/health-vault-magento-2

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Magento 2 adapter that validates the health of cron jobs and offline tasks for the 10n Job Health Vault (https://vh.10n-software.com/)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
