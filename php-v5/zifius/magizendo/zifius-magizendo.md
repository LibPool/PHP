# zifius/magizendo

> 标签: coding-standard, phpcs

## 简介

PHP_CodeSniffer rules for Magento 1 and Magento 2 (Based on ECG, Zend and PSR2 rules)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
