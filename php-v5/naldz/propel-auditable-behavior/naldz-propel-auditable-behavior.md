# naldz/propel-auditable-behavior

> 标签: Behavior, propel

## 简介

A propel behavior that will let you check the original field values of a model even after save or delete.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
