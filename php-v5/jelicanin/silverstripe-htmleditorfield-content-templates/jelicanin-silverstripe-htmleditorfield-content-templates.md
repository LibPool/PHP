# jelicanin/silverstripe-htmleditorfield-content-templates

> 标签: canned content, content, content template, htmleditorfield, silverstripe, tinymce

## 简介

SilverStripe module for creation and organization predefined content templates for your Html Editor Fields

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
