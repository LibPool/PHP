# emanuellopes/composer-vendor-cleaner

> 标签: composer, vendor

## 简介

Removes as much as possible from your vendor dir when space or number of files is important

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
