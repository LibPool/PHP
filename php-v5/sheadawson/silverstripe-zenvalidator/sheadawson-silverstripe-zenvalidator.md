# sheadawson/silverstripe-zenvalidator

> 标签: Forms, parsley, silverstripe, validation

## 简介

Faster, easier client and server-side form validation for SilverStripe

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
