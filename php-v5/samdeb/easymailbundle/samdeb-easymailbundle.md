# samdeb/easymailbundle

> 标签: Symfony2, mail

## 简介

The easyMailBundle, for Symfony2, provide an easy way to send email with temple

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
