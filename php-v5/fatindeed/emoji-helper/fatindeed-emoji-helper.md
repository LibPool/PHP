# fatindeed/emoji-helper

> 标签: emoji, laravel, lumen, middleware

## 简介

A middleware for Laravel or Lumen to remove emoji characters.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
