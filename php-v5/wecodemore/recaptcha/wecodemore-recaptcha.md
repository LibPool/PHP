# wecodemore/recaptcha

> 标签: captcha, form, recaptcha

## 简介

ReCAPTCHA is a free CAPTCHA service that protects websites from spam and abuse.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
