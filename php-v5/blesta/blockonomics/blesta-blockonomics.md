# blesta/blockonomics

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Accept Bitcoin Payments, each online sale is deposited directly into your wallet. No KYC documentation required.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
