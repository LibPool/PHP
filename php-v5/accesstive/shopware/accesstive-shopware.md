# accesstive/shopware

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Accesstive is an all-in-one accessibility platform that helps websites become inclusive, compliant, and user-friendly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
