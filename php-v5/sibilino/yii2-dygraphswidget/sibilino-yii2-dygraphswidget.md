# sibilino/yii2-dygraphswidget

> 标签: chart, dygraphs, graph, widget, yii

## 简介

Graphing and charting widget for Yii 2, based on Dygraphs.js

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
