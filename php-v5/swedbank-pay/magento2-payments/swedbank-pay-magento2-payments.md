# swedbank-pay/magento2-payments

> 标签: magento, payment, swedbank-pay

## 简介

A Swedbank Pay Payments module for Magento 2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
