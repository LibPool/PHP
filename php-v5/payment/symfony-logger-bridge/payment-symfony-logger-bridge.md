# payment/symfony-logger-bridge

> 标签: Bridge, logger, symfony

## 简介

Bridge between Symfony lower/equal 2.1.X Logger and PSR0 LoggerInterface

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
