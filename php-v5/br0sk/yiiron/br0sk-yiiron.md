# br0sk/yiiron

> 标签: MQ, cache, iron.io, workers, yii

## 简介

Yiiron is a Yii extension that integrates the services of iron.io in the Yii Famework

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
