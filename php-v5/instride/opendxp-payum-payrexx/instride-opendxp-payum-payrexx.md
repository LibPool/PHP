# instride/opendxp-payum-payrexx

> 标签: ecommerce, opendxp, opendxp-bundle, payment, payrexx, payum

## 简介

Integrates the Payrexx payment gateway with OpenDXP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
