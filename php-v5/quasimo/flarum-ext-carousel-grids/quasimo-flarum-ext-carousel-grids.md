# quasimo/flarum-ext-carousel-grids

> 标签: carousel, extension, flarum, grid, slider

## 简介

Display carousel grids with background images, logos, and auto-scroll

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
