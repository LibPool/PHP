# trexology5/taxonomy5

> 标签: laravel, taxonomy

## 简介

Create and manage a heirarchical taxonomy of terms within different vocabularies

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
