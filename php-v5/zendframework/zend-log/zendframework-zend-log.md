# zendframework/zend-log

> 标签: ZendFramework, log, logging, zf

## 简介

Robust, composite logger with filtering, formatting, and PSR-3 support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
