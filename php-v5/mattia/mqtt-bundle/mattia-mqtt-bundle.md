# mattia/mqtt-bundle

> 标签: bundle, iot, mqtt, symfony

## 简介

A Symfony bundle for seamless MQTT integration and IoT messaging

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
