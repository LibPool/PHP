# ymw/imap-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Bundle provide solutions to operate with the IMAP protocol, as well as the NNTP, POP3 and local mailbox access methods

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
