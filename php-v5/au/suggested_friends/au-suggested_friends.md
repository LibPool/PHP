# au/suggested_friends

> 标签: AU, elgg, friends, plugin, suggested friends

## 简介

This plugin will help the users to find people they may know (or should know), it can suggest friends of user's friends and/or members of user's groups

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
