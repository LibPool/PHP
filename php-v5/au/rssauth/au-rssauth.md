# au/rssauth

> 标签: AU, elgg, plugin, rssauth

## 简介

Provides a number of methods of getting rss feeds of non-public content as a logged in user

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
