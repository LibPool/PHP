# au/river_privacy

> 标签: AU, elgg, plugin, river_privacy

## 简介

Sets non-object oriented river items to private access (eg. friend relationships)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
