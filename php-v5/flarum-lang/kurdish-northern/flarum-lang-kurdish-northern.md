# flarum-lang/kurdish-northern

> 标签: flarum, flarum-locale, kmr-locale, kurdish-northern, kurdish-northern-locale, locale

## 简介

Kurdish (Northern) language pack for Flarum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
