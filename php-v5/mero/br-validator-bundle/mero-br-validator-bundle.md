# mero/br-validator-bundle

> 标签: SF, brazil, mero, symfony, validator

## 简介

Bundle for Symfony with validators for Brazilian location.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
