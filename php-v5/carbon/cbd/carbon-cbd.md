# carbon/cbd

> 标签: FUSION, Neos, backend, carbon, flow, helper

## 简介

Make complex content structure (e.g. slider, maps, etc.) easily editable in Neos. Fast and bullet-proof

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
