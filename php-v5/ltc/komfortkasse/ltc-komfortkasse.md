# ltc/komfortkasse

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Komfortkasse module for Magento 2. Automatically process payments by bank wire transfer, cash on delivery, and invoice.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
