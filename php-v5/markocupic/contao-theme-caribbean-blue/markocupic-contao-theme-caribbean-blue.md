# markocupic/contao-theme-caribbean-blue

> 标签: contao, frontend, theme

## 简介

Contao Frontend Theme Caribbean Blue for Contao 4.x or 5.x

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
