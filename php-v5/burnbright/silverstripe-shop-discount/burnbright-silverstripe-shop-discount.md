# burnbright/silverstripe-shop-discount

> 标签: coupons, discount, ecommerce, shop, shopping cart, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds Discount and Coupon support for SilverShop.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
