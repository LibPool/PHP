# rikless/instagram-php-scraper

> 标签: instagram, scraper

## 简介

Fork of r.kapishev Instagram PHP Scraper. Get account information, photos and videos without any authorization

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
