# espertomagento/magento2-abandoned-cart-reminder

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A module which sends reminder email to customer upon cart abandonment after certain time.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
