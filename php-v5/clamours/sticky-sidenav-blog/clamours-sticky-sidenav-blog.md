# clamours/sticky-sidenav-blog

> 标签: blog, flarum, sidenav, sticky

## 简介

Make the blog sidebar section sticky and scrollable.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
