# codelight-eu/woocommerce-lhv-hire-purchase

> 标签: lhv, woocommerce, wordpress

## 简介

WordPress and WooCommerce plugin for LHV Bank hire-purchase payment gateway

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
