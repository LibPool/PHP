# mrpck/email

> 标签: MailAddress, NetworkCredential, SmtpClient, email, imap, mime, pop3, smtp, ssl, tls

## 简介

The Email Class Library is a high level wrapper around the SmtpClient.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
