# humanmade/asset-loader

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Utilities to seamlessly consume Webpack-bundled assets in WordPress themes & plugins.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
