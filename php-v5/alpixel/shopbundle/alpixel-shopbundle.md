# alpixel/shopbundle

> 标签: Symfony2, alpixel, bundle, ecommerce, eshop, symfony

## 简介

This bundle is used by our team to build an e-commerce website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
