# frischkraft/craft3-webpack5-tailwind2

> 标签: Craft, cms, craftcms, project

## 简介

Craft CMS 3 Boilerplate with TailwindCSS 2 and Webpack 5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
