# vrisiniinfotech/contao-onepage-bundle

> 标签: contao, level, multi, noconfiguration, onepage, seamless, sitetree

## 简介

Convert a multisite website to onepage in an instant. Easy customizable options.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
