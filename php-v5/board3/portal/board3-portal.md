# board3/portal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds a portal with several blocks to your forum. You can change the settings, move the blocks, add new blocks and more in the ACP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
