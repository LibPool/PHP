# fp/klarna-invoice

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

It unofficial repo of Klarna Invoice service with Composer added. We use it with https://github.com/Payum/KlarnaInvoice

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：http://developers.klarna.com
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/formapro/KlarnaInvoice.git
