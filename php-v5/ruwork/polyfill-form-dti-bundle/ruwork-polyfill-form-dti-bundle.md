# ruwork/polyfill-form-dti-bundle

> 标签: bundle, form, polyfill, symfony

## 简介

Symfony Form polyfill bundle adding input=datetime_immutable to date types.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
