# shornuk/craft-upload

> 标签: Craft, asset uploader, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, front end

## 简介

Front-end asset upload for user photos, fields, volumes and folders.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
