# alexeyshockov/symfony-signal-helper

> 标签: cli, console, pcntl, symfony

## 简介

Symfony Console helper to handle process signals (like termination)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
