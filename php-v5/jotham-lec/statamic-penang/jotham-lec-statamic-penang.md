# jotham-lec/statamic-penang

> 标签: page-builder, starter-kit, statamic, tailwindcss, theme

## 简介

Penang: a Statamic 6 starter kit for small-business websites, with a page builder and a theme builder, on Statamic Core.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
