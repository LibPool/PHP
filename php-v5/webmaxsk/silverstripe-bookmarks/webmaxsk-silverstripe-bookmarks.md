# webmaxsk/silverstripe-bookmarks

> 标签: bookmarks, silverstripe

## 简介

Looged in members can bookmark any internal or external page.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
