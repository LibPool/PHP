# vint3/remove-projects-in-divi

> 标签: plugin, wordpress

## 简介

Remove the Projects Post Type in Divi by Elegant Themes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
