# worteks/yii2auth-oidc

> 标签: authclient, oauth2, openid-connect, openidconnect, yii2

## 简介

OIDC connector for yii2-authclient

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
