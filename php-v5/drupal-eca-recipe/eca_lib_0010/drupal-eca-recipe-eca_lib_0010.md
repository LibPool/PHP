# drupal-eca-recipe/eca_lib_0010

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Compares a user's roles before and after a save with two loops, and reports every role that was added or removed.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
