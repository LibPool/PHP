# bkief29/pcomposer

> 标签: composer, composer-installer, composer-plugin

## 简介

A composer plugin to provide the ability to share and reuse packages across the system.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
