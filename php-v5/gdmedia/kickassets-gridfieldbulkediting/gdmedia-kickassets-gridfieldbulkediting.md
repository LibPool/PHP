# gdmedia/kickassets-gridfieldbulkediting

> 标签: GridFieldBulkEditingTools, KickAssets, cms, gridfield-bulk-editing-tools, silverstripe, silverstripe-kickassets

## 简介

A compatibitly module to allow unclecheese/silverstripe-kickassets and colymba/gridfield-bulk-editing-tools to live happily together.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
