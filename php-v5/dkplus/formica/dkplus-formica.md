# dkplus/formica

> 标签: builder, setup, test, test-data

## 简介

Formica allows to easily use the builder pattern in your projects e.g. to setup your test data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
