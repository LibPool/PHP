# mhndev/yii2-rate

> 标签: Rate, Unlike, dislike, like, rank, yii, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 Multi Purpose Rate System (Supports like, dislike, rate with other numbers or text)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
