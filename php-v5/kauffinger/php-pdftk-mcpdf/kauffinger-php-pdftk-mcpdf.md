# kauffinger/php-pdftk-mcpdf

> 标签: pdf, pdftk

## 简介

A PDF conversion and form utility based on mikehaertl/pdftk and vigicorp/php-mcpdf. Dynamically usable with mcpdf.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
