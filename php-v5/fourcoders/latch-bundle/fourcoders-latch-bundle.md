# fourcoders/latch-bundle

> 标签: fourcoders, latch, php, security, symfony

## 简介

Easy integration of Latch in your symfony2 project.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
