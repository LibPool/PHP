# allyoullneed/livewire-forms

> 标签: Forms, cms, laravel, livewire, statamic

## 简介

Tag for rendering Statamic forms dynamically, powered by Livewire 4

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
