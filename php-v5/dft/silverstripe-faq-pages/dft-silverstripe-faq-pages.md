# dft/silverstripe-faq-pages

> 标签: FAQ, help, silverstripe, support

## 简介

Adds 'FAQ' pages (with questions, answers and 'was this helpful') to a SilverStripe CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
