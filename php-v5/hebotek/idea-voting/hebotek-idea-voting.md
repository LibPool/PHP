# hebotek/idea-voting

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS

## 简介

Adds an idea voting platform. Logged-in frontend users can submit their own ideas, view published ideas and vote on other users ideas.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
