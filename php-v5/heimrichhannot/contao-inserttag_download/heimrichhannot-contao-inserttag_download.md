# heimrichhannot/contao-inserttag_download

> 标签: contao, download, inserttag

## 简介

Contao download inserttag, to provide downloadable files inside paragraphs, etc.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
