# heimrichhannot/contao-linkchecker

> 标签: contao, html, linkchecker, links

## 简介

Simple link checker tool, that comes with an back end widget and LinkChecker class.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
