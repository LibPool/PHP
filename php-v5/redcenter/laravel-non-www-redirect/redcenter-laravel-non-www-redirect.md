# redcenter/laravel-non-www-redirect

> 标签: Simple, laravel, non-www, redirect, www

## 简介

A simple package to redirect non-www to www in your Laravel project.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
