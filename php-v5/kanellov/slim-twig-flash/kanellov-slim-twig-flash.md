# kanellov/slim-twig-flash

> 标签: extension, flash, framework, message, provider, slim, twig, view

## 简介

A Twig extension to access Slim Flash messages in templates

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
