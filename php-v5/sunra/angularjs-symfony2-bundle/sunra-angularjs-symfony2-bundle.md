# sunra/angularjs-symfony2-bundle

> 标签: angularjs, bindonce, symfony

## 简介

AngularJS and set of usefull thirdparty libs - composer packaged and in symfony2 bundle

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
