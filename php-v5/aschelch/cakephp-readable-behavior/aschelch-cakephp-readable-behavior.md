# aschelch/cakephp-readable-behavior

> 标签: Behavior, cakephp

## 简介

CakePHP ReadableBehavior provides a simple behavior to make a 'Mark as Read/Unread' feature for your models

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
