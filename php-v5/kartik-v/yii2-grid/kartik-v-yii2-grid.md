# kartik-v/yii2-grid

> 标签: extension, grid, widget, yii2

## 简介

Yii 2 GridView on steroids. Various enhancements and utilities for the Yii 2.0 GridView widget.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
