# eastslopestudio/craft3-sites-field

> 标签: Craft, cms, craftcms, field, sites

## 简介

Craft Field type that allows the selection of Craft sites.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
