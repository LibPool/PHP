# daan/rss-featured-image

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds the post's Featured Image as an enclosure to your WordPress blog's RSS feed so you can use it in your newsletters.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
