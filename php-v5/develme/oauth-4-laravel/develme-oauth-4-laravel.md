# develme/oauth-4-laravel

> 标签: Lusitanian, laravel, oauth, php, powerschool

## 简介

OAuth Service Provider for Laravel 4 with Pearson's PowerSchool support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
