# tal7aouy/indent

> 标签: deindent, files, indent, indentation, spaces, strings, tabs

## 简介

Library to detect and convert indentation in strings and files.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
