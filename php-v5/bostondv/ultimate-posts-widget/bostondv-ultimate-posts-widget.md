# bostondv/ultimate-posts-widget

> 标签: wordpress

## 简介

The ultimate widget for displaying posts, custom post types or sticky posts with an array of options.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
