# arsthanea/kunstmaan-algolia-bundle

> 标签: algolia, autocomplete, cms, indexer, kunstmaan, search

## 简介

Enable indexing Kunstmaan Bundles CMS pages in Algolia

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
