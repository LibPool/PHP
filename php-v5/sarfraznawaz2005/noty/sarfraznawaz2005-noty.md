# sarfraznawaz2005/noty

> 标签: flash, laravel, notification, notify, noty

## 简介

Laravel package to incorporate noty flash notifications into laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
