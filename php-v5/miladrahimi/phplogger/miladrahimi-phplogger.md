# miladrahimi/phplogger

> 标签: free, library, logger, neat, php, powerful, psr-3

## 简介

Free PHP logger for neat and powerful projects!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
