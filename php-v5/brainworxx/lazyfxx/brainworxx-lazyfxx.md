# brainworxx/lazyfxx

> 标签: ImageMagick, PageSpeed, graphicsmagick, image processing, lazy loading

## 简介

LazyFxx, image lazy loading with placeholders made by the TYPO3 image processing. You can also write your own image processors.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
