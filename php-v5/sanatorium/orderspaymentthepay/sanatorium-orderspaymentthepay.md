# sanatorium/orderspaymentthepay

> 标签: cartalyst, extension, laravel, madeinsane, platform

## 简介

Payment service provider for ThePay payments

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
