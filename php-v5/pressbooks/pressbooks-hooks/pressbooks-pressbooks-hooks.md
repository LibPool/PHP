# pressbooks/pressbooks-hooks

> 标签: academic, non-fiction, textbook

## 简介

Named for educator and social critic bell hooks, this theme is designed for nonfiction, reports, white papers, manuals, and other types of not-so-bookish content. Fonts include Noticia Text and Open Sans.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
