# commercekings/privacy-bundle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

CommerceKings privacy bundle: the consent bridge and the cookie banner in one require, for opt-in notifications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
