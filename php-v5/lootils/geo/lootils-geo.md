# lootils/geo

> 标签: coordinates, geo, geodetic, geolocation, math

## 简介

A simple library for dealing with Earth geo coordinate manipulations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
