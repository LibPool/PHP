# adamjenkins/moodle-local_textless_forum

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Augments mod_forum so that selected forums accept only RecordRTC-recorded audio or video as the body of a post

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
