# adamjenkins/moodle-local_nomoreai

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Makes it as hard as practical for AI agents to do students' Moodle activities for them, and measures how often they are seen.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
