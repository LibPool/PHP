# unsettlingtrend/trend_personal

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Trend personal site: the trend_base foundation plus journal entries (ut_journal) and cooking recipes (ut_recipes).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
