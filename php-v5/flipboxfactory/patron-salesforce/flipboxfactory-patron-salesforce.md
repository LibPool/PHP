# flipboxfactory/patron-salesforce

> 标签: cms, craftcms, force.com, salesforce, yii2

## 简介

A Salesforce OAuth2 provider for Patron and Craft CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
