# phile/youtube

> 标签: cms, content, phile

## 简介

A plugin that generates Youtube videos based on IDs. It can be used in your theme, or in your Markdown.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
