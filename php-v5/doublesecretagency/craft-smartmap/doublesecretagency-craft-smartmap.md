# doublesecretagency/craft-smartmap

> 标签: Craft, address, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, geolocation, google-maps, locations, map, proximity-search, smart-map

## 简介

The most comprehensive proximity search and mapping tool for Craft.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
