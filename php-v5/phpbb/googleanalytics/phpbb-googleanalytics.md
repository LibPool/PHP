# phpbb/googleanalytics

> 标签: analytics, extension, google, phpbb

## 简介

A phpBB official extension that allows administrators to easily add Google Analytics to their forums.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

## 官网

- 官网：https://www.phpbb.com/customise/db/extension/googleanalytics/
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/phpbb-extensions/googleanalytics.git
