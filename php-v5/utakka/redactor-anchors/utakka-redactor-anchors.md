# utakka/redactor-anchors

> 标签: Craft, Redactor, anchor, craft-plugin, craftcms, redactoranchors

## 简介

Support for anchors in Craft CMS Redactor

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
