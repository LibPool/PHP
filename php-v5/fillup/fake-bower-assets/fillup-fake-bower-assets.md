# fillup/fake-bower-assets

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Use Composer "replace" to fake out installing bower-asset dependencies that aren't really needed

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
