# skynettechnologies/craft-skyneta11ypdfremediation

> 标签: AI-powered, accessibility, craft-plugin, pdf, remediation, scan

## 简介

AI-powered PDF accessibility remediation - upload PDFs, scan website PDFs, and download remediated files.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
