# elefant/app-wires

> 标签: SPA, SSR, alpine, alpine.js, alpinejs, api, endpoint, laravel-livewire, livewire, rest, restful, server-side rendering, single-page application, wires

## 简介

Wires app for the Elefant CMS auto-connects Alpine.js for easy single-page app development

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
