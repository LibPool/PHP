# josephdpurcell/wbm2cm

> 标签: drupal

## 简介

Migrates from Drupal 8.4 Workbench Moderation to Content Moderation.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
