# olol/lolesp-cal

> 标签: Agenda, LoL, Match, calendar, esport, ical, league of legends

## 简介

Generates iCal files with eSport League of Legends matches for your agenda.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
