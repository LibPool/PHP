# siwaonline/social_stream

> 标签: Siwa, TYPO3 CMS, social stream

## 简介

Social Stream - Crawls the data from a Social Media Page and saves it as news record

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
