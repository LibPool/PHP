# bazilio/yii2-stubs-generator

> 标签: autocomplete, phpstorm, stubs, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 component stubs generator for Yii::$app

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
