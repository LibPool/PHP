# sidus/publishing-bundle

> 标签: doctrine, publish, publishing

## 简介

Publish your Doctrine entities (or any kind of PHP object) on different remote data consumers

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
