# neyric/inbound-email-bundle

> 标签: Mailjet, email, inbound, parse, sendgrid, symfony

## 简介

Inbound emails for Symfony apps with Sendgrid and Mailjet support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
