# boots/media

> 标签: boots, image resize, media, utility, wordpress

## 简介

Easily empower your pages with the wp media uploader and utility for image resizing on the fly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
