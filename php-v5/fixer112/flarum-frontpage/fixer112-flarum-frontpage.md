# fixer112/flarum-frontpage

> 标签: discussion, flarum-extension, frontpage

## 简介

Flarum extension to mark discussion as frontpage.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
