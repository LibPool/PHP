# zwo3/newsletter_subscribe

> 标签: extension, newsletter, subscribe, tt_address, typo3

## 简介

Subscribe / Unsubscribe Extension (e.g. for Newsletter), depends on tt_address

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
