# dyrynda/laravel-ldap

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Leverage Laravel's middleware to ensure your LDAP-authenticated users stay that way.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
