# checkitsedo/form-pincodes

> 标签: Pincode, form, typo3

## 简介

TYPO3 CMS extension for sending defined pincodes with form e-mail finishers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
