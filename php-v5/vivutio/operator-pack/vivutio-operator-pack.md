# vivutio/operator-pack

> 标签: operator, pack, tourism, tours, vivutio

## 简介

What a tour operator of vivutio installs: the tours it sells, and the rooms it requests from the camps it trades with.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
