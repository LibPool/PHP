# vivutio/supplier-pack

> 标签: accommodation, pack, supplier, tourism, vivutio

## 简介

What a supplier of vivutio installs: the camps, lodges and hotels it runs, and their bookings.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
