# chefkoch/morphoji

> 标签: emoji, unicode, utf8

## 简介

A library to convert UTF-8 emoji characters to latin1 placeholders and vice versa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
