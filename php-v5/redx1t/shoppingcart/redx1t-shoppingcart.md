# redx1t/shoppingcart

> 标签: laravel, shoppingcart

## 简介

Laravel Shoppingcart - fork of hardevine/shoppingcart with Laravel 13 support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
