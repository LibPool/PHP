# cracksalad/phpmailer-pgp

> 标签: gpg, mailer, open-pgp

## 简介

PHPMailerPGP enables PHPMailer to send OpenPGP/GPG encrypted and signed e-mails

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
