# wikibusiness/elastica-bundle

> 标签: elastica, elasticsearch, symfony

## 简介

Slim wrapper bundle for Elastica, when FOSElasticaBundle is too much.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
