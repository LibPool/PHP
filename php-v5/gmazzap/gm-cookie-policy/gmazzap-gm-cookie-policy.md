# gmazzap/gm-cookie-policy

> 标签: cookie, cookie policy, wordpress

## 简介

A simple plugin to show a message for compliance with EU cookie law.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
