# k4/k4encodeemails

> 标签: Craft, StandalonePHPEnkoder, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, k4encodeemails

## 简介

A simple plugin that allows you to encode email addresses in your CMS content.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
