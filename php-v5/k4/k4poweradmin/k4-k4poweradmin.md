# k4/k4poweradmin

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, k4-poweradmin

## 简介

A simple plugin to hide fields in the controlpanel from non-admins.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
