# unyii2/yii2-imap

> 标签: cron, extension, imap, inbox, mail, widget, yii2

## 简介

yii2 extension to read and process mails from IMAP and PHP

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
