# mvccore/ext-form-field-text

> 标签: Forms, HTML5, email, emails, ext, extension, form, framework, gsm, input, inputs, mobile, mvc, mvccore, password, phone, plug-in, plugin, search, tel, telephone, text, textarea, texts, url, webform

## 简介

MvcCore - Extension - Form - Field - Text - form field types - input:text, input:email, input:password, input:search, input:tel, input:url and textarea.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
