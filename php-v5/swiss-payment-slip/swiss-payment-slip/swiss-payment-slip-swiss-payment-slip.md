# swiss-payment-slip/swiss-payment-slip

> 标签: ESR, Einzahlungsschein, Inpayment slip, Payment slip

## 简介

A library for creating Swiss payment slips (ESR)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
