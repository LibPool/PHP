# diggin/diggin-robotrules

> 标签: crawler, robots, robots.txt, robotstxt, spider

## 简介

parser/handler for Robots Exclusion Protocol (robots.txt and more)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
