# pmingram/php-apple-signin

> 标签: JWK, apple, id, in, ios, jwt, login, php, sign, 苹果登陆

## 简介

A simple library to decode and parse Apple Sign In client tokens.苹果账号登陆

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
