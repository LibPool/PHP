# w-vision/newsletter-definition

> 标签: imports, pimcore, pimcore-bundle, pimcore-plugin

## 简介

Lets you easily create Newsletter Definitions and re-use them across all your newsletters

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
