# maba/avatar-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, avatar, avatar bundle, gravatar

## 简介

Symfony2 bundle for getting and displaying user's avatar

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
