# balancer/blib-morfology-ru

> 标签: library, morfology, php, russian

## 简介

Russian morfology based on phpMorfy. With dictionaries and UTF-8 support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
