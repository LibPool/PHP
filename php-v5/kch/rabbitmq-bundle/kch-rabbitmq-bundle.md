# kch/rabbitmq-bundle

> 标签: AMQP, Symfony2, message, queue, rabbitmq, symfony, symfony3, symfony4

## 简介

Integrates php-amqplib with Symfony & RabbitMq. Formerly oldsound/rabbitmq-bundle.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
