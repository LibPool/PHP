# asc/revisionable

> 标签: ardent, history, laravel, model, revision

## 简介

Keep a revision history for your models without thinking, created as a package for use with Laravel 4

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
