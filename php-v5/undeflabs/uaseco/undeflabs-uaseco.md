# undeflabs/uaseco

> 标签: ManiaPlanet, TrackMania, UASECO, controller

## 简介

UASECO is a fork of the XAseco2/1.03 controller for Trackmania and has been overhauled to support the Modescript Gamemodes of Trackmania² in Maniaplanet and changed to run with PHP/7.x or newer.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
