# gloomy/pager-bundle

> 标签: crud, datagrid, pager, pagination, paginator, paging, rest

## 简介

Paginator/datagrid/crud bundle for Symfony2 with advanced filtering & sorting

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
