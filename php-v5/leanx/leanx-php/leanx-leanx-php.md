# leanx/leanx-php

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

This package is designed to seamlessly integrate LeanX into your applications, providing a streamlined solution for handling all your payment processes with ease and efficiency.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
