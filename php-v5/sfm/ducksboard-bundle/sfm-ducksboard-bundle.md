# sfm/ducksboard-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, ducksboard

## 简介

Symfony2 Bundle to integrate an app with Ducksboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
