# ddebin/yii-ses-feedback

> 标签: aws, email, ses, sqs, yii

## 简介

Process SES feedback notifications so that your Yii app can react to Email Bounces and Complaints

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
