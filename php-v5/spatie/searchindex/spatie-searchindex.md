# spatie/searchindex

> 标签: algolia, elasticsearch, index, laravel, search

## 简介

Store and retrieve objects from Elasticsearch or Algolia

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
