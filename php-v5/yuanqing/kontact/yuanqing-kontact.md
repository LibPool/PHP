# yuanqing/kontact

> 标签: ajax, contact, email, form, mail

## 简介

An extensible contact form in PHP and vanilla JavaScript/AJAX.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
