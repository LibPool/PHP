# marxo/preslov

> 标签: Serbian, cyrillic, i18n, l10n, language, localization, translation, translit

## 简介

Transliterate Serbian from Cyrillic to Latin and vice versa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
