# wdnb/flarum-bbembed

> 标签: bilibili, flarum, video

## 简介

Seamlessly embed bilibili videos in your Flarum forum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
