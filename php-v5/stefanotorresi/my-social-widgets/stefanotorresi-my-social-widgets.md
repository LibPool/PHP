# stefanotorresi/my-social-widgets

> 标签: module, social, widgets, zend, zf2

## 简介

A Zend Framework 2 module providing view helpers to display widgetized informations retrieved from various social network APIs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
