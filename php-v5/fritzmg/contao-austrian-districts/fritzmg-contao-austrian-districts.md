# fritzmg/contao-austrian-districts

> 标签: austrian, contao, districts, element, field, form

## 简介

Contao extension to provide a form field for Austrian districts.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
