# salaros/is-biz-mail

> 标签: address, blacklist, checker, domain, email, free

## 简介

isBizMail tells you whether a given email address is free (gmail.com, yahoo.es, yandex.ru etc) or not

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
