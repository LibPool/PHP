# bashkarev/email

> 标签: arf, email, fbl, mail, mailparse, mime, mime-parser

## 简介

Faster MIME Mail Parser could be used to parse emails in MIME format.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
