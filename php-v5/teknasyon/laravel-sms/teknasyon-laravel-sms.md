# teknasyon/laravel-sms

> 标签: Simple, callfire, eztexting, labsmobile, laravel, mozeo, nexmo, receive, send, sms, text messages, twilio, verimor

## 简介

A fork of simplesoftwareio/simple-sms with Verimor driver.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
