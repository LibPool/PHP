# cinghie/yii2-masonry

> 标签: masonry, yii2, yii2 masonry, yii2 masonry asset

## 简介

Asset Bundle to include Masonry on your Yii 2 project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
