# cinghie/yii2-paypal

> 标签: payments, paypal, yii2 payments, yii2 paypal, yii2 paypal payments

## 简介

Yii2 Paypal to manage Paypal Payments on your Yii 2 project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
