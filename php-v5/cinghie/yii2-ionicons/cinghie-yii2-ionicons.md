# cinghie/yii2-ionicons

> 标签: ionicons, ionicons yii2, yii2, yii2 icon, yii2 ionicons

## 简介

Asset Bundle to include Ionicons on your Yii 2 project

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
