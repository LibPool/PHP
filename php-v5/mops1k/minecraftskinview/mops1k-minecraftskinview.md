# mops1k/minecraftskinview

> 标签: cape, cloak, head, minecraft, preview, skin

## 简介

Minecraft skin preview converter (support HD skins and capes)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
