# zircote/oauth2

> 标签: auth, library, oauth2

## 简介

OAuth2 Library, this is by no means complete nor is the test coverage optimal, mileage may (will) vary.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
