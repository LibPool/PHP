# staspiv/knp-time-bundle

> 标签: bundle, date, descriptive time, knp, knplabs, time

## 简介

Knplabs time bundle makes your dates look sensible and descriptive

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
