> 标签: TCA, TYPO3 CMS, ajax, cdn, content replacer, error, error handler, exception, exception handler, migration, slugs, switchableControllerActions, tools, translations

## 简介

Collection of some small tools.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
