# admad/cakephp-sequence

> 标签: cakephp, list, orm, sequence

## 简介

Sequence plugin for CakePHP to maintain ordered list of records

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
