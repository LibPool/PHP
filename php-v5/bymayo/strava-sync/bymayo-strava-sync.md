# bymayo/strava-sync

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, strava sync

## 简介

Connect to Strava with oAuth and sync activities etc to Craft CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
