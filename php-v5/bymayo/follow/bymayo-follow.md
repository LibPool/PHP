# bymayo/follow

> 标签: Craft, Follow, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Let users follow other users and elements (Categories, Tags etc) to generate lists & feeds.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
