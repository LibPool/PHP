# raffaelj/cockpit-imageresize

> 标签: cockpit, image, optimize, resize

## 简介

Resize and optimize uploaded images automatically in Cockpit CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
