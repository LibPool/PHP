# friendsofhyperf/compoships

> 标签: Hyperf composite keys, Hyperf relationships, hyperf, v3.2

## 简介

Hyperf relationships with support for composite/multiple keys.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
