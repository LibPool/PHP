# lorenzo/cakephp-email-queue

> 标签: cakephp, cron, email, preview, queue

## 简介

Queue, preview and and send emails stored in the database

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
