# wbrowar/contentstats

> 标签: Craft, cms, content stats, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Display info about your site's entries, categories, users, and assets.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
