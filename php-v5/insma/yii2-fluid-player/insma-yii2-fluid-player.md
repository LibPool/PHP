# insma/yii2-fluid-player

> 标签: Video player, cdn, extension, fluid player, online player, video, web video player, yii2

## 简介

Wrapper for fluid player to use it with Yii2 Framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
