# atkrad/hurad-seo

> 标签: Hurad, keywords, meta, meta description, meta keywords, plugins, seo

## 简介

Optimizes your Hurad blog for SEO (Search Engine Optimization).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
