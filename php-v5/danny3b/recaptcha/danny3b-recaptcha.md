# danny3b/recaptcha

> 标签: agiletechvn, anhtuank7c, bootstrap, cakephp, captcha, crabstudio, google, recaptcha

## 简介

Easily use Google Recaptcha in CakePHP 3.2+ projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
