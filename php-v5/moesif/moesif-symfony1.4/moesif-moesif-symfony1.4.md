# moesif/moesif-symfony1.4

> 标签: Moesif, api, debugging, log, logging, middleware, symfony

## 简介

Moesif Collection/Data Ingestion Middleware for Symfony1.4

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
