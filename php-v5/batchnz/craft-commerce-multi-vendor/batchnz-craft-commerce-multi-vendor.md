# batchnz/craft-commerce-multi-vendor

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft commerce multi vendor, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Support multi vendors on your Craft Commerce store.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
