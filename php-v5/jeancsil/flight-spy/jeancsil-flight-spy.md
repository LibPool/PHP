# jeancsil/flight-spy

> 标签: Trip, flights, skyscanner, travel

## 简介

Watch as much flights you want and get notified for YOUR budget. (E-mail and Slack).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
