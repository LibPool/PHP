# dunckelfeld/cra-by-fy

> 标签: Craft, cms, crabyfy, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Deploys craft fed gatsby frontend to netlify

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
