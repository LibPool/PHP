# venveo/view-count

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, popular entries

## 简介

Keep track of view counts over a period of time to show popular entries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
