# innerspirit/seo-bundle

> 标签: seo

## 简介

A Symfony bundle to generate SEO meta tags. Re-published to make it stable  & installable.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
