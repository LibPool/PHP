# tigerman/doctrine-generate-models-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, database, orm

## 简介

Symfony2 bundle to generate model classes from your mapping information from YAML.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
