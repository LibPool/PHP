# torann/laravel-pjax-middleware

> 标签: flight, flightjs, laravel, pjax, popstate, pushState

## 简介

Enable the use of PJAX in Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
