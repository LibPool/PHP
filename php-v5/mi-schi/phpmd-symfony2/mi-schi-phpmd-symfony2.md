# mi-schi/phpmd-symfony2

> 标签: Symfony2, clean code, extends, mess detection, phpmd, rules

## 简介

Extended mess detection rules from phpmd for Symfony applications.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
