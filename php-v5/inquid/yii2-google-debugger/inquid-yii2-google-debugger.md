# inquid/yii2-google-debugger

> 标签: debugger, extension, google, google cloud, logger, target, yii2

## 简介

Use Google Cloud Logger into your Yii projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
