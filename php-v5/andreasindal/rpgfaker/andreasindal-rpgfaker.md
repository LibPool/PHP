# andreasindal/rpgfaker

> 标签: faker, fantasy, generator, name, rpg

## 简介

RPGFaker is a library to generate names for your fantasy games.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
