# rolandalla/laravel-email-auditing

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The Email Auditing Service tracks all outgoing emails from your system, storing them in the `email_auditing` table.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
