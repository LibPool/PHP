# slince/shipment-tracking-ems

> 标签: E包裹, E特快, E邮宝, ePacket, shipment, 国际EMS

## 简介

A flexible and shipment tracking library for E邮宝、E包裹、E特快、国际EMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
