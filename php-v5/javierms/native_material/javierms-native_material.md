# javierms/native_material

> 标签: drupal, fonts, gdpr, local, material icons, material symbols, privacy

## 简介

Google Material Symbols Outlined and Material Icons fonts hosted 100% locally with zero-FOUT optimization and native HTML head preloading.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
