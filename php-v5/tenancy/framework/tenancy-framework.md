# tenancy/framework

> 标签: aws, gce, laravel, multi-tenant, saas, tenancy

## 简介

Creating multi tenant saas from your Laravel app with ease

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
