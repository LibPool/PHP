# pittacusw/magento2-currency-symbol-position

> 标签: Magento 2 Extension Free, currency symbol, currency symbol position, magento, magento 2, magento 2 extension

## 简介

Magento 2 change the currency symbol position from left to right or from right to left

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
