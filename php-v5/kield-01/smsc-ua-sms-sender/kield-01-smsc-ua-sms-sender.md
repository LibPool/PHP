# kield-01/smsc-ua-sms-sender

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

SMS/MMS/E-Mail/Viber/Voicemail messages sending with SMSC.UA service for CakePHP 3.3.x

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
