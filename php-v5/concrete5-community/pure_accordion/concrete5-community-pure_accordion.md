# concrete5-community/pure_accordion

> 标签: accordion, addon, cms, concrete, concrete5, concreteCMS, package, tab, tabs

## 简介

A simple accordion with permalinks for ConcreteCMS/concrete5

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
