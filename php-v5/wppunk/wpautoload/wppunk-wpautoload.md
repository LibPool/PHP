# wppunk/wpautoload

> 标签: autoload, composer, wordpress, wordpress-plugin, wp, wp-config, wppunk

## 简介

Autoload your classes, interfaces and traits in WordPress

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
