# pju/kirby-webhook-field

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A Kirby 3 plugin providing a field to easily use webhooks, e.g. for deploying your site to services such as Netlify

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
