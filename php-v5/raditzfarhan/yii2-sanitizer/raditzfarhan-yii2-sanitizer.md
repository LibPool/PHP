# raditzfarhan/yii2-sanitizer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Yii2 Sanitizer is an easy way to sanitize or filter your inputs.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
