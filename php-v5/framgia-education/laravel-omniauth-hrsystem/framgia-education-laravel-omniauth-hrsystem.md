# framgia-education/laravel-omniauth-hrsystem

> 标签: Framgia, laravel, oauth

## 简介

Framgia Education Laravel wrapper around OAuth 2 libraries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
