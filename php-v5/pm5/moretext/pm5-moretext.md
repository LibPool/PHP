# pm5/moretext

> 标签: cli, ipsum, lorem, placeholder, test

## 简介

Get dummy Chinese text (lorem ipsum) with Handlino serivce.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
