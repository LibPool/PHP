# mayoz/laravel-omnipay

> 标签: gateway, laravel, merchant, omnipay, payment

## 简介

An Omnipay bridge for Laravel 5.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
