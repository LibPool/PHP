# deft/iso3166-utility

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Library to convert ISO3166 alpha-2 country codes to their alpha-3 counterpart and vice versa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
