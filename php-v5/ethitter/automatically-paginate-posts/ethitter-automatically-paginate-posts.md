# ethitter/automatically-paginate-posts

> 标签: Quicktag, nextpage, paginate

## 简介

Automatically paginate posts by inserting the `<!--nextpage-->` Quicktag.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
