# imagecms/imagecms

> 标签: cms, e-commerce

## 简介

Content managment system in two editions: Corporate (Free) & Shop (Proprietary)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
