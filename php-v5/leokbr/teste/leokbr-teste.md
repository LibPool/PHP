> 标签: codemage, date, easy, functions, hour, schedule, time

## 简介

A simple way to work with date and time in PHP. It contains a powerful schedule maker.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
