# simonschaufi/macina-banners

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Banner management tool with banner placement on frontend, banner rotation, scheduling and statistics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
