# tinybeans/easy-form

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, easy form

## 简介

You can make a contact form easily. Just submit a form, and an email will be sent.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
