# lanin/laravel-extend-seeder

> 标签: csv, extension, framework, laravel, seeding

## 简介

Extension for Laravel seeder. Seed your database with CSV data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
