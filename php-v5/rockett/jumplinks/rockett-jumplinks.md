# rockett/jumplinks

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Manage permanent and temporary redirects in ProcessWire 2.6.1+. Version 2 in the works.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
