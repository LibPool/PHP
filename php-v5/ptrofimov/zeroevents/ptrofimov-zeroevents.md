# ptrofimov/zeroevents

> 标签: events, illuminate, laravel, zeromq, zmq

## 简介

Events between processes. Built on top of Illuminate\Events and ZeroMQ

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
