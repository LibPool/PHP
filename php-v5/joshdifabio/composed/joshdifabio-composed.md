# joshdifabio/composed

> 标签: composer, dependency, package

## 简介

Easily parse your project's Composer configuration, and those of its dependencies, at runtime.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
