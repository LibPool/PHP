# sbtheke/cookies

> 标签: SBTheke, TYPO3 CMS, consent, cookie, cookies, dsgvo

## 简介

Show cookie consent, optional with possibility to deactivate some or all of them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
