# daaner/unique-digits

> 标签: laravel, unique digits, unique number

## 简介

Creating a digital value for different needs (ticket numbers and others) for PHP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
