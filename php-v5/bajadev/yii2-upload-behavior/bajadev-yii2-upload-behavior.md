# bajadev/yii2-upload-behavior

> 标签: Behavior, file, image, resize, thumb, upload

## 简介

Yii2 file/image upload behavior for ActiveRecord

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
