# christianessl/js_logger

> 标签: extension, typo3

## 简介

JSLogger for TYPO3. Log errors and exceptions users are encountering in the frontend to the TYPO3 backend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
