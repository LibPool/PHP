# bolechen/nova-activitylog

> 标签: activity, laravel, log, nova

## 简介

A tool to activity logger to monitor the users of your Laravel Nova.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
