# lcjury/administrative

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Administrative provides an easy way to scaffold political administrative division (like, region, provinces, communes) to your app.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
