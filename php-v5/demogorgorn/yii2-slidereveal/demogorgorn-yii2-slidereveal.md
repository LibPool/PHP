# demogorgorn/yii2-slidereveal

> 标签: extension, offcanvas, slide, widget, yii2

## 简介

Yii2 wrapper for SlideReveal, A jQuery plugin to show a side panel by sliding from the left or right of the page (https://github.com/nnattawat/slideReveal)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
