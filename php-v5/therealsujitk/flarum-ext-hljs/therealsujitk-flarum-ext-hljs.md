# therealsujitk/flarum-ext-hljs

> 标签: code, highlight.js, highlighting, hljs, syntax

## 简介

Integrate your forum with highlight.js.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
