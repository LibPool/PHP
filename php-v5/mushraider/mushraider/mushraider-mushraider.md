# mushraider/mushraider

> 标签: Planner, cakephp, calendar, event, gaming, mmorpg, raid

## 简介

Modern Raid Planner for Modern Raiders on top of CakePHP 2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
