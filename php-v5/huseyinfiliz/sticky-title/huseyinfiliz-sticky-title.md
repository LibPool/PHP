# huseyinfiliz/sticky-title

> 标签: discussion, flarum, header, navigation, scroll, sticky, title

## 简介

StickyTitle keeps discussion titles always visible while scrolling in Flarum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
