# presta/initializer-bundle

> 标签: Symfony2, bundle, initializer, prestaconcept

## 简介

Presta Initializer bundle :  Initialize common tasks as we do at Prestaconcept

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
