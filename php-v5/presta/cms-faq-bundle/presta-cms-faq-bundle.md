# presta/cms-faq-bundle

> 标签: FAQ, Symfony2, bundle, cms, prestaconcept, symfony-cmf

## 简介

PrestaCMS FAQ bundle : Adds FAQ in PrestaCMS. FAQ are structured by category and administrated with SonataAdmin

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
