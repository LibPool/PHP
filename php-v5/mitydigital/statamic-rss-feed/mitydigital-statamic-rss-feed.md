# mitydigital/statamic-rss-feed

> 标签: atom, rss, statamic

## 简介

A fully-featured RSS and Atom feed generator for Statamic.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
