# zeus/email

> 标签: email, imap, mail, php, pop3, smtp

## 简介

A lightweight and efficient email handling library for PHP, providing SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
