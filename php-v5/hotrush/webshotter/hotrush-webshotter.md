# hotrush/webshotter

> 标签: gd, image, imagick, jpg, laravel, lumen, pdf, phantomjs, png, screenshot, webpage, webshot

## 简介

Make screnshots of webpages with phantomjs

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
