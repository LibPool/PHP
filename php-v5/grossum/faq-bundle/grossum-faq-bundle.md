# grossum/faq-bundle

> 标签: faq bundle

## 简介

This bundle added faq entity with fields: enabled, question, answer, position, createdAt and updatedAt

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
