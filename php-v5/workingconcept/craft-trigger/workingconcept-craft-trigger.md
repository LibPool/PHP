# workingconcept/craft-trigger

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craft3, craftcms, deployments, utility

## 简介

Utility for triggering deployments when they're needed.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
