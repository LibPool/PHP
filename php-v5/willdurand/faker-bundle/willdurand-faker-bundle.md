# willdurand/faker-bundle

> 标签: data, faker, fixtures

## 简介

Put the awesome Faker lib into the DIC and populate your database with fake data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
