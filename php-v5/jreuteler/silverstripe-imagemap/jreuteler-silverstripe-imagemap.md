# jreuteler/silverstripe-imagemap

> 标签: extension, silverstripe

## 简介

A Silverstripe DataExtension that allows to simply klick on the relevant parts of an image to create the according imageMap-coordinates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
