# scribe/clockwork-bundle

> 标签: Clockwork, Scribe, Symfony2

## 简介

Symfony bundle to handle interaction with clockworksms.com for SMS messaging

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
