# teamupdivision/fusionexport-upd

> 标签: client, export, fusioncharts, fusionexport

## 简介

PHP Client for FusionExport by UPD

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。

- 官网：https://www.fusioncharts.com/fusionexport
- 源码仓库：https://github.com/teamupdivision/fusionexport.git
- Packagist 页面：https://packagist.org/packages/teamupdivision%2Ffusionexport-upd
