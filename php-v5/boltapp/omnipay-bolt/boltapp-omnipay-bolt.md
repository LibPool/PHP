# boltapp/omnipay-bolt

> 标签: bolt, gateway, merchant, omnipay, pay, payment

## 简介

Omnipay driver for Bolt payments

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
