# oofbar/membership

> 标签: Craft, cms, commerce, craft-plugin, craftcms, membership, subscriptions

## 简介

Give your users special access based on their Commerce Subscriptions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
