# zurb/foundation

> 标签: JS, css, framework, mobile-first, responsive, sass, scss

## 简介

The most advanced responsive front-end framework in the world.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
