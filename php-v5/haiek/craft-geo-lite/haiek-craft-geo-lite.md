# haiek/craft-geo-lite

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft geolite, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

Fetches location based on IP from the GeoIP2 Lite database

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
