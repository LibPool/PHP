# justuno/social

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Justuno offers optimized marketing campaigns with customizable design, powerful targeting & segmentation, and A/B testing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
