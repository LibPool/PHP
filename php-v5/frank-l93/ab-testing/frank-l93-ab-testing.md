# frank-l93/ab-testing

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Sticky, weighted A/B testing for Statamic entries with conversion tracking and Control Panel reporting.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
