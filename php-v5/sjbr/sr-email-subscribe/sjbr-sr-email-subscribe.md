# sjbr/sr-email-subscribe

> 标签: address, email, frontend, subscription, typo3

## 简介

Email address subscription for TYPO3 CMS.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
