# 3dgoo/silverstripe-hcaptcha

> 标签: hcaptcha, silverstripe, spamprotection

## 简介

A spam protector and form field using hCaptcha for Silverstripe

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
