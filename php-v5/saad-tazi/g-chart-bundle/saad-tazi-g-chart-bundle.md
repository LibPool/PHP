# saad-tazi/g-chart-bundle

> 标签: chart, google

## 简介

A super simple Bundle that facilitate the usage of Google Chart Tool, Google Chart Image API and Google Infographics.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
