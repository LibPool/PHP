# codesquatch/twig_extender

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Extends Drupal 7 templating functionality to integrate into PatterLabs seamlessly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
