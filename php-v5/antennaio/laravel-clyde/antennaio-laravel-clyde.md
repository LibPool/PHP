# antennaio/laravel-clyde

> 标签: cache, glide, images, laravel, php, resize, upload

## 简介

Image uploads and manipulation for Laravel, a wrapper around Glide

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
