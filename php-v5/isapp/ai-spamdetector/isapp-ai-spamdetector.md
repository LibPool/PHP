# isapp/ai-spamdetector

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Protect your contact forms from spam and bots using AI. Lightweight, flexible, and powered by OpenAI GPT for smarter filtering.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
