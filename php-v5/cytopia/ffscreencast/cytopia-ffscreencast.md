# cytopia/ffscreencast

> 标签: ffmpeg, screen recording, screencast

## 简介

ffmpeg screencast with video overlay and multi monitor support.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
