# tubssz/u2f-two-factor-bundle

> 标签: Authentication, fido, symfony, two-factor, two-step, yubikey

## 简介

Use U2F-Keys as 2FA for Symfony, using scheb/two-factor-bundle

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
