# yireo/yireo_emailoverride

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Override Magento emails in your app/design/frontend/PACKAGE/THEME/locale folder

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
