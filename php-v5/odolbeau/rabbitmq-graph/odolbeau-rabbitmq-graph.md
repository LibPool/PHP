# odolbeau/rabbitmq-graph

> 标签: dot, graph, graphviz, rabbitmq

## 简介

Draw a graph from your RabbitMQ definitions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
