# sgalinski/sg-video

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

YouTube, Vimeo & Mediathek Video Element - Unified TYPO3 video/mediathek element for YouTube, Vimeo, and local FAL files; output videos, playlists, channels, and groups/showcases, and migrate from sg_youtube and sg_vimeo.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
