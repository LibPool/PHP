# repat/novutec-typosquatting

> 标签: domain, novutec, php, typo, typosquatting

## 简介

A domain name typo finder to determine typos of a domain name.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
