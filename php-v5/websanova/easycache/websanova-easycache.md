# websanova/easycache

> 标签: 5, 5.0, 5.1, cache, caching, collection, collections, easy, easy-cache, easycache, laravel, model, models

## 简介

Simple on demand caching extension for Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
