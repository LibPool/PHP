# fof/mailtrap-mail

> 标签: flarum, mail, mailtrap, sandbox

## 简介

Send your forum mail through the Mailtrap API, or catch it in a Mailtrap sandbox

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
