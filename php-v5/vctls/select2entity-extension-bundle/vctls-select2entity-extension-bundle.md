# vctls/select2entity-extension-bundle

> 标签: autocomplete, select2, symfony, typeahead

## 简介

An extension of TetranzSelect2EntityBundle with a couple helper classes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
