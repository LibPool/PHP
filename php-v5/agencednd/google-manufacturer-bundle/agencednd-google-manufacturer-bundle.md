# agencednd/google-manufacturer-bundle

> 标签: PIM, akeneo, attribute, dnd, e-commerce, ecommerce, google-manufacturer

## 简介

Agence Dn'D bundle to export products to Google Manufacturer

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
