# flarumchina/flarum-ext-mediaembed-pro

> 标签: embed, facebook, flarum, media, twitch, twitter, youtube

## 简介

Embed third-party content in your Flarum.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
