# gustavs-gutmanis/craft-symfony-dump

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, debugging, dump, symfony-dump, twig

## 简介

Symfony `dump` twig filter for Craft CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
