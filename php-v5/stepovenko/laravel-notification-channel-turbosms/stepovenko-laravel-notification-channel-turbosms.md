# stepovenko/laravel-notification-channel-turbosms

> 标签: laravel, notifications, turbosms

## 简介

Turbo SMS Notification channel for Laravel 5.3+.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
