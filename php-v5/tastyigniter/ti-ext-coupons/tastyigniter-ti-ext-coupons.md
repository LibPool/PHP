# tastyigniter/ti-ext-coupons

> 标签: coupons, discounts, tastyigniter

## 简介

Extend the checkout experience with coupons and discount codes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
