# wrep/notificato

> 标签: apns, aps, ios, notifications, push, push notifications, pushnotifications

## 简介

Takes care of push notifications in your PHP projects.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
