# sbwerewolf/stopwatch

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Primitive stopwatch with simple start and stop for measurement of every single time periods and whole time

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
