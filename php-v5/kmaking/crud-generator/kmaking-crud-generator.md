# kmaking/crud-generator

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

KMA CRUD Generator used to generate resourcefull controller with views, Route, UrlPresenter and Laratable

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
