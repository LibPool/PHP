# ubl/supportchat-switch

> 标签: chat, cms, extension, switch, typo3

## 简介

Supportchat switch to enable chat at frontend for Typo3 Supportchat extension

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
