# jeffkarney/blade

> 标签: blade, laravel, render, template, view

## 简介

The standalone version of Laravel's Blade templating engine for use outside of Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
