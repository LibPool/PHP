# zegert/cakephp-two-factor-auth

> 标签: auth, cakephp, two-factor

## 简介

CakePHP auth component and provider fot two-factor authentication

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
