# wowo/wowo-newsletter-bundle

> 标签: beanstalkd, newsletter, queue

## 简介

Newsletter Bundle for Symfony2 (with messages queuing in beanstalkd)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
