# quellabs/annotation-reader

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A PHP annotations reader that parses, processes, and caches docblock annotations for classes, properties, and methods.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
