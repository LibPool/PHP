# arlisaha/open-mind-parser

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A parser for ".mm" files (openMind format) to build objects out of it in order to then draw it or export it in another format.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
