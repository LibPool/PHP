# coradite/yii-google-analytics

> 标签: analytics, google, server

## 简介

Google certificate-based authentication in server-to-server interactions with google analytics (Forked from widop/google-analytics)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
