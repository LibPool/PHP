# redbeed/openoverlay

> 标签: Eventsub, OpenOverlay, bot, irc, laravel, twitch

## 简介

Your self hosted service for twitch web-based overlays and custom bot with Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
