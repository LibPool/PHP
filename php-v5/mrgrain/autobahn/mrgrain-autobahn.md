# mrgrain/autobahn

> 标签: boilerplate, composer, stack, wordpress, wp

## 简介

Autobahn is the Ueberholspur for your Composer-based WordPress stack. German Engineering.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
