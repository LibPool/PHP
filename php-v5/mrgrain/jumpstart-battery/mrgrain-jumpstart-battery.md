# mrgrain/jumpstart-battery

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The battery to Jumpstart your WordPress plugin. It's a collection of helper classes for faster development.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
