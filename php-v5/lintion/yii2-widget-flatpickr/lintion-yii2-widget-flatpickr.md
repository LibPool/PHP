# lintion/yii2-widget-flatpickr

> 标签: datetime picker, extension, plugin, widget, yii2

## 简介

easy and powerful datetime picker for yii2-widget-flatpickr.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
