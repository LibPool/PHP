# mindkomm/woocommerce-email-text-edit

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Modify email texts used in your WooCommerce emails without having to edit email templates.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
