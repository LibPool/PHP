# smso/sms-helpers

> 标签: sms encoding, sms length, sms message, smso

## 简介

A collection of helpers to handle SMS messages used in several projects around SMSO

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
