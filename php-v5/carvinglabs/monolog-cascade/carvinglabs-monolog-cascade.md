# carvinglabs/monolog-cascade

> 标签: cascade, log, logger, monolog, monolog extension, monolog plugin, monolog utils

## 简介

Monolog extension to configure multiple loggers in the blink of an eye and access them from anywhere

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
