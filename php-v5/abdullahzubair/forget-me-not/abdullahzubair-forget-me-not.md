# abdullahzubair/forget-me-not

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

ForgetMeNot - Exclude selected modules, themes, and Drupal core from update checks.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
