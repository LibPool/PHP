# payscrow/payscrow-gateway

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Payscrow Premium Escrow Service enables your store to easily connect with the Payscrow payment Service in Nigeria

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
