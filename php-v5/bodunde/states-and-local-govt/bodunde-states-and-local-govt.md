# bodunde/states-and-local-govt

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Populates your DB with all the states in Nigeria and their local governments. It also creates the models for you

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
