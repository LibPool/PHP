# rezzafr33/apaiio-laravel

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A simple Laravel 5 service provider for including the ApaiIO / Amazon Product Advertising PHP Library.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
