# bjornjohansen/do-upgrade-plans

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Upgrade your DigitalOcean droplets to better plans with the same cost.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
