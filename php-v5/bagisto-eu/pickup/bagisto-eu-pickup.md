# bagisto-eu/pickup

> 标签: bagisto, bagisto-shipping-methods, delivery-options, ecommerce, laravel, pickup, shipping, shipping-methods, store-pickup

## 简介

Allow customers to pickup orders from physical stores

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
