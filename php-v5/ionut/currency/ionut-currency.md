# ionut/currency

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Framework-agnostic currency conversion based on the European Central Bank rates. Integrates seamlessly with Laravel.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
