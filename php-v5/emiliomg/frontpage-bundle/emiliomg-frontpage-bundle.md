# emiliomg/frontpage-bundle

> 标签: Simple, frontpage

## 简介

Simple frontpage if your project needs no frontpage but a 404-page is just plain ugly.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
