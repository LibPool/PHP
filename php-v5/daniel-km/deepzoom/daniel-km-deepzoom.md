# daniel-km/deepzoom

> 标签: ImageMagick, OpenLayers, deepzoom, dzi, gd, image, imagick, openseadragon, stand-alone, tiled, vips, zoom, zoomable

## 简介

Stand-alone deepzoom tile generator (format DZI) for use with OpenSeadragon, OpenLayers and various viewers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
