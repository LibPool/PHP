# dorsetdigital/silverstripe-photoswipe-elemental

> 标签: gallery, images, silverstripe

## 简介

Allows embedding of a simple Photoswipe-powered gallery on a page

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
