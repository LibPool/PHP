# tkisilverstripeteam/tkicontactinfo

> 标签: address, contact, location, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds location and contact details to SilverStripe objects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
