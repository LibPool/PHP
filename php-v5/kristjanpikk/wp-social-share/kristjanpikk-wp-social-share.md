# kristjanpikk/wp-social-share

> 标签: wordpress

## 简介

A WordPress plugin which adds simple social share icons to posts/pages without any bloat

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
