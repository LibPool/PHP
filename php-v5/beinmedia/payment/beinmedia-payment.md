# beinmedia/payment

> 标签: laravel, payment

## 简介

Laravel package to implement MyFatoorah, Tap, PayPal payment gatways and paypal recurring billing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
