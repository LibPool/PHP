# tipi/php-apple-signin

> 标签: JWK, apple, in, ios, jwt, php, sign

## 简介

A simple library to decode and parse Apple Sign In client tokens.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
