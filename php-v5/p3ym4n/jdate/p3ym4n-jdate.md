# p3ym4n/jdate

> 标签: Jalali, date, datetime, hijri, laravel, shamsi, time

## 简介

A simple php date converter from Jalali to Georgian calendar and vice versa.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
