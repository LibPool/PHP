# mojoblanco/domainos

> 标签: domains, emails, laravel, mojoblanco, security

## 简介

A laravel package for blacklisting or whitelisting specific email providers or domains

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
