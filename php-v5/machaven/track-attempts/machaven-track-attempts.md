# machaven/track-attempts

> 标签: auth, cache, laravel, limit, otp, redis, track

## 简介

Track and limit any auth, OTP, or any other type of action.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
