# saada/yii2-factory-muffin

> 标签: factory, testing, yii2

## 简介

A Yii2 wrapper for league/factory-muffin with Gii generators.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
