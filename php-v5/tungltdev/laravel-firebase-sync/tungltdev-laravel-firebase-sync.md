# tungltdev/laravel-firebase-sync

> 标签: eloquent, firebase, laravel

## 简介

Synchronize your Eloquent models with a Firebase realtime database.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
