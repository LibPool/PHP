# adamb/salesstaff

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A revolving list of sales staff to enable emails/sales to be distributed between sales staff based on hours of work

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
