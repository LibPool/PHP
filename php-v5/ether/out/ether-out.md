# ether/out

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, csv, export

## 简介

A super-simple plugin for creating CSV exports of you Craft data

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
