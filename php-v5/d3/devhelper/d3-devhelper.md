> 标签: OXID, d3, developer, development, devhelper, e-mail, eshop, modules, template, thankyou, tools

## 简介

Manipulate shop standard processes for easier development

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
