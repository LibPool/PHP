# seguce92/filemanager

> 标签: bootstrap, filemanager, laravel, masterializecss, tinymce 4

## 简介

Filemanager integrator with tinyMCE for laravel framework.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
