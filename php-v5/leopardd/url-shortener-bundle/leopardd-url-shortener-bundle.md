# leopardd/url-shortener-bundle

> 标签: bitly, googl, link, shortener, symfony, symfony-bundle, url

## 简介

Symfony Bundle to generate and redirect short url

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
