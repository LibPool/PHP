# superbig/craft-url2png

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, url2png

## 简介

Lets you embed screenshots of webpages via the URL2PNG API.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
