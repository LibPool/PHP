# gdpr-extensions-com/gdpr-extensions-com-grh

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Show your Google reviews in your website in a GDPR conform reviews header content element. Powered by GDPR-extensions.com dashboard.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
