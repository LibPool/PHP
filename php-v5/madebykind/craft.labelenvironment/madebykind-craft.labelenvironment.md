# madebykind/craft.labelenvironment

> 标签: Craft, craftcms

## 简介

Provides colour-coded labels in your Craft CMS control panel to help easily distinguish between environments

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
