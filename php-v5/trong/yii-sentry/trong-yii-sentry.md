# trong/yii-sentry

> 标签: error, exception, log, sentry, yii

## 简介

Sentry for the Yii PHP framework. Fork of crisu83 with actual Raven version.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
