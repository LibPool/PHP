# aashro/at-kider

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Kider theme is a colorful and engaging TYPO3 template designed for kider, preschools, and early childhood education centers.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
