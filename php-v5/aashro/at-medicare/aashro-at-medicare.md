# aashro/at-medicare

> 标签: Aashro, TYPO3 CMS, medicare

## 简介

A professional TYPO3 healthcare template with appointment booking, doctor profiles, timelines, and testimonials—ideal for clinics and medical services.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
