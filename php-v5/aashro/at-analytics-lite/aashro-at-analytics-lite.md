# aashro/at-analytics-lite

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Privacy friendly analytics for TYPO3 CMS. It is a lightweight and simple analytics extension that respects user privacy and does not use cookies.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
