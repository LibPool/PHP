# pennebaker/craft-architect

> 标签: Architect, Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms

## 简介

CraftCMS plugin to generate content models from JSON/YAML data.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
