# sallycms/less-mixins

> 标签: css, less, lesscss, lessphp, mixin

## 简介

Collection of shared LESS mixins for all apps and addOns.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
