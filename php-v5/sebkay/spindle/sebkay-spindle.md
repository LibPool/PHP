# sebkay/spindle

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

An easy to use Slim 4 starter app. Comes with a DI container, Eloquent ORM, Twig for templating, VueJS and Sass.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
