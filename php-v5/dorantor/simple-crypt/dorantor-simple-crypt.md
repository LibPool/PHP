# dorantor/simple-crypt

> 标签: cipher, openssl, symmetric

## 简介

Very simple lib for symmetric en/decrypt messages so they could be sent through untrusted channels.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
