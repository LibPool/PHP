# falconmedia/magento2-deployer-ci

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Deployer v7 + GitLab CI templates and tasks for Magento 2 projects (hostless, variable-driven).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
