# huettig-und-rompf/webhub-proxy

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

A proxy script to include the Hüttig & Rompf Webhub Snippets on your website in complete privacy

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
