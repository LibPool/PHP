# teneleven/geolocator-bundle

> 标签: geo locator, geo search, location search, locator, map, store locator

## 简介

Makes your models geo-searchable on a google map

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
