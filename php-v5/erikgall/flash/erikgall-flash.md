# erikgall/flash

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Laravel 5.1 flash messages based off of Laracasts/Jeffery Ways flash notifcations.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
