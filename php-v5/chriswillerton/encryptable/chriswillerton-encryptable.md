# chriswillerton/encryptable

> 标签: encryption, laravel, model

## 简介

Trait for use with Laravel 5 models allowing easy encryption/decryption of attributes.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
