# impressa/yetipress

> 标签: composer, foundation, genesis, stack, wordpress, wp

## 简介

WordPress boilerplate based on Roots/Bedrock, Bones for Genesis 2.0 and ZURB Foundation

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
