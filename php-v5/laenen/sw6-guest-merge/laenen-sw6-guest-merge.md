# laenen/sw6-guest-merge

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Merge guest orders into authenticated Laenen customer accounts via verified email or trusted CSR action.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
