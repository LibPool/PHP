# stinger/ckeditor-lineutils-bundle

> 标签: CKEditor, lineutils, symfony

## 简介

Symfony2 Bundle to integrate the CKEditor plugin lineutils

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
