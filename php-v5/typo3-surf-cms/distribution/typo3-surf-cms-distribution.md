# typo3-surf-cms/distribution

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

TYPO3 Surf Distribution featuring tools to accomodate TYPO3 CMS deployments

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
