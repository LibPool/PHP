# jweiland/contributory-calculator

> 标签: TYPO3 CMS, contributory calculator, typo3

## 简介

Contributory Calculator - Extension to calculate contributories in the frontend.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
