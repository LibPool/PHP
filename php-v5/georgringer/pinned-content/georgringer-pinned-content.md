# georgringer/pinned-content

> 标签: extension, typo3

## 简介

Enhances the TYPO3 content element wizard with pinned items, templates, and personal pin lists for faster editing.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
