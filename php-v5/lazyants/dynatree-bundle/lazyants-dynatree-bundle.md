# lazyants/dynatree-bundle

> 标签: JS, dynatree, symfony, tree

## 简介

Integration of jquery.dynatree.js in Symfony 2

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
