# mihaeu/sub-modifier

> 标签: srt, subs, subtitle

## 简介

SubModifier modifies subtitle files by increasing or decreasing and by re-indexing the subtitle entries.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
