# manovotny/wp-mailchimp-signup-form-popup

> 标签: email, mailchimp, popup, signup, wordpress

## 简介

A MailChimp signup form popup for WordPress.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
