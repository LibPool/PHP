# studioespresso/craft-seeder

> 标签: Craft, cms, craft-plugin, craftcms, seeder

## 简介

Easy entries seeder for Craft CMS

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
