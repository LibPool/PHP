# power-captcha/integration-typo3

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

POWER CAPTCHA protects your TYPO3 forms against bots and unauthorized persons. GDPR compliant!

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
