# ngekoding/terbilang

> 标签: currency, money, numbers, terbilang, to, words

## 简介

A library to convert numbers and currency to words in Indonesian.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
