# thecodingcompany/cocsearch

> 标签: api, chamber of commerce, kvk, php

## 简介

Find company info directly from the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK data). PHP5 library

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
