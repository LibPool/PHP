# cteye/social-connect-bundle

> 标签: contao, facebook, google, linkedin, symfony, twitter

## 简介

Social Connect for Contao 4 connect your news archive with social media channels like facebook, twitter, google and much more. Its easy to maintain your professionel articles and share them.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
