# datatables.net/datatables.net-rowgroup-dt

> 标签: Row Grouping, datatables, jquery, rowgroup, table

## 简介

RowGroup for DataTables with styling for DataTables

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
