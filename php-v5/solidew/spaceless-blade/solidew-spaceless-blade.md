# solidew/spaceless-blade

> 标签: blade, laravel, spaceless, twig

## 简介

Adds the @spaceless tag to Blade. (works like in Twig)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
