# daxslab/yii2-sharelinks-widget

> 标签: extension, network, share, social, widget, yii

## 简介

Yii2 widget for popular social networks 'share link' generation

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
