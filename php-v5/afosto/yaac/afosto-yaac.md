# afosto/yaac

> 标签: ACME, acmev2, afosto, encrypt, lets, letsencrypt, v2

## 简介

Yet Another ACME client: a decoupled LetsEncrypt client

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
