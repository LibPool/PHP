# riclep/flarum-lettermint-mail

> 标签: driver, flarum, lettermint, mail

## 简介

A Lettermint mail driver for Flarum 2.0

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
