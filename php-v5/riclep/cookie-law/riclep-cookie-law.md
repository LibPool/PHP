# riclep/cookie-law

> 标签: Laravel 4, cookie law, cookies, eu, laravel

## 简介

A very basic cookie law package for legacy Laravel 4 projects

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
