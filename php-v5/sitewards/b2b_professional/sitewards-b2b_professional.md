# sitewards/b2b_professional

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

The Sitewards B2B Extension extends your webshop with some essential functions to realize a B2B Store

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
