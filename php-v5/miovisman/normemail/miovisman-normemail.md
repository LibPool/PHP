# miovisman/normemail

> 标签: ban, canonical email, email, normalization

## 简介

Normalization of email for bans, for request cache to SFS or for checking the uniqueness of new users.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
