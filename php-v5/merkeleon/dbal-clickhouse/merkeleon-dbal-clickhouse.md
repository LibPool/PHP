# merkeleon/dbal-clickhouse

> 标签: clickhouse, dbal, doctrine, driver

## 简介

Doctrine DBAL driver for ClickHouse

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
