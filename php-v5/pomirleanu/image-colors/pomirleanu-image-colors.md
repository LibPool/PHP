# pomirleanu/image-colors

> 标签: image-colors, pomirleanu

## 简介

Simple way to get the colors from a given image, will give tou the percentage and the colors hex.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
