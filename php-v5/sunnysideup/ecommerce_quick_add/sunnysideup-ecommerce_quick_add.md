# sunnysideup/ecommerce_quick_add

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, e-commerce, ecommerce, fast, fast add, list of products, quick add, shop, silverstripe

## 简介

Adds a page type to e-commerce that allows you to view all products at once for 'quickly' adding them to your cart

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
