# sunnysideup/electric-vehicle-calculator

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, clean, cms, electric, environment, ev, javascript, silverstripe, vehicle

## 简介

Silverstripe module that works out when it makes financial sense to switch to an electric vehicle

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
