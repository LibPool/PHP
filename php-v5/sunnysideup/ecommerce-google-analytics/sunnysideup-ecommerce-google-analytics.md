# sunnysideup/ecommerce-google-analytics

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, analytics, cms, e-commerce, ecommerce, google, seo, shop, silverstripe, tracking

## 简介

Add Google Analytics order tracking to your silverstripe e-commerce website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
