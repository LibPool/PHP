# sunnysideup/fontresizer

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, fontresizer, silverstripe

## 简介

adds the 'resize text' functionality to your Silverstripe website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
