# sunnysideup/ecommerce_product_variation

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, colours, e-commerce, ecommerce, product variation, shop, silverstripe, sizes, sub-types, types, variation

## 简介

create product variations within e-commerce (e.g. if you have a sock you can now sell a red XL sock or a small blue sock).

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
