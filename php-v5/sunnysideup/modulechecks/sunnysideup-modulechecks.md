# sunnysideup/modulechecks

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, addons, automation, cms, composer, license, modules, packagist, quality control, silverstripe

## 简介

Checks your modules on packagist, github, and the silverstripe add-ons site

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
