# sunnysideup/vimeoembed

> 标签: Silverstripe-CMS, cms, silverstripe, vimeo, vimeoembed

## 简介

allows you to embed vimeo videos into your silverstripe website

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
