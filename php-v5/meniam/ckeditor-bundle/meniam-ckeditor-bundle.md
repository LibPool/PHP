# meniam/ckeditor-bundle

> 标签: CKEditor, bundle, editor, symfony, symfony3, wysiwyg

## 简介

Symfony2 bundle for easy integration of the CKEditor WYSIWYG

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
