# titanium_iridium/yii2-oci8

> 标签: oci8, yii

## 简介

Oci8 Driver for Yii2 (created by Apa Oww, forked/modified by bogdik and titanium_iridium)

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
