# sanderjn/statamic-agentic

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Agentic — a Statamic starter kit that makes your site safely editable by an AI agent.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
