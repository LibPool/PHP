# kmielke/calendar-extended-bundle

> 标签: calendar, contao, events, exceptions, fullcalendar, registration, vacations

## 简介

Contao 4 LTS calendar extension bundle. Better recurring events and exceptions.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
