# dmzx/forumcountdown

> 标签: extension, forumcountdown, phpbb

## 简介

Forum Countdown extension display a countdown in your forums, just below the navbar. It can be configured in ACP.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
