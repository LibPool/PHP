# vfixtechnology/bagisto-facebook-pixel

> 标签: bagisto, bagisto-extension, bagisto-plugin, ecommerce-tracking, facebook-ads, facebook-pixel, laravel, laravel-ecommerce, meta-ads, meta-pixel

## 简介

Meta (Facebook) Pixel integration for Bagisto — tracks PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, ViewCart, InitiateCheckout, AddShippingInfo, AddPaymentInfo, CompleteRegistration, SignIn, Lead and Purchase standard e-commerce events.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
