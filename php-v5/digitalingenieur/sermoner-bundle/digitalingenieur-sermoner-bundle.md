# digitalingenieur/sermoner-bundle

> 标签: contao, podcast, sermon

## 简介

A administration tool to manage audio files of sermons, workshops, seminars, etc.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
