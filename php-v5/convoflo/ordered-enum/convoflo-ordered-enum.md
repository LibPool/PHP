# convoflo/ordered-enum

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Give your enums an order. Provides utilities such as greaterThan, lessThan, min, max and more.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
