# arberd/geonames

> 标签: eloquent, geonames, laravel, location

## 简介

A collection of models and commands to get all the power of GeoNames in Eloquent ORM.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
