# edisonthk/google-oauth-laravel4

> 标签: Lusitanian, google, laravel, oauth, php

## 简介

Optimize artdarek/oauth-4-laravel with Google OAuth 2.0 for laravel 4

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
