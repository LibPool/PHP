# saeven/zf2-poeditor

> 标签: edit, gettext, php, po, twig

## 简介

Front end to gettext that lets you translate your PHP and Twig files in a snap

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
