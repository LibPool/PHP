# drupal/ai_recipe_seo_optimizer

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

AI-powered SEO optimization recipe that extends the Drupal CMS SEO Tools recipe with focus keyword support and AI-driven content optimization.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
