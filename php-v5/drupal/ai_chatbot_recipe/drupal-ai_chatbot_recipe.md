# drupal/ai_chatbot_recipe

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Drupal recipe for AI chatbot with OpenAI and Pinecone integration.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
