# drupal/ai_recipe_comment_spam

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Adds AI-powered spam scoring, reasoning and auto-unpublishing for comments, with a moderation triage view.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
