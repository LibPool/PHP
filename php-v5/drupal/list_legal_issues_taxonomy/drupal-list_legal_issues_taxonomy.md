# drupal/list_legal_issues_taxonomy

> 标签: PHP

## 简介

Provides terms for the LIST: Legal Issues Taxonomy, a taxonomy of legal issues, needs, and situations that people may face.

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
