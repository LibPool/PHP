# blurcreative/elysium-slider

> 标签: banner, cms, plugin, shopware, slide, slider

## 简介

A flexible, reusable and feature rich Slider for Shopware 6

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
