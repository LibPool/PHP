# inimist/cakephp-captcha

> 标签: cakephp, captcha, plugin, recaptcha, security

## 简介

CakePHP Captcha Plugin, Image, Math and Google Recaptcha Support

按 require.php 推断最低支持 PHP 5；已收录于 php-v5, php-v7, php-v8。
